## Question

A client asked whether rising CPC, falling CTR and falling conversion rate during Black Friday were expected or had an underlying cause.

## Answer

Expect higher CPCs and lower CTR during big sales events, because competing bids rise and many shoppers browse without buying. Judge the account on impressions, ad conversion rate and rank over several days rather than on CPC alone. Move budget and dayparting toward converting campaigns during the event.

## Cause

Event-driven competition: more advertisers bid during sales events, so competing bids and the CPCs that follow them rise (first-party). The thread attributes the lower CTR to shoppers browsing and comparing deals; that part is the specialist's explanation, not a first-party rule. The thread found no account fault, and ad CVR and impressions over the prior week had risen.

## Fix

1. Compare CPC, CTR and CVR with the days before the event and with the prior week, not with a single day.
2. Check impressions and ad CVR to see whether visibility and conversion held up.
3. Shift budgets and dayparting toward converting campaigns during the event.
4. Check organic rank on main keywords as a cross-check.

## Verify

Impressions and ad CVR hold or improve over the week even as CPC rises; organic rank stays stable or improves.

## Stop before

Stop before any submission, appeal, case send, refund, price change or listing change unless the operator approved that exact action.
