## Question

A brand contact had created a Creator Connections campaign and a creator already had a sample, and asked how to handle the affiliate link for that creator.

## Answer

Do not build an affiliate link for a creator. Activate the Creator Connections campaign, send the creator the campaign name and ASIN, and have them join it from their Associates or Influencer account and use the campaign's "Get affiliate link". Only links from that campaign earn the campaign commission and show in its reporting.

## Cause

The brand does not create the affiliate link. The creator generates a campaign-specific link from their own Amazon Associates or Influencer account after joining the campaign; Amazon tracks sales through that link and applies the campaign commission.

## Fix

1. Make sure the Creator Connections campaign is live (a new campaign stays editable for 24 hours before it goes live), with the product added and the commission set.
2. Send the creator the product name, ASIN and the campaign name.
3. The creator logs in to Amazon Associates or Influencer and joins the campaign from the Creator Connections area (menu path Promotions > Creator Connections per the agency answer, not verified).
4. The help page says brands cannot choose specific creators and the campaign is visible to all eligible creators; the thread's 'approve her from the brand side' step is not confirmed.
5. The creator clicks "Get affiliate link" on the campaign page and uses that link (creator-side label from the agency answer, not verified).
6. Track clicks, orders and sales in the Creator Connections reporting portal; these orders are not linked to the order management center.

## Verify

The creator appears as accepted in the campaign and attributed clicks or orders show in Creator Connections reporting.

## Stop before

Stop before any submission, appeal, case send, refund, price change or listing change unless the operator approved that exact action.
