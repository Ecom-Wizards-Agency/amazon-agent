## Question

The lead asked a PPC specialist to launch branded campaigns for a newly onboarded brand plus auto campaigns that exclude branded terms so non-branded sales get captured, then Sponsored Brands campaigns.

## Answer

For a new brand, launch branded campaigns per product theme on every spelling variant of the brand name, plus auto campaigns that negate those variants, so branded and non-branded performance stay separate. Leave out out-of-stock products, lift very low bids to at least the suggested bid, and bid above the suggestion on branded terms when needed to win impressions. Add Sponsored Brands early; automatic collections need no custom banner image.

## Cause

Not a fault. A new account needs branded defence and a non-branded discovery layer kept apart, so branded searches do not inflate auto-campaign results.

## Fix

1. Confirm Ads console access for the brand before starting; Seller Central access alone is not enough.
2. When the catalog has many SKUs, group portfolios and campaigns by product theme or parent, matching the catalog structure.
3. Leave out-of-stock products out of the launch.
4. Launch a branded campaign per theme targeting the brand name and its spelling variants (joined, spaced, hyphenated, plural).
5. Launch auto campaigns with the brand name variants as negative keywords to capture non-branded sales.
6. Check bids: lift very low bids at least to the suggested bid, and bid above the suggestion on branded terms if needed to win impressions. Consider leaving out higher-priced product versions that are unlikely to pay back.
7. Launch Sponsored Brands campaigns. Automatic collections build the title and landing page from your products and brand logo, so no custom banner image is needed.

## Verify

Branded campaigns receive impressions on branded terms; auto campaign search term reports show no brand terms.

## Stop before

Stop before any submission, appeal, case send, refund, price change or listing change unless the operator approved that exact action.
