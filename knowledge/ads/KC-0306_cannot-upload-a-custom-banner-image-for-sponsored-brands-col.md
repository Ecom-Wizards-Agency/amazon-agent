## Question

A team member wanted creatives to launch a wave of Sponsored Brands banner ads; the team checked whether custom Sponsored Brands image ads are still possible.

## Answer

Do not plan custom banner artwork for Sponsored Brands collection ads; the builder offers a logo and a title, not a custom image. Put custom creative into Sponsored Brands video or Sponsored Display image ads. Check the campaign builder before briefing designers, because format availability changes.

## Cause

Two team members said custom image creatives are no longer available for Sponsored Brands collection ads. The first-party campaign-builder capture agrees that the collection ad settings offer no custom image field, only ad name, ad title, brand name and logo. It contradicts the thread on headlines: it still offers an AI-generated automated title or a title you write yourself, and it lists Brand Gallery with a lifestyle image under the reserve share of voice goal. The thread does not establish when the change happened.

## Fix

1. Do not brief custom banner artwork for Sponsored Brands collection ads.
2. Confirm the brand logo and choose between the automated ad title and a title you write yourself.
3. For custom creative, brief Sponsored Brands video ads or Sponsored Display image ads instead; Brand Gallery with a lifestyle image exists only under the reserve share of voice goal.
4. Check the live campaign builder before briefing designers.

## Verify

In the Sponsored Brands campaign builder, the collection ad settings show no custom image upload; note whether the ad title still accepts your own text.

## Stop before

Stop before any submission, appeal, case send, refund, price change or listing change unless the operator approved that exact action.
