## Question

Before Prime Day a brand saw only one approved Lightning Deal per product, each for a 12-hour slot on different days, plus a warning on the deal, and worried the products would not carry a Prime deal badge for the whole multi-day event.

## Answer

Plan Prime Day pricing in two layers. A Prime-Exclusive Lightning Deal runs for up to 12 hours in a slot Amazon chooses, and different products can get different days, so add a promotion that covers every event day: a Prime-Exclusive Price Discount for Prime-member pricing, or a Prime-Exclusive Best Deal when the deal badge must show for the whole event. Clear any discount warning on the deal before the event. Whether a Price Discount shows the same badge as a deal is not established.

## Cause

A Prime-Exclusive Lightning Deal runs for up to 12 hours (or until sold out) in a slot Amazon schedules, so it never covers the full event and different products can land on different days. Prime pricing on every event day needs a separate promotion: a Prime-Exclusive Price Discount (a Prime-member-only discount submitted under Price Discounts) or a Prime-Exclusive Best Deal, which the Prime Day capture says runs the entire event with deal badging. The warning on the deal said the discount had to be increased before the event.

## Fix

1. In Deals, open each Prime-Exclusive Lightning Deal and note its scheduled slot, deal price and fees.
2. Resolve any warning on the deal; a warning that asks for a bigger discount means lowering the deal price before the event (operator approval required for any price change).
3. Cover every event day separately: create a Prime-Exclusive Price Discount for the same SKUs under Advertising > Price Discounts (submissions close shortly before the event ends), or schedule a Prime-Exclusive Best Deal before the deal-scheduling deadline when a deal badge for the whole event is required (operator approval required before submitting).
4. Check that the discount or Best Deal shows as approved and covers the full event dates.
5. Tell the brand which product runs its Lightning Deal on which day and which promotion covers the other days.

## Verify

Deals shows each Lightning Deal approved with no warning, and Price Discounts shows the Prime-Exclusive Price Discount active for every event day.

## Stop before

Stop before any submission, appeal, case send, refund, price change or listing change unless the operator approved that exact action.
