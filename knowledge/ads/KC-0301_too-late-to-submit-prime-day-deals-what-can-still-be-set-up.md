## Question

Shortly before Prime Day, the brand owner asked how to prepare the account for the event after the deal scheduling window had passed.

## Answer

If the deal scheduling window for a tentpole event has passed, do not chase deals; run a price discount or Prime-Exclusive Price Discount, which stays open until shortly before the event ends. The agency pairs it with higher Sponsored Products bids and budgets and a Sponsored Display retargeting campaign aimed at product viewers. Plan deals for the next event early, because Best Deals and Lightning Deals are scheduled weeks to months ahead.

## Cause

Best Deals and Lightning Deals must be scheduled weeks to months before the event (scheduling closes well before event week), and Lightning Deals are subject to Amazon selection, so a late start leaves no deal slot. Price discounts and coupons stay open longer: the Prime Day readiness playbook says Prime-Exclusive Price Discounts close shortly before the event ends (it states both six and twelve hours) and coupons can be submitted once the event dates are announced, through the end of the event.

## Fix

1. Check in Seller Central > Advertising > Deals whether any Best Deal or Lightning Deal can still be scheduled for event week; if none, drop deals from the plan.
2. Set up a price discount (or a Prime-Exclusive Price Discount where the product meets eligibility) for the event window; submission is a promotion change and needs operator approval.
3. Raise Sponsored Products bids and budgets for the event period so the discounted offers win more traffic; this is a campaign change and needs approval.
4. Add Sponsored Display retargeting campaigns aimed at shoppers who viewed the products, to bring back pre-event browsers during the event.
5. Revert prices and bids after the event.

## Verify

The discount shows as scheduled or active in the promotions tool for the event dates, and the event-period campaigns show raised budgets and an active retargeting campaign.

## Stop before

Stop before any submission, appeal, case send, refund, price change or listing change unless the operator approved that exact action.
