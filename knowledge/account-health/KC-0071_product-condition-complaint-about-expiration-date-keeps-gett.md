## Question

A listing was deactivated after a product condition customer complaint about the expiration date. The seller had proof the product carries an expiration date, but the appeal kept being denied, so the agency asked its Amazon account manager to escalate.

## Answer

When an expiration-date product condition appeal keeps getting denied, send a complete evidence package rather than repeating the appeal: the product certificate and photos and video of a real unit showing the printed expiry date. Call Account Health support to get a case ID that carries the evidence, and if the account has an Amazon account manager, ask them to forward it and to name the document the reviewers want.

## Cause

Not established in the thread. The appeal reviewers kept denying the appeal without naming the missing document. It was accepted after the agency called Account Health support for a case ID with the certificate, images and video of the expiry date attached and the Amazon account manager forwarded the case to another team; the thread does not show which step or document unlocked it.

## Verify

The listing shows as active again and Amazon confirms the ASIN was reinstated; in the thread this came within a day of the escalated package.

## Stop before

Stop before any submission, appeal, case send, refund, price change or listing change unless the operator approved that exact action.
