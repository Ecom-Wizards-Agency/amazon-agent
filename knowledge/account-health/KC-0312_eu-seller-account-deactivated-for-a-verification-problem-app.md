## Question

The seller account in Germany was deactivated. The agency believed a catalog flag on an unused listing caused it and appealed, while Amazon kept stating it was a verification problem and asking for a virtual verification. The client asked whether to do the verification and whether the in-depth questions asked were normal.

## Answer

When Amazon deactivates an account and says it is a verification problem, complete the requested verification instead of relying only on an appeal against a suspected listing cause. In the source case the virtual verification for an EU marketplace included detailed questions on employees, the country of company registration and personal information, which the agency said is normal under EU rules. Follow up with Seller Support or the account representative once it is done.

## Cause

Amazon stated the deactivation was a verification problem and kept asking for a virtual verification, while the appeal argued a catalog cause. The account was back online after the account holder completed the virtual verification; the thread shows the sequence, not Amazon's confirmation of which step reinstated it.

## Fix

1. Read the deactivation notice and identify what Amazon is asking for; if it names verification, treat that as the blocking step.
2. Have the account holder complete the virtual identity verification Amazon requests. Only the account holder can do this.
3. Expect detailed questions on the business set-up, for example employment contracts, the country of registration and personal details; answer them consistently with the registration documents.
4. After the verification, follow up through Seller Support or the account representative. Case sends need operator approval.
5. Once reinstated, check sales and ads delivery.

## Verify

The account was back online after the virtual verification; the agency then monitored sales and PPC.

## Stop before

Stop before any submission, appeal, case send, refund, price change or listing change unless the operator approved that exact action.
