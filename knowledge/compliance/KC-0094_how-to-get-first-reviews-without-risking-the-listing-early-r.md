## Question

The seller asked about social media groups that offer reviews in exchange for free products, buying reviews, sending a post-purchase review email, and enrolling in the Early Reviewer Program.

## Answer

Use Amazon Vine, not paid reviews or free-product review groups, to get a new product its first reviews. The Early Reviewer Program has ended, and Amazon already sends buyers a review request. Vine requires a Professional account, a brand-registered product with an active FBA offer and fewer than 30 reviews, so have stock in place before you enrol.

## Cause

Not a fault: the Early Reviewer Program no longer exists, Amazon already sends a review request after purchase, and buying reviews or trading free products for reviews breaks Amazon review policy and can lose the listing.

## Fix

1. Do not buy reviews or use groups that give free products for reviews.
2. Check Vine eligibility: Professional selling account, Brand Representative or Reseller role on a brand in Brand Registry, an active FBA listing, fewer than 30 reviews, not an adult or digital product, and an image and a description.
3. Enrol the parent product in Vine once enough FBA stock is in place for the free units; the enrolment needs operator approval because it carries a fee.
4. Rely on Amazon's automatic review request or the Request a Review button; any seller message about reviews must stay neutral and follow the Customer Product Reviews policy.

## Verify

The product shows as enrolled in Vine and Vine reviews start to appear on the detail page.

## Stop before

Stop before any submission, appeal, case send, refund, price change or listing change unless the operator approved that exact action.
