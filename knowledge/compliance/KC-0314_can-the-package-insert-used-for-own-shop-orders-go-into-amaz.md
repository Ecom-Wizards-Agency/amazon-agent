## Question

The brand added an insert to its own-shop orders with a setup guide, QR codes to its own site, and a cashback offer for completing a survey and leaving a review on a third-party review site in exchange for a discount on the next own-shop order. They asked whether the same insert can go into Amazon orders, whether the QR code to the setup guide may stay, and what to put on the second side.

## Answer

Do not reuse an own-shop package insert in Amazon orders. Build a separate Amazon insert that carries only product-use information, with no QR codes or links to external sites, no review requests tied to an incentive and no off-Amazon discount offers. Treat any insert as Amazon-visible content, especially when packaging is transparent.

## Cause

The insert carried elements an Amazon order insert should not carry: a discount tied to leaving a review, QR codes sending Amazon customers to the brand's own site, and an offer for the next off-Amazon purchase. The agency rejected the review-linked offer and any QR code or external link; Amazon's buyer-communication rules bar marketing or promotional contact, including physical mail.

## Fix

1. Do not reuse the own-shop insert for Amazon orders.
2. Remove any review incentive, discount or cashback offer, and any request tied to a review on any platform.
3. Remove QR codes and printed links to external websites, including links to a setup guide hosted on the brand site.
4. Build a separate Amazon insert limited to product-use content such as setup or care instructions.
5. Have the agency review the final text before printing, since the insert may be visible through the packaging.

## Verify

Before printing, check the Amazon insert for any URL, QR code, review mention, discount or cashback offer, and confirm it contains only product-use content.

## Stop before

Stop before any submission, appeal, case send, refund, price change or listing change unless the operator approved that exact action.
