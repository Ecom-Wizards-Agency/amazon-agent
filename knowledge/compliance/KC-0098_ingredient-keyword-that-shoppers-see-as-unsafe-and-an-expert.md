## Question

While approving new listing copy, the client asked whether an ingredient name that some shoppers perceive as unsafe belonged in the title, whether an expert-recommended claim belonged in the bullets or only in A+ Content, and pointed out a bullet that described a feature the product does not have.

## Answer

Put a keyword shoppers may read as unsafe in backend search terms instead of the title, so it still indexes without hurting click-through; backend terms still have to comply with policy. Leave subjective endorsement claims such as expert or professional recommendations out of bullets unless they are verifiable, and if one appears in images or A+, keep the evidence on file and never present it as a quote or testimonial. Check every feature in drafted bullets against the real product before publishing.

## Cause

Visible copy carries both perception risk (a keyword that reads as a negative) and substantiation risk (a subjective endorsement claim Amazon may ask to be backed by evidence); a drafted bullet also described a feature the product lacked.

## Fix

1. Move a keyword that shoppers may read as a negative, but that is not itself a prohibited or restricted term, out of the title and into backend search terms, so it still indexes without being shown.
2. Keep subjective expert-endorsement claims out of the bullets. Show an endorsement in images or A+ only when the brand holds the substantiating documents, and never as a review, quote or testimonial, which the detail page rules bar from titles, bullets, descriptions and images.
3. Check every feature claim in the bullets against the actual product and rewrite any that describe a feature it lacks.
4. Get the client's confirmation of the revised copy before updating the listing.

## Verify

The live title no longer shows the sensitive ingredient, the term still indexes from the backend, the bullets carry no endorsement claim, and every feature in the bullets matches the product.

## Stop before

Stop before any submission, appeal, case send, refund, price change or listing change unless the operator approved that exact action.
