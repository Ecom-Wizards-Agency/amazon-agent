## Question

While reviewing new listing and A+ drafts for a weight-management tablet supplement, the client asked whether hunger or appetite suppression claims are banned. The client wanted to keep softer wording such as support in managing appetite because hunger is the main buyer pain point.

## Answer

Treat appetite suppression, fat burning, metabolism boosting and unrealistic weight loss as claims to remove from weight-management supplement listings, in images and A+ content as well as text, especially once Amazon has named them. Make the listing fully compliant before starting ads, and decide on any softer benefit wording only afterwards, as a separate step.

## Cause

Amazon remediation wording, quoted in the thread by the agency lead, names three claim types to remove: content suggesting the product suppresses appetite or burns fat, unrealistic weight loss claims in images, title and description, and metabolism-boosting claims. The thread does not show which notice or product that wording came from. The agency first suggested synonyms; the client chose to drop the claims entirely so the listing would be fully compliant before ads.

## Fix

1. Collect the exact remediation wording Amazon sent and treat each point as a checklist.
2. Remove every appetite-suppression, fat-burning, metabolism-boosting and unrealistic weight loss statement from title, bullets, description, images and A+ content.
3. Treat softened synonyms for the same effect as a separate, later decision rather than part of the compliance fix.
4. Get the client to approve the revised copy and image text before replacing the images (approval gate).
5. Start advertising only after the listing is fully compliant.

## Verify

Re-read title, bullets, description, every image and every A+ module against the remediation points; no appetite, fat-burn, metabolism or weight loss amount wording remains before campaigns launch.

## Stop before

Stop before any submission, appeal, case send, refund, price change or listing change unless the operator approved that exact action.
