## Question

A US seller preparing to send stock to Amazon Germany asked whether German product labelling is required from launch when the packaging is English with only a German manual inside, plus what else is needed (trademark, entity, FNSKU barcodes).

## Answer

Before shipping to an EU store, check the label language. The agency advised a local-language label or a translation sticker on the product, and treated an English label with a local-language manual inside as not enough. Plan the EU trademark, the EU Responsible Person on the label and the category certificates in parallel. Create the target-store listing first and take FNSKU labels from it.

## Cause

Per the agency's reading of EU labelling rules (no local first-party capture states it), the product label must be in the target market's language or at least carry a translation sticker on the product. The captured Sell Globally FAQ confirms that a US trademark is not enforceable in the EU for Brand Registry and that products must be labelled with an EU Responsible Person. Each country can ask for different documents.

## Fix

1. Check the product label language; if it is not German, add a German translation sticker with the required label information before shipping.
2. File or confirm an EU or German trademark for Brand Registry in the EU stores.
3. Collect the certificates the category needs (for example CE and RoHS for electrical products) and name an EU Responsible Person on the label.
4. Decide which legal entity sells in Germany; a different entity from the US one is possible.
5. Copy the listing to Amazon DE and print the DE FNSKU labels only after the DE listing exists.

## Verify

Packaging carries German label text or a translation sticker, the EU Responsible Person is on the label, and the DE listing is created and approved before inbound.

## Stop before

Stop before any submission, appeal, case send, refund, price change or listing change unless the operator approved that exact action.
