## Question

A listing came back after a compliance issue on its copy, and the agency designer rebuilt the image set. The brand then asked to add expert endorsements, award badges, a third-party test badge and a customer-count headline.

## Answer

Treat every badge, award, expert endorsement and customer count in listing images as a claim you may have to substantiate. Keep the certificate, award confirmation, logo usage rights and a verified count on file before publishing, state expert roles without implying medical approval, and hold a test badge until the certificate shows what was tested. Leave out testimonials, quotes and seller-specific offers such as an off-Amazon money-back guarantee, which Amazon's image and detail page rules exclude.

## Cause

Amazon's detail page rules bar reviews, quotes and testimonials in images and require claims that do not mislead. The image guide also bars claims such as free shipping and selling-partner-specific information in images. The per-badge judgments in the thread are the agency designer's assessment, not an Amazon decision: an expert image can imply medical approval, a test badge implies a specific certified result, and awards and logos need usage rights.

## Fix

1. Expert image: keep it only if the experts' advisory roles and qualifications are confirmed; use full names and avoid implying medical approval (residual rejection risk remains).
2. Customer count: use a neutral headline only if the number is verified.
3. Awards: use a neutral award headline, keep only verified award badges and show third-party test badges separately.
4. Hold any certification or test badge until the testing document is in hand and states what was tested.
5. Drop seller-specific offers such as an off-Amazon money-back guarantee; the image guide bars selling-partner-specific information and claims like free shipping in images.
6. Have the brand approve the final set, then upload (operator approval).

## Verify

Every badge, award, expert and number in the image set maps to a document on file (certificate, award confirmation, logo rights, verified count).

## Stop before

Stop before any submission, appeal, case send, refund, price change or listing change unless the operator approved that exact action.
