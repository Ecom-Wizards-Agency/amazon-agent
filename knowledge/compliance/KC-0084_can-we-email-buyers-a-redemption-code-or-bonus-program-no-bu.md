## Question

The client wanted to give every buyer access to a companion program and proposed emailing a redemption code with each order; the agency said email would not be Amazon compliant.

## Answer

Do not use buyer emails or messages to deliver codes, bonuses or programs: Amazon allows contacting buyers only to complete the order or answer service questions, and prohibits marketing contact by email, mail, phone or otherwise. Describe an included extra on the detail page, and treat any package insert that sends buyers to an outside portal as unverified until checked against the communication guidelines.

## Cause

Amazon only allows contacting buyers to complete the order or answer customer service questions; contacting them for marketing or promotion, by email or otherwise, is prohibited.

## Fix

1. Do not email or message buyers a code, bonus or portal invite through Buyer-Seller Messaging or any other channel.
2. If the program is part of the product, describe it on the detail page so buyers know it is included.
3. In-package delivery of access (insert, QR code) was not discussed or verified in the thread; check it against the communication guidelines and with the agency lead before printing, because content that sends buyers to an outside portal or collects their data may breach policy.

## Verify

No buyer messages are sent for the program.

## Stop before

Stop before any submission, appeal, case send, refund, price change or listing change unless the operator approved that exact action.
