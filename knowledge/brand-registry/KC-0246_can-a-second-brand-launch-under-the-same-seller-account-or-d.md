## Question

A client planning a second brand asked whether it can be launched under the existing seller account, what the pros and cons are, and whether one seller account can hold multiple stores.

## Answer

Launch an additional brand inside the existing seller account rather than opening a second one. Amazon allows one Seller Central account per region unless there is a legitimate business need for another. One account can list several brands, each with its own Brand Registry enrollment and Brand Store. Check first that the entity behind the account is the right owner for the new brand, and that the brand's trademark is registered or filed.

## Cause

Not a fault. A seller account is the business-level container; brands are attached to it through the brand portfolio, so one account can list and register several brands.

## Fix

1. Confirm the legal entity behind the existing seller account is the one that should sell the new brand (tax residence and ownership are a business decision for the client).
2. Confirm the new brand has a registered or pending trademark before applying for Brand Registry.
3. Add the brand to the existing account's brand portfolio (Brands > Manage your brands) and enroll it in Brand Registry from that account.
4. Keep each brand's Brand Store and advertising set up per brand inside the same account.

## Verify

Brands > Manage your brands lists both brands in the Brand portfolio of the one seller account.

## Stop before

Stop before any submission, appeal, case send, refund, price change or listing change unless the operator approved that exact action.
