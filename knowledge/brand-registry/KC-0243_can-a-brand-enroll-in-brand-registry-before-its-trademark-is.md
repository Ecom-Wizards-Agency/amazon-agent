## Question

A brand had only a foreign trademark, from an office not listed in the Brand Registry form, and a US filing still pending behind earlier conflicting filings. The team asked whether there was a workaround to enroll the brand before the US mark was decided.

## Answer

You do not need a registered trademark to enroll in Brand Registry: a pending application in the country of enrollment works, entered with its application (serial) number. A trademark from another country's office does not cover enrollment in this marketplace, and pending marks are accepted only from some offices, so check the country-specific list. A pending mark that resembles an existing store brand can still be rejected until it registers.

## Cause

Brand Registry accepts an active registered or pending trademark in each country where the brand enrolls, or a pending filing made through IP Accelerator. A pending mark is entered with the application number issued by the trademark office. The foreign mark could not be used because it came from an office outside the country of enrollment, and the form did not offer that office.

## Fix

1. Get the trademark application (serial) number for the pending filing from the trademark attorney and confirm the record on the trademark office search.
2. In Brand Registry, choose to enroll a brand with a pending or registered trademark and enter the serial number, selecting the pending status if no Verify button appears.
3. Upload a real photo of the product or packaging with the brand name permanently affixed.
4. When asked for the role, choose Seller.
5. Expect a possible Uniqueness Verification rejection if a similar brand already exists in the store; then wait for registration.

## Verify

The application shows Pending review, then Approved, on the Brand applications page.

## Stop before

Stop before any submission, appeal, case send, refund, price change or listing change unless the operator approved that exact action.
