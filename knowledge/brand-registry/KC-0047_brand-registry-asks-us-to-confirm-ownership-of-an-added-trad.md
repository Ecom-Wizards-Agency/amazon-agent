## Question

After a US trademark was added to an enrolled brand, Brand Registry asked the brand owner to confirm ownership. The owner opened the confirmation page, received only a verification code and did not know what to do with it.

## Answer

When Brand Registry asks you to confirm ownership of an added trademark, the verification code goes to the trademark correspondent on file, not into a field on your side. Copy the code from Manage intellectual property > Requests > Confirm ownership and email it to the contact listed there, usually the filing attorney. The correspondent has 30 days to confirm, after which the trademark shows as Connected.

## Cause

Amazon verifies an added trademark through the trademark correspondent listed at the trademark office, usually the attorney who filed it. The code shown on the Confirm ownership page is not entered by the brand owner; it must be emailed to that correspondent, who uses it to confirm ownership.

## Fix

1. In Brand Registry, go to Manage > Manage intellectual property and open the Requests tab.
2. Find the trademark and click Confirm ownership, then Copy code.
3. Check the email address shown next to Contact; this is the trademark correspondent, often the filing attorney.
4. Email the verification code to that correspondent, using Copy suggested email for the instructions (the brand owner sends this, not the agency).
5. Ask the correspondent to complete the confirmation within the 30-day window, then wait for Amazon's email.

## Verify

The trademark moves from the Requests tab to the Connected tab in Manage intellectual property.

## Stop before

Stop before any submission, appeal, case send, refund, price change or listing change unless the operator approved that exact action.
