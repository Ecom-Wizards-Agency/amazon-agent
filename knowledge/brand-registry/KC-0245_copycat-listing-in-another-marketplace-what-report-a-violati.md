## Question

A brand owner spotted a copycat listing of their product in a second marketplace and asked the agency to get it removed.

## Answer

To report a copycat listing through Brand Registry, the reporter needs the Rights Owner or Registered Agent role, so get the agency invited first. If the mark is not registered for that store, only the US, Canada and South Africa stores accept a common-law report. That report needs the first-use-in-commerce date (earlier than the copycat's first offer), an example of the mark in use, the goods it covers and a completed test buy with its order ID. Collect these and the reporter's contact details from the brand owner before you open the form.

## Cause

Removing a copycat through Brand Registry needs a Report a Violation submission from a user with the Rights Owner or Registered Agent role on the brand. The agency had no Brand Registry access until the brand owner, as Administrator, invited it. The form in the thread asked for the trademark's first-use date and the reporter's name. The common-law route (US, Canada and South Africa stores, unregistered mark) asks for those fields. The thread does not establish whether the report used that route or whether a test buy was made. Rights owners without Brand Registry access can also use the public Report Infringement form.

## Fix

1. Ask the brand owner to invite the agency user to Brand Registry with a protection role that can use Report a Violation (Rights Owner or Registered Agent); only an Administrator can assign roles.
2. Open Report a Violation, search the copycat ASIN, select the complete ASIN or the specific offer, click Report selected and choose Trademark infringement.
3. If the trademark is not registered for that store (common-law route, US, Canada and South Africa stores only), select Not one of these trademarks and answer No to Is the trademark registered?. Then enter the first-use date (it must be earlier than the date the copycat was first offered), an example of the mark in use and the goods it covers. Answer Yes to the test-buy question and enter the test-buy order ID; without a test buy the report cannot be submitted.
4. Before starting, collect the first-use date and the reporter's contact details from the brand owner. For the common-law route, also get a test-buy order ID.
5. Operator approval: submit the report only after the brand owner confirms the details are correct.

## Verify

The report appears under submitted reports in Brand Registry; check later whether the copycat listing or offer is removed. The outcome was not recorded in the thread.

## Stop before

Stop before any submission, appeal, case send, refund, price change or listing change unless the operator approved that exact action.
