## Question

A client forwarded an inbound inquiry about selling in Canada and asked the agency whether expanding the brand to Canada was worth it already.

## Answer

A US FBA seller can test Canada without sending stock there, either by shipping from the US or by enrolling eligible offers in Remote Fulfillment with FBA. Amazon's Canada page says in-country FBA and PanAmericas deliver in 1-2 days, against 5-8 days with Remote Fulfillment, at lower fees, and PanAmericas moves eligible high-demand Remote Fulfillment selection into Canada for you. Treat remote fulfillment as the low-commitment entry, and get explicit approval before enrolment because it publishes offers in a new marketplace.

## Cause

Not a fault. Canada can be served from US inventory, either by shipping from the US or by enrolling the US FBA offers in Remote Fulfillment with FBA, so expansion does not require a separate Canadian inventory pool at the start.

## Fix

1. Confirm the products may be sold in Canada; Remote Fulfillment with FBA also needs the US offers to be FBA.
2. Choose the route: ship orders from US stock, or enrol eligible ASINs in Remote Fulfillment with FBA (NARF); compare it with PanAmericas and in-country FBA on Amazon's Canada expansion page.
3. Get the client's go-ahead before enrolling, because enrolment creates live Canadian offers (operator approval).

## Verify

Canadian offers show as active for the enrolled ASINs and orders route to US fulfillment centres.

## Stop before

Stop before any submission, appeal, case send, refund, price change or listing change unless the operator approved that exact action.
