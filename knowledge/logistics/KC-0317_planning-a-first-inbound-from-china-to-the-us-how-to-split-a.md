## Question

A client preparing a launch inbound from China to the US asked for a freight forwarder recommendation and planned to split the order between air and sea so some stock reaches Amazon quickly.

## Answer

For a launch inbound from China to the US, consider shipping a small share by air to get stock live and sending the bulk by sea. Amazon Global Logistics ships ocean cargo from China directly to US fulfillment centres with integrated tracking in Seller Central, so price it before hiring a separate forwarder. Get units per carton, carton size, weight and carton count from the factory first, because no quote is possible without them.

## Cause

Not a fault. The agency recommended Amazon Global Logistics (AGL) for the ocean leg because it is booked directly inside Seller Central rather than through a separate forwarder, and asked for carton data before quoting.

## Fix

1. Decide the split: a small air portion to get launch stock live quickly and the bulk by sea. The share is a business decision, not a rule.
2. For the sea portion from China, consider Amazon Global Logistics, which ships ocean cargo from China to US fulfillment centres with tracking in Seller Central; follow Amazon's AGL onboarding and booking guides rather than looking for a separate forwarder contact.
3. Collect master carton data from the factory or warehouse first: units per carton, carton dimensions, carton weight and number of cartons.
4. Create the shipment plans with that carton data and compare the AGL quote with the air option before booking (booking needs operator approval).

## Verify

The AGL booking for the sea portion shows the carton details and a quote that can be compared with the air option.

## Stop before

Stop before any submission, appeal, case send, refund, price change or listing change unless the operator approved that exact action.
