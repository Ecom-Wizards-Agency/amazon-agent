## Question

A brand customer-experience lead, now answering Amazon messages through a helpdesk, asked how to handle FBA orders that customers report as not received, lost, stuck or damaged.

## Answer

For an FBA order that is late, lost or damaged, direct the customer to Amazon customer service, because Amazon handles customer service, refunds and replacements for FBA orders. Keep your own investigation process for seller-fulfilled orders. Any reply must follow Amazon communication guidelines.

## Cause

Not a fault; for FBA orders Amazon provides the customer service and handles refunds and replacements, so the seller team does not investigate them like its own warehouse orders.

## Fix

1. Confirm the order is Fulfilled by Amazon.
2. Reply to the customer, within Amazon communication guidelines, that Amazon handles delivery issues for this order and direct them to Amazon customer service.
3. Do not route FBA orders into the seller warehouse fulfillment-support process.

## Verify

The customer is pointed to Amazon customer service and no seller-side replacement is sent.

## Stop before

Stop before any submission, appeal, case send, refund, price change or listing change unless the operator approved that exact action.
