## Question

After expanding to Germany, the German detail pages kept showing the English images from the US listings even though localized images had been uploaded and a reset of the listing images was forced.

## Answer

When a listing in a new marketplace keeps showing another marketplace's images after you uploaded localized ones, another contribution is likely winning the display. Connect the brand to Brand Registry in that region with the regional trademark, ask support to apply your images, and escalate to the specialised catalog team if the change does not show. Start before stock arrives, because each step can take days.

## Cause

The new marketplace's pages kept displaying the image contribution coming from the original marketplace's account, so the seller's localized uploads did not display. The thread does not establish exactly why. The images changed only after the brand was connected to Brand Registry in that region with the regional trademark and the case was escalated to a specialised catalog team, so missing regional Brand Registry authority is the likely cause, not a confirmed one.

## Fix

1. Upload the localized images in Seller Central for the new marketplace and confirm the backend shows them.
2. If the live page still shows the other marketplace's images, open a Seller Support case asking Amazon to apply the seller's images (operator approval before submitting).
3. Connect the brand to Brand Registry in that marketplace region, using the regional trademark registration number.
4. Wait the period support quotes for the change to reflect (support asked for 72 hours in this case).
5. If the images still do not update, escalate through the account manager or partner rep to the specialised catalog team (named CIRA in the thread) with the Brand Registry connection in place.
6. After the fix, upload any remaining image changes and confirm each child page reflects them.

## Verify

Open each detail page on the new marketplace's retail site (with a local delivery postcode set) and confirm the localized images are live.

## Stop before

Stop before any submission, appeal, case send, refund, price change or listing change unless the operator approved that exact action.
