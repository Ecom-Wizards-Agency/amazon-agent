## Question

While creating a listing under an unregistered brand name with a GTIN exemption, the team had to supply real photos proving the brand is permanently on the product. The first submission used a few product photos without the packaging and was not accepted.

## Answer

Send a full photo set the first time. Amazon wants real, unedited photos of the product and of the packaging the customer receives, covering front, back, top, bottom and both sides, with the brand permanently on it and, for an exemption, no GS1 barcode. Fit all sides within the request's image limit. A partial set was rejected and cost a resubmission cycle.

## Cause

Amazon requires real, unaltered photos of all sides of the product and of its packaging, with the brand name permanently stamped, printed, stitched or affixed. The first submission showed only some angles of the product and none of the packaging, and it was not accepted. The agency specialist relaying the rejection read 'packaging' as the final form the customer receives, even a simple bag; the thread does not show whether that reading came from Amazon.

## Fix

1. Photograph the product as delivered to the customer, held in hand or placed on a table, with no digital editing.
2. Cover every side, front, back, top, bottom and both sides, of the product and of its packaging or tags. The MAG GTIN exemption SOP gives a limit of two to nine images, so combine views where needed to cover all sides within it.
3. Make sure the brand name is legible, matches the requested brand exactly (capitalization may differ) and is permanently affixed.
4. For a GTIN exemption, make sure no GS1 barcode appears; if one exists, use it as the product ID instead.
5. Resubmit the request with the new image set (submission needs operator approval).

## Verify

The brand name or GTIN exemption request is approved and the listing can be created under the brand without a product ID.

## Stop before

Stop before any submission, appeal, case send, refund, price change or listing change unless the operator approved that exact action.
