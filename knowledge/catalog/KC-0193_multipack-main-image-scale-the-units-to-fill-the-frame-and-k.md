## Question

An account manager asked a designer to turn single-unit renders into a multipack main image, showing just the units without a quantity badge or information, and asked for the units to fill the canvas with no whitespace at the sides.

## Answer

Build a multipack main image from the actual units the buyer receives, scaled so the group fills about 85% of the frame. Keep text, quantity badges and logos off the main image. State the pack count in the title and in secondary images instead.

## Cause

Amazon main images must show the product covering about 85% of the image, with no text, logos or graphics; a multipack laid out with empty margins or a quantity callout falls short of that.

## Fix

1. Show the exact number of units the buyer receives, arranged together.
2. Scale the group so it fills the frame (about 85% of the image) with no wide side margins on a pure white background.
3. Remove quantity badges, text and information panels from the main image; put the pack count in the title and secondary images.

## Verify

Product fills about 85% of the frame, background is pure white, and the image contains no text or graphics.

## Stop before

Stop before any submission, appeal, case send, refund, price change or listing change unless the operator approved that exact action.
