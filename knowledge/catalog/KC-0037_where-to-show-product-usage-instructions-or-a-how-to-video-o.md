## Question

A brand owner asked how to give shoppers instructions for using a product correctly, worried that without them buyers would think it faulty, return it or leave negative reviews; the instructions existed on the brand website as text and a video.

## Answer

Put usage instructions into the listing images and A+ content, not in a separate field or an external link. A how-to video inside A+ needs Premium A+, which Amazon enables only after a Brand Story is published on every ASIN of the brand and at least five A+ projects were approved in the last 12 months. Publish the Brand Story across the catalog first, then add the video once the Premium A+ banner appears in A+ Content Manager.

## Cause

The detail page has no dedicated instructions field; usage guidance belongs in the listing images and A+ content. Video modules sit in Premium A+, and Premium A+ access is gated: Amazon grants it only after A+ Brand Story is published to every ASIN the brand owns in A+ Content Manager and at least five A+ projects were approved and published in the past 12 months.

## Fix

1. Put step-by-step usage instructions into the listing image stack and standard A+ modules (instruction images).
2. To add the how-to video inside A+, check A+ Content Manager for the Premium A+ access banner.
3. If Premium A+ is not available, publish an A+ Brand Story to every ASIN the brand owns (A+ Content Manager > Start creating A+ Content > Brand Story) and keep approved, published A+ submissions up to the required count in the last 12 months.
4. Wait for the weekly eligibility re-evaluation; when access appears, add the video module (placed after the instruction images) and submit for approval. Submitting A+ content needs operator approval.
5. Meanwhile, the video can also be uploaded as a product video through Upload and manage videos in Seller Central.

## Verify

The A+ Content Manager shows the Premium A+ banner, the published A+ project contains the video module, and the detail page shows the instructions in images and A+.

## Stop before

Stop before any submission, appeal, case send, refund, price change or listing change unless the operator approved that exact action.
