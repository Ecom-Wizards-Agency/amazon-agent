## Question

A brand owner asked why the listing used the basic A+ format instead of Premium A+ or full-width banners, what was needed to qualify, and which image formats to brief the design team on. A participant answered that five products were needed, and a Premium A+ design framework was shared.

## Answer

Premium A+ is not a format you pick; Amazon unlocks it once every ASIN you own has a published Brand Story and you have had at least five A+ projects approved and published in the past 12 months. Count approved projects, not products, and check the Brand Story coverage first. Amazon re-checks weekly and shows a banner in A+ Content Manager when access is granted, and eligibility in one country unlocks it worldwide.

## Cause

Premium A+ is gated by eligibility, not by format choice. Per Amazon, a brand qualifies when every ASIN it owns in A+ Content Manager has a published A+ Brand Story and it has had at least five A+ Content projects approved and published in the past 12 months. Amazon re-evaluates eligibility weekly and turns access on automatically. The thread's own answer, five products, is a loose paraphrase of the project requirement and omits the Brand Story condition.

## Fix

1. Open A+ Content Manager and check whether a Premium A+ banner or creation link is shown; if it is, the brand is already eligible.
2. If not, publish an A+ Brand Story on every ASIN the brand owns in A+ Content Manager.
3. Get at least five A+ Content projects approved and published, following the A+ Content guidelines to avoid rejections.
4. Wait for the weekly eligibility re-evaluation; no contact with Amazon is needed.
5. Brief designers on Premium module sizes (full-width images at 1464 x 600 desktop) instead of the basic 970-wide modules.

## Verify

A+ Content Manager shows the Premium A+ access banner and lets you start a Premium A+ project.

## Stop before

Stop before any submission, appeal, case send, refund, price change or listing change unless the operator approved that exact action.
