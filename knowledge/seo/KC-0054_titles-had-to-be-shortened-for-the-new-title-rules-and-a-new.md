## Question

The agency lead told the client the title had been updated ahead of new title regulations taking effect; the client asked what changed.

## Answer

Keep titles to 75 characters and put the details that no longer fit into the Item highlights field, up to 125 characters of comma-separated phrases. Item highlights is its own field, not a bullet point. Allow 24 to 48 hours after submission before resubmitting or contacting support.

## Cause

Amazon introduced a 75-character title limit and an Item highlights field of up to 125 characters for the descriptive details that no longer fit in the title.

## Fix

1. Shorten the item name to 75 characters or fewer, keeping brand, product type and the key attribute first.
2. Move secondary details into Item highlights as comma-separated phrases, up to 125 characters.
3. Keep Item highlights separate from bullet points.
4. Submit in Manage All Inventory or by flat file and allow 24 to 48 hours for display.

## Verify

After 48 hours the shortened title and the item highlights show in search results and on the detail page.

## Stop before

Stop before any submission, appeal, case send, refund, price change or listing change unless the operator approved that exact action.
