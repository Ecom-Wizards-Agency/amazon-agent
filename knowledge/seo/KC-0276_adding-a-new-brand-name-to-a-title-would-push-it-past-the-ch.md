## Question

A client noticed shoppers searching the new brand name with a product term, while one product title still lacked the new brand name, and asked why it had not been added.

## Answer

When a rebrand or brand-name addition would push a title past the character limit, rewrite the whole title from fresh keyword research rather than guessing which words to drop. Check the current first-party limit first: the newer title requirements capture sets 75 characters for new titles and moves extra detail to Item highlights, while the older detail page rules capture still states a 200-character maximum. Move the displaced keywords to Item highlights, bullets or backend terms.

## Cause

Adding the brand name would have pushed the title over the character limit the agency was working to, so something had to be removed, and choosing what to remove needs keyword data.

## Fix

1. Check whether the title with the brand name added stays within the current title character limit for the category and marketplace.
2. If it does not, do not remove words at random; schedule keyword research for the product.
3. Rewrite the title from that research, keeping the brand name and the highest-value keywords and moving the rest to Item highlights, bullets or backend search terms.
4. Confirm the change with the client before submitting.

## Verify

The rewritten title contains the brand name, stays within the limit, and keeps the top keywords; keyword ranks hold or improve after the change.

## Stop before

Stop before any submission, appeal, case send, refund, price change or listing change unless the operator approved that exact action.
