# Plan your budget with Ads Planner

Learn how to generate and view campaign forecasts in Ads planner.

Updated on May 5, 2026

You must be signed in to a Manager account to use Ads planner.

## Plan your budget

Ads planner can help you plan your campaign budget for an audience opportunity. When you enter a budget amount, Ads planner will calculate key metrics for each ad, including reach percentage, reach, impressions, clicks, and detail page views. Start by opening an existing opportunity in Ads planner.

1. At the top-left corner of the audience opportunity you wish to create a plan for, click **Select ad products and formats**. Ads planner will suggest ad products and formats that are most aligned with your marketing goal.
2. Select the ad products and formats that you wish to include in your plan.
3. Click **Apply** in the top-right corner.
4. After you choose your ad products, each one will appear in its own row. For each selected ad product, input your budget.
5. Once you enter your budget and click **Apply**. Ads planner will generate key metric forecasts. Use this to decide what budget is most likely to achieve your desired results.
6. To view reach curves and edit additional metrics, click **See details**.

## Explore campaign forecasts

For each Ad product or format added to the plan, you can click **See details** to expose the full reach curve and your forecast settings. At the bottom, a summary row shows the combined totals for all selected ad products in your plan. To generate a forecast from new inputs:

1. Set your budget (enter it directly or click a value on the curve).
2. Set your preferred CPC/CPM- by default it's set to an average value based on the performance history of your account in the specific format.
3. Set your preferred CTR, DPVR, and Frequency (DSP formats only).
4. Click **Apply** to reforecast with your new settings.
5. Click **Save changes** to apply your chosen budget and settings to the plan output table.
