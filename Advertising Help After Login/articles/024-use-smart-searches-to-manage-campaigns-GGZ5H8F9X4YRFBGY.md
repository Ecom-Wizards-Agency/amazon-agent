# Use smart searches to manage campaigns

Smart searches helps you quickly find and analyze campaign performance data using natural language queries in campaign manager.

Updated on Sep 26, 2025

Note: This feature is currently in beta and isn’t available to everyone at this time.

Use smart searches to filter, sort, and edit campaigns using natural language, saving you time and effort when managing your campaigns. Type what you're looking for into the search bar to pull up campaign metrics, targets, conversion insights, and more. Learn more about finding your advertising campaigns.

For instance, if you want to find all campaigns that went past their budget recently, you can conduct a search for “campaigns that ran out of budget last week.” Your data will automatically update based on your search.

You can also use smart searches to easily switch between campaign and Sponsored Product targeting views. To do this, include "campaign" or "target" in your prompt, such as "campaigns with orders in last 7 days" or "targets with impressions in the last 3 days."

Tip: You can view your 5 most recent searches when you click the search bar.

## Search prompts and tips

Note: By default, the search bar will use your currently selected date range if not specified in the prompt. It will also default to active campaigns or targets unless otherwise specified.

Smart searches let you use natural language to find the data you need and apply multiple actions with 1 search.

**Example:** If you enter the prompt "increase bids by 3% for SP targets with ROAS>3," the search bar will filter the table to show sponsored product campaigns with targets that have a return on ad spend (ROAS) greater than 3. It will automatically prompt you to increase bids on these campaigns by 3%, but allow you to review before applying the bid increase.

To increase efficiency, use specific searches to combine multiple filters, sorting actions, column additions, and date-range changes into a single prompt. The search bar can filter and sort by any metric or setting in the campaigns or targeting data table, including the date range. For quick date adjustments, try prompts like "last 3 days."

Natural language search examples

- Keywords getting an order this week
- Targets with impressions and no orders

Specific search examples

- Clicks > 15, orders = 0, last 14 days sorted by Clicks descending
- Sponsored Brands, % of Orders NTB > 5%, sorted by % of Orders NTB descending
- Targets, conversion rate > 2%, impressions >100, last 3 days
