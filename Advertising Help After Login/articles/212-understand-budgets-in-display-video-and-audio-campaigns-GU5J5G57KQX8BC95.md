# Understand budgets in Display, Video, and Audio campaigns

Learn how budgets and flights help you set and control campaign spend.

## What is a budget flight?

Your budget is how much you want to spend on all the ad groups associated with a campaign during a flight. A flight is the time period during which ad groups associated with this campaign will deliver.

A campaign can have 1 flight, or multiple flights if you want to allocate different budget amounts to certain date ranges. Flight dates can't overlap, but you can have gaps between flights. When there's a gap between 2 flights, ad groups stop delivering until the next flight starts.

## How budgets work

Flighting allows you to set an overall campaign budget while also controlling when you spend it. You can track how efficiently it is spent as your ads deliver.

You can also...

- Structure your flight budgets to spend more during high traffic events, and less during historically slower periods.
- Set a budget cap to set a limit for how much your campaign can spend each day or monthly.
- Roll over unused budget to your next flight.
- Extend or resume a campaign within 5 days of it ending by adding a new flight.

## Budget rollover strategy

When you set your budget during campaign set up, you can choose a budget rollover strategy. This determines what happens with any leftover budget from completed flights.

Budget rollover affects flights that end after you enable the setting. Flights that completed before that remain unchanged.

- **Don’t change flight budgets**: leftover budget isn’t spent.
- **Roll prior flight unused budget:** leftover budget from the most recently completed flight is added to the next flight's budget.
- **Roll cumulative unused budget**: leftover budget from all previous flights is added to the next flight's budget. This approach is ideal for advertisers aiming to maximize overall budget utilization.

## Budget cap

Set a budget cap to set a limit for how much your campaign can spend each day or monthly. This refers to a calendar day or month, so applying this midday or month will assume that the cap is for the rest of the day or month. Unless you have a hard cap on daily or monthly spend, we don’t recommend setting a budget cap. It may prevent your campaign’s budget from delivering in full, resulting in under-delivery.
