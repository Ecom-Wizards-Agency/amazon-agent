# Stores

Stores are free, self-service branded destinations on Amazon where you can curate content that inspires, educates, and helps customers discover your brand and products.

Updated on Jul 14, 2026

Was this video helpful? Select feedback

Stores are digital storefronts where your brand can engage, convert, and build loyalty with millions of Amazon customers. You don’t need to advertise on Amazon to create Brand Stores. With Stores, you'll gain a dedicated URL for your brand on Amazon. Customers can access your Stores from the dedicated URL, the byline on your product detail page, or your Sponsored Brands and Sponsored Products campaigns. The URL can drive traffic to your store from anywhere on the web, including social media sites.

**Note:** Stores byline is the store link found near the top portion of the product detail pages. Once your store is live and approved, your byline is automatically created **within 4 to 7 days**. Learn more about Stores byline.

Stores are available for sellers enrolled in the Amazon Brand Registry, vendors, and agencies. You don’t need to advertise on Amazon to create a store; however, you must meet our Stores eligibility requirements to participate.

## Benefits of Stores

- Reach new and existing customers and help drive metrics like detail page views, awareness, and consideration.
- Showcase your brand story, highlight bestsellers and new arrivals, or promote events, deals, and products.
- Get daily insights about your store performance, including traffic and sales.

**Tip:**Using Video Reveal in your brand store can help you convey a unique brand message by sharing creative brand content, showcasing a product, or even promoting an upcoming event.

**Create a store**

In the left menu of your advertising console, click **Brand content**, then click **Stores**, then click **Create Store**. For guidance on how to get started, visit Create your Store.
