# Understand remarketing audience segments

Learn how purchases and views remarketing strategies help you target audiences based on purchase and browsing behaviors.

Updated on Aug 11, 2026

You must sell products or services on Amazon to use remarketing audiences in **Display** campaigns.

## What is a remarketing audience segment?

Remarketing audience segments help you engage Amazon customers based on their historical behaviors during a lookback window that you specify. You can use purchase behavior, views behavior, or both. Both strategies are available only to advertisers that sell products and services on Amazon.

- **Purchases remarketing** targets audiences based on past purchase behaviors on Amazon. This can help you: Drive repeat purchases and build customer loyalty Cross-sell to existing customers Reach new audiences
- **Views remarketing** targets audiences based on product detail pages they've viewed on Amazon. This can help you: Drive awareness during new product launches Encourage product consideration Convert missed sales by re-engaging customers who haven't yet purchased

**Example**: you can reach an audience that has purchased household supplies under $50 in the past 14 days (purchases remarketing), or an audience that has viewed children's running shoes under $250 in the past 30 days (views remarketing).

## How remarketing segments work

To add a remarketing audience:

1. Choose a strategy: purchases or views.
2. Choose a lookback window.
3. Add audience categories and/or dynamic segments. **Categories:** customers who purchased or viewed products within a specific category on Amazon during your selected lookback window. You can further refine these audiences based on brand, price band, Prime eligibility, or star rating. **Dynamic segments:** your advertised products or similar/related products.

Adding categories can help increase reach by driving discovery and visibility. You can refine these audiences based on brand, price band, Prime eligibility, or star rating. If you select a category audience that is too small, you'll receive a message indicating that the audience is too narrow. To broaden your category audience, consider a different category, remove some refinements, or increase your lookback window.

## Lookback windows

Lookback windows let you reach audiences based on their behaviors during a specific window of time. Purchases remarketing supports lookback windows up to 365 days; views remarketing supports up to 90 days.

**Note:** All lookback windows include consumers currently browsing content related to the specified categories.
