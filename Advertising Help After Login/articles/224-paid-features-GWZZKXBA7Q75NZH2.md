# Paid features

Paid features is the premium subscription model for Amazon Marketing Cloud (AMC).

Updated on Feb 25, 2026

Paid features provides options for deeper analysis of your audiences, campaigns, and outcomes. Navigate to your advertiser instance to subscribe to paid features (beta) offerings. You’ll have access to the paid features your administrator would have subscribed your instance to. Learn more about eligibility and availability of paid features here.

**Tip:**We recommend using the Amazon Marketing Cloud data sources to understand field information and definitions.

## Subscribe to paid features (beta)

Follow these steps to subscribe to some of the premium offerings AMC has to offer.

1. Go to the **Paid features (beta)** tab to view a listing of all paid features that AMC offers.
2. Click **View example in Use cases**to view use cases for each particular paid feature. You can click **Get a 60-day free tria**l to experiment with the paid feature.
3. Click on**See options**to review the **Total monthly price**.
4. Specify the billing account for your subscription. See the Billing and payments dashboard section of Manager accounts.
5. Read the Amazon Marketing Cloud terms and conditions and click the checkbox to accept the terms and conditions.
6. Click **Subscribe** to include the paid feature to an instance. All users associated with this instance will now have access to the subscribed paid features.

## Activate paid feature trial

Paid feature trials allow you and your users to try a paid feature and experience how the analysis and insights through premium tables can help you attain better outcomes.

1. Go to the **Paid features (beta)** tab to view a listing of all paid features that AMC offers.
2. Click the **Get a 60-day free trial** link of the specific paid feature you want to try out.
3. You will see **Free trial in progress** next to the feature.

## Cancel paid feature

You can cancel your subscription or a free trial to a paid feature.

1. Go to the **Paid features (beta)** tab.
2. Click **Your subscriptions** to list the details of all the paid features your instance is subscribed to.
3. Locate the paid feature that you want to unsubscribe from, and select the **Modify subscription**dropdown.
4. Click **Cancel**.

**Note:**You can re-subscribe to a paid feature that you cancel after a 24-hour wait period.
