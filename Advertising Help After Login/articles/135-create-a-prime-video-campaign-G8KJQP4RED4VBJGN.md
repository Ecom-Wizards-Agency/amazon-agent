# Create a Prime Video campaign

Learn how to create Prime Video Display, Video, and Audio campaign

Updated on Oct 1, 2026

This page only lists settings **specific** to creating an ad campaign for Prime Video.

## What's a Prime Video campaign?

Prime Video is an Amazon-owned global streaming TV destination that delivers premium content including movies, shows, Amazon Originals, and live sports. You can reach streaming audiences across desktop, mobile, and connected TV environments.

**Note**: Prime Video campaigns require deals. See Understand deals to learn more.

## Create a Prime Video campaign with these settings

To advertise with Display, Video, and Audio campaigns, you need to create a campaign, associate ad groups with the campaign, and assign ads to each ad group. This campaign type also requires activation via deals. Use the tabs below to view settings for each step of the setup process.

The settings below are specific to deals for Prime Video. Only the unique required fields for this deal type are listed — standard deal settings also apply. See Create a deal for complete instructions.

**Prime Video deal types**

Prime Video campaigns support 3 deal types:

- **Programmatic Guaranteed Deals:** Fixed pricing with guaranteed delivery
- **Preferred Deals:** Available to self-service entities that spent on self-service streaming TV
- **Private Auction Deals:** Invitation-only deals where you must be invited by the publisher

**Deal setup process**

- Contact your Amazon Ads representative to initiate the deal setup process
- Provide requested information for deal creation - your representative will respond within 2 business days
- Sign and return the Customer Agreement template after receiving forecast confirmation
- Amazon will create the live deal within 4 business days after agreement signing
- Your representative will share deal information including deal ID, deal name, and dates

**Locating your deal**

Once the deal is set up:

- Your Amazon Ads representative will let you know it's ready to be used
- The deal will appear automatically in your Amazon Ads Agent (formerly known as Amazon DSP) manager account via Amazon's API integration
- Search "Prime Video" in the Inventory section to locate all Prime Video deals

Next, set up your campaign.

## Activate your campaign and monitor performance

Once your foundational components are set up, activate your campaign so it delivers and shows to customers.

1. In the navigation side menu, click **Campaigns**.
2. At the top of the **Campaigns** page, click the **Display, Video, and, Audio** tab.
3. In the **Ad groups** page, activate the ad groups by clicking the **toggle** in the **Active** column.
4. Activate your campaign by clicking the **toggle** at the top of the window next to the campaign name.
