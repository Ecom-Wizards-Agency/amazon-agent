# Understand portfolios

Portfolios are a group of campaigns that you can organize to meet your advertising needs.

Updated on Feb 24, 2026

Was this video helpful? Select feedback

Portfolios within your advertising account provide structure and allow you to manage your advertising activity by brand, product category, or season. They don’t compete against each other to help ensure that the best performing campaigns continue to show to the customer.

Portfolios can simplify your day-to-day campaign management in multiple ways:

- **Multibrand management** – Group your campaigns by brand, business line or season to reflect your marketing needs.
- **Budget caps** – Define a date range or recurring monthly budget cap and ensure that your campaign Cost doesn’t exceed your desired marketing needs.
- **Share unspent campaign budget** – Automatically share unspent campaign budget from previous days in the month with other campaigns in the same portfolio that have already spent their full daily budget.
- **Track Performance** – Review portfolio performance and create reports for campaigns by portfolio.
- **Streamlined billing** – Simplified billing details broken down by aggregated portfolio Cost in each billing cycle.

You can track portfolio performance in the campaign manager, create reports for campaigns by portfolio, and see Cost (formerly known as Spend on sponsored ads) allocated by portfolio in your billing statements.

## Portfolio billing

The **Billing** page shows how much was spent on a brand, business line, or season during the billing cycle. Billing details will reflect the portfolio mapping at the time the invoice is issued. If you don't group your campaigns into portfolios, the invoices will be a list of campaign-level spend.

If your selected payment method is Deduct from payment or Invoicing, your downloadable invoice will not contain the portfolio breakdown. You can view this information in the Billing page.
