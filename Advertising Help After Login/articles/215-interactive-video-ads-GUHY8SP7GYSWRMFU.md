# Interactive video ads

Learn about how Interactive video ads let viewers engage with your brand, available ad formats, experiences, and location-based customization options.

Updated on Jul 16, 2026

Interactive Video Ads (IVA) are available to all advertisers using Amazon DSP. Location-based customization is available for eligible Connected TV devices on Prime Video inventory in the following marketplaces: US, CA, MX, and BR. In BR, IVA is only available in Portuguese. In US, IVA is not available in Spanish.

## What are interactive video ads?

Interactive Video Ads (IVA) invite viewers to interact with brands using their remote or mobile device for an added value experience. IVAs turn passive viewers into active participants, allowing them to engage with your brand or product through features like QR codes or television remote clicks.

Learn how to create interactive video ad campaigns.

## IVA ad formats

**Default**

Prominent interactive overlay attracts viewer attention (appears on left side of screen only). When remote interactivity isn't supported for a device, the ad displays a QR code instead which can be scanned by mobile phones.

**Mini**

Minimizes visual footprint of overlay, appearing opposite the ad countdown timer. This alternative option works well if the default overlay covers key elements in the creative. If the device doesn't support remote interactivity, your ad appears without a QR code or prompt.

## Benefits of IVA

IVA helps you achieve different advertising goals depending on which experience type you choose:

**Add to cart**

Enables customers to add your product to their cart via television remote click or a QR code scan.

- **When to use “add to cart”** You want to drive customers to immediately add your products to their cart from your Streaming TV ads.
- **Benefits** Seamless 1-click purchasing with remote or a QR code which takes to product detail page Automatic data integration (pricing, deals, reviews, Prime shipping). Automatic deal badging (Prime Day, Limited Time Deals) when applicable.

**Example**: An electronics brand showcases their latest headphones. Viewers click their remote to add the product directly to cart while watching their favorite show. The overlay displays current pricing, Prime shipping eligibility, and customer ratings.

**Send more info**

Allows customers to save your product or brand information for future reference. Customers can receive a push notification and email to their registered Amazon account, or scan a QR code with their mobile device to access the ad landing page.

- **When to use “send more info”** You want to viewer to learn more about the products and/or about the brand. You want customers to explore your Brand Store. You want to direct customers to off-Amazon destinations, such as an external website.
- **Benefits** Increases consideration by delivering information without interrupting viewing experience. Supports both on-Amazon (endemic) and off-Amazon (non-endemic) destinations. Multiple delivery methods (push notification, email, and QR code when remote interactivity is not available).

**Example**: A furniture brand advertises their new collection. Viewers who want to learn more receive a push notification with a link to the brand's Store on Amazon, where they can browse the full collection.

**Location-based customization**

**Note**: Location-based customization is available for eligible Connected TV devices on Prime Video inventory in the following marketplaces: US, CA, MX, and BR. In BR, IVA is only available in Portuguese. In US, IVA is not available in Spanish.

Use this feature to customize your "send more info" IVA experience with location-specific content based on viewer location data (such as postal codes, states, or provinces). Transform a single TV commercial into thousands of local variants without creating multiple ads.

- **When to use Location based IVA** You're a non-endemic advertiser with location-specific offers. You want to display local pricing, dealership locations, or agent details. You need to direct viewers to location-specific landing pages.
- **Benefits** Use a single ad (formerly known as Creative on Amazon DSP) and customize it for every location to build brand awareness at scale while maintaining local relevance. Deliver relevant viewer experiences with local value propositions at no additional cost. Drive measurable impact through interactivity.

**Example**: An automotive brand runs a single national TV commercial. A viewer in Toronto sees their nearest dealership's name and address in the overlay, with a click-through to that specific dealer's website. A viewer in São Paulo sees their local dealership information instead. Both experiences come from the same ad asset.

**Example**: A restaurant chain runs a single ad promoting a local store offer. A viewer near the downtown location sees a "buy-1-get-1" deal, while a viewer near the waterfront location sees a free appetizer offer. Both experiences come from the same ad asset, applicable across any country.
