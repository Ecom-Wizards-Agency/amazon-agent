# Update billing details

Learn how to update the company address that appears on your advertising invoice.

Updated on Apr 9, 2026

## How to update your billing details

Update your billing deails in your billing profile.

1. In the left menu, click **Administration**.
2. Under **Billing and Payment**, select **Billing**.
3. At the top, select **Payment Settings**.
4. Click **Change billing profile**.
5. Click **Edit**.
6. In the **Billing profile** section, enter the name and physical address of the company that’s being billed.

## How to update your billing details (Sellers)

Sellers must update their billing details in Seller Central.

1. From Seller Central , go to **Settings** and select **Account Info**.
2. In the **Business Information** section, select **Business Address and Phone Number**.
3. Click **Edit Address**, then enter your address information and select **Save**.

If you’re having issues updating your business address, click **Help** in Seller Central to contact the Seller Support team.

Ensure you've registered or updated your billing company address before the end of the month so this data appears on the invoice for this period.

Certain countries with Value Added Tax (VAT) or Goods and Services Tax (GST) have introduced rules for taxing Electronically Supplied Services (ESS) at the place where they're received. VAT/GST charges on your advertising fees depend on your account information at the time of invoicing, including your business address and tax registration information. Make sure that you provide your VAT registration number to Amazon in the VAT Information page in Seller Central.

## Global billing profiles

Global billing profiles allow you to copy your existing billing information when setting up advertising accounts in new countries. You can choose to apply your saved billing details when setting up accounts in new marketplaces or edit and save billing information as new profiles for use across other countries.
