# Out-of-stock products in Stores

Best practices to improve the customer experience when the ASINs you promote in your Store are out of stock.

Updated on May 26, 2025

Implement these best practices to improve the customer experience when the ASINs you promote in your Store are out of stock:

- If you have fewer than 16 ASINs in a product grid, don't use **Hide out of stock**. Instead, you can show all ASINs so customers don’t land on empty product grids if your ASINs are out of stock.
- If you have more ASINs, and expect some of them to be out of stock, you can use the **Hide out of stock** option to highlight only in-stock ASINs to customers.
- If you expect a large portion or your entire product grid to be out of stock, we recommend updating your grid to highlight only your most important ASINs. In this case, you can also use a full-width product tile for your top ASIN and not use the **Hide out of stock** option. When your ASINs are back in stock, you can expand the collection you share with customers again.
- If your page features only a product grid, consider adding other content to the page, such as a video, to drive engagement when ASINs in the grid are out of stock.

Go to back to Stores Builder to update your out-of-stock products.
