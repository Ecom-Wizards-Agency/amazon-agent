# Create a Netflix campaign

Learn how to create a Netflix Display, Video, and Audio campaign.

Updated on Oct 1, 2026

This page only lists settings **specific** to creating an ad campaign for Netflix.

## Supported vendors

Netflix maintains strict third-party (3P) vendor requirements for all ads and measurement solutions. Netflix supports:

- **First-party ads**: Amazon Ads Agent (formerly known as Amazon DSP)-hosted ads, such as Streaming TV or Online Video
- **3P ad serving vendors**: Google Campaign Manager 360 and Innovid
- **3P measurement and verification vendors**: DoubleVerify (DV), and Integral Ad Science (IAS)

All other 3P vendors are not supported for Netflix inventory. If you're currently using a different vendor, you'll need to switch to one of the approved options or use Amazon Ads Agent (formerly known as Amazon DSP) hosted ad options.

## Create a Netflix campaign with these settings

To advertise with Display, Video, and Audio campaigns, you need to create a campaign, associate ad groups with the campaign, and assign ads to each ad group.

Netflix supply is available for private auction and programmatic guaranteed deals only.

Before creating a campaign, you'll need a Netflix deal with the correct configuration. Existing deals aren't compatible and won't work for Amazon audience-enabled campaigns. You can get a deal in 1 of 2 ways:

1. **Work with Netflix directly**: Contact Netflix or your Amazon Ads representative to set up a new deal. Once it's ready, Netflix or your representative will let you know, and the deal will appear automatically in your manager account via Amazon's API integration. Read Create a deal for more information.
2. **Submit a deal proposal**: Create and submit a deal proposal to Netflix directly in the ads console. Read Create a new proposal for step-by-step instructions.

Next, set up your campaign

## Activate your campaign and monitor performance

Once your foundational components are set up, activate your campaign so it delivers and shows to customers.

1. In the navigation side menu, click **Campaigns**.
2. At the top of the **Campaigns** page, click the **Display, Video, and, Audio** tab.
3. In the **Ad groups** page, activate the ad groups by clicking the **toggle** in the **Active** column.
4. Activate your campaign by clicking the **toggle** at the top of the window next to the campaign name.

**Frequency capping**

Frequency caps are fully enforced for Netflix private auction and preferred deals, . For programmatic guaranteed deals, impressions count toward caps but can't be suppressed on the Netflix line. Read Understand frequency to learn more about cross-publisher frequency capping.

**Reach and frequency reporting**

De-duplicated reach and frequency metrics are now available for Netflix ad group lines. Metrics populate automatically without any setup, opt-in, or additional cost. Thse metrics are available in all 11 Netflix ad-supported locales. Read Traffic metrics for a full list of available reach and frequency metrics.

## Limitations for Amazon audiences on Netflix

- Amazon audiences are available on private marketplace run-of-network deals only. Amazon audiences are not applicable to programmatic guaranteed (PG) deals.
- Don't layer 3P audiences, genre detargeting, or Netflix-side targeting (except sensitive content blocks) on Amazon audience-enabled deals, as this will stop delivery.
- Similar audiences and durable audiences are not available on Netflix inventory.
- Ad Exposure remarketing is not available for Netflix.
- Audience-level reporting is not available.
- Brand safety settings through Amazon Ads Agent (formerly known as Amazon DSP) are not available for Netflix inventory. Contact Netflix for brand safety options.
