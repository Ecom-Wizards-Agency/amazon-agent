# Understand Streaming TV

Understand how Streaming TV connects you with new and existing customers, even if you’re new to TV advertising.

Updated on Sep 11, 2026

Streaming TV is available for vendors and brand owners, including book vendors. If you're looking for information about streaming campaigns through Amazon DSP, check out our guides here .

## What is Streaming TV?

Powered by Amazon shopping and streaming signals, **Streaming TV** campaigns can appear on Prime Video, Twitch, live sports, Fire TV Channels, and other Streaming TV services. They’re full-screen, nonskipable video ads that appear before, after, or during streaming content. Even if you're new to TV advertising, you can use video and interactive elements to reach new and existing customers.

## How Streaming TV works

To help shoppers engage with your ads, all **Streaming TV**ads are eligible for interactive overlays and burnt-in elements. For example, viewers may scan a QR code within your ads to be directed to your brand’s product detail page or directly to your brand store on their mobile device.

When you create a campaign you'll:

1. Set a campaign budget and duration.
2. Upload a creative (video) to display. If you don’t have TV quality creative, Amazon Ads can help you produce TV quality video ad via our creative services.
3. Select audiences, demographics, and locations to target.
4. Once your campaign is approved, customers see your ad across Amazon’s exclusive streaming services, as well as third-party services through Amazon Publisher Direct.
5. When advertisers interact with your ad, they're directed to a landing page of your choice.
6. Through metrics such as Branded Searches and % of orders New-To-Brand, you can measure your campaign’s impact on reaching new customers and generating awareness. We also offer metrics such as Detail Page Views and Add to Carts to help you measure your campaign’s impact in driving consideration.

QR code interaction allows Brands to review the value of their ads in real time, including insights on viewer interactions, to learn how audiences engage with their **Streaming TV** ads.

Check our Streaming TV Ad Policies and our Video Specs for your video to meet our requirements.
