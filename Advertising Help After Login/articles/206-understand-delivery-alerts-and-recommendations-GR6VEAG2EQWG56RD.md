# Understand delivery alerts and recommendations

Learn what alerts and recommendations you may see in the campaign manager, and what they mean.

Updated on Apr 23, 2026

## Alerts

Delivery alerts flag campaigns (formerly known as Orders on Amazon DSP) that are underpacing and direct you to take action. When paired with relevant campaign data, they allow you to diagnose the root cause behind delivery issues, and independently resolve. You'll also see an alert if your campaign is not delivering due to a rejected ad (formerly known as Creative on Amazon DSP)

## Recommendations

In the campaign manager, alerts appear an orange triangular explanation point. Click **View recommendations** to view all recommendations associated to the campaign and take a quick action by implementing the proposed recommendations. In the recommendation panel, you can select one or multiple recommendations to action at a time. Recommendations that are successfully applied will be noted as such in the campaign change history.

The guidance panel experience will surface the top 100 recommendations relevant to a campaign or ad group (formerly known as Line Item on Amazon DSP).

**Alert and recommendation overview**

| **Type** | **Name** | **Description** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Alert | Rejected Ad (formerly known as Creative on Amazon DSP) | The campaign includes ad groups with rejected ad. Advertiser is advised to review the rejection reason, make any necessary changes, and submit the ad again. |
| Alert | Underpacing | This campaign is underpacing and is on track to spend less than its budget. Advertiser is advised to take action based on recommendations for the campaign's ad groups. This alert is paired with specific recommendations that highlight optimal changes for advertisers. |
| Recommendation | Increase maximum average CPM | This line item has a maximum average CPM, which is restricting pacing. Amazon DSP will recommend a new maximum average CPM that may help improve pacing and performance. |
| Recommendation | Increase frequency cap | This line item has a frequency cap, which is restricting pacing. Amazon DSP will recommend a new frequency cap that may help improve pacing and performance. |
| Recommendation | Decrease targeted viewability | This line item has selected a viewability targeting setting that is restricting pacing. Amazon DSP will recommend a new viewability targeting setting to improve pacing. |
| Recommendation | Add missing creative sizes | The line is missing key creative sizes which may improve pacing. Amazon DSP will identify relevant approved ad from active ad groups in the samme campaign and suggest them to you. |
