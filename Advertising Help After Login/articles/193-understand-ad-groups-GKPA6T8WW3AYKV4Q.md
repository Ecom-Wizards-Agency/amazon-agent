# Understand ad groups

Learn how to use ad groups to organize and manage ads within a Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands, or Display campaign.

Updated on Feb 24, 2026

Was this video helpful? Select feedback

Available multi-language captions are best viewed in the Google Chrome browser.

Ad groups are available for Sponsored Products and Display campaigns. Each campaign consists of one or more ad groups. The first ad group is created when you create a campaign. You can add more ad groups to the campaign after you've saved it.

We recommend creating ad groups with products that are in the same category. This helps optimize your bidding strategy, relevancy, and targeting.

For Sponsored Products campaigns, you set a bid that is used when an ad group's keyword or product targeting triggers an ad to appear. This is called a cost-per-click (CPC) bid. You can also set bids for individual keyword or product targets within the ad group. In auto-targeted campaigns, you can apply a single bid to the whole ad group. In manual-targeted campaigns, you can select a common group of keyword or product targets to all the products within the ad group.

For Display campaigns, you can set bids for individual product, category, or audience targets within the ad group.

Best Practices: Setting up Ad Groups:

- **Ad group name:** Assign an ad group name that is descriptive and meaningful to you. Ad group names must be unique within a campaign, but you can use the same ad group name in different campaigns. Your ad group name will only be used for display purposes in the campaign manager and won't be visible to shoppers.
- **Ad Group:** For Sponsored Brands, ad groups will enable you to structure your campaign by grouping of targeting, brands, products, categories, or ad formats. You can set the ad format and landing page at ad groups that can be shared across multiple different Adsvariations. The ad group can be associated with multiple Ads variations.
- **Ad:**For Sponsored Brands, ads will enable you to create different variations of the same ad format and to create multiple ads within the same ad group. You can associate multiple ads to an ad group; the ads associated to an ad group can share the same landing page but can also have different ASINs, headlines, LifeStyle images, product category, or videos.
- **Products:** Select the products from your inventory to include in your ad group. Each of the products that you select will be displayed as an ad. Keyword or product targeting, and bids apply to all products within an ad group, so we recommend choosing products that are closely related to each other. When creating an ad group in a Sponsored Products campaign with manual targeting or a Display campaign, add as many similar products to the ad group as you can. More products in your ad group increase the likelihood of receiving ad impressions because the products share the same keywords in your ad group. You can add new products to an existing ad group at any time. To add products, select the ad group and go to the**Products** page (Sponsored Products) or **Ads** page (Display). Click **Add products**, and you will see your list of eligible products to add to the ad group. You can add 1,000 products to an ad group at a time.
- **Keyword or product targeting:**For Sponsored Products campaigns you can use Amazon's suggested keywords, your own keywords, negative keywords, or target by product. For Display campaigns you can use Amazon's suggested categories, products, or audiences, or use your own categories and audiences, or add negative products or brands.
