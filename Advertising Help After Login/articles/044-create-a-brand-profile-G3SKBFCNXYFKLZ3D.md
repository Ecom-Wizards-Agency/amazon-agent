# Create a brand profile

Learn how to create a brand profile in the ads console to store your brand logo and color.

Updated on Feb 23, 2026

To create your brand brand profile

1. In the left menu, click the Brand home icon .
2. Click **Brand profiles** to access the brand profile information page.
3. Enter your **Brand name**. This must match the brand name that you enrolled in the Amazon Brand Registry. This field is case-sensitive.
4. Upload your **Brand logo** or Choose from Ad assets, if you’ve uploaded your logo in the past. Your brand logo will be displayed in a circle crop on Amazon. We recommend that you follow our policies and recommendations to get your brand profile logo up and running quickly. Learn about the policy for brand logo.
5. Select your **Brand color** or add your hex color. This helps shoppers recognize your brand on Amazon.
6. Click Submit for review.

When you submit your brand profile for review, it will go through our moderation review process to ensure that it meets our policies. The moderation review process is usually completed within 24 hours, but it may take up to 3 business days. We’ll email or notify you in-app to let you know if your Brand profile was approved or if you need to make changes.
