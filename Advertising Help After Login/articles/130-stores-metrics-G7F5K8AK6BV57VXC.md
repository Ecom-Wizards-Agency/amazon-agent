# Stores metrics

The Stores insights dashboard provides detailed traffic and sales data to help you understand your Store's performance.

Updated on Oct 24, 2025

The Stores insights dashboard provides information about your store's performance, including traffic and sales. You can access Stores insights from the Stores builder, or from the Stores main page.

**Note:**Brand store reports aren’t available. As an advertiser, you can export only certain aspects of the Stores insights as a CSV, but there are no dedicated reports for Stores insights available in the **Measurement and Reporting** section of the console.

The dashboard provides the following metrics:

| Metric | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| **Purchase (formerly known as Order on sponsored ads)** | Estimated total purchases placed by store visitors within 14 days of their visit. Purchases contain 1 or more units sold. |
| **Sales** | Estimated total sales generated by Store visitors within 14 days of their last visit. Learn more about Ad campaign attribution. |
| **Sales/Purchases** | Average sales per purchase. |
| **Sales/Visitor** | Average sales per visitor. |
| **Units/Purchase** | Average units per purchase. |
| **Views** | Number of times that more than 50% of a product in your Store was viewed for more than 1 second. |
| **Views/Visitor** | Average number of page views per visitor. |
| **Visitors** | Total visitors to your Store within the selected date range, calculated based on daily unique users or devices. **Note:**A visitor can visit more than 1 page, and they can visit your Store from more than 1 traffic source. The total visitors by page or source may sum up to a value larger than the total visitors by day to the Store or to the page. |
| **Visits** | Total visits to a page or from a traffic source within a single day. Each visitor can visit more than 1 page, and they can visit your Store from more than 1 traffic source. |

Metrics are available by the following dimensions:

| Dimension | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| **Date** | Date of the visitor’s visit. |
| **Page** | The Store page by name. Data for deleted pages, if it exists, is aggregated as “deleted page.” |
| **Traffic source** | The traffic source from which the visitor arrived to the Store. |

The Stores insights dashboard provides a breakdown of metrics by traffic source. We categorize traffic sources as follows:

| **Traffic source** | **Definition** |
| --- | --- |
| **Sponsored Brands** | Traffic from Sponsored Brands ads on Amazon. |
| **Amazon organic traffic** | Traffic originating from your brand link on Amazon product detail pages. |
| **Your tags** | Traffic originating from your Store's source tags. Learn more about how to Create a source tag. |
| **Other sources** | All other traffic sources that aren't categorized. This type of traffic source can occur when the store URL has been shared on different social platforms and got the traffic from there. |
