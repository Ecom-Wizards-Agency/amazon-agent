# Supply and format-specific requirements

Learn about supply-specific ad requirements for Alexa, Fire tablet, Twitch, Streaming TV, and other Amazon Ads channels.

Updated on Apr 10, 2026

This section covers requirements for ad specifications across different supply.

Please reference our supply specific guide for additional information about specific image sizing, aspect ratios, and bit rate requirements for each supply type.

## Alexa

The following requirements apply to all Alexa devices:

- Ad copy must contain consistent capitalization
- Ads must not contain exclamation marks (“!”) in the product title
- Ads must not contain personalized language (such as “you/your”) or words that may lead customers into believing the ad is addressing them directly
- Ads that include an Alexa voice call to action (CTA) such as "Alexa, order Bounty paper towels" must not include a second CTA, such as "Shop now."

## Fire devices

All ads must contain a call to action (CTA).

Kindle e-reader and Fire tablet

- Author profile picture or publisher logos must: Be your likeness, name or initials, or relevant to the series of book title being advertised Fill the entire frame Not contain additional overlaid text Not contain poorly cropped images Not contain book covers Not contain letter or pillar box formats
- If borders are included, they must be evenly distributed
- Lockscreen images must have a width: height ratio between 0.6 and 1
- Images must not contain additional overlaid text other than text native to the image
- Creatives must not be individual or multiple book covers on a solid or transparent background
- Videos must not contain customer reviews
- Must not contain letter or pillarbox formats

## Onsite

The following requirements apply to all Onsite ads:

- Pop-up tabs, windows, or modals are prohibited unless they’re relevant to the service advertised and beneficial to the customer experience
- Pop-unders are prohibited
- Animations may not loop more than 3 times
- Video ads may be up to 3 minutes, although 30 seconds or less is recommended
- CTA must contain no more than 1 punctuation mark
- Mobile creative must not mimic elements of the Amazon Mobile homepage, such as a white border bar dividing the creative
- Billboard creatives must not contain vivid, or highly saturated colors used as background colors
- Billboard creatives can't be animated

## Streaming TV

The following requirements apply to all Streaming TV ads:

- Must have an aspect ratio of 16:09
- Must have a bit rate above 4 Mbps (note that Prime Video Display has stricter guidelines)
- Must not contain both pillarboxing and letterboxing (using only one is acceptable)
- Must clearly contain the advertiser’s brand name or logo in an identifiable manner, and for at least 3 seconds
- Must fill the majority of the visual space with movement, rather than a static image or overlay
- Third-party watermarks are prohibited
- Must not contain content that could trigger photosensitive epileptic seizures
- Must not contain pixelation, glitches, or effects intended to lead customers into believing their streaming device or connection may be failing

## Twitch

The following requirements apply to all Twitch ads:

- Ad text must contain consistent capitalization
- Must not contain excessive or improper punctuation
- Special characters (such as “@” or “$”), ASCII art, and emojis are prohibited unless part of the advertiser’s logo; however, hashtags (“#”) are permitted
- Ads must not use the Twitch logo without prior consent

## Physical stores

The following requirements apply to all Physical stores ads:

- Ads must contain a “Sponsored” label in the creative.
- If a product isn't nationally available at the retailer where the ad is served, ads must mention that the product is only available in select stores.
- Ads must not include audio.
- Disclosures must be placed horizontally.

More information can be found here: In-Store Digital Signs Ads,Amazon Fresh Display Ads, Whole Foods Market Display Ads.

## Brand Stores

The following requirements apply to all Brand Stores:

- Ad copy must contain consistent capitalization
- Excessive use of emojis or ellipses is prohibited
- Logos must fill the entire image, or be placed on a solid color background that fills the entire image
- Logos can’t contain multiple images, additional logos, or crowded elements
- Text overlay must not obscure important elements of the creative, such as text, logo, or the product
- Video length must not exceed 5 minutes

## Sponsored Display

The following requirements apply to all Sponsored Display ads:

- Special characters are only permitted when part of the brand or slogan
- Emoji and ASCII art aren’t permitted
- Animated ad elements must not pulsate for more than 3 seconds
- Animated ad elements must not mimic user interaction
- Call to action (CTA) must be fewer than 30 characters (15 for Japan)
- CTA must contain no punctuation
- Must not contain any distracting images (such as flashing text or high-contrast transitions)
- Must not contain distracting or jarring sounds (such as sudden changes in volume)

**Custom images**

- Can't be individual or multiple product images on a solid or transparent background
- Can't be individual or multiple brand logos
- Can't be a selected image from the onsite product page
- Can't contain additional or overlaid text, other than text naturally present in the image, such as on product packaging
- Can't contain letterbox or pillarbox formats

**Video**

- Video ads must not be longer than 15 seconds

**Ad copy text**

- For **Desktop**, ad copy text must be between Arial 12pts and 25pts or equivalent, or between 9.5 and 45pt UD Shin Go (Japanese).
- For **Mobile**, ad copy text must be above 16pt Arial regular or equivalent, or above 16pt UD Shin Go (Japanese).
- For Kindle e-reader and Fire tablet, ad copy text must be between 32 to 50pt Arial regular or equivalent.
