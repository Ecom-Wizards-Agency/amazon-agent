# Sites selection for Sponsored Ads campaigns

Sites helps you select where your ads will appear.

Updated on May 7, 2026

For Sponsored Product, as default, your ads appear on Amazon, including both Amazon retail and Amazon Business, as well as select sites and apps off Amazon. For Sponsored Brands, your ads appear on Amazon, including both the Amazon Store and the Amazon Business Store.

Sites are where specific ad placements can appear. The Sites card enables you to create campaigns specifically reaching business shoppers on Amazon Business or value-focused shoppers on Amazon Haul. This helps you reach business shoppers, who demonstrate higher purchase intent after viewing product pages compared to other consumers. Business shoppers also show lower return rates and tend to place larger bulk orders than typical shoppers.

If you create a campaign with a specific Sites selection, all future ad groups in that campaign are defaulted to the same. You can’t change your Sites selection once your campaign is launched.

Based on your eligibility, you can select where ads will appear from the Sites card:

## Amazon and Beyond

Your existing Sponsored Products campaigns are set to **Amazon and beyond** by default, while Sponsored Brands campaigns default to **Amazon**(includes Amazon Store and Amazon Business Store). You can’t edit an existing campaign to update the Sites selection.

## Amazon Haul

Amazon Haul enables you to bring lower priced products into one convenient destination. The storefront makes it easier for customers to discover sought-after products at lower prices across a wide range of categories, including fashion, home, lifestyle, and electronics. Amazon Haul is available on your Amazon app, mobile web, and desktop.

Note: The Amazon Haul site selection is only available in the US.

**Eligibility**

- Only eligible advertisers have the option to create Amazon Haul exclusive campaigns. Advertisers must be enrolled in the Fulfilled by Amazon (FBA) Haul seller program to be eligible. Learn more about enrollment.
- Ensure that you’re using an updated version of the Amazon app to access Amazon Haul.

**Inclusions and Exclusions:**When running Amazon Haul campaigns, note the following:

- Only Haul ASINs can be advertised in a Haul Sponsored Product campaign.
- Cross-marketplace campaign copying not supported.
- Sponsored Products campaigns using Haul as the site selection can’t be converted to any other ad type.

## Amazon Business

Amazon Business Store simplifies buying for organizations of any size with business-relevant selection, tools, and features like Business Prime, plus the convenience and value of Amazon. Only eligible advertisers have the option to create Amazon Business exclusive campaigns.

Note: The Amazon Business Sites selection is only available in the following marketplaces: US, CA, UK, FR, DE, IT, ES, IN, JP and MX.

Amazon Business site helps you to create campaigns to better reach business shoppers, separate from your Amazon retail advertising efforts. You can tailor your B2B advertising strategy by:

- setting a specific B2B budget
- promoting ASINs you want to showcase to B2B shoppers
- using B2B-focused keywords
- adjusting your bids and strategies to boost sales specifically for business shoppers

**Exclusions**

- All campaign settings apply exclusively to Amazon Business, and your ads will only appear on the Amazon Business Store, not on Amazon.com.
- With Amazon Business Sites selection, you won't have access to audience cohort bid adjustments or Amazon Business placement bid adjustments.
- Amazon Business doesn’t support ads on other apps and websites, or ASINs specific to Amazon Haul, Amazon Fresh, and Whole Foods.

After you’ve created a campaign with Amazon Business Sites selection, all existing Sponsored Products reports and Sponsored Brand reports will automatically reflect performance only on Amazon Business. You can leverage these reports to review campaign performance on Amazon Business. To view your products and ads on Amazon Business, you can sign up for a free Amazon Business account at www.business.amazon.com..

Learn more about Amazon Business and Amazon Business accounts.
