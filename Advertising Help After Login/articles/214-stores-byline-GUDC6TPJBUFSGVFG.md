# Stores byline

The byline is a link that is positioned on the top portion of the product detail page. It's used to attribute a product to an authorial, owning, or otherwise representative entity.

Updated on May 14, 2026

Your store's byline is located above or below the product name. Bylines help promote your brand store on Amazon by driving organic customer engagement through a hyperlinked brand name that directs to your product detail page.

Note: Once your store is live and approved, your byline is automatically created within 4 to 7 days. However, some ASIN detail pages might not link correctly to the store, depending on the initial ASIN setup and your Amazon Brand Registry profile configuration.

## Create your store byline

- Your store must be live and approved.
- Enrolled in the Amazon Brand Registry to be eligible to create a byline for your store.
- Your byline will support connections to 1 store and 1 brand only.
- The brand name and brand ID must have consistent naming. Your ASIN’s brand name and brand ID should match those in the Amazon Brand Registry.

## Exclusions

- This feature doesn’t support connections between multiple brands and 1 store.
- If your product falls under the following categories, the byline will redirect users to the artist's page instead of the store: Music DVD Digital music Book Digital e-book
- Products included in the Amazon Fresh program are not supported by byline feature and will instead direct customers to the search results page.
