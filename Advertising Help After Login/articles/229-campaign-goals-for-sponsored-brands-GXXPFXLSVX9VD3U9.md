# Campaign goals for Sponsored Brands

Campaign goals help you optimize your Sponsored Brands advertising strategy by focusing on specific success metrics that align with your business objectives.

Updated on Aug 11, 2026

With campaign goals, you can decide what you want your campaign to accomplish and further optimize your campaigns against key success metrics such as top-of-search impression share and clicks.

- **Top of search impression share (TOS IS):** This shows the amount of impressions the advertiser received for the reserved keyword divided by the total amount of impressions across all ads for the reserved keyword.

**Example:**Kitchen Smart has 1,000 impressions for their keywords. We show shoppers 600 impressions of Kitchen Smart ads and 400 impressions from other advertisers. The Kitchen smart top-of-search impression share is 60% (600/1000).

- **Clicks:**How many times a customer clicks on your ad

In the Sponsored Brands campaign builder, there are 3 goals available when creating campaigns: **Drive page visits,****Grow brand impression share**, and **Reserve share of voice (SOV)**.

You can choose only 1 goal per campaign, and this goal can’t be changed after the campaign is created. However, you can launch different campaigns, with different goals. Combining these goals in the campaign portfolio can help you structure your campaign strategy and deliver short-term goals and long-term sustainable growth.

## Goal recommendations and settings

**Note:**Reserve share of voice is not available in Spain.

Each goal provides recommendations and settings to help deliver on its success metrics.

**Recommendations and settings by goal:**

- **Drive page visits:** Cost-per-click (CPC) bid recommendations.
- **Grow brand impression share:** Cost-per-1,000-viewable impressions (vCPM) bid recommendations, which is the amount you pay each time customers view your ad 1,000 times.
- **Reserve brand share of voice:** Fixed, upfront pricing for top of search visibility for your branded keywords for the duration of your campaign.

For example, the Drive page visits and Grow brand impression share goals provide associated targeting and bid recommendations to help deliver success metrics. Bid recommendations are built from historical winning bids for each specific target (keyword or product) and the impression opportunity for each specific goal.

You can either target individual keywords or create target groups, which consists of a set of keywords. We recommend you use targeting groups to start a campaign quickly. Learn more about targeting keywords and products.

Instead of bid recommendations, Reserve brand share of voice (SOV) provides a fixed, upfront price for top of search visibility for your branded keywords without having to bid in auction. You can upload or enter 5 to 1,000 branded keywords for your own brands, or choose from suggested keywords Amazon provides. Your pricing is for the duration of your campaign, which must be between 30 to 92 days.

We strive to deliver 95% SOV at the campaign level when campaigns are properly set up with relevant creative assets that align with the selected keywords. This SOV percentage is based on the amount of impressions the advertiser received out of all ad impressions delivered in the slot at the top of Amazon search results pages in response to the applicable search keywords during the term of the campaign. Please review additional terms that apply to Reserve Share of Voice campaigns here.

## Compare campaign goals for Sponsored Brands

| Goals | Success metrics | Strategic objectives | Ads format and placement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Drive page visits** | Clicks | - Brand consideration - Short-term sales | - **All ad formats:** Product collection, Store spotlight, and Video - **Placement:** Search pages, detail pages, and Amazon homepage |
| **Grow brand impression share** | Top-of-search impression share | - Top-of-search visiblity - Brand discovery | - **Ad formats with brand Stores only:** Product collection, Store spotlight, and Video - **Placement:** Top-of-search |
| **Reserve share of voice** | Top-of-search impression share | - Top-of-search visiblity - Brand discovery | - **Ad formats with brand Stores only:**Product collection, Store spotlight, and Video - **Placement:**Top-of-search |
