# Set your Display, Video, and Audio campaign budget

Learn how to set an overall budget and add flights to your campaign or ad group.

Updated on Sep 28, 2026

## What's a budget?

Your budget is how much you want to spend on all the ad groups associated with a campaign during a flight. A flight is the time period during which ad groups associated with this campaign will deliver.

You set your overall budget during campaign (formerly known as Order on Amazon DSP) setup. Then, there are additional budget and delivery settings to configure during ad group (formerly known as Line Item on Amazon DSP) setup. Follow the instructions below to set or edit your budget.

**Note**: If your total ad group budget is the same as or more than the flight budget of the campaign, the flight budget will be delivered according to the budget ratio of each ad group.

**How to add a flight**

1. In the navigation side menu, click **Create Campaign**.
2. Click **Multimedia solutions with Amazon DSP**.
3. Scroll down to the budget and delivery section.
4. To edit a flight, change the budget, start, or end date. To add a new flight, click **Add flight**.
5. For a new flight enter your budget, start, and end date. Flight dates can't overlap in time and a budget is required for each flight. When there's a gap between 2 flights, ad groups stop delivering. When a new flight starts, they start delivering again automatically.
6. Add a name to each flight to help identify them later.
7. Choose a budget rollover strategy.
8. Click **Save**.

A campaign can have 1 flight, or multiple flights if you want to allocate different budget amounts to certain date ranges. Flight dates can't overlap, but you can have gaps between flights. When there's a gap between 2 flights, ad groups stop delivering until the next flight starts.

**How to remove a flight**

Click **Remove** to the right of a flight to remove it, and then click **Save**.

**How to edit an existing flight**

1. Navigate to your campaign settings Click **campaigns**. Select **Display, Video, and Audio**. Click **Campaigns**. Click the name of the campaign that you want to view. Click **Campaign settings**.
2. Scroll down to the budget and delivery section.
3. To edit a flight, change the budget, start, or end date. To add a new flight, click **Add flight**.

You can create up to 12 future flights at a time. If you already have 12 future flights, you must wait for 1 to end before adding more.

## Update locked PG ad group settings with Smart-Sync

If your deal terms are revised (for example, extended flight dates or increased budget), you can accept the updated values through PG Deal Smart-Sync. Your locked fields update and re-lock with the new values. To update your ad group settings:

1. In **Campaign manager**, look for the Smart-Sync alert on the troubleshooting widget.
2. Click the alert to open the change summary page. You'll see your current values compared to the updated deal terms.
3. If there's a campaign ineligibility alert, it means your campaign's flight budget or dates can't accommodate the new ad group values. The **Accept & apply** button is grayed out until you resolve the conflict.
4. Follow the link in the ineligibility alert to the campaign page. Update your campaign flight end date and flight budget to accommodate the new deal terms, then click **Save**.
5. Return to the ad group targeting the revised PG deal. You'll see a banner alert on the ad group settings page notifying you of the revised terms.
6. Click the banner alert to return to the change summary page. The campaign ineligibility alert should no longer appear.
7. Click **Accept & apply**. Your ad group's locked fields update to the new values and remain locked.

**Note:** Smart-Sync is the only way to update locked PG ad group fields. You can't edit them manually.

## Best practices

- You can allocate budget to specific ad groups, but the flight total is your overall budget for the entire campaign.
- If your budget is different at the campaign and ad group level, the system will use the lowest amount to determine how to pace ad delivery.
- Once a campaign is live, you can extend the flight, but not change the start date.
- You can add additional flights up to 180 days after the last flight in a campaign ends.
- You can change a flight's budget at any time, but if you reduce the budget below the amount already spent during the flight, the campaign will stop delivering, and you'll be charged for the amount spent. For example, If you change your flight budget from $1000 to $500, and your flight has already spent $800, you'll be charged $800 for that period. When you add a new flight, it cannot overlap with existing flights. It can start on the same day, but the start time must be after the previous flight ends. If your flights overlap you'll see an error message.
