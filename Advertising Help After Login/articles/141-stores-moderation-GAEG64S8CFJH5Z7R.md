# Stores moderation

When you submit your store for publishing, it will go through our moderation review process to ensure it meets our policies for Stores.

Updated on Oct 9, 2025

Your store must meet these requirements to successfully pass moderation :

- Review Store eligibility requirements.
- Review Stores Content Guidelines and Acceptance Policies to go live.

## Moderation review process:

- Moderation review typically completes within 24 hours but can extend up to 3 business days. We’ll email and/or notify you in-app with updates after the review is complete.
- You can't edit your store during the review.
- Once the review is complete, you can submit any revisions based on the review status.
- Approved stores are published immediately or on a pre-selected date. Once published, the store will be visible to your customers.

**Tip:**We recommend you plan ahead and publish your store with plenty of time before major sales, deals, or holiday events.

All assets in your Store will go through moderation and must be in the primary language of the locale in which they’re delivered. This includes but isn’t limited to videos (including audio), images, text, and other Creative elements. Learn more about Supported primary language per country.

Review each moderation status to track the progress of your store launch:

| Moderation status | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| **Not submitted** | The current draft store isn't submitted or there's no live store. |
| **Pending review** | The current draft is under review by moderation. |
| **Live** | The submitted draft was approved and published, or scheduled to publish. |
| **Not approved** | The previous draft wasn't approved. You'll be notified about the cause via email and/or in-app. |
