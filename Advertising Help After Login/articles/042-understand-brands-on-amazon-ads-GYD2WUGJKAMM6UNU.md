# Understand brands on Amazon Ads

Learn how to enroll in the Amazon Brand registry.

Updated on Feb 25, 2026

On Amazon, a brand is a name that represents a product or set of products. The brand usually has a name, logo, or other signals that appear on product lists and/or packaging. Some advertisers manage one brand, while others manage multiple brands under a single Seller or Vendor account.

You can manage your brand assets using a brand profile, and promote your brand with Sponsored Brands campaigns and Brand Stores. You must enroll in the Amazon Brand registry to use these features. Visit the Brand Registry website for registration requirements and instructions.

To register, you must be the brand owner and provide:

1. Your brand name. The brand name must have an active registered trademark.
2. The associated government-registered trademark number.
3. A list of product categories (for example, apparel, sporting goods, electronics) in which the brand should be listed.

In addition to the account requirements, brand profiles must meet the Brand profile content guidelines and acceptance policies to pass the moderation review.
