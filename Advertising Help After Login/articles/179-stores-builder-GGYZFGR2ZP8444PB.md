# Stores builder

The Stores builder is a self-service tool that lets you easily create, customize, edit, and publish your Store.

Updated on May 13, 2026

This page provides information on the different sections of the Stores builder. If you’re creating a new Store, visit Create your Store for detailed steps on creating your Store.

The Stores builder has 4 main sections:

## 1. Page manager

The **Pages** panel shows you the pages that you’ve added to your store. In this section, you can add, edit, reorder, or delete pages from your Store. Additionally, you can access the page settings to change the page title and description.

This panel is divided into 3 sections:

- The **Store settings** section allows you to edit your brand display name, change your brand logo, or delete your Store.
- The **Add page** button creates a new page.
- The page navigator section displays the hierarchy of pages in your Store. Clicking a page will open it for editing.

To learn more about how to edit your pages, visit Edit Store pages.

## 2. Preview area

The **Preview area** provides a live view of the page that you’re editing. In the edit section of an already built page, you can select a section or tile to edit.

This area is divided in 2 sections:

- The preview toggle allows you to switch between desktop and mobile previews. It allows you to preview how your Store looks to customers.
- In the content title section, clicking a tile will select it for editing in the tile manager.

The **Preview area** may at times only shows placeholder icons rather than the products that will actually render. It also gives you the option to share a password-protected preview of your Store with others.

**Note:**If you’ve added a tile in the builder, but you don’t see it in the preview window, it’s possible the tile isn’t saved. To fix this, go back to the builder to find the missing tile and save it by clicking **Done** to ensure that your content saves.

## 3. Page sections manager

The **Page sections** panel shows you the sections and tiles that you’ve added to the current page. It allows the store creator to add, edit, move, and delete tiles from the current page. To learn about the different content tiles available on Stores, visit Content tiles on Stores.

This panel is divided in 3 sections:

- The **Add new section** button allows you to add a new section filled with tiles.
- The content tile list is a directory of all the content tiles on the current page. It gives you the option to add additional tiles if needed. Clicking an existing tile will open it for editing.
- The section settings can be accessed by clicking the settings icon on the right of each section.

To learn more about how to edit tiles from your Store, visit Edit Store pages.

## 4. Status bar

The status bar lets you know when your latest draft was saved and the moderation status of your Store. In this section, you’ll see whether your store is live or not, the scheduled publication date (if any), and the current moderation review status of a publishing request. To learn more about the review moderation process and status definitions, visit Stores moderation.

Additionally, if there are issues with your Store, such as a tile having no content or not meeting policy, they’ll be called out here so you can correct them and resubmit.
