# Add accounts to your Manager account

Learn how to link and unlink accounts to your Manager account for consolidated account management in Amazon Ads Agent.

Updated on Oct 5, 2026

Only manager account admins can link existing advertiser accounts to their manager account.

## Link accounts to your manager account

You can link a manager account to another manager account to create a hierarchy of accounts for easier, more consolidated account management. Learn more about manager account hierarchy.

1. In the left menu, click **Administration**.
2. Select **Account access and settings**.
3. Click **Accounts**.
4. Click Add account
5. Choose one of the following options: Link existing account Find the account that you want to link from the list, or use the search bar to locate. Click **Add**next to the accounts you want to add. Choose the desired **Access level**from the drop down. Click **Link account** in the top-right corner. Create new sponsored ads account Follow instructions to register a new account. Once the new account is created, follow steps 1-5a above to link it. **Create new account (this will create an Advertiser Account that includes Display, Video, and Audio campaigns).** Follow instructions to register a new account. Once the new account is created, follow the steps 1-5a above to link it.

Only manager account administrators can link accounts to a Manager account. If you’re not an administrator, click the request access tab and enter the Account or Advertiser ID, and marketplace of the account you want to link. A request will be sent to that account's administrator to review and assign permissions.

## Unlink accounts from your manager account

Display, Video, and Audio campaigns advertiser accounts created under the manager account (with Admin linking permission) can't be unlinked. All other account types can be unlinked (including other Display, Video, and Audio campaign advertiser accounts, sponsored ads advertiser accounts, and manager accounts).

1. In the left menu, click **Administration**.
2. Select **Account access and settings**.
3. Click **Manager accounts**.
4. To unlink a parent manager account from your account: Under **Manager Accounts**, locate the desired manager account and click **Unlink** under Actions.
5. To unlink a linked account from your manager account: Under **Accounts**, locate the desired account and click **Remove** to unlink the specific account.
