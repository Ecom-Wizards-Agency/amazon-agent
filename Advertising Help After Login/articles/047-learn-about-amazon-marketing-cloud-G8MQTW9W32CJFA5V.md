# Learn about Amazon Marketing Cloud

Amazon Marketing Cloud (AMC) is a secure, privacy-safe, clean room solution in which you can easily perform analytics and generate custom audiences.

Updated on Nov 19, 2025

In AMC’s cloud-based, clean room environment, you can query across inputs such as your Amazon Ads campaign events. For example, you can query impressions, clicks, and relevant conversions from Amazon DSP and sponsored ads campaigns, as well as your own inputs to build aggregated and anonymized analyses.

## AMC analytics

AMC analyses can help with campaign measurement, audience refinement, supply optimization, and more, enabling advertisers to make informed decisions about their cross-channel marketing investments.

Use AMC to draw insights such as your optimal campaign reach and frequency, the combination of the marketing channels that deliver results, and the total impact of your media campaigns across the marketing funnel. You can also generate custom metrics and insights tailored to your business, enabling you to make more informed decisions on how much you spend on your campaign. AMC creates endless possibilities for you to discover, measure, experiment, and validate your marketing efforts across Amazon Ads channels.

Common AMC analytics use cases include:

- **Performance deep dive**: Obtain more in-depth findings about a particular campaign.
- **Audience insights**: Look for connections between engagement actions and audience attributes to engage with your optimal audience segment.
- **Shopping journey analysis**: Illustrate the common paths to conversion in terms of media exposure, frequency, timing, and other attributes.
- **Media mix analysis**: Understand the collective impact of your Amazon Ads media channels and isolate the incremental impact of a particular media usage.
- **Omnichannel impact**: Assess how Amazon media contributes to sales at various Amazon store types and the advertiser’s direct-to-consumer (D2C) channels.
- **Custom attribution**: Assign conversion credit to your media using custom logic on engagement type, lookback windows, and other criteria.

## AMC audiences

Use AMC audiences to create custom audiences that can be activated and used in Amazon DSP and sponsored ads.

Similar to how you can run queries in AMC to build custom analyses you can define your own queries to create custom audiences that best support your advertising and business goals. AMC audiences can be used along the audience options available in Amazon DSP and sponsored ads.

You can build custom audiences using most of the same signals in your AMC instance, which may include: Amazon DSP and sponsored ads traffic events, ad-relevant conversion events, first-party tables, and select paid features tables.

Common AMC audience use cases include:

- **Ad engagement**: Audience that has engaged with your ads but not yet purchased.
- **Cart abandoners**: Audience that added to cart but hasn’t yet purchased.
- **High-value lookalikes**: Audience similar to high-volume customers of your products.

Audiences created in AMC are directly sent to your corresponding Amazon DSP and sponsored ads accounts. You can select AMC custom audiences for relevant ad groups (formerly known as Line Items on Amazon DSP) similar to how other audiences are selected today. You can then monitor campaigns and audience performance via Amazon DSP and sponsored ads or use AMC to generate insights and optimize your audiences.
