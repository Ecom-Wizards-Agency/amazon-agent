# Understand brand suitability controls

Learn how to use brand suitability controls in Display, Video, and Audio campaigns to help protect your brand across inventory sources and media types.

Updated on Oct 5, 2026

## What are brand suitability controls?

Brand suitability controls allow you to customize where your ads appear based on your brand's values and risk tolerance. There are 2 control types available:

- **Inventory tier**: Choose from 3 graduated protection levels (Expanded, Standard, Restrictive)
- **Content exclusion categories**: Opt out of specific topics that don't align with your brand

Both controls can be set at the advertiser level affecting new ad groups, or at the ad group level during creation. Learn more about brand safety and suitability.

**Note**: Currently, brand suitability controls apply to third-party (3P) web/mobile and Twitch inventory for Display and Online Video (OLV) media types.

## Inventory tiers

Inventory tier provides graduated control across content types, balancing reach with brand safety. Choose the tier that matches your brand's risk tolerance.

| Tier type | Distinction | Content type | Likely meets the needs of... |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Expanded** | Maximizes reach while maintaining baseline safety standards. | Includes content that may contain graphic, excessive use or glamorized depictions. | Brands that want to maximize their reach. |
| **Standard** (default) | Retains reach while increasing suitability protections by excluding some sensitive content categories. | Includes content presented in professional, journalistic, or entertainment contexts. | Brands looking to retain reach while aiming to increase suitability protections. This tier is the default selection for all campaigns. |
| **Restrictive** | Blocks most content categories that might not meet the strict guidelines for some brands. | Includes content that may contain fleeting mentions, or information presented in an educational context. | Brands that want to prioritize content suitability over reach. |

## Content exclusion categories

Content exclusion categories let you opt out of specific topics that don't align with your brand values. These work as on/off switches. When you select a category, your ads won't appear alongside that content type.Display, Video, and Audio campaigns classifies content into the following brand suitability categories:

Content related to sensitive tragedies, man-made or natural disasters and calamities, including content that graphically depicts such events.

Content related to the general consumption of alcohol.

Content in a fictional entertainment context that contains blood, gore, or acts of violence.

Content related to crime, such as law enforcement efforts, criminal behavior, crime prevention, and justice systems.

Content related to substance use, drugs, and other mind-altering substances.

Content related to gambling, such as instructions how to play, accessories like home poker sets, and discussions of specific odds or bets. It doesn’t include online gambling services where money or items of value can be wagered in exchange for the opportunity to win prizes with real-world value.

Content related to highly debated and politically or socially divisive topics, which is reasonably likely to cause offense to the average person with opposing views.

Content related to politics, governments, political science, political parties, elections, and political issues of public debate.

Content containing excessive use of strong language, explicit, offensive, or sensitive words and expressions.

Content related to religious and spiritual beliefs.

Content that contains references or depictions that are mildly provocative, or mature in nature, whether real, simulated, or animated. It doesn’t contain sexually explicit content.

Content that may cause shock, fear, or unease. It includes supernatural, disturbing elements, and horror themes.

Content related to the smoking of cigarettes, cigars, pipe tobacco, smokeless tobacco, and other tobacco or nicotine products.

Content that hasn’t been classified. This covers games on Twitch not rated by ESRB.

Content related to realistic weapons, such as firearms, bladed weapons, bows and arrows, and military equipment and vehicles.

## How content exclusion categories work with inventory tiers

Inventory tiers and content exclusion categories serve different but complementary functions:

- **Inventory tiers** balance brand protection with reach by applying different levels of protection across content types, from broad (Expanded) to strict (Restrictive).
- **Content exclusion categories** operate as on/off switches to completely avoid specific topics.

Together, these tools enable precise brand suitability management. Set your overall protection level with inventory tier, then use content exclusion categories to create clear boundaries around topics that don't align with your brand values.

## Set up brand suitability controls

**Prerequisites**

Before you begin, make sure your campaigns use Display or Online Video (OLV) media types.

Setting controls at the advertiser level applies them to all new ad groups that you create. Existing ad groups maintain their current settings.

1. In the navigation side menu, click **Campaigns**.
2. At the top of the **Campaigns** page, click the Display, Video, and, Audio tab.
3. In the page menu, click **Advertisers**. Find and select the advertiser your want to set the inventory tier for.
4. Next to **Update these settings when manager account inherited settings are changed**, click the toggle to disable it. This step allows you to customize the brand suitability control.
5. In **General**, next to **Brand Suitability**, click **Change**.
6. In the window that appears, select your desired inventory tier or content exclusion categories: Use **inventory tier** for broad protection levels across multiple topics and categories Use **content exclusion categories** for topic-specific blocking
7. Click **Save**.

**Things to keep in mind about advertiser-level controls**

- **New ad groups inherit advertiser-level settings:** When you create a new ad group, it automatically uses the brand suitability controls set at the advertiser level.
- **Existing ad groups:**Existing ad groups maintain their current brand suitability setting, regardless of whether they match the advertiser-level setting. To apply the advertiser-level setting to an existing ad group that hasn't inherited it, you can either: Copy an ad group that has your current brand suitability setting. Create a new ad group to inherit the advertiser-level inventory tier or content exclusion categories you set.
- **Changes take effect immediately:** Updates to brand suitability controls apply to new ad groups right away.

**Note**: Make sure to review your advertiser-level inherited settings to ensure future ad groups use the suitability controls that align with your brand’s requirements. Contact your Amazon Ads Agent (formerly known as Amazon DSP) representative or submit a support ticket if you'd like assistance applying these settings to your existing campaigns.
