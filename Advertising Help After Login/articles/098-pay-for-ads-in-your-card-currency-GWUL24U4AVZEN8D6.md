# Pay for ads in your card currency

You have the option to pay for your ads using your card currency and save on fees.

Updated on Jan 12, 2026

## How it works

When you purchase an ad for an Amazon store that's in a different country to where your card was issued, your bank charges about 2%* to convert the payment. For example, if you use a UK credit card to buy ads on Amazon.com (US), your bank may charge extra to process the conversion between British Pounds and US Dollars.

Amazon also provides an option to pay in your card currency. For example, you can use your UK card and pay in Pounds instead of US Dollars. With this option, you will be charged in your card currency and will not incur currency conversion fees. (Some banks may charge Foreign Transaction Fees which are different to Currency Conversion Fees. Please check with your bank to confirm)

Instead, Amazon charges a 1% service fee for this option, with no other fees or hidden markups. Compare this fee to your card's currency conversion fees to determine savings.

## Paying in card currency benefits

- 1% fee - half the industry average conversion fees which can exceed 2%*
- No more currency conversion calculations
- Less fees and less hassle, so you can focus on growing your business on Amazon

## How can I set this up?

1. Go to **Payment Settings**
2. Select your card's active country
3. Click **Change Payment Profile**
4. Choose and edit your preferred card
5. Select your payment currency

## More details on choosing the right option

Consider these key factors when choosing your payment option

**Find out what you pay for currency conversion**

Most credit and debit cards charge conversion fees, averaging 2%** Some banks bundle these fees into the final payment amount rather than listing them separately. Others may advertise "no Foreign Transaction Fees" - but may still charge other fees related to currency conversion. If you're not sure what the exact fee is, contact your bank to confirm.

**Calculate your potential savings**

Compare paying in your card currency to using your bank's conversion. Our fees are transparent so you can easily compare.

**Check your card's currency is eligible**

The following local card currencies are eligible for pay in card currency:

AED (United Arab Emirates), BGN (Bulgaria), CAD ***(Canada), CHF (Switzerland), CNY (China), CZK (Czech Republic), DKK (Denmark), EUR (European Union), GBP (United Kingdom), HKD (Hong Kong), HUF (Hungary), ILS (Israel), INR (India), JPY (Japan), KRW (South Korea), NZD (New Zealand), PLN (Poland), RON (Romania), SEK (Sweden), TRY (Turkey), TWD (Taiwan), USD (United States), VND (Vietnam)

*Based on a 2024 survey of 400 major credit card programs.

** Some banks may charge Foreign Transaction Fees which are different to Currency Conversion Fees. Please check with your bank to confirm.

***Excludes billing addresses in British Columbia.
