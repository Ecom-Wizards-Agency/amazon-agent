# Set bids in a Sponsored Products campaign

Learn how to set bids, choose a bidding strategy, and apply bid adjustments to optimize your Sponsored Products campaign performance.

Updated on Sep 28, 2026

You select your bidding strategy and set bids during campaign set up. You can change your bids or bid strategy once your campaign is live in the campaign manager:

1. In the navigation side menu, click **Campaigns**
2. Click the campaign you want to edirt to open it.
3. In the left menu, click **Campaign settings.**

## Step 1: Choose your bid type

The process to set a bit differs based on what type of targeting you're using in your campaign.

On the automatic targeting card:

1. Choose to set 1 default bid, or unique bids for each targeting group
2. Enter your bid in the text box The **"suggested bid for regular days"**is calculated from a group of recent winning bids similar to yours. The **suggested bid range** is also displayed. We recommend choosing a bid in this range. If a special event (such as Prime Day or a major holiday) is coming up in the next 45 days, we’ll also provide a suggested bid and bid range to use leading up to that event. During high-volume shopping events, bids may increase due to increased advertising activity. Higher CPCs typically occur in the weeks leading up to and during major shopping events.

**Note**: Cost-per-click bids that are set at the keyword level take precedence over the default cost-per-click bid set at the ad group or campaign level.

## Step 2: Choose a campaign bidding strategy

In Sponsored Products campaigns, you choose between a dynamic, fixed, or rule-based bidding strategy:

- **Dynamic bids - down only:** Amazon will reduce your bids for clicks that may be less likely to convert to a sale. If we consider that your ad may be less likely to convert to a sale, we might lower your bid for that auction.
- **Dynamic bids - up and down:** Amazon will increase your bids for clicks that may be more likely to convert to a sale. With this strategy, we'll also reduce your bids for clicks that are less likely to convert to a sale.
- **Fixed bids:** Amazon will use your exact bid for all opportunities and won't adjust your bids. Compared to dynamic bidding strategies, you may get more impressions, but fewer conversions for your ad spend with this strategy.

## Step 3: Set bid adjustments (optional)

**Note**: Higher CPCs typically occur in the weeks leading up to and during major shopping events such as Prime Day, Black Friday, and Cyber Monday. You may wish to increase CPC bids leading up to and during these events.

You can adjust bids by placement, audience, or video to increase your bid when your ad is eligible to appear in certain locations and/or to certain groups of advertisers. This can help you be more strategic with where your budget is spent. We recommend this strategy for experienced advertisers who have insights about where they want their ads to serve.

Enter a percentage amount to increase your bid when your ad is eligible to compete for one or all of these placements:

- **Top of search (first page):** Up to the first 5 positions in the top row of the first page search results on desktop, and up to the first 4 positions on mobile.
- **Rest of search:** the middle or bottom of shopping results and the second page of shopping results and beyond.
- **Product pages:**Sponsored Products ads on the product details page, and all nonshopping placements such as the add-to-cart page.
- **Amazon business placements:** all Sponsored Products placements that appear on Amazon Business including Top of Search, Rest of Search, and Amazon Business product pages.

You can enter your placement bid adjustments during new campaign set up, or add them to an existing campaign.

1. In the navigation side menu, click **Campaigns**
2. Open the campaign where you want to add a placement adjustment.
3. In the left menu, click **Bid adjustments**.
4. Next to your desired placement type and bid strategy, enter the value by which you want to adjust your bids by placement.

## Step 4: Set bid rules (optional)

Once your campaign is set up, you can also implement bid rules:

- **Rule-based bidding:**set a rule with a performance goal (example: Returns on Ad Spend or ROAS). Amazon then adjusts your base bids for each ad impression, increasing or decreasing them as needed to try to achieve your performance target.
- **Schedule bid rules:** schedule bid increases for specific times of the day, days of the week, date ranges, or high traffic events (example: Black Friday). This lets you take advantage of high-impact time windows to help you drive more sales.

To apply a bid rule:

1. In the navigation side menu, click **Campaigns**
2. Open the campaign where you want to add a bid rule
3. Next to the campaign name, click **More**, then select **Edit settings**.
