# Bidding strategies for Sponsored Products

Learn about bidding strategies and options to optimize your sponsored ad campaigns.

Updated on Sep 28, 2026

In Sponsored Products campaigns, you choose between a dynamic, fixed, or rule based bidding strategy. You can also adjust your bids by audience.

## Dynamic bids - down only

When you choose the dynamic bids - down only strategy, Amazon will reduce your bids for clicks that may be less likely to convert to a sale. If we consider that your ad may be less likely to convert to a sale, we might lower your bid for that auction. We may decrease your bids by up to 100% for all placements based on performance. This could happen, for example, on a less relevant search query or on a placement that doesn’t perform well, etc.

This strategy is ideal for:

- When you want to optimize for conversion but set the maximum bid amount.
- New advertisers
- Strict budgets.

## Dynamic bids - up and down

Amazon will increase your bids for clicks that may be more likely to convert to a sale. With this strategy, we’ll also reduce your bids for clicks that are less likely to convert to a sale. We may increase or decrease your bids by up to 100% for all placements based on performance. Since this strategy adjusts your bid up and down in proportion to the likelihood of a conversion, it may deliver more conversions for your ad spend compared to the other 2 strategies.

If we consider that your ad is more likely to convert to a sale, we might increase your bid for that auction. This could happen, for example, if your ad appears for a highly relevant search query, for a shopper who is highly likely to purchase, or on a placement that performs well. If we notice another opportunity that looks less likely to convert to a sale, we might lower your bid for that auction.

This strategy is ideal for:

- Advertising a product with good historical performance data.
- Campaigns targeting highly sought-after placements.
- Flexible budgets with room for higher bids.

Example: Amazon can adjust your bid of $1.00 up to $2.00 for all placements.

## Fixed bids

Amazon will use your exact bid for all opportunities and won’t adjust your bids. Compared to dynamic bidding strategies, you may get more impressions, but fewer conversions for your ad spend with this strategy.

You can manually adjust your base bids to optimize campaign performance. You can also use placement bid adjustments with fixed bidding campaigns to increase your bids for specific placements.

This strategy is ideal for:

- When you have historical campaign data and a specific target ACOS.
- Testing new products.

## Rule-based bidding

Rule-based bidding automatically adjusts your advertising bids to match your campaign goals. For any Sponsored Products campaign that meets the eligibility criteria, you can apply a rule with a guardrail of return on ad spend (ROAS). Amazon may then adjust your base-bids up and down to increase conversions while maintaining your guardrails. Learn more about bidding rules.

This strategy is ideal for:

- Large catalogs requiring automated management.
- Experienced sellers with established performance history.
- Campaigns with clear ROAS objectives.

## Bids by audience

**Note**: Advertisers outside of the US marketplace can only select up to 1 audience at this time.

In addition to selecting a bidding strategy, you can also adjust your bids by audience, which enables you to reach and engage custom-designed audience audiences. Select up to 10 audiences to boost your bids for specific groups of shoppers. Choose from Amazon audiences or AMC audiences and set an independent bid boost percentage for each one. Learn more about audience bid adjustments.

To get started, create your custom audiences in Amazon Marketing Cloud. These audiences will automatically appear in your sponsored ads account when you create new campaigns, allowing you to set specific bid modifications for each selected audience to enhance engagement. You can track how these audiences perform through the Audiences tab in the all campaigns page.
