# Create and manage a Creator Connections campaign

Learn how to set up and manage your Creator Connections campaign to engage Amazon Creators and drive sales for your products.

Updated on May 12, 2026

## Before you begin

- You must have a US advertising account based in the US, China (CN), or Hong Kong (HK).
- You must be registered in Brand Registry as the brand representative or authorized reseller.
- Sellers: Log in to Seller Central and go to **Creator Connections** under **Advertising** in the navigation bar.
- Vendors: Log in to the advertising console with your Brand Stores account and go to **Creator Connections** under **Brand Content** in the left navigation.

**Note**: If your brand is having access issues, admins can grant access in Seller Central by going to **Settings** > **User Permissions** > Target User > **Creator Connections**. If you're a new advertiser, allow up to 7-10 business days to gain access after creating your advertising account.

## Campaign setup

To create a Creator Connections campaign:

1. In the navigation side menu, click **Brand content**.
2. Click **Creator connections**.
3. Click **Create Request** next to your desired brand. Then select **Affiliate+ campaign**.
4. On the next page, complete the following in order: Basic information: Campaign name (80 characters maximum): Choose an engaging title that encourages Creators to participate. Message to the Creator (5,000 characters maximum): Include brand and product information, content guidelines and requirements, any specific campaign instructions, and required disclosures (terms like "Sponsored," "Advertisement," or "Ad"). Additional disclosure Start and end date: Select campaign duration between 30-365 days. Payment: Campaign budget: Enter the maximum amount (minimum $5,000) to spend on Creator commissions. Commission rate: Set the percentage (minimum 10%) Creators earn for qualifying sales. Products: Use search, enter list, or upload to add items to your campaign. You can add up to 1,200 ASINs per campaign.
5. At the top of the page, click **Create campaign**.

**Tip**: Select products with sufficient inventory for the campaign duration to maximize conversion potential.

## Tips for working with creators

- **Trust Creators' expertise** in connecting with your target audience. Creators understand their followers and how to create engaging content that resonates with them. Provide clear campaign objectives while allowing Creators creative freedom to develop authentic content.
- **Give Creators comprehensive but concise information** about your brand and products. Include essential details in your campaign message section without overwhelming them. This helps Creators produce relevant content while maintaining their authentic voice.
- Select products strategically by **featuring items that have demonstrated popularity or those you want to highlight**. Offer enough product variety to give Creators options for creating diverse content.

## Campaign management

After creating your campaign, you'll have 24 hours to edit or cancel the campaign before it goes live. After this window, you can only extend the end date or increase the budget.

- Once live, you can modify the Creator message, budget, and end date.
- You can monitor Creator participation in the Creator Connections portal.
- Use direct messaging to communicate with Creators who've accepted your campaign. Access messaging by clicking the message icon in the top right corner or by opening the messaging drawer in the bottom right corner of Creator Connections. You can only message Creators who've accepted your campaign. Samples aren't provided through Creator Connections, but you can offer them on a case-by-case basis through direct messaging.

Set competitive commission rates to attract Creators to your campaign. Higher commission rates can incentivize more Creators to participate and potentially lead to better engagement. For campaigns performing well, consider:

- Extending your campaign duration.
- Increasing your campaign budget.
- Run multiple campaigns simultaneously to promote different products. Group similar products together in campaigns for more organized management.

## Campaign performance metrics

You can monitor your campaign performance with the following metrics in the Creator Connections portal:

| Metric | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Spend** | The total charges incurred for a campaign. |
| **Sales** | The total value of products sold to shoppers within 24 hours after they clicked on a creator's link. Sales amounts are finalized within 7 days after the campaign ends, based on whether the products were shipped to customers. Sales totals are adjusted to exclude orders that are canceled within 72 hours or fail payment. |
| **Orders** | The number of Amazon orders placed by shoppers within 24 hours after clicking on a product link. |
| **Clicks** | The number of clicks on creator links to your products during the campaign. |

**Note**: Creator Connections orders are not linked to the order management center. To view orders and sales attributed to your campaign, go to the Creator Connections reporting portal directly. While you can't choose specific Creators, the campaign will be visible to all eligible Creators in Creator Connections. You only pay for qualified sales.
