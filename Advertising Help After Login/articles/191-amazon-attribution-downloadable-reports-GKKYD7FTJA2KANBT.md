# Amazon Attribution downloadable reports

Amazon Attribution offers reporting capabilities through Excel files to help you action on campaign results.

Updated on Nov 21, 2025

Reports are available to be generated and scheduled in the downloadable reports section. We recommend downloading and saving these reports monthly to retain the metrics. When you download a report, it shows the time zone under which the account is registered.

The following reports are available in the ads console:

**Campaign**

This report provides summary or daily performance metrics for all campaign and ad groups. Use the summary option to surface the best-performing strategies or tactics. The daily option shows one ad group per day, and it's useful for analyzing in detail or uploading into your analytics tools.

**Channel publisher**

This report aggregates data at the channel-publisher level and reporting is aggregated into a single time period. Use this report to surface the best-performing publishers or channels, and optimize toward channels or publishers that drive the most efficient reach.

**Keyword or Ad (formerly known as Creative on Amazon DSP)**

The Keyword or ad reports are only available for campaigns created using the bulk creation method. Bulk-created campaigns have 3 levels of campaign hierarchy (campaign, ad group, keyword/ad) and this report is the only way to view performance of the third level (keyword/ad). The report is similar to the campaign report and available in summary and daily views. Use the summary view to surface the best-performing strategies or tactics. This view shows 1 row per keyword/ad and summarizes performance for the selected time period. The daily view provides the same information, except broken out daily.

**Promoted product**

Analyze performance of products associated with your campaign. This report shows 1 row per ad group per product (ASIN) per day. This report helps you understand how your campaigns impact shopping activity and sales for your products on Amazon.

**Promoted product (Rollup)**

This report is same as Promoted product tab, but variation products (ASINs) are rolled up to the parent product level. Use this report for a more summarized analysis.

**Brand halo product**

Analyze your brand halo products’ daily or summary performance broken out by campaign, channel, and publisher. This daily view shows 1 row per campaign-channel-publisher combination per product per day. The summary reporting is aggregated into a single time period.

**Brand halo product (Rollup)**

Performance for the brand's products that weren't promoted (also known as Brand Halo), with all product variations (for example, size, color) rolled up to the parent product level. Use this report for a more summarized analysis.

**Brand halo snapshot report**

Analyze brand halo impact at a high level. Products are rolled up to the brand - product group level, not displayed individually. Campaigns are split up by campaign, channel, and publisher. Reporting is aggregated into a single time period.
