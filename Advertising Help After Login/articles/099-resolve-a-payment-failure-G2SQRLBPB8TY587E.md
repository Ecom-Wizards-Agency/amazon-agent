# Resolve a payment failure

Learn how to resolve payment failure notifications depending on your chosen payment method.

Updated on Jul 17, 2026

Was this video helpful? Select feedback

When Amazon can't charge your default payment method, your campaigns will pause. Most payment failures happen because of insufficient funds, expired or incorrect payment method information, and bank restrictions. You'll be alerted when your payment fails, and you must take action to update your payment method. Once you update your payment method, we automatically retry your payment and reactivate your account if the payment is successful.

## Insufficient funds

If you use **Deduct from payment (DFP)** to pay for advertising ensure the vendor codes associated with your payment profile have enough funds to cover your invoice in full. To add or remove vendor codes, go to Payment settings and edit your payment profile.

- **Funds available**: The amount of money in your account that is ready to be used to pay for advertising campaigns or disbursed to your bank account. This is your total account balance minus any holds, reserves, or pending charges. You can spend up to this amount on ads without needing to add additional payment methods.
- **Reserve funds**: A portion of your account balance that Amazon holds temporarily to cover potential chargebacks, refunds, or other adjustments. These funds are not available for spending on campaigns but will be released back to your account once the hold period expires or the associated transactions are finalized.
- **Total balance**: The complete amount of money in your Seller account, including both available funds and reserved funds. This represents your total account value before any pending transactions or future charges are processed.

Tip: Add a credit or debit card as a backup payment method in your payment profile. If your default payment method has an insufficient balance or fails, your card will be charged instead.

## Debit or credit card issues

If your debit or credit card fails, ensure that the card is active and the information was entered correctly.

- Confirm the name, number, and expiration in your payment profile matches your physical card.
- Assess if your card has sufficient funds (check your available credit limit).
- Ask your card issuer if they restrict electronic purchases or specific currencies.
- Check that your card hasn’t been reported as lost or stolen.

## Invoice issues

If you pay via invoice, make sure to submit your payment with enough time to process before the due date. Check payments can take 3 to 5 days to be applied and wire payments are typically applied within 24 hours.

If you receive a notification about an unpaid invoice but can’t locate any invoices on the Billing page, this may be due to previous payment attempts that have been written off. To correct this issue, update your payment method and we will automatically retry your payment.

## Payment failures for single global accounts (SGA)

To resolve payment failure if your payment method is expired, invalid, or declined, select **Update payment profile**. You'll be prompted to enter a new payment method. Once you update your payment method, we automatically retry your payment and reactivate your account if the payment is successful.

**Note**: If you add a new credit card, Amazon may issue a $50 or equivalent pre-authorization charge for verification purposes. This charge is refunded within 7 days. Please ensure your card has enough balance to handle the pre-authorization charge.

After the workflow completes, you'll see a status of Successful or Failed for each country in your account:

- **If all countries are successful**: your account is fully active and campaigns resume immediately.
- **If some countries fail**: review the failure reasons shown for each country. Resolve any issues (like updating your card or adding funds) and retry. Once resolved, we automatically retry your pending payments.
- **If all countries fail**: review the failure reason and resolve the issue if possible. If you still can't resolve it, contact support.

**Note**: While the system processes your invoices, you may see a "Payment in progress" status in your invoice table. This is temporary and will update to "Paid in full" or "Failed/Issued" once processing completes.
