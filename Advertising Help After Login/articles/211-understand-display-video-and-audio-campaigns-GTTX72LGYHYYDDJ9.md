# Understand Display, Video, and Audio campaigns

Learn how Display, Video, and Audio campaigns are structured and what goals they support.

Updated on Sep 16, 2026

## What are Display, Video, and Audio campaigns?

Display, Video, and Audio campaigns enable you to reach and engage with audiences across multiple formats including display, video, audio, and Streaming TV on desktop, mobile, and connected devices. When audiences interact with your ads, they can be directed to your product detail pages on Amazon, brand store, other custom landing pages or you can link out to your website.

**Note**: Some advertising options and features may vary by region.

## Account and campaign structure

Within your account, you can manage multiple advertisers—each representing a brand or client. The advertiser is the top-level entity that contains all campaigns, audiences, and ads. Each advertiser can have multiple Display, Video, and Audio campaigns. Campaigns are set up in 3 parts to organize and deliver your multimedia advertising across different formats:

- **Campaigns** set your overall advertising goal, budget, and key performance indicator (KPI). Every campaign contains 1 or more ad groups that inherit these top-level parameters. **Ad groups** define how, where, and to whom your ads appear. Within each campaign, you can create multiple ad groups with different targeting strategies, placements, and bidding approaches. **Ads** are the display, video, or audio assets that customers see. Each ad links to an ad group and serves based on the targeting and bidding rules you configured.

Each tier has its own workflow and plays a distinct role in your overall campaign strategy.

## Campaigns

Campaigns set the foundation for your advertising strategy. When you create a campaign, you define 3 key parameters:

- **Goal**: What you want to achieve (awareness, consideration, or conversion) Your goal determines which KPI options are available
- **Budget**: How much you'll spend
- **Key performance indicator (KPI)**: Metric that specifies which outcome to optimize your campaign for You can change your KPI after launch, but this resets your campaign's performance learnings and may impact optimization

Every ad group that you create within a campaign inherits these parameters, ensuring consistent strategy across your advertising.

**Campaign goals and KPI options**

| Awareness | Consideration | Conversion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The awareness goal increases exposure for your brand or product by reaching a larger audience. **Use awareness when** your goal is top-funnel visibility, brand recall, or reaching new customers. | The consideration goal encourages potential customers to learn more about your product through engagement. **Use consideration when** your goal is to drive clicks, product detail page views, or video engagement. | The conversion goal focuses on driving measurable actions like purchases, leads, installs, or sign-ups. **Use conversion when** your primary goal is sales or lower-funnel outcomes on or off Amazon. |
| **Reach** The number of unique users exposed from campaign start. **Frequency**(beta) The number of times an ad is shown to 1 user. | - **Click-through rate (CTR):** Number of click-throughs relative to impressions - **Cost per click (CPC):** Average cost for each click - **Cost per video completion (CPVC):** Average cost to acquire a video complete conversion - **Video completion rate (VCR):** Number of video completions relative to video starts - **Cost per detailed page view (CPDPV):** Average cost to acquire a detail page view for a promoted product on Amazon - **Detail page view rate (DPVR):** Number of detail page views for a promoted product relative to ad impressions | - **Return on ad spend (ROAS):** Average return on ad spend for promoted products sold on Amazon - **Total products ROAS (T-ROAS):** Average return on ad spend for all of a brand's promoted products sold on Amazon - **Combined ROAS (C-ROAS):** Average return on ad spend for all of a brand's promoted products sold on and off Amazon - **Cost per action (CPA):** Average cost per completed action - **Cost per install (CPI):** Average cost per mobile app install - **Cost per sign up (CPSU):** Average cost to acquire a new free trial or paid app subscription for an Amazon Prime Video channel or streaming app - **Cost per first app open (CPFAO):** Average cost to acquire a first-time app open |

**KPI target**

The KPI target generates reports in campaign management tables under the **Goal metric** column and facilitates optimization when you select the right bid priority.

**Example**: PureWave Home's goal is to increase visibility for their new air purifier among shoppers and streaming audiences. To do this, they create 2 separate campaigns with Amazon DSP:

**Campaign 1: Display campaign**

Create a display campaign with budget, flight dates, and delivery strategy. Set an awareness goal with a reach KPI target. Add an ad group to reach shoppers browsing air-quality products. Configure audience targeting, placements, and bidding. Add display ads (static images or HTML5 ad).

**Campaign 2: Streaming TV campaign**

Create a Streaming TV campaign with budget, flight dates, and delivery strategy. Set a consideration goal with a video completion rate KPI target. Add an ad group to reach a broader "healthy home" audience. Configure audience targeting, placements, and bidding. Add video ads (15-30 second Streaming TV ad).
