# Get started with Partner Network

Amazon Ads Partner Network (known as the Partner Network) is a self-service hub for agencies and tech partners to manage their business relationships with Amazon Ads.

Updated on Mar 6, 2025

## Getting started

Amazon Ads Partner Network is designed for all companies that serve advertisers on Amazon. You can register with the Partner Network to access marketing, learning resources, and upcoming events. These tools can help showcase your offerings and distinguish your company to prospective clients.

**First steps with Partner Network**

Once your business registration is approved, we recommend you complete the account set up by adding users and advertisers you support.

Through the Partner Network, you can also submit your company's directory listing, which will be reflected in the partner directory.

Make sure you're the representative of your organization and you're authorized to accept the Amazon Ads Terms and Conditions on behalf of your business, and to create and operate your organization’s Partner Network account. You'll be able to add additional users with other access levels during the account setup process.

**Partner Network assets**

If your company is registered on the Partner Network, the admins for your account will have the ability to download the Amazon Ads logo from the Partner Network. You can also view our marketing guidelines for using these assets.
