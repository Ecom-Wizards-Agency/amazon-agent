# Live Shopping (beta)

Learn how to use Amazon Live to showcase products through livestream shopping in your Brand Store.

Updated on May 5, 2026

Amazon Live is in beta and available for select advertisers in the US.

Amazon Live is a creative advertising format that gives brands an interactive live shopping tool to engage customers with immersive storytelling. Viewers can shop while they watch, making live shopping a powerful way to build awareness and increase consideration.

Your livestream appears in your Brand Store, where customers can discover it organically. To maximize reach, combine Amazon Live with paid advertising through Sponsored Brands, Display, or Amazon DSP directing your campaign to the page of your brand store featuring your livestream.

## When to use Amazon Live

Amazon Live works best for brands that want to create deeper connections with customers beyond traditional advertising.

Consider using Amazon Live when you want to:

- Launch new products by showcasing features and benefits through interactive demonstrations that answer customer questions in real time
- Tell your brand story by building emotional connections and sharing the people, values, and craftsmanship behind your products
- Educate customers by demonstrating product usage, sharing tips, and providing tutorials that help customers make informed purchase decisions
- Drive seasonal campaigns by creating excitement during key shopping moments like holidays, Prime Day, or back-to-school season

## Distribution

**Maximize your stream's impact by promoting it at every stage.**Drive tune-in before your stream goes live and extend engagement afterward by promoting the video-on-demand (VOD) content. Leverage Sponsored Brands, Display, and Amazon DSP campaigns to amplify reach across the Amazon Ads canvas. Additionally, share your streams on social media platforms to drive incremental traffic to your Brand Store.

## Use a proven media mix

- Start with the formats and channels that have historically performed well for your brand, then expand and test new options as you scale
- Keep content fresh and varied by balancing between static and video formats to keep content engaging
- Apply ad formats strategically—a 50/50 mix of static and video creatives tends to drive the best results, combining awareness with deeper engagement
- Make ads clickable by ensuring every asset includes a clear call-to-action so customers know how to engage (leading back to the Brand Store page featured on your livestream)
- Limit heavy branding—ads with minimal or subtle logos typically perform better than those with oversized or heavy-handed branding
