# Create a campaign with Brand+ or Performance+

Learn how to use Amazon Ads machine learning and first-party signals to automate campaign setup with Brand+ or Performance+ on Amazon DSP.

Updated on Jul 16, 2026

This page only lists settings **specific** to creating an ad campaign for advertisers that want to use Brand+ or Performance+ .

## What's Brand+ and Performance+?

Brand+ and Performance+ are AI-powered advertising solutions that leverage Amazon first-party data to optimize campaign performance and delivery while achieving your campaign goal.

Brand+ helps identify and engage prospective customers, allowing you to reach them **before** they enter the market for your products.

- Drive brand awareness and engagement by reaching prospective customers that are highly likely to engage with your brand
- Control brand safety, geography, and inventory selection
- Gain insights about campaign impact including time to convert and shopper traits
- Boost upper- and mid-funnel campaign effectiveness

Performance+ drives consideration and conversions, allowing you to reach customers when they're **currently** in the market for your products.

- Drive sales, leads, detail page views, or desired objectives on Amazon or beyond
- Engage the right customers with unified consideration, customer acquisition, remarketing, and retention strategies throughout the purchasing journey
- Leverage Amazon's exclusive shopping and entertainment signals
- Reconnect with customers who searched for relevant products but didn’t convert

Use Brand+ and Performance+ to create ad groups (formerly known as Line Items on Amazon DSP) that use tactics (strategies that create audiences via machine learning aligned to your desired objective).

Review the eligibility requirements below to ensure your campaign meets the minimums listed.

## Tactic types and their eligibility requirements

Tactics require customer interaction history such as search, purchase, and watch behavior. Minimum audience size requirements vary by advertiser type and tactic. Audience sizes may need to be higher than the minimums listed to account for consumer opt-outs and duplicate removal.

**Tactic types and minimum audience size requirements**

**Tactics available to**: All advertisers

- **Prospecting**: No audience size requirement

**Inventory requirements** Online Video (OLV) or Display ad groups that use tactics require non-Amazon owned inventory, like: Third-party (3P) exchanges Amazon Publisher Direct (APD) 3P deals Inventory groups that contain deals with non-Amazon supply See a complete list of inventory supported by ad groups.

**Eligibility requirements** Amazon DSP checks eligibility when you create your campaign: **Eligible campaigns**: The tactic widget appears after you save your campaign settings. **If it's not eligible**: Tactics recommendations won't appear. You'll see an alert to add more ASINs or off-Amazon conversion events. You can still create your campaign using standard targeting options. **During the campaign run**: If unique users drop below the minimum audience size requirement, your ad group will automatically suspend and resume when audience size requirements are met again. This applies only to tactics with audience size minimums.

## Create a campaign with Brand+ or Performance+ using these settings

To advertise with Amazon DSP, you need to create a campaign, associate ad groups with the campaign, and assign ads to each ad group.

To create a campaign with Brand+ or Performance+ :

1. First, decide which tactic aligns with your advertising objective.
2. Then, follow the steps in Create a campaign. Under **Goal and optimization**, select the goal and KPI settings outlined below that make your campaign eligible for that tactic type.

**Brand+ tactics**(for all advertisers)

- **Prospecting** Campaign goal: **Awareness** or **Consideration** KPI: **Reach**, **Frequency**, **Cost per video completion rate (CPVC)**, or **Video completion rate (VCR)** Conversion tracking: **Featured product ASIN** (**Off-Amazon conversion events**for non-endemic advertisers)

**Performance+ tactics**(for endemic advertisers)

- **Customer acquisition** Campaign goal: **Conversions** KPI: **Return on ad spend** (ROAS / tROAS) Conversion tracking: **Featured product ASIN**
- **Remarketing** Campaign goal: **Conversions** KPI: **Return on ad spend** (ROAS), **Click through rate** (CTR), **Cost per detail page view** (CPDPV), **Detail page view rate (DPVR)**, or **Cost per click (CPC)** Conversion tracking: **Featured product ASIN**
- **Retention** Campaign goal: **Conversions** KPI: **Return on ad spend (ROAS)**, **Click through rate (CTR)**, **Cost per detail page view (CPDPV)**, **Detail page view rate (DPVR)**, or **Cost per click (CPC)** Conversion tracking:**Featured product ASIN**
- **Search** Campaign goal: **Conversions** or **Consideration** KPI: **Return on ad spend** (ROAS), **Cost per detail page view** (CPDPV), **Detail page view rate** (DPVR), **Click through rate** (CTR), or **Cost per click** (CPC) Conversion tracking:**Featured product ASIN**
- **Unified consideration** Campaign goal: **Consideration** KPI: **Cost per detail page view** (CPDPV) or **Detail page view rate** (DPVR) Conversion tracking: **Featured product ASIN**

**Performance+ tactics**(for non-endemic advertisers)

- **Customer acquisition** Campaign goal: **Consideration** KPI: **CPA** Conversion tracking: **Off-Amazon conversions** (any type with "Optimize" checked)
- **Remarketing** Campaign goal: **Conversions** KPI: **Return on ad spend** (ROAS), **Click through rate** (CTR), **Cost per detail page view** (CPDPV), **Detail page view rate** (DPVR), or **Cost per click** (CPC) Conversion tracking: **Off-Amazon conversions** (Page View event type)
- **Retention** Campaign goal: **Conversions** KPI: **Return on ad spend** (ROAS), **Click through rate** (CTR), **Cost per detail page view** (CPDPV), **Detail page view rate (DPVR)**, or **Cost per click (CPC)** Conversion tracking: **Off-Amazon conversions** (Off-Amazon Purchases event type)

After you save your settings, Amazon DSP will automatically generate and recommend tactics eligible for ad groups in your campaign, with the appropriate tags appearing in the management table.

Next, set up your ad group and attach it to your campaign.

## Activate your campaign and monitor performance

Once your foundational components are set up, activate your campaign so it delivers and shows to customers.

1. In the navigation side menu, click **Campaigns**.
2. At the top of the **Campaigns** page, click the **Display, Video, and, Audio** tab.
3. In the **Ad groups** page, activate the ad groups by clicking the **toggle** in the **Active** column.
4. Activate your campaign by clicking the **toggle** at the top of the window next to the campaign name.
