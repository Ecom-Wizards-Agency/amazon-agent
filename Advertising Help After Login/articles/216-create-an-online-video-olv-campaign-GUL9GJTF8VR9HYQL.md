# Create an Online Video (OLV) campaign

Learn how to create Online Video (OLV) campaigns on Amazon DSP.

Updated on Sep 28, 2026

This page only lists settings **specific** to creating an ad campaign for Online Video (OLV).

## What's an OLV campaign?

OLV is an Amazon Ads video solution that allows you to reach audiences on Amazon-owned sites (Alexa, Twitch, and IMDb.com) and across the web on third-party (3P) publisher sites (Amazon Publisher Direct and third-party exchanges). OLV ads appear both in-stream (before, during, or after video content) and out-stream (in nonvideo environments between text and images) across desktop, mobile, and tablet.

**Note**: OLV is best suited for awareness and consideration goals.

## Create an OLV campaign with these settings

To advertise with Display, Video, and Audio campaigns, you need to create a campaign, associate ad groups with the campaign, and assign ads to each ad group. Use the tabs below to view settings for each step of the setup process.

If you have any custom deals that you'd like to run, ensure that they are set up on their own ad groups. This will help maximize performance and delivery, simplify troubleshooting, and will help to accurately adhere to spend commitments, if applicable.You can choose to target prepackaged curated Amazon Publisher Direct (APD) deals, as well as third-party exchange partner (3PX) deals. Contact your Amazon representative if you can't find a deal or would like more details on any APD or 3PX deals.

**Things to keep in mind**

- Ensure that bids for ad groups targeting deals are adjusted to the deal floor CPMs.
- Don't add any audience targeting to ad groups for deals as that may limit the scale of delivery.
- Private auction and preferred deals can be targeted in the inventory section.

Learn more about troubleshooting deals.

Next, set up your ad group and attach it to your campaign.

## Activate your campaign and monitor performance

Once your foundational components are set up, activate your campaign so it delivers and shows to customers.

1. In the navigation side menu, click **Campaigns**.
2. At the top of the **Campaigns** page, click the **Display, Video, and, Audio** tab.
3. In the **Ad groups** page, activate the ad groups by clicking the **toggle** in the **Active** column.
4. Activate your campaign by clicking the **toggle** at the top of the window next to the campaign name.
