# Live Events Optimizer campaign management

Learn how to manage and optimize Live Events Optimizer campaigns, including timing guidelines, inventory options, and access requirements.

## Optimization timing

**When to start optimizing**

Campaign optimization timing depends on flight duration and several factors, including budget, flight dates, and audience reach settings.

- **Flights ≥ 1 month:** Conduct first optimization 5 to 7 days after launch
- **Flights ≤ 1 week:** Conduct first optimization 2 to 3 days after launch

At this stage, the system generates initial learnings and you'll have visibility into campaign performance.

**Measuring optimization impact**

Performance improvements take time to show effect after optimization. Wait 24 to 48 hours minimum to measure performance impact after making changes. Data tables update only 2 to 3 times daily in the user interface, which affects when you can see results.

## Inventory management

**Mixing inventory types**

You can mix live events inventory with regular streaming TV inventory within standard "All Content" Streaming TV ad groups. However, for optimal performance with live events content, use dedicated Live Events Optimizer ad groups that provide event-specific pacing and forecasting designed for live content's unique demands.

**Reaching specific events**

You reach audiences through broader event series or leagues via deals rather than specific individual games. Select deals that provide access to the events properties that align with your campaign objectives.

## Pacing and budget management

**Event-specific pacing**

Event-specific pacing distributes budgets across scheduled game windows based on viewership patterns, rather than spreading spend evenly across all days in your flight. This helps prevent budget waste on non-event days and ensures delivery during high-value moments when audiences are watching live content.

**Budget automation**

Budget automation isn’t available for Live Events ad groups. Due to the unique traffic profiles of live events, explicit budget amounts that remain stable help ensure accurate pacing and forecasting across concentrated event windows. This helps the system reliably deliver your budget during scheduled events.

## Creative requirements

Live Events campaigns use standard Amazon DSP video creative specifications. Refer to the creative specifications guide for detailed requirements. Creatives must meet both Amazon DSP standards and individual publisher requirements for approval.

## Availability and access

**Geographic availability**

Live Events Optimizer is available globally where Amazon DSP operates. The majority of supply is currently available in the United States, with inventory availability dependent on publisher certification in each country.

**Access requirements**

Live Events Optimizer is available through the standard Amazon DSP user interface, public API, and bulksheets. Advertisers with existing Amazon DSP access can create Live Events Optimizer ad groups without additional onboarding or access provisioning beyond standard Amazon DSP permissions.

**Existing campaigns**

You can continue to run campaigns with live events inventory using standard Streaming TV ad groups. Preexisting campaigns won’t be disrupted. However, to access specialized Live Events features (unified live events inventory access, event-specific pacing, and enhanced forecasting and optimization), create new campaigns using the dedicated Live Events Optimizer ad group experience.
