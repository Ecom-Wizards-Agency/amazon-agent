# Ads content moderation

Amazon Ads uses a combination of automation and expert human review to monitor ads, and to process and respond to reports of ads that don’t comply with the Amazon Ads policies.

Updated on Jul 2, 2026

## Ads moderation

Amazon Ads uses a combination of automation and expert human review to monitor all aspects of ads for infringing, noncompliant ads, and to process and respond to reports of illegal ads and ads contrary to the Amazon Ads policies. Our tools use natural language processing and machine learning. This means that new information is fed into them daily and they learn and get better at proactively and automatically blocking suspicious ads.

Beginning February 2026, to reduce the volume of emails you receive, we’ll no longer send email notifications when an ad is approved or under review. You can check the status of your ads at any time in the ad console (usually within 24 hours, but this could take up to 3 business days). Review times may vary by ad product and may be extended during periods of high submission volume, including large numbers of simultaneous submissions.

If your ad doesn’t meet our policies, you’ll receive an email notification with the reason for rejection and guidance on how to make updates. **Exception**: For Sponsored Products Books campaigns, we’ll no longer send email notifications, but you can check the status of your ads at any time in the ads console.

## Appeals process

If you believe that your ad is compliant with Amazon Ads policies and the ad rejection is incorrect, you can submit an appeal using the link provided in our communication to inform you of our decision. Once your appeal is reviewed, you’ll be notified of the outcome.

## Account suspension and reinstatement

To ensure a high customer experience bar, Amazon Ads requires that advertisers comply with all Amazon Ads policies. Ads, assets, destinations and other content that violate Amazon Ads policies can be blocked by Amazon Ads, and repeated violations may result in account suspension. The duration of the suspension and possibility for reinstatement will correspond to the severity of policies violation. If you disagree with our decision or believe there’s been an error and that you haven’t violated Amazon Ads policies, you can submit an appeal using the link provided in our communication to inform you about the suspension. Once your appeal is reviewed, you’ll be notified of the outcome.
