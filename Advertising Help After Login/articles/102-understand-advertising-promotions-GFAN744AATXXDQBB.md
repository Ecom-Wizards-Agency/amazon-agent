# Understand advertising promotions

Learn about the process for redeeming promotions and receiving ad credits.

Updated on Aug 11, 2026

Was this video helpful? Select feedback

**Note**: Promotional credits apply only to **future advertising costs** after you receive the credits. They don't cover costs from the promotional period itself.

## How promotions work

Amazon Ads may offer ad credits to eligible advertisers through promotional campaigns. If you're eligible, you can participate in a promotion either **by invitation** or **with a promo code**. Use the tabs below to learn more about each participation method.

Amazon runs promotions based on specific eligibility criteria. If you're eligible for a criteria-based promotion, you'll receive communication from Amazon and see it in the **Promotional offers** section of the dashboard. You may also see public offer promotions available to all advertisers on the dashboard.

**Note**: If you're a new advertiser, allow up to 2 business days after registering for Amazon Ads to view eligible offers.

Advertising promotions on Amazon Ads typically follow this process:

1. **Notification**: You'll receive communication with the promotion offer and related terms and conditions if you're eligible.
2. **Qualifying Action**: You'll need to complete specific actions and, in some cases, spend a certain amount on advertising within a specified time frame to receive the ad credits.
3. **Credits issued**: Once you've completed the qualifying action, the system automatically distributes these credits without requiring manual intervention. If you've performed the action according to the terms and conditions, Amazon automatically applies the ad credits to your account for future ad spend.
4. **Usage**: You can use these ad credits for your advertising campaigns before they expire as per the terms of the promotion. **Campaign-type restrictions**: Certain promotional funds can only be used for specific campaign types. For example, Sponsored Brands promotional funds can only be applied to Sponsored Brands campaigns. **Future costs only**: Credits apply only to future costs, not those you've incurred during the promotional period. **Automatic application**: Funds are automatically applied to qualifying invoices. You don't need to take any action to use the funds apart from running an eligible campaign.

**Example**

You get an email that you're eligible to receive $100 ad credits for completing the following Qualifying Actions:

- (i) reactivate a Sponsored Products campaign by January 15 and
- (ii) Spend $50 by February 15.

If you reactivate and spend the required amount within this timeframe, and meet the terms and conditions, you'll receive a $100 ad credit automatically applied to your account.

**Note**: You're responsible for advertising costs incurred to complete the qualifying action. In the example above, you'll pay the $50 from your own advertising budget. The $100 ads credit will apply to future advertising spend.

## Terms and conditions

Terms and conditions (T and Cs) may vary across promotion and countries. Review the applicable promotion terms and conditions in full before you participate to ensure you've met all requirements:

- **Invitation promos**: The T and Cs will be in the invite email.
- **Promo codes**: View T and Cs here.
- **New Seller Incentives:** Check your promotion's terms and conditions for marketplace-specific tier thresholds and requirements.

They contain:

- **Ad product eligibility**: If the promotion is specific to Sponsored Brands, Sponsored Products, Display, or Streaming TV.
- **Qualifying action**: Description of the action or actions you need to take to qualify for the ad credits.
- **Promotional period**: The required time period where you need to perform the qualifying action to qualify for the ad credits.
- **Offer amount**: The amount of ad credits offered.
- **Ad award date**: The date you'll receive your ad credits by.
- **Ad expiration date**: When the awarded ad credits will expire.

**Note**: Promotional credits are marketplace-specific. A credit issued for a marketplace can't be applied to invoices in different marketplace.
