# Understand billing and payment profiles

Learn about managing global billing and payment profiles for Amazon Ads to simplify account setup and maintenance across multiple countries.

## Billing profiles

A billing profile establishes who is going to pay for advertising and contains info like billing address, tax details, and contact info. Your billing profile is used for tax calculation and generating commercial invoices for advertiser cost.

When editing your account information, you can identify your account as an **Advertiser/ brand owner** or an **Agency**.

- If you select **Advertiser/ brand owner**, you’ll need to enter your advertiser name, address, and emails. You can click the checkbox **Use this address as the billing address** to add a separate **Billing address**, and enter your **VAT number**, if applicable.
- If you select **Agency**, you’ll need to enter your agency name and choose from a drop-down menu your holding company, if applicable. You’ll also be required to enter an address and email address, which will be used to send your payment records. You can click the checkbox **Use this address as the billing address** to add a separate **Billing address**, and enter your **VAT number**, if applicable.

Additionally, you can add your purchase order (PO) number to your billing profile. A PO or a booking reference number is a payment reference that you can add on your invoice to help you identify it. Once you enter it, your invoices will continue to display that number until you update this field again.

## Global billing profiles

Global billing profiles can help you save time when setting up accounts in new countries by copying existing billing information. You can manage all your accounts from a single page, where each billing profile appears as a tile. These tiles work across multiple countries, allowing administrators to edit billing addresses, tax IDs, payment methods, and POs.

When setting up new countries, you can either use an existing profile as your default or create a new one. The system automatically applies your default profile settings to newly registered countries. If additional information is needed after copying the default settings, you'll receive a notification prompting you to provide the missing details. Once the system confirms that your default account details are compatible with the country requirements, you can proceed to add payment information.

## Global payment profiles

You can use global payment profiles to copy existing payment methods when setting up accounts in new countries, which can help you save time during account setup across different regions.

You can manage all payment profiles from a single page, where each payment profile appears as a tile usable across multiple countries. Administrators can edit payment methods and set a default profile that automatically applies to newly registered countries, or create new profiles as needed.
