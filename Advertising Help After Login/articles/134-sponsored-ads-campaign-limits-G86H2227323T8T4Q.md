# Sponsored ads campaign limits

Sponsored ads have the following maximum limits.

Updated on Jul 29, 2026

Sponsored ads have maximum limits to ensure optimal campaign performance and system stability. These limits apply to Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands, and Display campaigns across all advertiser types (sellers, vendors, and authors).

## Understanding campaign limits

Campaign limits define the maximum number of campaigns, ad groups, ads, keywords, and targeting elements you can create in your advertising account.

**Note:**Advertisers can archive campaigns, ad groups, ads, or targets to free up space. Amazon now increases customer limits as they approach a limit beyond the limits listed below. Customers can contact us to request ad hoc increases, if they are planning large account changes or restructures.

## Campaign structure limits

| Type | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Campaigns in your account (active and paused) | 10,000 |
| Ad groups in a campaign | 20,000 |
| Ad groups in an account | 200,000 |
| Ads in an ad group | 10,000 for manual targeting |
| Ads in an account | 2,000,000 |

## Targeting limits

| Type | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Keywords in an ad group (excluding negative keywords) | 1,000 for manual targeting |
| Keywords in an account (including negative keywords) | 2,000,000 |
| Category and product targets in an ad group | 10,000 |
| Campaign negative keywords | 10,000 |
| Ad group negative keywords | 10,000 |
| Negative products in an ad group (product targeting) | 10,000 |

## Negative keyword character limits

| Match type | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Negative phrase | 4 words or 80 characters |
| Negative exact | 10 words or 80 characters |
