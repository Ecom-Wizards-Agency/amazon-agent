# View your portfolio reports

You can download portfolio data from your portfolios page or from the Reports tab.

Updated on Feb 26, 2026

**Access Reports from the Portfolios page:**

In campaign manager, you can create reports from the campaign or portfolio view and download chosen performance metrics to a spreadsheet. The report will include all data filtered in the table at the time of download.

1. Go to **All campaigns** view. Click**Portfolios** if you want to download data for all portfolios, or select the individual portfolio.
2. Click **Export** if you want to download data for all portfolios.
3. To download data for an individual portfolio, click the portfolio name, then click **Export**.
4. To add or remove metrics to the report, click the **Columns**button and then **Customize columns.**
5. Check the box next to a metric to add or remove it from the report. When finished, click **Apply** and **Export**.

**Reports tab:**

You can export campaigns and keyword data with a portfolio column in Excel for easy pivot table creation.

1. Click the **Measurement & Reporting** icon on the left.
2. Click **Sponsored ads reports**.
3. Click **Create report**.
4. Configure your report settings.
5. Click **Run report**. (You may have to click this twice.)

**Note:**The campaign performance history always stays at campaign level.
