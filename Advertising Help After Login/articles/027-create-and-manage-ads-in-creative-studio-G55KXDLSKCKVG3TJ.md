# Create and manage ads in Creative Studio

Learn how to use Creative Studio to create image and video ads using AI.

Updated on Sep 16, 2026

Creative Studio provides multiple ways to create AI-powered ad assets. The experience you choose depends on your advertising goals and preferred workflow. All ads you create using Creative Studio will require moderation and approval before they can be used in campaigns.

## Access Creative Studio

Follow the steps below to access Creative Studio.

1. In the left navigation, click **Creative Tools**. Then, click **Creative Studio**.
2. Choose your creative method and follow the steps below.

## Collaborate with Creative Agent to create ads

**Note**: Creative Agent is only available to advertisers in the US.

Follow the steps below to plan and strategize your campaigns, create videos and images, and write scripts with Creative Agent.

1. Type a message in the chat box or click a suggestion button to start talking to Creative Agent.
2. Follow the conversation to create campaign ideas and materials like video ads, concepts & storyboards, video, and audio.
3. To get better results, click the **+** button anytime to upload your own materials. Creative Agent will use these as examples for new ads. Make sure to: Only use materials that you have permission to use. Adhere to the requirements shown in the upload window. Click **Done**to save your uploads.

## Create images

Follow the steps below to create images with AI and save them to your assets folder to be used in your campaigns.

1. Click**Generate images** under **Other ways to create**.
2. Choose how you want to make your image:
  1. Click **Add a product** to pick one of your products.
  2. Click **Describe an image** to tell AI what you want
  3. Click **Upload an image** at the top of the window to use pictures from your computer
3. Click on an image thumbnail to preview, edit, or delete it.
4. Save the image by clicking **Save.**

## Create videos

Follow the steps below to create videos with AI and save them to your assets folder to be used in your campaigns.

1. Click**Generate videos**under **Other ways to create**.
2. Search for your products by name or ASIN.
3. Click **Add** next to the product you want to use.
4. Click **Generate video**.
5. To improve your videos: Click **Assets > Update assets** so AI can use them as examples. Click **+ Logo** at the bottom navigation bar to add your logo.
6. Click a video thumbnail to watch the video.
7. (optional) Make more video options by clicking **Generate more**.
8. (optional) Edit a video’s logo and text as needed by hovering over the thumbnail and clicking **Edit**. Your changes will be saved automatically.
9. Save videos by hovering over a thumbnail and clicking **Save**
10. If you’re ready to create a Sponsored Brands campaign with a generated video: In the top-left corner of the video thumbnail, Click **Select.** Click **Create Sponsored Brands campaign**. Your campaign settings will prepopulate with your relevant products and video you created.

## Manage your saved AI-generated assets

After creating images and videos in Creative Studio, you can find and manage them in your Creative Assets library. You can also access them anytime when creating campaigns in the campaign manager.

- Learn more about the Creative assets library.
- Learn how to find and manage your saved assets.

## Generation limits and troubleshooting

**Note**: Generation limits cannot be increased. Wait for the date shown in the error message before trying again.

Creative Studio limits the number of assets you can generate within a rolling 30-day period. This applies to both image and video generation. If you reach this limit, you'll see an error message with the date when you can resume generating assets. This limit will automatically reset as older asset generations age out of the 30-day window.

**If you see a generation limit error:**

1. Note the date shown in the error message.
2. Stop retrying until that date, as additional attempts will not succeed.
3. Return to Creative Studio after the date shown to resume generating assets.
