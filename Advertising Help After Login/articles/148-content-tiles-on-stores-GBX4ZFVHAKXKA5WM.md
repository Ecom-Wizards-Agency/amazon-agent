# Content tiles on Stores

There are multiple content tile styles available to personalize your Store.

Updated on Jul 22, 2026

Every Store page is divided into tiles that you can customize to personalize your Store. All Store content tiles have 4 sizes: full width, large, medium, and small.

## Header

Every Store page has a header section at the top of the page. The header section includes: hero image, brand logo, and navigation bar.

- **Hero image:** To support various screen resolutions, the image will be cropped on resolutions below 1500px wide. Up to 16% of the left and right sides will be cropped on small screens. The brand logo will cover up and additional 18% of the left side of the hero image on small screens. Any Logos or text in the hero image must be in the safe area to prevent cropping.
- **Brand logo:** The brand logo acts as a home button and is displayed on all pages in the center of the navigation. It’s configured in the Store settings.
- The navigation bar is automatically generated as the Store is built. The arrangement of items in the menu can be controlled by reorganizing your Store using the page navigator tool in the Store builder.

## Text

Text tiles contain free form text. They can link to a Store or product detail page. The text can be positioned left, right, center or justified. The number of lines available will depend on the tile size.

## Image

Image tiles can link to a Store page or product detail page. Adding a link title to an image tile will cover some of the uploaded image.

- For rectangle images (medium and full width tiles) with link titles, about 19% of the image will be obscured at the bottom.
- For square images (small and large tiles) with link titles, about 12% of the image will be obscured at the bottom.

We don’t advise uploading images with embedded text as the text won’t be readable by external search engines or users who rely on-screen readers to navigate your Store. To add text to images, use the “Image with text” tile option.

## Image with text

Image with Text tile has 2 layout options: Text over image or Image next to text.

- **Text over image** allows you to overlay text directly on an image. It includes the ability to: Link to a Store page or product detail page Add multiple text elements such as prefix, heading, body, and link text Choose text size, alignment, and content padding Configure the colors and placement of the text overlay on the image
- **Image next to text** allows you to add text beside an image. It includes a heading and description text.

## Video

Video tiles require an image and a video file. On a desktop, they play in line with the option to expand to full-screen. On mobile, they play full-screen. Image size, video resolution, and aspect ratios vary with the tile size.

## Video Reveal

With Video Reveal, you can upload a short video (2 to 8 seconds long and less than 4MB) about your brand. This video will be showcased as a full-screen video to customers before revealing your Store pages.

You can define different video files to play for mobile vs desktop users.

## Product

Product tiles highlight a single product and can be customized to various sizes. All product tiles display price and Prime status and they link to the product detail page.

For full-width product tiles, you can choose between 2 layouts:

- Product detail layout automatically displays information from the detail page.
- Product editorial layout displays a custom title and description.

Large and small product tiles show price and prime status. Medium product tiles can include a custom product title and description.

Custom text requirements will vary on tile type and size.

## Product grid

The product grid tile displays products in a product grid. The list of products can be selected manually or based on search keywords. Each row will display 4 products in a row on desktop and 2 products in a row in mobile.

This tile type displays product name, product image, price, star rating, and shopping actions such as the **Add to cart** button. Each product in the grid links to the product detail page.

**Add to cart button**

Products in a product grid display an **Add to cart** button that allows customers to add the item to their cart directly from your Store. However, some products won’t show the **Add to cart** button, for the following reasons:

- The product has multiple variations to choose from before purchasing such as sizes or colors.
- The product allows for clipping coupons.
- The product allows for Subscribe & Save.
- The product is out of stock in your inventory, but is available through third-party sellers.
- The product is categorized as **Apparel**.

## Gallery

The gallery tile displays up to 8 images.

## Best seller

The best seller tile displays the top selling products in the brand. It displays 5 products at a time, and won’t be shown if the brand doesn’t have best seller products. There’s no setting required for this tile type. If the tile meets the criteria, it will display automatically.

## Recommended products

The recommended products tile displays products from the brand that are recommended to the customer based on previous shopping history. It displays 5 products at a time, and won’t be shown if the brand doesn’t have recommended products for the customer. There’s no setting required for this tile type. If the tile meets the criteria, it will display automatically.

## Featured deals

The featured deals tile shows products with active promotions. The products will automatically appear in the tile for as long as the promotions last. When a promotion ends, products associated with the promotion won’t appear in the tile.

Promotions that show in the tile include: Best Deal, Deal of the Day, and Limited Time Deal. Coupon-based promotions are not eligible in Belgium, Germany, Spain, France, Italy, the Netherlands, Poland , and Sweden.

You can choose for the tile to feature:

- **All products**: All of your Store products and the products in the catalog associated with your Store will appear in the tile when they have active deals.
- **A curated set of products**: You enter the ASINs of specific products that you want to feature, and they’ll appear in the tile when they have active deals and are in stock. The products will appear in the order in which you enter them. Child ASINs must be used as parent ASINs aren’t supported.
