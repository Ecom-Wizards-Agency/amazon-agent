# Live Events Optimizer

Learn how to set up and optimize ad groups within your STV campaigns optimized for live events inventory including sports, concerts, award shows, and cultural programming.

Updated on May 7, 2026

## Overview

Live Events Optimizer is a purpose-built programmatic advertising solution within Amazon DSP that helps you buy live events inventory with specialized workflows designed for live content's unique demands. Unlike traditional programmatic solutions that use generic video optimization, Live Events Optimizer provides event-specific pacing, enhanced forecasting, and streamlined campaign and creative management to help you reliably deliver budgets during peak engagement moments.

## Key benefits

**Unified workflow and supply access**

Live Events Optimizer consolidates live inventory from Amazon's owned properties and certified open internet publishers through a single workflow. This helps eliminate the need to navigate separate workflows for each publisher.

**Purpose-built infrastructure for live events**

Event-specific pacing distributes budgets across scheduled event and game windows based on viewership patterns. Intelligent forecasting uses actual event schedules rather than generic historical data to help provide reliable delivery during extreme traffic spikes.

**Advanced optimization capabilities**

- Cross-channel frequency management helps prevent redundant impressions when viewers switch between content
- Unified audience reach across all live properties enables sophisticated optimization unavailable through direct buying approaches
- Integration with broader video strategies allows you to manage live events within your existing streaming TV campaigns

**Reduced operational friction**

- Creative pre-registration reduce approval timelines and creative rejections
- Consolidated reporting across all publishers eliminates manual reconciliation
- Streamlined campaign setup with dedicated ad groups (formerly known as Line Items on Amazon DSP) enables you to focus on strategic optimization

## Setting up a Live Events ad group

**Step 1: Create a new ad group with Live Events content type**

1. Navigate to your campaign in Amazon DSP
2. Select Create Ad group within an existing or new campaign (formerly known as Order on Amazon DSP)
3. Choose Streaming TV as your Media Type
4. Select Live Events Optimizer as your Media Strategy

When you select Live Events Optimizer as your media strategy, specialized workflows activate throughout the ad group configuration process, including automated filtering for live events inventory, event-specific pacing options, and streamlined settings designed for live content's unique demands.

**Note:**The media type and strategy selection becomes permanent once saved to ensure consistent behavior and help prevent accidental configuration changes that could disrupt campaign performance.

**Step 2: Configure Products and Services**

Configure your Products and Services settings as you would for standard Streaming TV campaigns. There are no changes to this section for Live Events ad groups.

**Step 3: Select live events inventory**

When you select Live Events Optimizer, the Deals widget automatically filters to show only live events-tagged inventory. This helps you discover inventory without manually searching through hundreds of generic video deals.

To select live events inventory:

1. Navigate to the Inventory section of your ad group settings
2. Browse deals automatically filtered for live events content
3. Select the deals that align with your campaign objectives

You can add nonlive events deals to other ad groups within the same campaign, enabling unified campaign and frequency management across both live events and standard streaming TV (or other) inventory.

**Tip:**Use Inventory Hub to discover deals by browsing available live events inventory from certified publishers. Look for deals labeled with live events indicators to help identify relevant inventory.

**Step 4: Configure audience and location targeting**

Live Events Optimizer supports standard geographic and audience targeting options to help you connect with relevant audiences:

**Location targeting**

- City
- State
- Country
- DMA®
- Postal code

**Audience targeting**

- In-market
- Interest
- Lifestyle
- Demographic
- Lookalike
- Custom built
- Third-Party

As you configure your targeting settings, the forecasting widget provides projected reach, spend, and eCPM estimates based on actual event schedules rather than generic historical data. This helps you plan campaigns with confidence knowing your forecasts account for the concentrated viewing windows unique to live events.

**Step 5: Set up delivery and pacing**

Live Events Optimizer uses specialized pacing designed for live content's unique traffic patterns.

To configure delivery settings:

1. Navigate to the Delivery section of your ad group settings
2. Set your budget amount
3. Configure your flight dates
4. Review the default pacing profile: Deliver Budget Across Events

**Deliver Budget Across Events pacing profile**

This pacing option distributes your budget across events in your flight based on expected viewership and engagement patterns, rather than spreading spend evenly across all days. This helps ensure reliable budget delivery during the concentrated windows when live events actually occur.

The system allocates the budget to days when games or events are scheduled, helping prevent spend attempts on nonevent days and ensuring proportional budget distribution across events in your budget's flight.

**Frequency capping**

Configure frequency caps as you would for standard Streaming TV campaigns. When you create both Live Events and standard video ad groups within the same order, frequency management works across both ad group types to help prevent overexposure.

**Step 6: Configure bidding settings**

Configure your bid price settings as you would for Streaming TV campaigns optimizing for reach objectives.

**Step 7: Submit creatives for preregistration**

Live Events Optimizer includes streamlined creative pre-registration that helps accelerate approval timelines and reduce rejection rates.

To submit creatives:

1. Navigate to the Creatives section of your ad group settings
2. Upload your video creatives following standard Amazon DSP creative specifications
3. Submit creatives for approval

Creative approval process:

- Automated validation checks creatives against publisher requirements
- You receive approval status and detailed feedback if rejections occur

**Best practice:** Submit creatives well in advance of your campaign start date to allow sufficient time for approval before your first scheduled event.

**Step 8: Review and launch your campaign**

1. Review all ad group settings to confirm your configuration
2. Confirm your budget, flight dates, inventory selection, and audience reach settings
3. Select Book ad group to create your ad group
4. Once creatives are approved, your campaign will begin delivering according to your event schedule

## Optimization best practices

- Check the campaign dashboard for performance over time. If the campaign performance is trending in the right direction after the first 7 to 10 days, it's likely your setup is working. If you see a dip or no improvement, that's a good sign that you'll need to optimize.
- Allow time between each optimization so that you can isolate what's working and what isn't. Give each change 4 to 7 days before making further changes.
- Monitor delivery throughout your flight to ensure pacing is tracking as expected across scheduled events.
- Use frequency metrics to help ensure you're reaching audiences efficiently without overexposure.
- Review video completion rates to understand creative performance during live events.

- Don't set overly restrictive targeting settings, budget caps, or frequency caps that could constrain budget delivery.

## Planning and setup optimization

**Plan campaigns around event schedules**

Use the forecasting tools to understand projected reach and spend based on actual event schedules. This helps you allocate budgets appropriately across your budget's flight and set realistic performance expectations.

**Leverage frequency management**

Create both Live Events and standard video ad groups within the same order to enable unified frequency management with order-level frequency caps or frequency groups. This helps prevent overexposure across your streaming TV campaigns and may improve cost efficiency.

**Allow sufficient time for creative approval**

Submit creatives well in advance of your campaign start date to help ensure approval before critical event windows. This helps prevent last-minute rejections that could cause you to miss high-value moments.

**Use dedicated Live Events ad groups for optimal performance**

While you can mix live events inventory with standard streaming TV inventory in regular ad groups, dedicated Live Events ad groups provide superior performance through event-specific pacing and optimization designed for live content's concentrated traffic patterns.

**Delivery optimization**

- Consider adjusting the frequency cap to deliver more impressions to each audience. This can be done by either increasing the impressions (2 impression per day to 4 impressions per day) or shortening the time unit (1 impression per 12 hours to 1 impression per 6 hours). Keep in mind that while this could strengthen delivery, it could also weaken performance.
- Traffic additional ad sizes to expand reach opportunities.
- Leverage multiple deals from different publishers to diversify supply access.
- Don't set overly restrictive targeting settings, budget caps, or frequency caps that could constrain budget delivery.

## Performance optimization

**Budget adjustments**

Allocate more budget to the best-performing ad groups if their delivery rate is favorable (higher than 95%). Monitor performance across different events properties to gain insights about audience engagement.

**Bids and frequency**

Adjust in-flight the bids and the frequency caps per ad group depending on the performance and delivery of each.

**Audience reach**

- Remove the least performant audience segments from your reach settings, but pay attention to the budget delivery scale of the remaining segments.
- Test additional audience segments that overlap with your intended audience to expand your reach.

**Creatives**

Optimize the creatives by isolating specific sizes that perform best for your campaign objectives. Review video completion rates to identify which creative formats resonate most with live events audiences.

## Reporting

Live Events Optimizer integrates with Amazon DSP's existing reporting infrastructure. You can view performance using the same reporting tools you use for other Streaming TV ad groups and campaigns.

**Available metrics**

- Reach and frequency
- Impressions and spend
- Video completion rate
- Conversion tracking

To access reporting:

1. Navigate to the Campaign Manager in Amazon DSP
2. Select your order containing Live Events ad groups
3. View ad group performance metrics in the campaign dashboard
4. Export reports for detailed analysis as needed

You can also use Amazon DSP's standard reporting tools to create custom reports that include your Live Events ad groups alongside other campaign ad groups for consolidated performance visibility.
