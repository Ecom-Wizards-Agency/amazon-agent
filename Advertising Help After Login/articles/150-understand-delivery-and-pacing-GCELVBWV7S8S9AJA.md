# Understand delivery and pacing

Learn how delivery metrics help you pace your Display, Video, and Audio campaigns.

Updated on Oct 5, 2026

Delivery metrics for active flights don't appear until they've been running for 2 days.

## Delivery

Use the **delivery rate** metric to monitor the efficiency and pacing of your spending. It is capped at 100%.

Delivery rate is a historical metric. It reflects what has already been spent. A delivery rate of 100% means your campaign is on pace as of today, but does not guarantee that your full budget will be spent by the end of the flight.

## Pacing

The rate at which your budget is spent throughout a flight is also called pacing. When you set up an , you'll choose a pacing profile (even. pace ahead, or ASAP) to determine how we’ll attempt to spend budget and deliver impressions throughout your campaign. ## Pacing profiles

When you set up an , you choose a pacing profile to determine how your budget is spent throughout the flight:

**Even**

Select this option when you'd like your to spend roughly the same amount each day.

If underdelivering, this will attempt to spread the deficit across the remainder of the flight, instead of trying to catch up to 100% delivery immediately.

Delivery rate will trend upward over the remainder of the flight so long as budget spend isn't constrained by settings such as bid price and audience targeting.

**Pace ahead**

Select this option if your top priority is to spend your budget over an entire flight, without risking potentially underspending. Pace ahead can deliver up to 25% more than Even pacing.

If underdelivering, this will attempt to spread the deficit across the remainder of the flight and won't attempt to catch up to 100% delivery immediately.

**ASAP**

Select this when you want to spend a large budget in a day, such as when adding new budget towards the end of the flight.

For events longer than one day, use even pacing to distribute your budget across the entire event period. ASAP won't improve delivery if your is limited by narrow targeting parameters.
