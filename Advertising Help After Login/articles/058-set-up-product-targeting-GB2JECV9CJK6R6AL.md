# Set up product targeting

Learn how to add product targeting to your campaign.

Updated on Feb 20, 2026

## What is product targeting?

**Product targeting** allows you to choose specific products, categories, brands, or other product features that are relevant to the product in your ad. You can target categories and products individually or target a combination of categories and brands in the same campaign. Use this strategy when:

- You’re looking for broad reach (rather than targeting specific keywords)
- You’re launching a campaign in a foreign marketplace and don’t want to rely on translations to do keyword research.

## Add targeting to an existing campaign

Return to a campaign you set up previously to add more product targets:

1. In the navigation side menu, click **Campaigns**
2. Select the desired campaign.
3. Select the desired ad group.
4. Click **Targeting** in the left-hand menu.
5. Click **Add products**.
6. Choose to target categories or individual products. **Categories:** you can target suggested categories that are based on relevance to your product. You can refine categories by brand, price range, and review star ratings. Sponsored Products and Display: you can also refine categories by Prime shipping eligibility. In addition, in the Toys & Games category, you can refine by the product's age range. For the Books and eBooks, you can refine by genre. **Individual products:** you can target suggested individual products that are similar to the product in your ad. You can also search for specific products in the search field.
7. Browse suggested products, search, or upload a list. Click **Add next** to each product you want to include.
8. Click **Add product targets**.

## Expanded product targeting

Expanded product targeting helps you discover additional products related to the items you’re already targeting. It is the default setting for campaigns. The Expanded box next to the targeted product will be automatically selected during campaign set-up.

Expanded product targeting works by:

- Choosing closely related products **based on past interactions and views**. This can help you discover and scale to other high-performant product targets.
- Only expanding each product to a limited number of additional products with the **highest performance potential**.
- **Automatically updating** the underlying products targeted daily. This helps ensure that high-performant product targets are included in the expanded list without having to constantly curate new product targets.
- Including expanded product targets appear in available reports with a “Product-expanded” prefix in the targeting column. This allows you to **identify the expanded product targets** that meet your performance criteria to promote them as individual product targets. Additionally, you can use negative targeting to exclude individual products or brands from the expanded list.
