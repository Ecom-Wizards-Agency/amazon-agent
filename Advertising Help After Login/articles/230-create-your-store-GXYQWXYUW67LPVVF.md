# Create your Store

You can create or edit your store using Amazon’s self-service Store builder. It’s easy to create a multipage experience, and no design expertise is necessary.

Updated on Jul 22, 2026

Stores templates and widgets are all designed for mobile web, app, and desktop. As you build your Store, you can view how your Store looks on different devices by toggling the view between **Mobile** and **Desktop**. Additionally, you can add device-specific assets to optimize your content by device type.

When you create or edit your Store, it will go through our moderation review process to ensure it meets our policies before it goes live. The review process is usually completed within 24 hours, but may take as long as 3 business days. We’ll email you to let you know if your Store was approved or if changes need to be made.

## How to create a Store

1. Go to **Brand content** > **Brand Stores**.
2. Click **Create Brand Store**. **Note:**For Amazon sellers, if your Brand Registry status is **Approved**, but you’re seeing an error when creating your Store, use the **Contact us** link on this page for support.
3. Enter your **Brand display name**. This is your brand's customer-facing name and will be used in the Store’s URL and navigation bar. Follow our policy requirements when entering your brand name: **Note:**The brand display name is case-sensitive. We suggest using the exact brand name shown in catalog systems or your Vendor or Seller Central.
  1. Leave out call-to-action (CTA) such as “Shop now.”
  2. Exclude extra phrases or information such as “LLC” or “Inc.” It’s not permitted to use “Store or Stores” in this space unless it’s a part of your brand’s name.
  3. Be consistent with either using a title or sentence case.
4. Upload your **Brand logo** and select whether your logo should be displayed in the Hero region of your Store.
5. Choose your **Product grid style** to determine how you’d like your products to show in your Store when they appear in a grid.
6. Click **Next**.
7. Enter a brief summary of the page content in the **Page meta description** box. This will appear in shopping results under the page title.
8. Select a template. We’ve provided page templates to help you quickly set up the page with preorganized tiles or start with a blank page.
9. Click **Create page** and start adding content to your Store. To learn how to customize and manage content, visit Edit Store pages. Tip: Alternatively, you can use the Brand Store Builder Agent to create your brand store pages quickly using AI-generated content and layouts that follow brand store best practices.
10. Once you’ve completed your store, click **Submit for publishing**. Stores offer 2 publishing options:
  1. **Standard publishing:** This option makes your Store available to customers as soon as your content is approved by our moderation team. To select this option, click **Submit** in the pop-up window.
  2. **Scheduled publishing:** This option gives you the opportunity to request the date and time when you would like your store to be made available to Amazon customers. To use this option, select the **Request publish date** checkbox, choose your desired date and time, and click **Submit**. **Note:**Your requested date and time must be at least 24 hours in the future to allow time for moderation review. If your store isn’t approved, you'll need to make corrections and resubmit your store and scheduling for publishing.
11. After you submit your store using either publishing option, it will be reviewed to ensure it meets our policies. We’ll notify you when the review is complete, and you can check the status in the status bar. Learn more about Stores moderation.

**Tip:**Before you publish your store, you should check for the following:

- Spelling or punctuation errors.
- All changes are included in the draft version.
- Use the mobile preview to make sure that your store is optimized for mobile experience.
- Check your images and videos to make sure that any text in them can be easily viewed (including on mobile).
