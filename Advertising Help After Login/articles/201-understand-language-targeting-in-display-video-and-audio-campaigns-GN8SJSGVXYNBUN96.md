# Understand language targeting in Display, Video, and Audio campaigns

Learn how to use language targeting to serve ads in the preferred language of your audience.

Updated on Sep 28, 2026

## What is language targeting?

When you use language targeting, you can upload multiple ads in different languages and attach them to the same ad group. The feature will automatically select the appropriate ad based on the supply content language.

**Example**: You select language targeting at the ad group level and attach 2 ads:

- Ad 1 in English
- Ad 2 in French

When the ad is served, the French version will appear on French language content, and the English version will appear on English language content.

You can enable language targeting during line item setup, or with Bulksheets. We recommend using bulksheets to manage language targeting for multiple ad groups. In the bulksheet column titled Language, click the drop-down menu and select Enable.

## Limitations

For Programmatic Guaranteed (PG) deals, language targeting is available for select first-party publishers (example: Amazon Prime Video and Twitch). Language targeting settings for PG deals are configured on the publisher side. During deal creation, inform your Amazon representative that you want to create a language-targeted PG deal. The check box for language targeting won’t appear under targeting settings, it must be enabled during deal creation.

## Multilanguage ads

When using multilanguage ads, you don't need to enable Language targeting in your line item settings. Even when language targeting is disabled, for multilanguage ads, Amazon uses available language signals in the supply to automatically select and serve the most appropriate language variation of the ad.

**Example**: If you enable multilanguage support for a Responsive e-commerce ad (REC) in the US, and the supply has language signal Spanish, even with language targeting disabled, the Spanish language variant of your multilanguage ad will be displayed.
