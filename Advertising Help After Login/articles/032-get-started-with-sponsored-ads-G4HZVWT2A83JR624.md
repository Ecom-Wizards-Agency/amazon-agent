# Get started with sponsored ads

This guide will help you prepare your account for advertising and choose a campaign type to begin advertising.

Updated on Apr 30, 2026

## 1. Familiarize yourself with a few basic terms

- **Bid:** The amount you’re willing to pay when shoppers click or view your ad.
- **Conversions:** An action that a customer completes; this phrase can be used to refer to any number of actions, including clicking “Add to Cart,” completing a purchase, etc.
- **Featured Offer:** the section of the product detail page with an “Add to Cart” button that customers can use to add items to their shopping cart or “Buy Now.”
- **Impressions:** The number of times an ad was rendered on a page or device.
- **Keyword:** These words or phrases are used to target your ads to relevant shopping queries.
- **Placement:** The place where an ad appears.
- **Reach:** The total number of users who are shown the same ad on any web page or screen.
- **Shopping Query:** Words or phrases Amazon customers use to look for products.

## 2. Review sponsored ads eligibility requirements

To advertise with Amazon, you must:

- Be a professional seller, vendor, author, or advertising agency.
- Have an active account in good standing with Amazon.
- Posses the ability to ship to the country in which you're advertising.
- Set up a valid payment method.

## 4. Get your product detail pages ready for advertising

One of the most important parts of a successful advertising campaign is to set up your products properly. At Amazon, we’ve analyzed what makes your products most likely to perform well when they’re advertised. Review our 6 top tips below. For more tips and information, read how to improve your products for advertising.

**1.** Create a strong product title **(informative, easy-to-read, approximately 60 characters long).**

**2.** Feature at least 4 high-quality product images.

**3.** Select products with a rating of 3.5 stars or more and at least 15 customer reviews.

**4.** Price products competitively to boost their chances of being the featured offer.

**5.** Keep products in stock to provide a positive customer experience.

**6.** In the description, include at least 3 bullet points that give an overview of your product’s key features.

## 5. Link accounts (if applicable)

Vendors: you can link a Vendor Central account to your advertising account to find products you may want to advertise more easily when building campaigns. You’ll also receive better product recommendations from Amazon to help improve your return on advertising.

- Visit the **Access and Settings** section in the advertising console, select **Selling Account**, and then select the option to **Link your Vendor Central account**.

If you’re advertising with Sponsored Brands or Stores, you must enroll in the Amazon Brand Registry. Visit the Brand Registry website for registration requirements and instructions.

## 6. Create your first campaign

In the navigation side menu, click **Create campaign**. Choose which type of campaign you’d like to get started with. As you build your campaign, click the links throughout the workflow to access related help or click the question mark at the top right of the page to visit the Support Center.
