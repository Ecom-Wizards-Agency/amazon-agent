# Manage your Partner Network account

Amazon Ads Partner Network (known as the Partner Network) is a self-service hub for agencies and tool providers to manage their business relationships with Amazon Ads. It is designed for all companies that serve advertisers on Amazon. You can register with the Partner Network to access marketing, learning resources, and upcoming events. These tools can help showcase your offerings and distinguish your company to prospective clients.
