# Understand targeting in Display, Video, and Audio campaigns

Learn about the targeting options available in Display, Video, and Audio campaigns to reach specific audiences with your display, video, and audio ads.

Updated on Sep 28, 2026

## What is targeting?

Targeting is how you specify which shoppers you want to reach with your display, video, and audio ads. You can show relevant ads to potential customers based on their shopping behaviors, interests, the content they're engaging with online, and more.

Targeting in Amazon Ads Agent leverages Amazon's first-party insights based on billions of observed shopping patterns to help you:

- connect with shoppers most likely to be interested in your products.
- combine different targeting expressions to control reach.
- serve ads based on in-the-moment browse, buy, and stream events (contextual targeting).

During ad group set up, you'll have the opportunity to add targeting criteria. Based on the media type you selected, certain criteria will display by default.

## Targeting attributes

**Product:** reach shoppers in-market for specific products, and expand your reach to shoppers in-market for complementary products. **In-market categories:** reach high-intent shoppers by selecting from comprehensive product categories. This combines contextual category targeting with behavioral signals to reach customers most likely to purchase in your selected categories. **Keyword:** add keywords sourced from Amazon search queries, detail pages, and third-party websites to engage shoppers who have demonstrated recent purchase intent through their search behavior. Learn how to set up mult-signal targeting.

## Audio and video targeting attributes

When you create an audio or video ad, you have additional targeting options to consider.

**Content Blocking** - Exclude content based on available content signals.

- **Explicit** - Excludes content that rights-holders have marked as explicit. This is the only content you can block for Podcast.
- **Unrated** - Excludes content for which we have no rating from rights-holders.
- **News** - Excludes content tagged as news.

**Genre blocking:** Exclude up to 5 genres across music inventory (Amazon owned properties and third-party inventory).
