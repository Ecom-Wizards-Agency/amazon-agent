# Understand Creative Studio

Creative Studio is a central place in the Amazon Ads Console where you can find all AI-powered tools (like Creative Agent) for making ads. Access different ways to create images and videos to improve your creative workflow.

Updated on Sep 15, 2026

## What is Creative Studio?

Creative Studio is a central destination to access generative AI and agentic AI creative tools and solutions - enabling advertisers of all skill levels to develop professional-level quality content. It brings together different ways to create ads, including Creative Agent (a chat-based AI tool), image creation, and video generation features.

Creative Studio has a tutorial for first-time users to help you get started. All AI-generated content goes through Amazon Ads moderation review before you can use it.

Learn how to create and manage ads in Creative Studio.

## Benefits:

Creative Studio makes your creative work easier by putting all AI-powered tools in 1 place. Instead of going to different areas for different ad tasks, you can do everything from 1 location.

This approach lets you pick the ad creation method that works best for you. Whether you like chatting with AI through Creative Agent or creating images directly, Creative Studio supports different work styles while keeping your ads consistent.

**How it works with campaigns**

Use Creative Studio in your campaign building workflow. Generated assets can be saved to your Creative Assets library and can be selected when building campaigns in Campaign manager. You can use these assets across multiple ad types and campaigns.

## Ways to create ads

Creative Studio offers 3 main ways to create ads:

- **Creative Agent**: This can be used to chat with AI for guided help. Enables you to create ad concepts, storyboards, video, images and audio, all while leveraging audience insights from multiple data points across Amazon and marketplace. **Note**: Creative Agent is only available to advertisers in the US.
- **Image generation**: To generate images using AI from your product ASINs or custom ideas. You can upload and edit photos with different styles.
- **Video generation:** Transform a single product image or video and ASIN into professional quality video ads with AI.

There are limits to the number of assets you can generate within a rolling 30-day period. If you see an error, wait for the date shown in the error message before trying again. Learn more about generation limits and troubleshooting.
