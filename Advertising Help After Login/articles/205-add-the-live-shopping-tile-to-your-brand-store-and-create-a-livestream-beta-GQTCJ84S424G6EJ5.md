# Add the Live Shopping tile to your Brand Store and create a livestream (beta)

Follow step-by-step instructions to add the Live Shopping tile to your Brand Store and create a livestream. See below for details on configuring streaming equipment, selecting products, and scheduling your livestream for maximum visibility.

Updated on Apr 9, 2026

Amazon Live is in beta and available for select advertisers in the US.

Amazon Live is a creative advertising format that gives brands an interactive live shopping tool to engage customers with immersive storytelling, and drive product consideration and discovery with educational and inspirational content experiences. Viewers can shop while they watch, making live shopping a powerful way to build awareness and increase consideration.

## Add the Amazon Live tile to your Brand Store

**Tip**: Schedule the Live Shopping tile on your Brand Store's most visited page or on multiple pages and make sure it's placed on the same page your marketing campaign is driving traffic to for maximum visibility.

**Steps**

1. From your Amazon Ads Console account, navigate to your Brand Store by selecting **Brand Content**, then **Brand Stores** in the left menu.
2. This will take you to your Brand Stores homepage. If you have multiple Brands, choose the Brand Store where you want to add the Amazon Live tile.
3. Click **Edit Brand Store**.
4. Make sure you are editing the correct version by locating the **Version** dropdown at the top of your Brand Store.
5. Select **Add new section** under **Home page**, **Page sections** in the right sidebar.
6. Select **Live Shopping** from the **Choose Tile Type** right sidebar.
7. This will take you to the **Editing: Live Shopping** screen. Under **Reposition livestream video after**, select how long you want your video to show up on your Brand Store. You can choose from 1 to 60 days after the stream concludes.
8. Make sure **Chat in livestreams** is enabled.
9. Select **Play all livestreams, by recency** to play the most recent livestreams first or select **Play a single livestream of your choice** to highlight specific content. Choose **Select a livestream** to choose the livestream you want to feature.

Once you've added the Live Shopping tile to your Brand Store, leave it there. It will automatically appear when you go live and disappear once your scheduled duration ends.

## Create a livestream

**Tip**: Read our Live Shopping best practices before creating your first livestream.

Before you begin, ensure that you have:

- A Brand Store where your livestream will appear
- Products from your catalog to feature during the stream
- Streaming equipment (mobile device, external camera with OBS software, or professional studio setup)

**Steps**

1. From your Amazon Ads Console account, navigate to **Creative Tools** in the left menu
2. Select **Live Shopping**.
3. Click to open the Amazon Live web application in a new tab
4. Select your streaming equipment (device camera or external cameras through OBS)
5. Add a title and description for your stream
6. Choose products from your catalog to feature in the interactive carousel
7. Configure your streaming quality and chat settings
8. Use **Practice Mode**to test your audio, video, and product carousel before going live
9. Choose to schedule your stream for a specific date and time, or go live immediately
