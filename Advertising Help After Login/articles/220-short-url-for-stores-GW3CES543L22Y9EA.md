# Short URL for Stores

Once you’ve launched your Store, you can update the long URL to a shortened URL.

Updated on Jan 5, 2026

Once your store is launched, a long URL is automatically generated. Short URLs transform long URLs into smaller, easily recallable web addresses. A short URL can be assigned only to the homepage of your store.

Using the **Contact Us** link on this page, you can submit a request to the support team providing the current Store link and the short link you want to have.

**Example:**

- Long URL: http://www.amazon.com/l/storename/ref=lp_surl_2812789011
- Short URL: http://amazon.com/storename

Tip: We recommend that you plan ahead and manage Short URL operations such as editing or deleting with plenty of time before major sales, deals, or holiday event.

## Best practices to create a short URL

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| The short URL must include your brand name or a name associated with your Amazon Ads account. Example: amazon.com/[brand name] | Don't use generic category names, including Amazon-owned category pages. Example: Amazon.com/toys. |
| The short URL can be requested in lowercase, uppercase, or a combination of capital letters. Example: amazon.com/[BrandName] or amazon.com/[brandname] | Brands with names that conflict with Amazon internal products or trademarks won’t be given short URLs. Example: Fire, Kindle, Alexa, Echo. |
| Characters such as "-" and "_" are allowed but only in place of spaces in brand names. Example: amazon.com/brand_name. | Don't use special characters such as "@," "$," "#," "*," "/" in short URLs. Short URLs can't begin or end with "-" or "_". |
| The short URL can be processed if the city name is part of the brand name. Example: amazon.com/Boston Lager. | Brands with names that conflict with city names, such as Seattle or Australia, won’t be given short URLs. |

**Note:**Short URL functions in all marketplaces, but will display only on the URL for Amazon.com.
