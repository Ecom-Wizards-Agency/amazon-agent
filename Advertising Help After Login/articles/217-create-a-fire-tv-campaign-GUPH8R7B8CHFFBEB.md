# Create a Fire TV campaign

Learn how to create Fire TV campaigns on Amazon DSP to reach customers with native display and full-screen video ads on their streaming homepage.

Updated on Sep 28, 2026

This page only lists settings **specific** to creating an ad campaign for Fire TV.

## What's a Fire TV campaign?

Fire TV campaigns are full-screen, video-forward ads that customers see before they dive into their favorite streaming apps and channels. When creating a Fire TV campaign, you'll choose from the following ad formats based on your advertising goals and ad (formerly known as Creative on Amazon DSP) assets:

| Ad format | Appearance | Ad group | Ad compatibility |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Feature Rotator** - **Online Video (OLV) specific Feature Rotator ads** are only available to advertisers in the US that are creating campaigns without deals. | Ads that appear in a visible carousel above the fold of the Fire TV UI. This ad format shows in a larger size than Inline Banners or Sponsored Tiles. See the Feature Rotator specs page for marketplace availability. **Unselected** **Selected** | Online Video (OLV) | Standard video |
| Display | Fire TV - Feature Rotator |
| **Inline Banner** | Ads that appear as full rows in each major browsing section of the Fire TV user interface (UI). See the Inline Banner specs page for marketplace availability. **Unselected** **Selected** | Display | Responsive eCommerce creatives (REC) or Fire TV - Inline banner |
| **Sponsored Tile** - Available only to Prime Video Channel & TVOD advertisers. | Ads that appear as separate tiles **within** a row of the Fire TV and Prime Video UI. See the Sponsored Tile specs page for marketplace availability. **Unselected** **Selected** | Display | Fire TV and Prime Video - Sponsored Tiles |

**Note**: Display ad groups can support both Inline Banner Feature Rotator, and Sponsored Tile ads, while OLV ad groups can only support Feature Rotator ads.

- If you're a UK and EEA advertiser that's transmitting customer data in those regions, you must use 1 of these consent formats: Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF) Amazon Consent Signal (ACS) Global Privacy Protocol (GPP)
- Mobile measurement partners (MMPs) including Adjust, AppsFlyer, Airbridge, Branch, Kochava, Singular, and Tenjin can share consent signals with Amazon Ads.

## Create a Fire TV campaign with these settings

To advertise with Display, Video, and Audio campaigns, you need to create a campaign, associate ad groups with the campaign, and assign ads to each ad group. Use the tabs below to view settings for each step of the setup process.

The settings below are specific to campaigns for Fire TV. Only the unique required fields for this campaign type are listed — standard campaign settings also apply. See Create a campaign for complete instructions.

**Media**

- Type: **Streaming TV**, **Display**, or **Online Video** Choose multiple types if you plan on adding more than 1 ad group type to your campaign

**Goal and optimization**

- Goal: To expose your brand or products to potential customers: **Awareness** To drive potential customers to your store or website: **Consideration** To drive conversions: **Conversion**
- KPI: Select from the available options that best match your campaign strategy

**Targeting and optimization**

- Frequency: Uncheck **Limit the number of times a shopper can see this ad**. We recommend setting your frequency when creating your ad group.

Next, set up your ad group and attach it to your campaign.

## Create a Fire TV ad

**Note**: The ad types that you can create depend on which ad group type you set up. When you create an **Online Video (OLV)** ad group, you can only create **Feature Rotator** ads. When you create a **Display** ad group, you can create **Inline Banner**, **Sponsored Tile**, and **Feature Rotator** ads.

1. In the navigation side menu, click **Campaigns**.
2. At the top of the **Campaigns** page, click the **Display, Video, and, Audio** tab.
3. In the **Campaigns** page side menu, click **Ads**. Then, click **Create ad**.
4. Choose an advertiser, if applicable.
5. On the **New ad** page, click the appropriate tab to access the ad setup page compatible with the ad group you created. ## Activate your campaign and monitor performance

Once your foundational components are set up, activate your campaign so it delivers and shows to customers.

1. In the navigation side menu, click **Campaigns**.
2. At the top of the **Campaigns** page, click the **Display, Video, and, Audio** tab.
3. In the **Ad groups** page, activate the ad groups by clicking the **toggle** in the **Active** column.
4. Activate your campaign by clicking the **toggle** at the top of the window next to the campaign name.
