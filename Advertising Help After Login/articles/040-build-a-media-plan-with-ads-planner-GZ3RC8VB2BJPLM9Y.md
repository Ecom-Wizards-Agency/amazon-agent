# Build a media plan with Ads planner

Learn how to build media plans in Ads planner to get campaign forecasts and budget insights.

Updated on Feb 17, 2026

You must be signed in to a Manager account to use Ads planner.

In Ads planner, you can view insights about what audiences, channels, and ad formats will best serve your goals- and create a media plan that reflects these learnings.

## 1. Create your plan

Enter your plan details:

1. Open Ads planner. In the left menu, click **Insights and planning**. Click **Ads planner**. Select a Manager account and choose a country if prompted. Click **Create plan**.
2. Enter your account information or choose the account you want to create a plan for.
3. Optional: Choose a brand category to get suggested categories for your plan.
4. Select an industry that best aligns with the products or services being advertised, then click **Done**.
5. Choose your media plan objective Achieve multiple marketing goals: reach customers across their shopping journey. Build brand awareness: improve familiarity with your brand and products. Optimize streaming TV campaigns: create a plan to strategically allocate your budget across streaming services.
6. Enter a name, time period, and budget for your media plan.
7. Choose whether to advertise Products sold on Amazon Click **Add** to select up to 10 categories you want to see shopper traffic data for. Products and services not sold on Amazon.
8. In the upper right, click **Next**.

## 2. Choose your goals

Choose your audience type and KPI:

1. Select one or more types of audiences that you want to forecast, Awareness: learn about who isn't aware of your brand or products. Add Product categories or Audiences that are relevant for your potential customers. Consideration: learn about who isn't engaging with your brand. Search by Product categories or Audiences. In the Include section, you can combine multiple audience selections. You can also specify audiences to exclude. Conversion: learn about who's viewing and purchasing. Search by product name, ASINs, or upload a template. Loyalty: engage customers who have already purchased your products (only available for products sold on Amazon).
2. For each goal, view the default KPI chosen. To change it: Click **Change**. Choose a new KPI. Click **Apply**.
3. Click **Next**.

## 3. Refine your audience opportunity

Adjust your audience settings:

1. Audience: Click **Change** to add or exclude audiences from your forecast.
2. Location: Click **Change** to add or exclude locations from your forecast.
3. Consideration opportunity: Click **Change** to select one or more product retail categories or audiences to define your targeting objective. Amazon DSP: Browse, select, and create a combination of audience segments to define the attributes you want to explore. Third-party audiences aren't supported, Sponsored ads: Select the categories where your brand lists its products. You can also choose related or adjacent categories to reach potential customers.
4. Click **Create plan** in the top right corner.

## 4. View insights

Once you click save, Ads planner will generate audience size, demographic and behavioral insights. Next, Ads planner can help you plan your budget and explore forecasts.
