# Manage users in a Manager account

Learn how to invite users to your Manager Account (MA), set up access permissions, and manage linked accounts.

Updated on Feb 24, 2026

## Invite users to your Manager account (MA)

Was this video helpful? Select feedback

Follw these steps to invite new users to your MA.

1. In the left menu, click **Administration**.
2. Click Account access and settings.
3. In the left menu, click **Users**:
4. Click **Invite users**.
5. Enter the user's email and click Add.
6. Click **This manager account** to invite them to the manager account (they’ll have access to all accounts within the manager account) or **Selected accounts** to invite them only to a specific selection of accounts.

Edit the permission level of an existing user

1. In the left menu, click **Administration**.
2. Click Account access and settings.
3. In the left menu, click **Users**:
4. Locate the user (you can use the search bar to find them quickly).
5. Click the drop-down menu in the Actions column.
6. To edit access, click **Change permissions**. Select the desired permission level. Click **Save permissions**.
7. To remove, click **Remove user** In the popup window, click **Remove user** to confirm.

**Note**: To edit permissions for multiple users at once, use the check boxes to select multiple users, and then click the bulk actions drop-down.
