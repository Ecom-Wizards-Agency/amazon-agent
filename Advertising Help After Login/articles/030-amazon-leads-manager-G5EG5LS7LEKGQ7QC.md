# Amazon Leads Manager

Amazon Leads Manager (ALM) is a self-service database where you can access lead information from your lead generation campaigns.

Updated on Aug 11, 2026

Lead generation ads can help advertisers that sell products and services outside of the Amazon store capture customer leads directly through their ads. Once customer lead information is submitted through the ad, the information is sent to ALM where you can access it in a self-service manner.

You can find links to the ALM portal in various locations within the ads console. You may also access the portal by clicking the link at the bottom of this help page. To register an account, you can use the email that is associated with your Amazon Ads account.

Once you sign in, you can download CSV files or integrate your customer relationship management (CRM) with ALM using the **CRM Profiles** tab. We currently support Salesforce or a custom CRM with a webhook URL. If your privacy policy allows for it, you'll have the option to have agency partners access lead information on your behalf. To do so, you'll need to register your agency partner’s email in the **Users** tab, and grant lead information access to one or more accounts. You can revoke access at any time.

To protect customer information, leads get auto-deleted in 14 days and these leads can’t be accessed by Amazon.

To get started using the database, go to Amazon Leads Manager and sign in with your Amazon Ads account credentials.

Use of ALM is subject to the Amazon Lead Generation Ads and Leads Manager Agreement.
