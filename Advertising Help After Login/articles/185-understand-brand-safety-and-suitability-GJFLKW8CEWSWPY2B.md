# Understand brand safety and suitability

Learn about brand safety and suitability on ADSP and how they help protect your brand when displaying ads to customers.

Updated on Nov 14, 2025

Brand safety and suitability controls on Amazon DSP provide filtering capabilities across inventory sources. These controls help make sure that your ads appear alongside content that aligns with your brand values and campaign objectives and are available to all advertisers using Amazon DSP.

## Brand safety tools

Brand safety represents the baseline protections that Amazon DSP provides against inappropriate content.

- We require that all supply providers comply with inventory policies by filtering inventory containing disallowed content.
- Read Content Adjacency Policies for Amazon DSP for a list of disallowed content categories.

Amazon DSP also includes brand safety tools that you can use to protect your brand against inappropriate content:

- **Domain inheritance:** Upload a custom list of websites and apps to customize your ad placement. Learn more about domain inheritance.
- **Third-party (3P) pre-bid targeting**: Use 3P pre-bid targeting controls from ad verification vendors to filter supply. Learn more about 3P pre-bid targeting.
- **3P measurement and verification:**Use 3P verification vendors to check for fraud and invalid traffic, brand safety, and viewability. View a list of 3P measurement integrations and features.

## Brand suitability controls

Brand suitability controls allow you to customize where your ads appear based on your brand's values and risk tolerance. You can set these controls at the advertiser and ad group (formerly known as Line Item on Amazon DSP) level:

- Learn more about brand suitability controls.

## Reporting and transparency

Once you’ve customized your brand safety and suitability settings, monitor your ad placements to ensure that they adhere to your brand values.

- Learn more about brand suitability reporting and transparency.

## Considerations and limitations

Amazon DSP implements brand suitability measures on a best-effort basis. However, there may be some situations where ads could appear misaligned with content, due to the following:

- **Content classification errors:** Inaccurate or missing publisher signals may occasionally result in misaligned ad placements.
- **Language support constraints:** Brand suitability controls may have limited effectiveness for unsupported languages. Content screening may not be comprehensive for all global markets.
- **Dynamic content changes:** Changes to publisher content can affect brand suitability. Publisher content can change after bid placement, potentially altering the context in which an ad appears.
