# Create a Sponsored Products campaign

With Sponsored Products, your ad is eligible to display in various placements across Amazon.

Updated on Sep 28, 2026

**Good to know**

- Only active, in-stock ASINs can be advertised. If your product doesn’t appear, check that it’s active and available to buy. Read about additional product restrictions.
- Metrics can take up to 12 hours to populate. Don't expect data immediately after launch.
- Review times vary by advertiser type. If you are a seller or vendor, campaigns can launch in 2 hours or less. If you are a book seller or author, moderation may take up to 3 business days

## Prerequisites

Read Improve your advertised product listings before setting up your campaign to ensure that you have the highest chance of your products being clicked on by customers. Once your products are ready to be advertised, follow the steps below.

## Access the campaign builder

To access the Sponsored Products campaign builder:

1. In the navigation side menu, click **Create campaign**.
2. Click **Sponsored Products.**

## Create an ad group

**Ad group settings**

- Name your ad group. Products advertised together in a single campaign automatically become an ad group.

**Products**

- Add the products that you want to advertise. Your products won’t appear here if their ASIN is inactive or out of stock.
- When you create a sponsored ads campaign, you choose which products to advertise in that particular campaign. There are 3 ways to add products to your campaign: **Search**: Enter a product name or ASIN and click **Add** for each product. **Enter list**: Type or paste multiple ASINs, separated by commas, spaces, or new lines, then click **Add**. **Upload**: Edit the downloaded CSV template, and then reupload it by dragging and dropping the file or clicking upload to select the file from your computer.

**Targeting**

- Choose your targeting strategy- **automatic** or **manual**. The next step will change based on which targeting strategy you choose:

Choose between:

- **Set default bid:** Use the prepopulated default bid or set your own in the **Default bid** box.
- **Set bids by targeting group**: Differentiate your bids by each targeting group.

We’ll select the keywords and products to target for you with either automatic targeting option.

**Negative keyword or negative product targeting** (optional)

- Configure negative keywords or negative products. See Add negative keywords or negative products to learn more.

## Set up your campaign and bidding strategy

**Campaign bidding strategy**

- Choose from the following strategies: **Dynamic bids - up and down**: We'll raise your bids (by a maximum of 100%) in real time when your ad may be more likely to convert to a sale, and lower your bids when less likely to convert to a sale. **Dynamic bids - down only**: We'll lower your bids in real time when your ad may be less likely to convert to a sale. **Fixed bids**: We'll use your exact bid and any manual adjustments you set, and won't change your bids based on the likelihood of a sale.

**Bid adjustments** (optional)

- Adjust your bid to help improve where your ad appears and which audiences see it. You can choose to adjust your bid in 2 ways: **Placement**: Enter a percentage to increase your base bid for any specific Amazon or Amazon Business placements. Learn more about placements. **Audience**: Select up to 10 audiences to boost your bids for specific groups of shoppers. Choose from Amazon audiences or AMC audiences and set an independent bid boost percentage for each one. Learn more about audience bid adjustments. **Note**: Advertisers outside of the US marketplace can only select up to 1 audience at this time.

**Settings**

1. **Campaign name**: Give your campaign a name (for example, Sponsored ads | Prime Day | Barbecue grills)
2. **Start** and **End** dates: Choose your campaign duration (start date and optional end date)
3. **Daily budget**: Enter the amount that you're willing to spend each day

## Global Sponsored Products campaign

Global campaigns enable you to create and launch one Sponsored Products campaign across multiple countries. Instead of setting up individual campaigns for each country, you can launch and manage cross-border campaigns from one centralized campaign while maintaining the flexibility to customize settings for each country. When you create a global campaign: Amazon Ads will find your appropriate matching advertised products across countries. Products must be available for sale in all the countries you select for advertising. Amazon uses generative AI to generate high-performing localized targets and corresponding keywords recommendations based on local search trends and ads for the same or similar products in the target country. View the values of all currencies in your preferred currency. The bids and budgets aren’t converted and are only for viewing. Manually customize settings, like bids, budget, and product targeting for each country you selected. This allows you to account for regional differences in competition, seasonality, and market conditions. To add more countries, click **Add countries to this campaign****using the** same campaign.

## Launch your campaign

If you're not ready to launch your campaign yet, click **Safe as draft** and access it on the drafts page in the campaign manager later.

Click **Launch campaign**.

Campaigns are usually eligible to go live in 2 hours or less.

## Monitor and adjust your campaign

After your campaign launches, monitor metrics to determine campaign performance. Performance metrics can take up to 12 hours to populate. For more detailed information on accessing and interpreting metrics, read Introduction to reports and metrics.

To view change history for Sponsored Products and Sponsored Brands campaigns, including campaign settings, targeting, negative targeting, at the individual campaign, ad groups, or all-campaigns level navigate to Campaigns and click **History**.
