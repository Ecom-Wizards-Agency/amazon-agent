# Understand Sponsored Products video

Learn how to use Sponsored Products video to showcase your products through manual upload or auto-assembled video.

Updated on Sep 30, 2026

Sponsored Products video is only available in the US and is currently not available for Books.

## What is Sponsored Products video?

Sponsored Products video allows you to add product feature videos within your ad to help you highlight your product's unique benefits to shoppers. Videos appear alongside your Sponsored Products ads to increase engagement.

You can add videos to your campaigns in 2 ways:

- **Manual upload:** Upload your own video through campaign manager.
- **Auto-assembled video:** This is an opt-in feature where we create a video ad from video assets you've already uploaded or that appear on your product detail page. No new video content is generated.

You pay on a cost-per-click (CPC) basis. You're only charged when shoppers click your video ad. Auto-assembled video uses your existing campaign budget at the same CPC bid as your standard ads with no additional charge.

## How it works

You add videos manually to your campaigns through campaign manager:

1. In the left menu, click **Sponsored ads** > **campaign manager**.
2. Open the campaign where you want to add Sponsored Products video.
3. Choose an Ad group.
4. Go to **Ads**.
5. Select the **Video** tab.
6. Click **Add video**.
7. Upload a video from your computer or add from creative assets.

**Tip:** You can make bid adjustments specifically for your Sponsored Products video in campaign manager. Bid increases will apply on top of other placement adjustments. Learn more about bid adjustments for Sponsored Products Video.

Each video should be at least 6 seconds and demonstrate 1 unique product feature. A minimum of 1 product feature video can appear on an ad at a time, and you can upload up to 5 product feature videos per product.

If you upload more than 3 product feature videos, Amazon will select a minimum of 2 videos to be displayed based on signals like search relevance. You can find all of your videos in creative assets.

Videos must meet Sponsored Products video guidelines and go through an approval process. Approvals take 48 to 72 hours.

## Auto-assembled videos

**Note**: Auto-assembled videos are assembled within 72 hours after the feature is activated on your campaign. You retain full control to review, edit, pause, or opt out at any time.

Auto-assembled videos create a Sponsored Products video ad from video assets you've already uploaded or that appear on your product detail page. The video highlights your product's features to shoppers without generating new content.

## How auto-assembled video works

- We identify eligible video clips from your product detail page videos and videos you uploaded.
- We assemble a video at the ASIN level. If your ad group has multiple ASINs, each ASIN gets its own video.
- Once approved, the video is eligible to serve. Approval does not guarantee immediate delivery. The ad still competes in the auction like any other Sponsored Products ad.
- There is no charge to assemble the video. When the video serves, it uses your existing Sponsored Products campaign budget at the same cost-per-click (CPC) bid as your standard ads. You can apply an optional video bid adjustment to bid more for video placements.

## How to opt-in to auto-assembled videos

To turn on the auto-assembled videos feature:

1. Open your campaign in the ads console.
2. Click on **Campaign settings** in the left hand sidebar menu.
3. Go to the Sponsored Products video section and check the box for **Auto-assembled video**.

**To find your auto-assembled videos:**

1. Open your ad group.
2. Go to the **Video** tab under **Manage Ads** in campaign manager.

Auto-assembled videos are labeled separately from manually uploaded videos. From there, you can preview or edit the video, pause delivery, or activate the video.
