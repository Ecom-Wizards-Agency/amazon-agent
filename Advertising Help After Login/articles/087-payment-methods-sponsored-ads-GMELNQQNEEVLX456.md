# Payment methods (Sponsored ads)

Learn about what payment methods that you can use to pay for advertising.

Updated on Aug 3, 2026

**Note**: Payment method eligibility is subject to periodic review. We'll contact you if there are any changes to your payment options.

## Types of payment methods

You can choose from several payment methods to pay for your advertising charges. Methods vary by account type. You can view your current default payment method on your payment profile.

If you’ve upgraded to an Advertiser Account, you must pay by invoice for all campaigns, including sponsored ads.

- **A debit or credit card**
- **Your Seller account:** Charges will be deducted from the **Funds Available** in your Seller Account- not your Total Balance. If your amount due exceeds the funds available, your payment will fail. You'll be charged when you reach your credit limit or at the end of the month.
- **Invoice:** Receive a monthly invoice that you can pay via bank check or wire transfer. Your payment is due within 30 days and includes all charges and credits on your account. The bank account information for wire transfers and shipping details for bank checks are available at the bottom of each invoice.

If your payment method fails after multiple attempts, your advertising account will be placed into payment failure status and your campaigns will be paused. If you’re updating your payment method due to a payment failure, it can take up to 24 hours for your account to be enabled.

Tip: Add a credit or debit card as a backup payment method in your payment profile. If your default payment method has an insufficient balance or fails, your card will be charged instead.

All advertising charges will be charged to your selected default payment method in the currency of the marketplace where the ads were created.
