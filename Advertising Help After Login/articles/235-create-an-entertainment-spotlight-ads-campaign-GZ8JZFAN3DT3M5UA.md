# Create an Entertainment Spotlight Ads campaign

Entertainment Spotlight Ads can help you promote your video content by reaching relevant audiences using automatically generated creatives that have the familiar Amazon look and feel.

Updated on May 7, 2025

Entertainment Spotlight Ads is available to select home entertainment providers to advertise digital titles for purchase, rental, and viewing. Your ads may appear on Fire TV and Prime Video sponsored placements.

To create a campaign:

1. Go to the campaign manager and click **Create campaign**.
2. Enter information in the **Settings** section.
  1. **Campaign name**: The campaign name that you choose will be seen only by you in the campaign manager. It won't appear in your ads and will never be visible to shoppers. It's helpful to name your campaign based on the type of products you'll be advertising, or seasonal products you'll promote.
  2. **Start and End Date**: Select a start and end date that works for you. You can start your campaign immediately, or set a start date in the future. You can pause a campaign at any time and start it up again later.
  3. **Budget**: Specify the total amount that you're willing to spend by setting a daily budget. Learn more about budgets.
3. Select a goal:
  1. **Drive subscriptions**: Optimize your bids to help drive new subscriptions by showing your ad to people who are more likely to subscribe to your Prime Video channel or Fire TV app. **Success metric**: Cost per subscription sign-up.
  2. **Increase first app opens**: Optimize your bids to maximize first-time app opens by showing your ad to people who are more likely to download and open apps with Fire TV. **Success metric**: Cost per appstore first open.
  3. **Increase rentals and purchases**: Optimize your bids to increase transactions of your movie and TV content by showing your ad to people who are more likely to rent and purchase the content. **Success metric:** Return on ad spend.
  4. **Drive impressions and clicks**: Optimize your campaign to help drive more impressions and clicks by showing your ad to people interested in your titles. An impression happens when your ad is displayed and a viewer sees it. However, you don’t pay for impressions. You pay only when viewers click your ad. **Success metric:** Impression and clicks.
4. Choose an ad format:
  1. **Sponsored Tiles:** Ad placements within a row that allow promotion of content titles and apps in Fire TV. These appear in the form of tiles in the sponsored row of the home screen that looks native to the current experience.
  2. **Feature Rotator:** Large format ad placement that is located above the fold on the Home tab as soon as users turn on their FireTV. The placement remains as customers navigate through tabs to explore content.
  3. **Sponsored Screensaver:** Full-screen ad placement integrated with the original landscape image screensaver experience on Fire TV. The placement is available to entertainment advertisers that have an actionable call to action on Fire TV.
5. Select the products that you want to advertise. **Note:**If your title has multiple prices, you'll see the lowest price listed when selecting the product to advertise during the campaign creation process. For example, if your title’s price includes “Free with Prime,” you'll see $0 listed in the campaign. Users clicking on the ad on Fire TV will see the full range of prices depending on the title and their Prime membership status.
6. Preview your creative.
7. Review your bid. Your advertising fees are charged to your Amazon account during an invoice period. You have the option to switch your payment method to deduct from a credit card. Each time your credit card is charged, you receive an invoice. Learn more about billing and payments
8. Select one or more interest or location targets for your campaigns. Targeting by interests delivers relevant ads to shoppers with certain interests.
9. Submit your ad. Before submitting, review all of your information to ensure you're happy with the settings.

Your ad will go through our review process before your campaign goes live. We'll email you to let you know if changes need to be made. The review process is usually completed within 24 hours, but may take up to 3 business days.

Once your campaign is live, you'll have access to standard performance insights through the campaign dashboard or downloadable reports.
