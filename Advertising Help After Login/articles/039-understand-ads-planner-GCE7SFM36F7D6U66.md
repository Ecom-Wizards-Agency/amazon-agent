# Understand Ads planner

Learn how Ads planner uses media plans to manage all of your advertising activity.

Updated on May 5, 2026

You must be signed in to a Manager account to use Ads planner.

## What is Ads planner?

Ads planner leverages Amazon's insights and algorithms to build a media plan for single or multiple-format ad campaigns. It helps you create the optimal mix of audiences, channels, and ad formats to achieve your advertising objectives. You can view all of your Amazon Ads activity in 1 place and examine how different formats work together.

With Ads planner you can:

- Create a cohesive strategy and forecast reach across ad products and formats (on- and off-Amazon).
- View audience opportunity size at each conversion stage based on Amazon first-party, third-party, and/or custom audiences.
- Access demographic, shopping, and viewing behavior audience insights.
- Plan and optimize your budget.

**Note**: Ads planner is available in: US, CA, MX, BR, UK, DE, FR, IT, ES, NL, SE, TR, AU, JP, IN, KSA, and UAE.

## Media plan structure

You'll follow these steps to create a media plan.

1. Define your audience by selecting the products or categories that you want to promote (Sponsored ads) or choosing audience segments (Amazon DSP).
2. Choose the types of audiences you want to reach Awareness: build brand recognition and reach new customers. Consideration: drive traffic by reaching customers who viewed products similar to yours. Conversion: reach customers who viewed your product detail pages but haven't made a purchase. Loyalty: engage customers who have already purchased your products.
3. Receive customer opportunity, demographic, and behavioral insights.
4. View suggested ad products and formats that are most aligned with your marketing goal.

## Ads planner insights

Each customer can be only in 1 step of the purchase path, so Ads planner organizes your total potential customer opportunity into distinct groups for customers who:

- Have purchased your products in the last 12 months (loyalty).
- Have researched but not purchased (consideration).
- Are shopping for competitive products in your aisle (conversion).
- Haven’t yet shopped in your aisle (awareness).
