# Sponsored Products video guidelines

Learn about the guidelines for creating and submitting Sponsored Products videos, including content requirements and approval processes.

Updated on Sep 29, 2026

**Note:**Sponsored Products video is only available in the US and is currently not available for Books.

All Sponsored Products videos must go through an approval process before they are displayed on your ad. To ensure your video is approved, follow the guidelines below.

## Video guidelines

A minimum of 1 product feature video can appear on an ad at a time, and you can upload up to 5 product feature videos per product. If you upload more than 3 product feature videos, Amazon will select a minimum of 2 videos to be displayed based on signals like search relevance. You can find all of your videos in creative assets.

**Video requirements**

- Use MP4 or MOV video files
- Make videos at least 7 seconds long
- Each video can demonstrate 1 or more unique product feature
- Show a human interacting with the product, if possible
- Ensure your product takes up 50% or more of the video frame.

**Prohibited content**

Videos containing any of the following will not be approved:

- Blurry frames, slideshows, and letter boxing
- Explicit branding or logos unless it's on the product
- Emojis and emoticons
- Nudity
- Audio
- Slideshows of the product
- 360 degree rotational view of the product

**Video filename format**

Label your videos using the following format for your video filenames:

- **Single videos for a single ASIN:** Must have clear video filename labels in the following format: (i.e. 00:00 - 00:07: “Product in use”, 00:08 - 00:14: “Waterproof”, etc.) . Single videos should be taken in similar lighting and weather conditions to avoid harsh transitions
- **Multiple videos for a single ASIN:** Must have labels and ASIN’s in the video filename (i.e. “ASIN 0000001, ”Product-in-use“), (“ASIN d0000002, ”waterproof“)

## Feature and description guidelines

Learn more each component of your video:

- **Product feature video:**Product feature videos are the individual videos uploaded by advertisers. These videos give an in-depth overview of a specific feature and showcase a unique selling point of the individual product being sold (vs. brand stories or videos showing multiple products) to help shoppers explore, compare and understand why your product best fits their use case. Videos may be clipped based on placement, page, or shopper intent. For example, a 2 minute uploaded video may be clipped to only show 30 seconds of the most relevant feature/segment to the shopper.
- **Product feature title** (up to 3 words, 15 characters or less): You must enter a feature name that describes the feature you're demonstrating for each video you upload. These names will appear as the title in the interactive thumbnails.
- **Descriptions**(up to 6 words, 55 characters or less): These are optional descriptions of your feature and appear on transition scenes in the final video.

Follow the guidelines below when creating names and descriptions: Match all feature names and descriptions to video content Make feature names clear and descriptive, using up to 3 words and a total of 22 characters or less Keep descriptions succinct, using 6 words or less. Avoid using jargon or technical terms

**Note:** Approvals take 48 to 72 hours to review. Changes can also take up to 48 hours to show in preview.

## Video best practices

- **Do not use overly animated videos -**Animation should only be used in videos when absolutely necessary. Overly animated videos may not build trust in the product as much as videos demonstrating the real product.
- **Use good lighting.**Film videos in a brightly lit environment. Natural light is a good substitute for a professional lighting setup. A neutral background helps keep the focus on the product.
- **Show the product in action.**Show how the product works in real-life scenarios.
- **Include people, if possible.**Showing a person using or reacting to your product adds authenticity.
- **Use a tripod.**Shaky hand held footage may distract consumers.
- **Think outside the narrative.** Don’t overfocus on your narrative, and think more about your product features. Why? Amazon automatically reorders Sponsored Products videos based on customer shopping intent. For example, if a customer is interested in the waterproof aspect of your product, that video will show first.
