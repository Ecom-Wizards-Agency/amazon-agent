# Page templates

You can use templates to create pages with preorganized tiles or start with a blank page and add your desired content.

Updated on May 13, 2026

Choose your template depending on the goals that you have in mind for your brand, product line, or the types of content you want to promote. Some templates work best for your homepage or to feature a product line. Other templates work best if you want to quickly add groups of related products.

You can add, reorder, or delete content tiles from a page template at any time to customize it to fit your needs. To learn about the different content tiles available on Stores, visit Content tiles on Stores.

**Note:**If you want to change the template after you’ve created a page, you’ll need to delete the page, create a new page, and then select the template you want.

After you choose a template, you’ll see content tile placeholders in the preview canvas. This lets you preview your updates in real time. You can add, reorder, or remove content in the preview canvas to see what works best for the page.

To learn how to customize and manage content, see Edit Store pages. For inquiries related to your Store byline, visit Stores byline.

## Marquee template

Marquee templates work well for a homepage and brand story with products in multiple categories. This is a good choice if you have high-quality images or videos to link to your Store product pages.

## Product highlight template

Product highlight templates feature a flagship product or top product line for your brand. This is achieved by focusing on high-quality images to accompany your brand message. This template also works well as your homepage if you want to create a brand story for a smaller selection of products.

## Product grid template

If you have 8 products or more in your Store, you can use a product grid template to add multiple products to a page at once. This template works best for displaying related products or products that are in the same category.
