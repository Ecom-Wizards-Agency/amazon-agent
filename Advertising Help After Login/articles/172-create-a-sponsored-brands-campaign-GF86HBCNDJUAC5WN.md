# Create a Sponsored Brands campaign

Learn how to create a Sponsored Brands campaign.

Updated on Sep 30, 2026

## Before you begin

To make sure you have the highest chances of your products being clicked on by customers, read the following pages before creating your campaign:

- Optimize products for advertising
- Create your Store
- Sponsored Brands eligibility requirements

Once your Store and products are ready to be advertised, follow the steps below. Authors will also need to claim a pen name and 3 eligible book titles.

You must have at least 3 unique book titles under a single pen name claimed in your Author Central account to create Sponsored Brands ads. Claim your pen name in Author Central. The Author Central account must be the same account you use to access Amazon Ads.

1. Go to your Author Central account.
2. Click the **Books** tab, and click **add it now** located at the top of the page.
3. In the **Search** field, enter the book title, ISBN, or pen name and press **Enter**.
4. Once you've located the book, click the book cover, and if it's your book, click **Add this book**.

If you're claiming a new pen name, we'll notify you after we verify that you're the rights holder for the selected book. Once verified, it may take up to 48 hours for new claims to be reflected in your Amazon Ads account.

- Amazon Author Central allows you to self-create up to 3 pen names, but you can manage up to 7 with a single account. To claim more than 3 pen names, contact Amazon Author Central Customer Service.
- Managing multiple pen names in a single Amazon Author Central account doesn't impact the individual Author Pages. Books from 1 pen name won't appear on another Author Page.
- To switch between pen names, click the drop-down menu next to your name in the upper right corner of Amazon Author Central.
- In the Sponsored Brands pen name selector, only the pen names claimed in Amazon Author Central will appear.
- If you're managing an Amazon Author Central account that already has 7 pen names claimed, you'll have to create a new account. Alternatively, you can continue using a single advertising account by requesting access to the other advertising account.

Follow these steps to ask an author to invite you to access their advertising account:

1. Sign in to your Amazon Ads account.
2. Click **Administration Settings** at the bottom left of the screen.
3. Go to **Access and settings**.
4. Go to **Invite user** and enter user name, email ID, and select the **Access level** that you want to grant.

## Set up your campaign basics

**Go to the Sponsored Brands campaign builder**

1. In the navigation side menu, click **Create campaign**.
2. Find **Sponsored Brands** and click **Continue**.

## Create campaign

**Goals**

**Note**: The steps to create your Sponsored Brands campaign will change based on your campaign goal selection. You can choose only 1 goal per campaign, and this goal can't be changed after the campaign is created.

Choose a campaign goal: **Drive page visits (DPV)**, **Grow brand impression share (BIS)**, or **Reserve share of voice (RSOV)**. Click a tab below to see the settings related to your goal selection:

**Settings**

1. **Campaign name**: Name your campaign (for example, Sponsored Brands | Prime Day | Barbecue grills)
2. **Start** and **End** dates: Choose your campaign duration (start date and optional end date)
3. **Budget**: Set a daily (recommended) or lifetime budget We recommend starting with a daily budget and no end date You can change your daily budget at any time from the campaign manager You can't switch between a daily budget and a lifetime budget when your campaign is active

Many advertisers start with a daily budget of $30. This is a good range for testing and improving the performance of your campaign as you get started, while controlling how much you spend.

**If you're an author**, you won't see campaign goal options. Set up your campaign with the following settings:

1. **Campaign name**: Name your campaign (for example, Sponsored Brands | Prime Day | Your book series)
2. **Start** and **End** dates: Choose your campaign duration (start date and optional end date).
3. **Budget**: Enter your campaign's daily or lifetime budget.

**Bidding**

- **Automated bidding**: Use the toggle to allow Amazon to automatically optimize bids for placements below top of search based on conversion rates.

Then proceed to **Create an ad group and ad** below.

## Create an ad group and ad

**Ad group**

Ad groups are groups of ads within a campaign that can share the same targeting tactic, bid, and/or creative type. Think of each product in a campaign as an individual ad. When you create a campaign with multiple products, the ads for those products automatically form an ad group. Read Ad groups to learn more.

Choose the author to promote in your ad using the drop-down. Name your ad group something descriptive and meaningful to you.

**Note**: If you want your campaign to contain multiple ad groups, you can add more in the campaign manager after you save the campaign. For more information, see Add a new ad group to an existing campaign.

**Ad format**

- **Ad format:** Select an ad format (a template that helps you create an ad based on what you want to promote). Choose from: **Collections**: Showcase multiple products and decide how products appear in your ad and what landing pages are shown to customers. These ads feature your brand logo, a custom headline, and 3 or more products. **Brand Gallery**: Feature a primary lifestyle image with optional text overlay, your brand logo, a headline, and 3 to 5 subcategory images that link to pages within your Brand Store. **Brand Gallery is only available when you select the reserve share of voice (RSOV) campaign goal.** **Store spotlight:** Help drive traffic to your Store. These ads feature your brand logo, a custom headline, and your Store on Amazon with at least 4 or more pages, each with 1 or more unique product. **Video:** Showcase a single product or help drive traffic to your store. You can choose between the product detail page or your Store as the landing page for your video.
- Click a tab below to see the settings related to your ad format selection:

Choose between 2 collection types (**automatic** or **manual**) to promote related products to the right shoppers across high visibility placements on the Amazon Store.

| Automatic collections | Manual collections |
| --- | --- |
| **How they work** Automatic collections highlight to shoppers the best combination of products based on your keyword targets and shopper queries without the need to manually setup and optimize multiple campaigns. Our AI engine dynamically groups the most relevant products from your catalog into collections based on your keyword targets and shopper search queries. We'll create relevant titles and landing pages for your collections that'll continuously update to stay relevant. Your ad title, logo, and brand name link to the landing page, while individual products link to their product detail pages. **When to use them** Use automatic collections if you want to scale campaign creation and optimization with minimal effort while driving product discovery across your catalog. | **How they work** You select 3-10 related products to feature in your ad. We'll create relevant titles for your collections. Choose between driving traffic to your Brand Store or to a new landing page that automatically features related products from your brand. Your ad title, logo, and brand name link to the landing page, while individual products link to their product detail pages. **When to use them** Use manual collections if you want to promote specific product sets or have a small catalog. Good quality collections help shoppers discover more of your brand and drive stronger engagement. For the best results, choose distinct products that complement each other, not variations of a single product or unrelated items (such as different sizes, colors, etc). |

**If you're an author:** Collections showcase 3–10 unique but related titles. We'll create a relevant title for your collection. Choose between driving traffic to an **Author page** or creating a **New landing page** that features your selected titles:

- **Author page** (including subpages): Select your landing page when you create your ad
- **New landing page**: We'll create a new landing page that features your selected titles

**Products**

**Note**: This section only appears if you chose the **Manual collection** ad format, or **Video** ad format with **Product detail** landing page.

Add the products that you want customers to see when they click on your ad by following 1 of the methods below:

1. **Search:** Enter a product name or ASIN and click **Add** for each product.
2. **Enter list:** Type or paste multiple ASINs, separated by commas, spaces, or new lines, then click **Add**.
3. **Upload** > **Download the CSV template**: (Not available for authors) Edit the CSV template, and then reupload it by dragging and dropping the file, or clicking the upload interface to select the file from your computer.
4. Optionally, preview how your landing page will look to customers by clicking **Preview your landing page**.

**Considerations for each ad format**

**Note**: Vendors can advertise audiobook ASINs in the following marketplaces: Australia, Canada, France, Germany, Italy, Japan, Spain, United Kingdom, and United States. Authors can advertise audiobook ASINs in the US marketplace. Sellers can't advertise or sell audiobooks. When adding products to a collection, choose 3 to 10 products that: Share the same category or similar keywords. Are distinct but related — each product should highlight a different aspect of your brand, not variations of a single product (such as different sizes or colors) or unrelated items. Have in-stock status (a minimum of 2 products must be in stock). Maintain at least a 4-star rating. Feature high-quality or A+ brand content on their detail pages. Good quality collections help shoppers discover more of your brand and drive stronger engagement. For the best results, choose distinct products that complement each other, not variations of a single product or unrelated items (such as different sizes, colors, etc).

**If you're an author:** Collections ad formats require a minimum of 3 book titles. Add up to 10 book titles you want customers to see when they click on your ad.

**Ad**

Click a tab below to see the settings for the ad you want to create.

- **Ad name**: Name your ad group something descriptive and meaningful to you.
- **Ad title**: Select an automated title option or choose to write your own. Each collection includes an automated title which is an AI-generated title based on the products in your collection. Automated titles are designed to help shoppers evaluate your products and make more informed purchase decisions by providing product information at a glance. Automated titles may reference: The product titles of items in your collection Features and attributes of the products in your collection Your brand name and the common product category of the products in the collection
- **Brand name**: Use your registered brand name to help shoppers identify and connect with your brand.
- **Logo**: Use your registered brand logo to help shoppers identify and connect with your brand.
- **Product exclusions** (optional): To exclude certain products related to your keywords that you don't want to advertise, enter up to 1,000 ASINs in the **Product exclusions** box or upload an excel file with respective ASINs. This field only appears if you chose the **Automatic collection** ad format.
- **Landing page**: Choose between driving customers to visit a **New landing page** or a **Brand Store on Amazon.**

**If you're an author**

- **Ad name**: Name your ad group something descriptive and meaningful to you.
- **Author name**: This field is prefilled with the pen name you claimed in Author Central.
- **Author profile picture**: Upload an author profile picture. Make sure your picture meets our requirements book ads policies.
- **Landing page**: Choose between driving customers to visit your Author page or a New landing page based on the book titles you selected earlier.

**Targeting**

Select between **Keyword** and **Product** to choose how you want to target your ads.

- Read Targeting keywords and products to learn more about targeting.
- (optional) Read Add negative keywords or negative products to learn more about negative targeting.

**Campaign bid adjustments** (optional)

Adjust your bid to help improve where your ad appears and which audiences see it. You can choose to adjust your bid in 2 ways:

1. **Placement**: In the drop-down, choose between Increase by or Decrease by, and the percentage amount.
2. **Audience**: In the drop-down under Audience, choose 1 prebuilt audience segment you want to adjust your bid for. If you have any custom audiences configured, you can select those here.

Read Adjust Sponsored Brands bids by placement and audiences to learn more.

## Submit your campaign for review

Click **Submit for review** to start the moderation review process. This process is usually completed within 24 hours, but may take up to 3 business days. We'll email you to let you know if your ad was approved or if changes need to be made.

Learn more about Sponsored Brands moderation.

## Monitor and adjust your campaign

After your campaign launches, monitor metrics to determine campaign performance. Performance metrics can take up to 12 hours to populate. We recommend starting with:

1. **Impressions** and click-through rate (CTR): Measure reach and visibility
2. **New-to-brand metrics**: Track new customer acquisition over the last 12 months

For more detailed information on accessing and interpreting metrics, read Introduction to reports and metrics.

To view change history for Sponsored Products and Sponsored Brands campaigns, including campaign settings, targeting, negative targeting, at the individual campaign, ad groups, or all-campaigns level navigate to **Campaigns** and click **History**.
