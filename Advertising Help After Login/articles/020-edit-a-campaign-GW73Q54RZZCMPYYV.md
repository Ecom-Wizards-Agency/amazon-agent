# Edit a campaign

You can modify your campaigns by clicking any editable fields in the campaign manager, or change your campaign's status by modifying the start and end date parameters.

Updated on Nov 12, 2025

To edit campaign settings or details like budget allocation, targeting, or ad groups…

1. Navigate to the **campaign manager**.
2. Locate the campaign that you'd like to edit. See Find your advertising campaigns for more information.
3. You can edit some fields, like campaign end date, daily budget allocation, and bid adjustment percentage by clicking directly on the field in the table. Editable fields are surrounded by a dotted line.
4. Click the campaign name for additional editing options such as keyword and product targeting, bids, and negative keywords.
  - For Sponsored Brands, click the **Edit creative** button in the **Creative** tab to edit products, ad headline, and logo.

**Managing Campaign Status**

Campaigns can't be enabled or paused once they've been archived. Archived campaigns remain available for future reference. If you plan to use an ad later on, set the campaign to **Paused** instead of **Archived**.

You can also use a bulk file to make large-scale changes to campaigns. Learn more about bulk operations
