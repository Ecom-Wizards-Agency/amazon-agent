# Stores translations

Amazon supports multiple languages for customers shopping on Amazon. To help provide a consistent experience for customers in their language of preference, we translate your Store content into the website’s supported languages.

Updated on May 13, 2026

Once you submit your Store for publishing and it’s approved by moderation, we automatically translate your Store text into the supported secondary languages on Amazon. While translation time varies, most Stores will be translated within 5 business days.

Once your Store text is translated, it will be shown in the language corresponding to the customer’s preferred language setting. To view your Store in the translated language, navigate to your Store and select another language in the Amazon language switcher.

When you submit a new version of your Store for publishing, previous translations will be removed and new ones will be created, repeating the process. While your Store is being translated, we’ll show all customers your Store in the default language, regardless of their language setting.

If you see any issues with your Store translation and would like us to remove it, use the **Contact us** link on this page for support.

- If Amazon Ads translation service isn’t able to provide a translation that meets our quality and ad policy bar, we won’t provide a translation, and your Store will be shown in the default language.

## Image localization

Amazon provides Immersive Localization for Store images that contain embedded text. This automated workflow detects and extracts text from your images, translates it into the customer’s preferred language, and places the translated text back into the image while preserving the original background and visual styling.

This ensures that shoppers viewing your Store in their preferred secondary language will see both translated text and localized images, rather than images in the marketplace's default languages.

**How Immersive Localization works:**

1. Text detection and extraction: The system automatically detects and extracts overlay text from your Store images
2. Background preservation: Original text is removed while preserving the background through advanced "in-painting" technology
3. Translation: Extracted text content is translated into the customer's preferred language
4. Visual styling preservation: Translated text is embedded back into the image with the original font styling maintained

This process ensures that shoppers viewing your Store in their preferred secondary language will see both translated text and localized images, rather than images in the marketplace's default language.

**Quality assurance:**

- Images are processed through machine learning models with quality estimation to ensure they meet acceptance criteria
- High-visibility stores images undergo post-editing by professional linguists. For all other stores, images where defects are detected remain untranslated

**Managing localized images:**

If you need to override automatically localized images, you can do so manually through the Store builder. Contact support using the link on this page, and we'll process your request with a 24-hour service level agreement (SLA).

## Translation moderation

The Stores content guidelines and acceptance policies also apply to the translated Store.

When you edit a translation through the Store builder, the updated text goes through moderation and is subject to the same turnaround times as your default language ad. The **Edit translation** feature is unavailable for certain languages.

All assets in your Store will go through moderation and must be in the primary language of the locale in which they’re delivered. This includes but isn’t limited to videos (including audio), images, text, and other creative elements. Learn more about Supported primary language per country.
