# Find your Amazon Marketing Cloud audience

After you’ve created an audience, it is automatically pushed to Amazon DSP and sponsored ads.

Updated on Feb 24, 2026

## View your audience in Amazon DSP

1. Sign in to Amazon DSP and navigate to the relevant advertiser.
2. Select the **Audiences** tab.
3. If you built the rule-based audience from uploaded 1P data only, filter the list of audiences by **Advertiser audiences** and then **Amazon Marketing Cloud (AMC)**. For all other audiences (rule-based using any non-1P data and lookalikes), you can filter the list of audiences by **Custom-built** and then **AMC**.
4. You can view the audience status as Active, Failed, Processing, and others. An audience with the status of **Processing** may take up to 48 hours to become **Active**.

## View your audience in sponsored ads

In sponsored ads, you can see the audiences that you're able to add target bids for on each individual campaign.

**Sponsored Products and Sponsored Brands**

1. Sign in to sponsored ads and navigate to the campaign manager in the left-hand sidebar.
2. Select your Sponsored Product or Sponsored Brands campaign.
3. Click **Bid adjustments** in the sidebar.
4. Click on the **Audiences** tab.
5. Click **Change audience**.
6. Select "Increase bids on a custom audience created in Amazon Marketing Cloud (AMC)."
7. Click on the carrot next to **Choose an audience** to see all available audiences you can add target bids for on your selected campaign.

## View your audience in Display

1. Sign in to sponsored ads and navigate to the campaign manager in the left-hand sidebar.
2. Select your Display campaign.
3. Click into the ad group that you want to see audiences for.
4. Select **Targeting** from the left-hand side bar.
5. Click the blue **Add targets** button.
6. Click **Custom audiences**. You can add target bids for a specific audience here.
