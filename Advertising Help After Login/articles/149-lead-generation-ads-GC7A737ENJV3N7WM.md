# Lead generation ads

Sponsored Display advertisers that don’t sell products and/or services in the Amazon store can capture leads within ad creatives at scale.

Updated on Aug 11, 2026

By creating lead generation ads, you can measure and optimize for in-creative leads, and access your leads for follow-through to help drive your desired business outcomes.

Through lead generation ads, customers can engage with your ads to sign up for information directly in the ad creative. Customers don’t get diverted from the websites where they interacted with your ad. They can sign up instantly to get the information that they want, and then continue on their shopping, entertainment, or browsing journey without interruption.

You can only use lead generation ads if you’re eligible for Sponsored Display and promoting a business that doesn’t sell products and services in the Amazon store. We recommend using this campaign type if your primary goal is to capture customer lead information. Lead generation ad campaigns can collect leads at scale from customers browsing on various websites and apps that engage with your ads and sign up for information directly in your ad. During set-up, you can select relevant lead actions such as **Sign up**, **Inquire**, or **Request a quote**.

Lead generation ads is designed for self-service use. With just a few clicks, you can add a predefined call-to-action (CTA) button to an ad creative using Sponsored Display’s creative builder. A lead form template with prepopulated contact information fields, privacy policy fields, and form completion messages can help you build effective forms quickly.

Once you’ve launched your campaign, you can easily download your customer lead information from the Amazon Leads Manager database, and then follow-through with the customer leads directly and drive their desired outcomes. In the database, you can select your ad campaign and desired dates to download a CSV file. To protect customer information, leads get auto-deleted in 14 days and these leads can’t be accessed by Amazon.

In addition to the existing traffic metrics, you can access lead generation metrics including in-creative link-outs, in-creative lead form opens, in-creative leads, in-creative lead conversion rate, and cost per in-creative lead. These metrics can be reviewed at ad group, ad, and targeting level within the Ads Console, the API, and through offline downloadable reports.

To get started using lead generation ads, use the self-service template in the Sponsored Display campaign builder.

Use of lead generation ads is subject to the Amazon Lead Generation Ads and Leads Manager Agreement.
