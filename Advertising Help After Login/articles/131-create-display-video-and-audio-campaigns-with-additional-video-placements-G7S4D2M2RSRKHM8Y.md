# Create Display, Video, and Audio campaigns with additional video placements

Create additional video ad variations automatically.

Updated on Sep 28, 2026

Note: This feature is currently only available in the US.

## What are video ad variations?

When you upload a video asset that meets both Streaming TV (STV) and Online Video (OLV) specifications, you can choose to run it across both formats in a single workflow. Selecting OLV also opens access to additional Amazon device placements, including Fire TV Feature Rotator, Alexa, and Fire tablet. Amazon's AI automatically generates device-optimized display creatives from your STV video, expanding your reach across additional premium inventory.

**Benefits:**

- Increase campaign reach across Amazon devices without additional creative costs
- Generate display options in seconds from your existing video asset
- Access premium Fire TV Feature Rotator and Alexa+ inventory with AI-optimized creatives
- Maintain creative control for all generated assets

## Create an ad with additional video placements

**Prerequisites**

The campaign and ad group where you want to run this add meet these characteristics:

- **Campaigns**: Select both OLV and STV
- **Ad group**: In media settings, select OLV or STV.

1. In the navigation menu, click **Campaigns**.
2. At the top of the Campaigns page, click the **Video, Audio & Display ads** tab.
3. In the page menu click **Ads** and then click **Create ad**.
4. Select the campaign and ad group for which you want to create your ad.
5. Click **Video**, and click **Continue** next to **Standard video**.
6. Upload your STV video asset. Ensure your ad meets Streaming TV specs.
7. **Ad Experience**: Select **Streaming TV**. If your asset meets the OLV specs as well, OLV and Amazon devices will be auto-selected.
8. **Additional ad placements**: Boost your reach across Amazon's video network using this feature. You can run the same video ad across STV and OLV. Adding OLV helps your ad reach Amazon's premium devices (Fire TV and Alexa home screen)
9. Review the AI-generated display options: Multiple creative variations will be generated automatically Each option shows different frame extractions and compositions Headline is auto-generated but can be customized
10. Select your preferred display creatives or upload custom alternatives: You're not required to use the AI-generated options You can modify the headline for your selected creatives
11. Configure your targeting and campaign settings as usual.
12. Click **Save**.

After your ad is **Saved**, associate your ad to the ad group. All assets included in your ad will go through the moderation review. Moderation can take up to 24 hours.
