# Create an Amazon Attribution campaign

In Amazon Attribution, campaigns are used to generate attribution tags and associate products you want to measure to gain insights about conversions.

For Google search and Facebook ads campaigns, you can use bulksheets to create campaigns. This option enables you to easily tag and measure up to 100,000 Google search keywords or 8,500 Facebook ads by uploading a single file.

Step by Step: Create an Attribution campaign

1. Go to **Campaigns**, click **Create campaign**.
2. Select **Creation method.**
  - Create manually option will prompt you to set up campaign structure and fill in the details.
  - Upload file to create in bulk. Choose this method for Google search and Facebook ads campaigns only. This option enables you to easily tag and measure up to 100,000 Google search keywords or 8,500 Facebook ads by uploading a single file.
3. Campaign name: The campaign name that you choose will be seen only by you in the campaign manager. It's helpful to name your campaign based on the type of products you'll be advertising, or your campaign goal.
4. Select products: Under **Products**, you should see all the products you're currently selling on Amazon. If the product isn't in stock or doesn't have a photo or a price, it won't be eligible for measurement. We recommend selecting the specific products you're promoting, and any associated variations, by selecting **Add variations.** Performance for all other products under the same brand will be captured within “Total” metrics in reporting. If you don’t see the products you’re looking for: Note “variation parents” will never appear in Amazon Attribution’s product selector, but their children should be available.
  - If you're a seller, follow the instructions for managing brand-to-product mapping. If you cannot find your product there, visit the **Product’s detail** page and after seven days the product should become available.
5. Create ad group(s): Create 1 ad group per non-Amazon media strategy, tactic, or ads (formerly known as Creatives on Amazon DSP). Each ad group receives a unique attribution tag and will allow you to measure Amazon conversions for the products selected during order creation. To create an ad group:
  1. Ad group name: Enter an identifiable name for your ad group. Name your ad group based on the strategy or tactic for this tag.
  2. Publisher: Select the publisher that corresponds to your ad or post. If you don’t see the publisher listed, select “New” and enter the publisher’s name.
  3. Channel: Select the channel that corresponds with the campaign you’re looking to measure.
  4. Click-through URL: Enter the Amazon store or product detail page that you want to drive customers to once they click your ad. When selecting the destination URL, we recommend thinking about your goals and the products you selected during order creation. If your goal is to drive sales for a specific product, we suggest including a link to a product detail page. If your goal is to drive consideration across a number of products, we suggest providing a link to your Store on Amazon.
6. Once you’ve created all your ad groups, select **Create**.
7. Once you’ve successfully created your campaign, you’ll see an attribution tag generated for each ad group shown in Attribution tag column. You can download all tags in a CSV file by selecting Download all tags in CSV file link or copy each tag individually.
8. Next, follow the tag implementation steps to implement the tags and begin measuring your non-Amazon campaigns.
