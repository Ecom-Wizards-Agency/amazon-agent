# Manage your brand and products

Learn how to access your brand, and how to remove brands and products.

Updated on Oct 5, 2026

Was this video helpful? Select feedback

Vendors and Display, Video, and Audio campaign advertisers can add or remove brands they manage from their account. Sellers, authors, and book vendors can't manage multiple brands with an account, but can register one brand with the Amazon Brand Registry. Any changes made to brands will impact all ad campaigns you’re running at the time and any campaigns that you run in the future.

You can add or remove brands from your account and associate products with those brands on the Brands tab of your account settings.

## How to access your brands

To navigate to your brand in the ads console:

1. In the left menu, click **Administration**.
2. Select **Account access and settings**.
3. Click **Brands**.

## Add or remove a brand

To add or remove a brand from your account:

1. Access your brands.
2. Search by brand name. This will list all brands that match the search text you entered.
3. Select the brand that you want to add or remove. To add: Click **Review the products** to see what products are associated with this brand. or click **Skip to add brand** to submit your request. To remove: Click **Remove brands** (this button will appear in the table header when you select a brand) and then **Submit to remove**.

If you can't find your brand, check that you have completed your Brand registry enrollment and that you have permissions to access the brand. To check this, sign in to the brand registry and access user permissions.

## Add products to your brand

To add products to your brand:

1. Access your brands.
2. Click the name of the brand that you want to add products to.
3. Click **Add products.**
4. Find the product that you want to add by searching the product name or ASIN. You can add up to 100 ASINs, separated by commas ",".
5. Click **Add**.
6. Click **Submit for approval**.

Note: New ASINs require 4-7 days for approval. Once approved, historical data is automatically restated for the previous 28 days, with corrections processed over a 3-week period.

## Remove products from your brand

To remove products from your brand:

1. Access your brands.
2. Click the name of the brand that you want to remove products from.
3. Click the check box next to the products that you want to remove.
4. Click **Remove** (this button will appear in the table header when you select a product).
5. Click **Request removal**.

When you submit a request to add or remove brands/products, we'll approve it within 2 to 5 days. You can review the status of your request by accessing your brands and clicking the **Requests** tab.
