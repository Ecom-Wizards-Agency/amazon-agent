# Create a Twitch campaign

Learn how to create Twitch campaign.

Updated on Oct 1, 2026

This page only lists settings **specific** to creating an ad campaign for Twitch.

## What's a Twitch campaign?

Twitch is a global live streaming platform that delivers interactive gaming content, esports, music, talk shows, and creative programming. You can reach engaged streaming audiences across desktop, mobile, and connected TV environments through 2 campaign types:.

- **Twitch Video campaigns**: Run your media budget specifically on non-skippable Twitch-only supply (website, mobile app, and connected TV app). These campaigns require deals: **Programmatic guaranteed deals**: Fixed pricing with guaranteed delivery **Private auction deals**: Dynamic pricing with non-guaranteed delivery See Understand deals to learn more.
- **Twitch Display campaigns**: Reach audiences through display ads on Twitch desktop inventory. Unlike video campaigns, Display campaigns don't require deals.

**Brand suitability controls**

Use brand suitability controls to exclude content across 3 inventory tiers: expanded, moderate, or restrictive. See Twitch brand suitability tools to learn more.

## Create a Twitch campaign with these settings

To advertise with Display, Video, and Audio campaigns, you need to create a campaign, associate ad groups with the campaign, and assign ads to each ad group. This campaign type also requires activation via deals. Use the tabs below to view settings for each step of the setup process.

The settings below are specific to deals for Twitch. Only the unique required fields for this deal type are listed — standard deal settings also apply. See Create a deal for complete instructions.

- Twitch video campaigns require **private auction** or **programmatic guaranteed deals**.
- Twitch display campaigns don't require deals.

Once the deal is set up:

- Either Twitch or your Amazon Ads representative will let you know it's ready to be used.
- The deal will appear automatically in your manager account via Amazon's API integration.

Next, set up your campaign.

## Activate your campaign and monitor performance

Once your foundational components are set up, activate your campaign so it delivers and shows to customers.

1. In the navigation side menu, click **Campaigns**.
2. At the top of the **Campaigns** page, click the **Display, Video, and, Audio** tab.
3. In the **Ad groups** page, activate the ad groups by clicking the **toggle** in the **Active** column.
4. Activate your campaign by clicking the **toggle** at the top of the window next to the campaign name.
