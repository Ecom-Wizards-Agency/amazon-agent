# Understand your invoice

Understand the invoice billing cycle and adjustments.

Updated on Dec 11, 2025

**For Brazil advertisers:**Starting in 2026, new taxes (IBS at 0.1% and CBS at 0.9%) will appear on your invoices for informational purposes only. These new taxes will not be charged to you during 2026. Your current tax structure (PIS/COFINS and ISS) remains unchanged, and no action is required from you at this time.

The invoice table displays your advertising charges and payment information in the ads console. You can access, review, and download invoices directly from your account. Use this table to monitor your advertising expenses and maintain active campaigns.

- **Invoice date (Issued on):** When you reach your credit limit or the second day of the month.
- **Invoice number:** Click this number to review an invoice preview.

**Invoice status:** Provides a quick glance at the status of each invoice.

- **Issued:** Payment is required by the due date listed. It can take **up to 3 hours** for your payment to process and the status to change from **Issued** to **Paid**.
- **Failed:** There’s a payment failure with the invoice. Check your payment settings to ensure that your billing and payment profiles are complete. If you’re paying with your Seller account, you likely don’t have enough funds available to cover your balance. If you’re using a credit card, the charge likely failed due to an issue with your bank.
- **Written Off:** Balance has been written-off likely due to a lack of payment. Contact Advertiser Support to reissue the written-off invoices and enable your account.
- **Paid in Part:** Partial payment was made, but there’s still a balance due.
- **Paid in Full:** Confirms complete payment. No further action is required.

You can pay for your advertising charges in several ways. On the payment settings tab, you can view or change your default payment method. Check payments can take 3 to 5 days to be applied. Wire payments are typically applied within 24 hours.

## Invoice overlap

It's possible that you have multiple invoices with the same amount even on the same date. These aren't repeat charges for the same invoice. Overlapping invoice dates may occur for 2 reasons:

- **Same-date overlap:** Invoices close when they reach the credit limit, and a new invoice opens immediately to accumulate the advertising charges for the day. This creates overlapping invoice dates as your advertising cost accumulates throughout the day based on clicks and impressions received. For example, if your credit limit is $500 and you spend $1000 in one day, you’ll receive 2 invoices for $500 each.
- **Overlap by several days:** This happens when clicks that were received prior to the invoice dates were invalidated.

## Invoice adjustments

Credits or debits to your account are shown on your invoices as adjustments. We apply credits automatically to your advertising invoice before charging your payment method. You may receive credits for:

- **Promotional credits:** You might have received free click credits for your account.
- **Invalid clicks:** If we detect invalid clicks on your ads or we validate clicks initially considered invalid, you may receive a credit or a debit to your account to compensate for those clicks. Learn more about traffic validation.
- **Over-delivery credits:** If a campaign receives clicks that exceed the overall monthly budget, the difference between your ad cost and your monthly budget may be credited back to your account.

You’ll find any adjustments in the **Total adjustments** section in the summary section at the bottom of the invoice.
