# Study setup best practices

Learn about the best practices to optimize your Brand Lift study setup to maximize the accuracy, quality, and usefulness of the results.

Updated on May 2, 2025

Adhering to these best practices may help eliminate bias from study results and ensure clean control groups.

**Spend budget in full**

Eligibility is initially determined based on budget, then re-evaluated after study submission for 2 days. If your delivered ad spend is less than your daily budget, it will impact your ability to receive results.

**Campaign start dates should be after brand lift study submission.**

Campaigns should have a start date after the brand lift study submission date. This way, we can capture impressions from the inception of the ad campaign, which improves our chances of generating statistically significant results. Additionally, we can be more confident in having a clean control group of panelists who weren’t exposed to the ad.

Although we recommend that campaigns start running after the brand lift study submission date, in-flight campaigns, including always-on campaigns, are permitted.

**Don’t only measure campaigns targeting branded keywords.**

Customers who made a branded keyword search are more likely to be biased towards being aware of the brand or product.

**Don’t include remarketing campaigns.**

Remarketing audiences are more likely to be biased towards being aware of the brand or product.

**All included campaigns should be advertising the same brand or product and have consistent messaging.**

To ensure clear and accurate survey results, all campaigns selected for a study should be related to the same brand or product. Campaigns with creatives that advertise multiple products under the same brand name are permitted. For advertisers wishing to perform campaign measurement studies on multiple brands or products, we suggest running a separate study for each.

**Only have 1 active study for a given brand or product, and campaign.**

To avoid biased study results, avoid having multiple active studies for a given brand or product. Additionally, campaigns should only be included in one active Amazon Brand Lift study at a time.

**Higher estimated impressions and longer campaign lengths mean a higher probability of statistically significant results.**

The higher the number of impressions, the greater the chance that enough surveys can be distributed to ad-exposed panelists to generate a result we can be confident didn’t occur by chance.

A minimum campaign length of 30 days ensures adequate time for the ad campaigns to be seen by panelists and to measure recall of the ads.
