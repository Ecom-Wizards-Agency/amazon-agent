# Pay an invoice

Learn how to pay an invoice, including wire transfer and check payment procedures, documentation, and processing times.

Updated on Aug 28, 2026

Was this video helpful? Select feedback

When using Pay by Invoice, you can pay through a wire transfer or using a bank check. Your ad spend is invoiced at the end of each month, with payment due within 30 days of the invoice date (Net 30). Each invoice includes all charges and credits on your account for that billing period.

## Pay by wire transfer

Your bank must include all invoice numbers associated with the payment in the "notes" field. Ensure that your bank automatically sends the electronic payment form to the Amazon cash application email address printed at the bottom of your invoice. Wire payments are typically applied within 24 hours.

If your bank can't include the invoice numbers associated with the payment in the "notes" field, follow these steps:

1. Send an email to **advertising-receivables@amazon.com** on the same day that you make the payment to the email address displayed on your Amazon invoice copy.
2. Use the subject line “Remittance advice for [your customer/business name] for [total payment amount]”.
3. Include the invoice number in the email body. For multiple invoice payments, include an attached Excel or PDF document that contains the invoice number and amount.

**Note**: We can’t accept screenshots or scanned remittance images as proof of payment.

## Pay by check

Check payments can take 3 to 5 days to be applied.

1. Write your invoice numbers on your check. If you're paying for multiple invoices, submit these on a separate sheet with their respective amounts and include with your payment.
2. Send the physical check and the back-up to the address displayed at the bottom of your invoice.

## Documents to set up Amazon as a vendor

Use the following documents to set up Amazon as a vendor or supplier with your organization:

- **Third-party sellers**: If you connected your Seller Central account with the advertising console, your invoices are issued by Amazon.com Services LLC. Use the following documents: W9 Bank Letter

- **All other accounts**: For all other account types, use the following documents: W9 Bank Letter Account Setup Form

**Note**: The W9 form includes Amazon tax information such as company address, taxpayer identification number (TIN), and exemption coding. The Bank Letter confirms Amazon's collaboration with Wells Fargo Bank.
