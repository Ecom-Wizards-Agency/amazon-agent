# Apply Schedule bid rules for Sponsored Products

Learn how to apply schedule bid rules for Sponsored Products campaigns and specific promotional events.

Updated on Feb 24, 2026

Schedule bid rules is a feature available for Sponsored Products campaigns that allows you to schedule bid increases for specific times of the day or days of the week. You can also apply Schedule bid rules around recommended promotional events. This lets you take advantage of high-impact time windows to help to drive more sales. This rule can be applied to **Dynamic bids - up and down**, **down only**, and **fixed** bidding strategies. Learn more about bidding rules for Sponsored Products.

**Apply Schedule bid rules**

1. Sign in to Amazon Ads Console, then in the left menu, click **Sponsored Ads**.
2. Select **Campaign manager**, then locate the campaign you wish to schedule bid rules for.
3. Next to the campaign name, click **More**, then select **Edit settings**.
4. Next to **Campaign bidding strategy**, click **Add schedule rule**.
5. Below **Settings**, complete all of the requirements, then click **Add rule**.

**Apply Schedule bid rules for specific promotional events**

1. Sign in to Amazon Ads Console, then in the left menu, click **Sponsored Ads**.
2. Select **Campaign manager**, then locate the campaign you wish to schedule bid rules for.
3. Next to the campaign name, click **More**, then select **Edit settings**.
4. Next to **Campaign bidding strategy**, click **Add schedule rule**.
5. Click the circle next to **Events**, then click the **drop-down arrow** to see the list of recommended promotional events.
6. Choose from the list of individual events.
7. Once you select an event from the dropdown menu, complete all of the requirements, then click **Add bid rule**.

Please note that not all events will be available in the drop-down menu. You can view the retail holiday calendar for the full list of events. You can also view the guide to advertising for international events .
