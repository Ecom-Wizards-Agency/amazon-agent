# Report column definitions for Amazon Attribution

Use the column definitions to help interpret the information in your reports.

Updated on Nov 22, 2025

## Amazon sellers

| Report | Report column | Definition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Campaign, Keyword, or creative, Promoted product, Promoted product (rolled up) | 14-Day ATC | The number of times a promoted product is added to a customer's cart within 14 days from the date that your ad was clicked. |
| Brand Halo product report, Brand Halo product report (rolled up), Brand Halo snapshot report | 14-Day ATC BH | The number of times a product other than the promoted products is added to a customer's cart within 14 days from the date your ad was clicked. Products must be of the same brand as the promoted product. |
| Campaign, Keyword, or creative, Promoted product, Promoted product (rolled up) | 14-Day DPV | The number of views of the advertised product's detail pages on Amazon within 14 days from the date your ad was clicked. |
| Brand Halo product report, Brand Halo product report (rolled up), Brand Halo snapshot report | 14-Day DPV BH | The number of views of the detail page of products other than the promoted products on Amazon within 14 days from the date your ad was clicked. Products must be of the same brand as the promoted product. |
| Promoted product, Promoted product (rolled up) | Purchases (new-to-brand) (formerly known as 14 day new-to-brand orders on sponsored ads) | The total number of orders within 14 days from the date your ad was clicked, from customers who purchase a product from the brand for the first time in last 12 months. |
| Brand Halo product report (rolled up), Brand Halo snapshot report | 14-Day New-to-brand Product Sales BH | The total sales of products other than the promoted products within 14 days from the date your ad was clicked, from customers who purchase a product from the brand for the first time in last 12 months. Values are provided in local currency. Products must be of the same brand as the promoted product. |
| Brand Halo product report (rolled up), Brand Halo snapshot report | Purchases (new-to-brand)BH | The total purchases of products other than the promoted products within 14 days from the date your ad was clicked, from customers who purchase a product from the brand for the first time in last 12 months. Products must be of the same brand as the promoted product. |
| Promoted product, Promoted product (rolled up) | Sales (new to brand) (formerly known as 14 day new-to-brand sales) | The total sales within 14 days from the date your ad was clicked, from customers who purchase a product from the brand for the first time in last 12 months. Value are provided in local currency. |
| Promoted product, Promoted product (rolled up) | Units sold (new to brand) (fka 14 day new-to-brand units (#)) | The total number of units sold within 14 days from the date your ad was clicked, from customers who purchase a product from the brand for the first time in last 12 months. |
| Brand Halo product report (rolled up), Brand Halo snapshot report | Units sold (new to brand)BH | The total number of units sold of products other than the promoted products within 14 days from the date your ad was clicked, from customers who purchase a product from the brand for the first time in last 12 months. Products must be of the same brand as the promoted product. |
| Campaign, Keyword, or creative, Promoted product, Promoted product (rolled up) | 14-Day Product Sales | The total sales of promoted products purchased by customers on Amazon within 14 days from the date your ad was clicked. Values are provided in local currency. |
| Brand Halo product report, Brand Halo product report (rolled up), Brand Halo snapshot report | 14-Day Product Sales BH | The total sales of product other than the promoted products purchased by customers on Amazon within 14 days from the date your ad was clicked. Values are provided in local currency. Products must be of the same brand as the promoted product. |
| Campaign, Keyword, or creative, Promoted product, Promoted product (rolled up) | 14-Day Purchases | The number of times any number of promoted products are included in a purchase event within 14 days from the date that your ad was clicked. Purchase events include new Subscribe & Save subscriptions. |
| Brand Halo product report, Brand Halo product report (rolled up), Brand Halo snapshot report | 14-Day Purchases BH | The number of times any number of products other than the promoted products are included in a purchase event within 14 days from the date your ad was clicked. Purchase events include new Subscribe & Save subscriptions. Products must be of the same brand as the promoted product. |
| Channel publisher, Campaign, Keyword, or creative | 14-Day Total ATC | The number of times a product is added to a customers' cart within 14 days from the date that your ad was clicked. This count adds for promoted products as well as products from the same brands as the products tracked in the campaign. (Total ATC = ATC + ATC BH) |
| Channel publisher, Campaign, Keyword, or creative | 14-Day Total ATCR Clicks | The number of times a promoted or brand halo product is added to a customer’s cart within 14 days from the date that your ad was clicked divided by clicks. |
| Channel publisher, Campaign, Keyword, or creative | Detail page views (fka 14 day detail page views (DPV)) | The number of ad-attributed detail page views on Amazon within 14 days from the date your ad was clicked. This includes views for promoted products as well as products from the same brands as the products tracked in the campaign. (Total DPV = DPV + DPV BH) |
| Channel publisher, Campaign, Keyword, or creative | 14-Day Total DPVR Clicks | The number of views of the promoted product’s detail pages and other products within 14 days from the date your ad was clicked divided by clicks. Products must be of the same brand as the promoted product. |
| Channel publisher, Campaign, Keyword, or creative | 14-Day Total Purchase Rate Clicks | The number of purchases attributed to ad click-throughs within 14 days from the date that your ad was clicked divided by clicks. |
| Channel publisher, Campaign, Keyword, or creative | 14-Day Total Purchases | The number of times any number of products are included in a single purchase event within 14 days from the date that your ad was clicked. Purchase events include Subscribe & Save subscriptions. This counts purchases for promoted products as well as products from the same brands as the products tracked in the order. (Total purchases = Purchases + Purchases BH) |
| Channel publisher, Campaign, Keyword, or creative | Sales (formerly known as 14 day total sales) | The total sales of promoted products and products from the same brands as the promoted products purchased by customers on Amazon within 14 days from the date that your ad was clicked. Values are provided in local currency. |
| Channel publisher, Campaign, Keyword, or creative | 14-Day Total Units Sold | The total quantity of promoted products and products from the same brand as promoted products purchased by customers on Amazon within 14 days from the date that your ad was clicked. A campaign can have multiple units sold in a single purchase event. |
| Campaign, Keyword, or creative, Promoted product, Promoted product (rolled up) | 14-Day Units Sold | The total quantity of promoted products purchased by customers on Amazon within 14 days from the date that your ad was clicked. A campaign can have multiple units sold in a single purchase event. |
| Brand Halo product report, Brand Halo product report (rolled up), Brand Halo snapshot report | 14-Day Units Sold BH | The total quantity of products other than the promoted products purchased by customers on Amazon after exposure to an ad within 14 days from the date that your ad was clicked. A campaign can have multiple units sold in a single purchase event. Products must be of the same brand as the promoted product. |
| Channel publisher, Campaign, Keyword, or creative | Clicks | An ad click that directs the user to a destination outside of the creative. |
