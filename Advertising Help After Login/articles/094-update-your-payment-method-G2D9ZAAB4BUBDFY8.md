# Update your payment method

Learn where and how to update the payment method you use to pay for advertising.

Updated on Feb 23, 2026

Only advertising console administrators or the Seller Central primary account owners (the users whose email address was used to create the account) can update the payment method.

1. In the left menu, click **Administration**.
2. Under **Billing and Payment**, select **Billing**.
3. Click the **Payment Settings** tab.
4. Click **Change payment profile**.
5. Next to your current default payment method, click **Edit**.
6. Select the payment method that you want to use. If you don’t see a payment method listed here, such as pay by invoice, your account isn't eligible for that type of payment.
7. Enter the required information for your chosen payment method (example: credit card details).
8. Click **Save**.

Your new payment method will take effect within 1 hour. However, if you’re updating your payment method due to a payment failure, it can take up to 24 hours for your account to be enabled.
