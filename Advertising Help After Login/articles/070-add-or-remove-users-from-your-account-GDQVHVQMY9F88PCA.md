# Add or remove users from your account

Learn how to invite or remove users from your sponsored ads, Amazon DSP, or Manager account.

Updated on Oct 5, 2026

Only Admins can invite, update, or remove users.

**To invite users to your Display, Video, and Audio campaign, sponsored ads (non-seller), or Advertiser account:**

1. In the left menu, click **Administration**.
2. Select **Account access and settings**.
3. Under the **Users** tab, click Invite users.
4. Enter the user’s email address and click **Add**.
5. Select the **Admin**, **Editor**, or **Viewer** access levels, or click Custom to set custom controls.
6. Select countries for the user to have access to. (Sponsored ads only)
7. In the top right, click Invite users.

**Seller account admins** can invite users in Seller Central. Go to User Permissions in your Seller Central account and select **Add User**.

To remove users from your **Display, Video, and Audio campaign or sponsored ads advertiser account:**

1. In the left menu, click **Administration**.
2. Select **Account access and settings**.
3. Under the **Users** tab, find the user you want to update To remove a user, click Remove User or Leave account. You can’t leave the account if you're the only admin. To update a user’s access, click Change permissions.

**Manager account admins** can also invite users to Display, Video, and Audio campaign advertiser accounts from their manager account:

1. While logged in to your Manager account, click **Administration**.
2. Select **Account access and settings**.
3. Click Accounts.
4. Click Add account to link an existing account or create a new sponsored ads, Amazon DSP, or Advertiser Accounts to link.
5. Click Link existing account. Find the account that you want to link from the list, or use the search bar to locate it.
6. Click Add next to the accounts you want to add.
7. Choose the desired **Access level**from the drop down.
8. Click Link account in the top-right corner.
