# Amazon Brand Lift

Learn how Amazon Brand Lift can help you measure your Sponsored Display, Sponsored Brands, and Sponsored TV campaigns’ impact on customers’ perceptions of your brand.

Updated on Jun 19, 2025

With Amazon Brand Lift, you can evaluate whether an audience who saw your ad is more likely to respond favorably when asked about your brand compared to a similar audience who didn’t see your ad. Amazon Brand Lift provides the ability to understand how brand awareness and consideration are changing over time. You can run an Amazon Brand Lift study on the Studies page of the Amazon Ads console.

Amazon Brand Lift uses surveys and machine learning models to measure the impact of advertising on customer perceptions of your brand. Customers complete all surveys in the Amazon Shopper Panel mobile app. The app is an opt-in, invitation-only program where participants can earn monthly rewards by sharing receipts from purchases made outside of Amazon.com. They do so by completing short surveys and enabling ad verification for the ads that they see from Amazon’s own advertising or third-party businesses that advertise through Amazon Ads. For more information about this app, visit Amazon Shopper Panel.

Survey responses may be collected each day as long as measured campaigns are active and not ended or paused. As responses are collected, Amazon Brand Lift calculates the results and generates a report. The **Status** and **Results** columns in the Studies dashboard will update once results are available. Reports will update weekly starting when 200 survey responses are collected and until all campaigns attached to the study are paused or ended, or the study is canceled.

Results are shown as absolute lift, which is the difference between the ad-exposed and control groups’ response rates to the desired responses for a given question. For example, if 50% of ad-exposed respondents chose the desired response, and only 40% of control respondents did the same, the absolute lift would be 10 percentage points. Learn more about how to View and interpret study results.

Amazon Brand Lift studies are offered at no cost for US Sponsored Display, Sponsored Brands, and Sponsored TV campaigns that meet the minimum budget threshold. Studies that meet the threshold will be monitored up to 2 days after submission to verify feasibility. Up to 100 of a brand’s active campaigns can be measured in one study, or you can select to measure a subset of active campaigns during study setup subject to the same spend minimums. You can add or remove campaigns from an active study at any time, but they should only be for the same brand or product. Removing campaigns may impact study feasibility. If a campaign is removed, it will not be reflected in study results going forward. The aggregate budget and impressions of those campaigns determine whether they’re eligible for a study. You can have only 1 active study at a time per registered brand.

Note: Within the first 30 days after launching a study, included campaigns must match with enough Amazon Shopper Panel members to produce results, or the study will be canceled. Pausing campaigns or reducing budgets will impact your ability to receive results. If your study is marked 'Not feasible' that means it didn't have enough impressions for Amazon to find enough ad-exposed panelists. To fix this, you can add more campaigns, and/or add more budget to the existing campaigns and create a new study.
