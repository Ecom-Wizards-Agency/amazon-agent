# Create interactive video ad campaigns

Learn how to set up interactive video ads on Amazon DSP including location-based customization for Prime Video.

Updated on Sep 28, 2026

This page only lists settings **specific** to creating an ad campaign for interactive video ads.

## What are Interactive video ads?

Interactive Video Ads (IVA) enable viewers to interact with brands Streaming TV ads using their remote or mobile device. Learn more about interactive video ads. Before creating your IVA, make sure you have:

- A video asset that meets Streaming TV specifications
- Featured ASIN (if you’re creating an "Add to Cart" experience)
- Click-through URL for your destination (if you’re creating a “Send more info” experience)

If you're creating an IVA with location-based settings, you must use First Party Ad Serving (1PAS) on Prime Video in a supported marketplace (US, CA, MX, or BR). Location-based IVAs are:

- Only available on Prime video ads and connected TV devices
- Not available if you opted into Additional Restrictions: **Only use Amazon audiences without demographic signals** checkbox in Advertiser settings

## Create your IVA campaign

To advertise with Display, Video, and Audio campaigns, you need to create a campaign, associate ad groups with the campaign, and assign ads to each ad group.

The settings below are specific to campaigns for IVA. Only the unique required fields for this campaign type are listed — standard campaign settings also apply. See Create a campaign for complete instructions.

**Campaign settings**

- Amazon deal: Ensure this is **NOT checked** for a non-guranteed campaign.

**Media**

- Type: **Streaming TV**

**Goal and optimization**

- Goal: **Awareness**
- KPI: **Reach**
- Optimization strategy: Select the budget management strategy that fits your campaign needs

**Budget and delivery**

- Agency fees: (optional) Apply as needed

**Conversion tracking**

- Products: Select the products and/or off-Amazon events you want to track before launching

**Targeting and optimization**

- Frequency: (optional) Note that campaign-level settings will overpower any ad group-level frequency settings.
- Frequency group: (optional)

Next, set up your ad group and attach it to your campaign.

## Preview your IVA

Before saving your IVA, preview it under **Streaming TV Preview** to ensure that the overlay is not obstructing key elements on your ad.

- If your ad does not meet interactive ad policy, select **Mini**as the overlay position and preview it again.
- If your ad does not meet interactive ad policy, but does meet our general streaming TV ad policies: Upload a new video under **Asset**and preview again. Save your ad without interactivity enabled.

Next, follow the steps in Assign ad to ad group to attach it to your campaign.

## Activate your campaign and monitor performance

Once your foundational components are set up, activate your campaign so it delivers and shows to customers.

1. In the navigation side menu, click **Campaigns**.
2. At the top of the **Campaigns** page, click the **Display, Video, and, Audio** tab.
3. In the **Ad groups** page, activate the ad groups by clicking the **toggle** in the **Active** column.
4. Activate your campaign by clicking the **toggle** at the top of the window next to the campaign name.
