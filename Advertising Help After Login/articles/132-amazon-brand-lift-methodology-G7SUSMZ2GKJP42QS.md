# Amazon Brand Lift methodology

Learn about the statistical techniques, sampling methods, and algorithms that power Amazon's Brand Lift measurement.

Updated on May 2, 2025

Surveys are sent to two similar groups. The first group is an audience of panelists who saw an ad: the “ad-exposed” group. The second group is composed of panelists who didn’t see the ad, but share similar demographic attributes such as age range, as well as shopping behaviors: the “control” group.

## Sampling ad-exposed and control panelists

For each Amazon Brand Lift study, only a sample of panelists receives surveys. To improve the chances of generating statistically significant results, Amazon Brand Lift collects a minimum of 200 responses across the ad-exposed and control groups using the following process:

1. **Determine population:** Amazon Brand Lift determines the audience of Amazon customers who were exposed to the ads in the past 2 days.
2. **Create an ad-exposed audience:** Amazon Brand Lift issues surveys to customers who saw the ads who are also panelists.
3. **Create a control group:** After establishing the ad-exposed audience, Amazon Brand Lift employs coarsened exact matching (CEM) to create the control group. The goal of the matching process is to select panelists that are most similar to the ad-exposed panelists in terms of demographic and shopping attributes, but didn’t see the same ad.

## Delivering surveys

Amazon Brand Lift studies measure impressions delivered each day and issue surveys to panelists who were exposed during the past 2 days as well as a sample of panelists who weren’t exposed. Amazon delivers surveys within the Amazon Shopper Panel app, where panelists have 7 days to complete each survey.

## Results

Once 200 survey responses are collected, Amazon Brand Lift automatically calculates the results and generates a report. Results will continue to update weekly until the study is cancelled or all campaigns are paused or ended. Results are shown as absolute lift, which is the difference between the ad-exposed and control groups’ response rates to the qualifying responses for a given question. Learn more about how to View and interpret study results.

## Use of machine learning

Awareness studies also use machine learning to improve accuracy of results. In addition to the survey sampling process above, for Awareness studies, non-panelists are also included in the ad-exposed and control groups. Instead of receiving surveys, Amazon Brand Lift predicts non-panelists’ awareness of a brand based on their on-Amazon engagements, such as branded searches and add-to-carts.

**Note:**Consideration studies will continue to use only surveys for their measurement.
