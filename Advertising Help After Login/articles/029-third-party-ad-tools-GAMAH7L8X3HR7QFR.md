# Third-party ad tools

Learn how to create high-performing ad creatives using third-party creative tools

Updated on Apr 27, 2026

Design professional ad creatives using integrated third-party tools directly within your Amazon Ads workflow. Access Canva and Adobe Express to create, edit, and manage your creative assets without leaving the advertising console.

## Benefits

- Design and upload creatives directly in the advertising console.
- Generate high-quality ads with AI-optimized templates.
- Keep brand consistency using your Creative Assets library (logos, colors, elements).
- Launch faster with pre-built, Amazon-ready templates that meet all specs automatically.
- Collaborate easily with shared designs, comments, and version tracking.

## Supported ad products

- Sponsored Brands
- Display
- Stores
- Amazon DSP (where applicable)

## To access third-party tools

1. Sign in to Adobe Express or Canva account.
2. In the navigation menu, click **Apps**, then search for “Amazon Ads”. You’ll see Amazon Ads in the apps.
3. Select **Use in existing design** or **Use in new design** to start using Amazon Ads app.
4. Follow the integration prompts to authorize the connection between your Amazon Ads account and the third-party tool.
5. For **Adobe**, review education webinar and training articles provided on Adobe Learn.
6. For **Canva**: To **reuse assets**: Select an advertiser profile in your Amazon Ads account, search for existing assets in the Search tab, then drag them into a Canva template. To **upload and export** design: In **Check Design,** select **Upload Design** to preview, choose PNG (images) or MP4 Video (videos) as the file type, and then click **Export**. To **save design**: Enter the**Asset Name** (and optional Tag/ASIN information), complete required fields, then click **Save** design to publish to Amazon assets.

Template designs are built based on best practices for Amazon Ads creatives, however, overall conversion rates also depend on factors like campaign budget, targeting, and keyword relevance.
