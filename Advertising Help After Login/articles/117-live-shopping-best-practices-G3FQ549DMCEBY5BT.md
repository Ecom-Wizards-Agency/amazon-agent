# Live Shopping best practices

Maximize your Amazon Live reach and engagement with best practices for driving traffic, managing your stream, building followers, and measuring performance. Learn technical requirements, video quality standards, and post-stream optimization strategies.

Updated on Apr 9, 2026

Amazon Live is in beta and available for select advertisers in the US.

Amazon Live is a creative advertising format that gives brands an interactive live shopping tool to engage customers with immersive storytelling, and drive product consideration and discovery with educational and inspirational content experiences.

Make sure to follow the livestream best practices below to maximize your reach. Click here for step-by-step directions to create a livestream and add the Amazon Live Shopping tile.

## Drive traffic

Your livestream appears in your Brand Store where customers can discover it organically. To maximize reach, create advertising campaigns that drive traffic to your stream:

- In the Live Shopping Hub, click **Boost Livestream**—this connects you to Campaign Manager, where you can create Sponsored Brands, Display, or Amazon DSP campaigns
- You can also share your livestream on social media platforms to drive additional traffic from external channels.

## During your stream

While streaming, manage your product carousel by highlighting different items as you discuss them. Monitor and respond to viewer chat messages in real time to encourage participation.

**Encourage customers to follow**

To ensure you are maximizing your follower count, encourage customers throughout your livestream to hit the "follow" button to stay up-to-date with your content. Your follower count is your highest-performing customer segment:

- Followers generate 137% more exact product purchases and 88% more same-category purchases than non-followers
- Followers spend 94% more time watching content and have 25% more views per customer than non-followers
- Customers who follow your brand receive a push notification from the Amazon Shopping App when you go live if they opt-in to receive notifications

## After your stream

Keep your advertising campaigns active for 3 weeks after streaming, as 90% or more of purchases and engagement occurs within this window. Access the Live Shopping Hub to view performance metrics including impressions, views, engagement rate, click-through rate, and purchases. Review this data to optimize your future streams.

## Technical requirements

You must livestream from a location with at least 50mbps upload/download internet speed to ensure stability.

Recommended equipment includes:

- Camera setup with proper lighting
- Quality microphone/audio system
- OBS or similar encoding software
- Backup recording capability
- Closed captioning software
- Backup internet capability in case something goes wrong

**Video quality standards**

- Resolution: 1280x720p
- Framerate: 29.97
- Bitrate: Max 4mbps
- Keyframe interval: 2
- Use multiple camera angles (2-3 as a best practice)
- Ensure proper lighting and sound (lavalier mics recommended)

## Policies and guidelines

The Video Content Guidelines ("Content Guidelines") cover the rules for video submissions uploaded to Amazon. These videos must also comply with Amazon's Community Guidelines. Please note that these Content Guidelines may be modified from time to time, so you're encouraged to check back for updates.

**Moderation**

Amazon Live monitor livestreams to ensure compliance with community guidelines. Streams that violate these policies can be terminated immediately.

## Performance Measurement

**Understanding Your Performance**

This dashboard provides a complete view of your livestream performance, tracking the customer journey from initial awareness through final purchase and measuring both engagement quality and business impact. The metrics cover three key areas: how many customers you reached, how effectively they engaged with your content, and the revenue results you generated. Each metric includes trend indicators showing performance changes over time, with detailed breakdowns available through the "View details" links.

You can download your report by selecting "**download report**" at the bottom of the "**Live Shopping metrics**" tile.

**When to Review Performance**

Your content continues driving results after you go live. Traffic to your content post-live extends reach and maximizes your investment.

- **Data availability:** Metrics populate around 9 hours after your livestream ends and remains for 90 days.
- **Recommended timing:**Wait at least 2 weeks before conducting a deep performance analysis

This 2-week window captures both live and post-live engagement, giving you the complete picture of your content's total effectiveness.
