# Consent requirements for uploading data to Amazon Ads

Learn about consent requirements for uploading data to Amazon Ads in the UK and EEA.

Updated on Oct 31, 2025

Advertisers and partners can securely upload data to Amazon Ads products such as Amazon Marketing Cloud (AMC), Ads Data Manager (ADM), and Amazon Demand Side Platform (ADSP). This data can support planning, measurement, audience creation, and campaign optimization. If you upload data to Amazon Ads you must either use (i) the Interactive Advertising Bureau (IAB) European Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF) signals, (ii) the Global Privacy Protocol (GPP), or (iii) Amazon Consent Signal (ACS), to communicate your United Kingdom (UK) and European Economic Area (EEA) users’ privacy choices to Amazon Ads.

You can send consent when uploading data using a third-party data partner or directly to AMC, ADM, or ADSP.

## Third-party data partner

Amazon Ads supports many widely used third-party data partners such as customer data platforms (CDPs) to simplify the setup of ACS. If you use a CDP, please refer to your provider’s documentation for guidance on enabling ACS and ensuring UK and EEA users’ privacy choices are correctly communicated to Amazon Ads.

If your CDP supports the IAB TCF or the GPP, you can choose to share either TCF or GPP as your preferred consent signal.

Note: If you’re unsure whether your CDP supports ACS, contact your CDP or your Amazon Ads representative for guidance.

## Directly to AMC

If you upload data directly to AMC you must either use (i) the IAB European TCF signals, (ii) the GPP, or (iii) ACS, to communicate your UK and EEA users’ privacy choices to Amazon Ads.

Refer to Consent Management in AMC for additional details.

## Directly to ADSP

If you upload data directly to ADSP you must either use (i) the IAB European TCF signals, (ii) the GPP, or (iii) ACS, to communicate your UK and EEA users’ privacy choices to Amazon Ads.

Refer to Provide Consent for Uploads for additional details.

## Directly to ADM

If you upload data directly to ADM you must either use (i) the IAB European TCF signals, (ii) the GPP, or (iii) ACS, to communicate your UK and EEA users’ privacy choices to Amazon Ads.
