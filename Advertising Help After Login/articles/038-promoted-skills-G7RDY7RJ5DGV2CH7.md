# Promoted Skills

Alexa skill builders can create campaigns to promote their skills on Echo Show device home screens.

Updated on Jan 6, 2023

Promoted Skills is a simple way to promote skills on Echo Show Home Screens. It’s a self-serve ad product for skill builders to promote their skills on Alexa channels, starting with Echo Show Home Screen.

Skill builders can easily create ad campaigns for skill promotions using the self-service Amazon Ads Console. You’ll also be able to view the performance of the ad campaign in the Ads Console. Customers are able to invoke your skill by simply tapping on the screen or speaking the hint text that appears in the bottom left of the screen (Alexa, open [skill name]).

To get started, go to Alexa Developer Console and choose the skill you’d like to promote. Next, visit the new **Promote your skills** tab, and check if your skill is eligible with a single click. After that, you can immediately begin creating your campaign in Amazon Ads console. As your campaigns run, you can view performance metrics and attribution metrics that are updated on a daily basis in the campaign manager on the advertising console.
