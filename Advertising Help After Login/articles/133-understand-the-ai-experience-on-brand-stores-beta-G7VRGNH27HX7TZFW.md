# Understand the AI experience on Brand Stores (beta)

Learn how Amazon's AI-powered Brand Store experience enhances the shopping experience on your Brand Stores.

Updated on May 13, 2026

The AI assisted experience on Brand Stores is currently in beta for select US brands for a defined test period.

To enhance the customer shopping experience, Amazon introduces new widgets to Brand Stores that enable shoppers to discover relevant products and information by starting an interactive conversation with an AI chat agent. The chat agent serves as guide to the brand's catalog and provides responses tailored to the shopper's queries and known context.

## What is the AI assisted experience on Brand Stores?

Amazon is adding AI-powered widgets to your Store's landing page. It helps shoppers find relevant products and learn more about your brand through conversational experience. This includes 2 components:

- **Top Zone Widget:** Only shows at the top of the home page, and has prompts like **"Help me choose"**and **"Is this right for me"** at the top of your Store's home page. When a shopper selects one of the prompts, a bottom sheet opens to start an interactive conversation between the shopper and the AI chat agent. The AI chat agent provides relevant product recommendations from your brand, answers questions about your brand, and shares additional brand information, it may also highlight other relevant products, such as best sellers or popular items from your brand.
- **Ask about [Brand name]:** A floating chat icon that appears at the bottom of all Store pages, allowing shoppers to access the AI assisted experience from anywhere within your Store. When a shopper selects the icon, a bottom sheet opens to initiate a conversation with the AI chat agent, enabling shoppers to ask questions, explore products, and receive recommendations.

## Opt out of the AI assisted experience

AI powered enhancements powering conversational experience are automatically enabled for selected Stores. However, brand can opt out via any of the following options:

- **Account Manager–assisted opt-out:** Contact your account manager to disable the AI assisted experience on your Brand Store.
- **Self-service opt-out:** Use the Amazon Support feature in Seller Central or Vendor Central to submit a request. The Support team will assist you in opting out of this feature.
