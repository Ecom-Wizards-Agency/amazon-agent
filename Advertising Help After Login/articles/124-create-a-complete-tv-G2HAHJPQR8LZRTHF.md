# Create a Complete TV

Learn how to create Complete TV campaigns on Amazon DSP.

Updated on Sep 28, 2026

This page only lists settings **specific** to creating an ad campaign for Complete TV.

## What's Complete TV?

Complete TV is a campaign type within Display, Video, and Audio campaigns designed specifically for TV buyers. It combines the reliability of traditional TV buying with advanced programmatic advertising capabilities.

Complete TV helps you manage upfront commitments and scatter budgets through a single interface. It also:

- Optimizes campaigns for awareness KPIs while helping to ensure commitment fulfillment
- Provides real-time insights into campaign performance and commitment pacing
- Offers access to premium Streaming TV inventory across multiple publishers

**Benefits of Complete TV**

Complete TV can help you efficiently manage fragmented Streaming TV investments, simplify budget allocation across multiple streaming platforms, and improve visibility into cross-platform reach and frequency. Use it to:

- Maximize campaign performance without sacrificing full upfront delivery
- Optimize for incremental reach beyond linear TV
- Save time with auto-generated ad groups across investments
- Transform scatter investments using supply recommendations
- Drive efficiency using insights across publishers and investments

Use Complete TV if you're:

- A traditional TV buyer or linear TV investment team.
- A media agency or brand that's managing significant upfront commitments.
- An advertiser looking to streamline and automate your Streaming TV buying process.

**Note**: Complete TV is currently only available for advertisers in the US.

## Create a Complete TV campaign with these settings

To advertise with Display, Video, and Audio campaigns, you need to create a campaign, associate ad groups with the campaign, and assign ads to each ad group.

The settings below are specific to campaigns for Complete TV.

**Brand**

- Select the brand to feature in your campaign (if applicable). If your brand isn't available, please reach out to your Amazon representative. Brand selection is required for TV incremental reach campaigns.

**Products and Services**

- Choose the category that your Complete TV campaign falls under

**Goal and optimization**

- KPI: Choose your desired KPI

**Commitments**

- Select the commitments that you want to link to your campaign to auto calculate your budget and delivery

**Targeting**

- Click **Add audiences** and choose the audiences that you want to reach with your ad

**Budget and delivery**

- Agency fee (optional): Percentage deducted from your total campaign budget
- Review the automatically calculated Committed Spend based on linked commitments
- Enter the Scatter Budget amount (if applicable)
- Choose the **Start** and **End dates** for budget pacing

**Frequency**

- Frequency: Set the number of times a customer sees your ad. Learn about frequency caps
- Frequency group (optional): Learn about frequency groups

**Finish setting up your campaign**

Click **Create campaign and ad groups** at the top of the window to automatically create ad groups and associate them with your Complete TV campaign.

Next, set up your Complete TV ad (formerly known as Creative on Amazon DSP) and attach it to your ad groups.

## Activate your campaign and monitor performance

Once your foundational components are set up, activate your campaign so it delivers and shows to customers.

1. In the navigation side menu, click **Campaigns**.
2. At the top of the **Campaigns** page, click the **Display, Video, and, Audio** tab.
3. In the **Ad groups** page, activate the ad groups by clicking the **toggle** in the **Active** column.
4. Activate your campaign by clicking the **toggle** at the top of the window next to the campaign name.

**Tip**: Monitor Complete TV campaigns with Reach and frequency metrics.
