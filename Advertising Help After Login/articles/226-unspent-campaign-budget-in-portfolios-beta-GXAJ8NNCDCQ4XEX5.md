# Unspent campaign budget in portfolios (beta)

Learn how to share unspent campaign budget with other campaigns in the same portfolio.

Updated on Feb 26, 2026

This feature is currently in beta, and isn't available to everyone at this time.

## What's unspent campaign budget?

Portfolios can automatically share unspent campaign budget with other campaigns in the same portfolio. This budget comes from campaigns that didn't spend their full daily budget in previous days of the month. When **unspent campaign budget** is turned on for a portfolio, your Sponsored Products campaigns that have already spent their full daily budget can automatically use other Sponsored Products campaigns' unspent campaign budget, **allowing them to spend up to 100% more than their average daily budget on any given day**. This allows you to benefit from high traffic days.

**Note:** With this feature turned on, individual campaigns can spend beyond their own monthly budget. The portfolio as a whole will never exceed its overall budget cap.

## Monthly spending limit for Sponsored Products campaigns

(Daily budget × Number of days in the month) = Maximum monthly spend

**Example:** If you have 3 campaigns:

- Campaign A: $10 daily budget
- Campaign B: $15 daily budget
- Campaign C: $20 daily budgetYour total portfolio won't spend more than $1,395 in a 31-day month: ($10 + $15 + $20) × 31 days = $1,395.

**Note**: This is a maximum limit. Your actual spend may be lower depending on campaign performance.

## How portfolio budget caps affect unspent campaign budget

Portfolio budget caps act as an overarching control, ensuring that the **unspent campaign budget** feature doesn't lead to unexpected overspending. To learn more, see Portfolio budgets.

| **Priority of budget caps** | **Spending limits** | **Safe usage** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Portfolio budget caps always take precedence over unspent campaign budget. | Even if unspent leftovers are available, spending will not exceed the portfolio budget cap. This ensures you won't overspend beyond your set portfolio limit. | You can safely use the **unspent campaign budget** feature without worrying about exceeding your portfolio budget cap. |
| Once a portfolio reaches its budget cap, all spending for campaigns within that portfolio will stop. | The system automatically manages spending to stay within your specified limits. |

## Turn on unspent campaign budget

To use unspent campaign budgets:

1. In the navigation side menu, click **Campaigns** Then, click **Portfolios**.
2. Select the portfolio where you want to turn on **unspent campaign budget**, then click **Modify portfolio**.

**Example**: Here's how unspent campaign budget works across 3 campaigns in a portfolio:

| Portfolio |
| --- |
|  | Campaign 1 | Campaign 2 | Campaign 3 |
| Daily Budget | $100 | $100 | $100 |
| Day 1 Spend | $70 | $50 | $100 |
| Unspent | $30 | $50 | - |
| **Total unspent** | $80 |

Each campaign has a $100 daily budget. At the end of day 1, Campaign 1 has $30 left over and Campaign 2 has $50 left over. Campaign 3 spent its full budget. That leaves $80 in unspent budget available to the portfolio.

The next day, if Campaign 3 spends its full $100 daily budget, it can use the $80 in unspent budget to get additional clicks and impressions — spending up to $180 that day. This continues through the calendar month. The unspent budget resets to $0 at the start of each month and begins accumulating again.

Your campaign can still use its own unspent budget from previous days using the average daily budget system. The unspent campaign budget sharing system is offered to help campaigns that still run out of budget, even after spending their own leftovers from previous days. If your campaigns spend more than your monthly budget, your invoice will be adjusted. You won't be charged for any amount above your monthly spending limit.
