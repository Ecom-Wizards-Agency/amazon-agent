# Sponsored ads translation

Amazon supports multiple languages for customers shopping on Amazon.

Updated on Feb 12, 2026

Note: This feature is currently only available in the following marketplaces: AE, CA, DE, IN, JP, SA, US.

Automatic translation helps you reach customers in their preferred language across supported marketplaces. Your campaigns are automatically translated within 24 hours after going live, ensuring your ads connect with diverse audiences.

| Marketplace | Secondary language supported |
| --- | --- |
| AE | Arabic |
| CA | English, French |
| DE | German, English, Turkish, Polish |
| IN | Hindi, Tamil, Bengali, English |
| JP | Japanese, English |
| SA | Arabic |
| US | English, Spanish |

## Supported ad products

| Topic | Sposored Brands | Display |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **What's translated** | Headlines, custom text, and video subtitles | Headlines and product titles |
| **Timeline** | Within 24 hours after default language campaign goes live | Within 24 hours after ad approval |
| **Your control** | Edit translations using the Creative page and click on **Edit Translations** button OR submit your own translations. |
| **Video support** | Videos automatically include translated subtitles | Video translations not supported |
| **Eligibility** |  | Only available to advertisers selling products/services on Amazon (not available for non-endemic advertisers) |
| **Restrictions** | - GL exceptions: JP marketplace excludes Pharmaceutical and Grocery categories - Can't turn off translated ads (optimal shopper experience) | - Onsite placements only - Excluded categories: media (books, music, movies, software), food/beverages, prescription products, certain personal care products - Non-endemic advertisers cannot use this feature |

When Amazon’s ad translation service isn’t able to provide a translation that meets our quality and ad policy bar, we won’t provide a translation. In this scenario, the ad won’t be shown to customers shopping in the specific secondary language.
