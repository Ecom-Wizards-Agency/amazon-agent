# Monitor product performance

Monitor your product performance and take recommendations to improve your product listings.

Updated on Apr 23, 2026

Was this video helpful? Select feedback

The Products page displays all advertised products in a single place and shows their advertising performance metrics. Within the Products page you can check impressions, clicks, sales, return on advertising spend (ROAS), new-to-brand (NTB) sales, and other metrics, aggregated from the campaign and ad group levels.

## To access

To access the products page:

1. In the navigation side menu, click **Campaigns**
2. At the top of the **Campaigns** page, click the**Sponsored Products** tab.

Based on your requirements, you can select between the following:

**Advertised Products** help you view and analyze product-level performance for all advertised products. You can review listing improvement recommendations to help improve your performance. You can:

- Analyze performance by campaign and ad group
- Review listing improvement recommendations
- Search by campaign name or ASIN
- Create new campaigns with selected ASINs
- Add ASINs to existing campaigns

**Available metrics**: Impressions, Clicks, Sales, Return on advertising spend (ROAS), New-to-brand (NTB) sales
