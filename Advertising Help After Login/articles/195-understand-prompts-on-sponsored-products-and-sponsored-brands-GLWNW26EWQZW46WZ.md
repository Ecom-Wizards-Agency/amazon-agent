# Understand prompts on Sponsored Products and Sponsored Brands

Learn about prompts and how they work with your existing campaigns.

Updated on Mar 26, 2026

## What are prompts?

Prompts are an AI-powered extension to Sponsored Products and Sponsored Brands campaigns that help shoppers discover products through conversational experiences on Amazon. They integrate into the Amazon shopping experience, enhancing product discovery at shopper decision points. They surface relevant product information through AI-generated suggestions and guiding questions, and require no additional setup.

Prompts are available to advertisers selling in the United States with active Sponsored Products or Sponsored Brands campaigns.

## How prompts work

Prompts are generated from brand-provided inputs (such as detail page, Brand Store, and campaign data) and other Amazon-verified first-party signals. They undergo both AI-based moderation and human audits, including fact-checking and removal of outdated references.

Prompts are charged on a cost-per-click (CPC) basis using your existing campaign budget—no additional budget or setup is required. Prompts with clicks will appear in existing Sponsored Products and Sponsored Brands reporting.

## Manage your prompts

All existing and new Sponsored Products and Sponsored Brands campaigns in the US are automatically enrolled in prompts (enrollment isn't required). To view and manage your prompts:

1. In the navigation side menu, click **Campaigns**
2. Select your campaign.
3. Click **Ad Groups**, then select your ad group.
4. Click **Ads**, then **Prompts**.

If prompts appear in this view, your campaign has prompts that have driven at least 1 click. If the view is empty, your campaign has no prompts with clicks yet.

This view displays the prompt text, the associated ad, and key performance metrics including impressions, clicks, and orders. You can pause individual prompts at any time.

You can also monitor performance through downloadable reports or API. See Create a report for steps on how to download reports.
