# Prompt writing for Brand Store Builder Agent

Guidelines for writing effective prompts when using the Brand Store Builder Agent.

Updated on Oct 13, 2025

When writing your own prompts using Brand Store Builder Agent to create and edit your brand's store pages, we recommend the following best practices:

- **Be specific and clear:** Clearly articulate the exact product categories that you want to feature rather than using generic terms. Example: Create a page showcasing my premium wooden cutting boards, featuring our top 5 bestsellers. Organize products from largest to smallest size, with pricing and wood type clearly displayed for easy comparison.
- **Include key page elements:** Detail all the desired content types you want to incorporate, including specific mentions of images, text blocks, and product features. Outline any specific layout preferences that you have for the page structure and organization. When relevant, include information about your target audience to ensure that the content resonates with the intended demographic. Example: Create a product page for our organic skincare line with a hero image at the top, followed by 3 benefit-focused sections. Include customer testimonials in the sidebar widgets and end with a comparison chart of our top 3 moisturizers.
- **Define content parameters:** Detail all the desired content types you want to incorporate, including specific mentions of images, text blocks, and product features. Outline any specific layout preferences that you have for the page structure and organization. Example: Create a new arrivals page for our athletic wear with punchy, Gen-Z focused headlines. Maximum 8 words each. Use casual, energetic language and highlight sustainability features in bullet points of 3-4 words each.
- **Provide context:** Include important background information about your brand that helps shape the layout and content appropriately. Add the purpose of the page, whether it's showcasing products, highlighting features, or featuring promotions. Include specific customer actions that you want to encourage through the page content. Example: Create an 'About Us' page highlighting our 25-year history as a family-owned bakery. Emphasize our use of traditional European recipes, local ingredient sourcing, and our signature sourdough process. Include a section about our weekly community donations program.

## Sample prompts

- "Create a page showcasing all my dog litter products. I have fewer than 10 products in that category. Organize them for easy browsing and comparison."
- "Create a page focused on highlighting my best selling dog litter product, with comprehensive information and features."
- "Create an **About Us** page telling my brand story, company values, and what makes my business unique. Insist on where we source the product from and on the fact that we’re organic first."
- "Create a **Deals** page featuring current promotions, discounts, and special offers to drive sales. Promote products with the largest discount first."
- "Create a **New Arrivals** page showcasing your latest products and recent launches to highlight what's new. Use the image that I added to the prompt as the first component on the page. Use tag lines that are no longer than 10 words aimed at a younger generation."
- "Create a **Best Sellers** page highlighting my most popular and top-performing products that customers love. Show highest rated products first."
- "Create a **Gifts for Him** page featuring my best selling products with taglines aimed at men in their 40s."
