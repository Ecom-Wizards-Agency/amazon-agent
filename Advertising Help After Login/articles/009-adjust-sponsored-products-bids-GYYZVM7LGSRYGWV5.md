# Adjust Sponsored Products bids

Learn how to set your bids to increase when your ad is eligible to show in certain locations or to specific groups of shoppers.

Updated on Sep 28, 2026

You can adjust bids by placement and/or audience to increase your bid when your ad is eligible to appear in certain locations and/or to certain groups of advertisers. This can help you be more strategic with where your budget is spent.

## Adjust bids by placement

Enter a percentage amount to increase your bid when your ad is eligible to compete for one or all of these placements:

- **Top of search (first page):** Up to the first 5 positions in the top row of the first page search results on desktop, and up to the first 4 positions on mobile.
- **Rest of search:** the middle or bottom of shopping results and the second page of shopping results and beyond.
- **Product pages:**Sponsored Products ads on the product details page, and all nonshopping placements such as the add-to-cart page.
- **Amazon business placements:** all Sponsored Products placements that appear on Amazon Business including Top of Search, Rest of Search, and Amazon Business product pages.

The specified increase works in addition to your selected bidding strategy (dynamic or fixed). Adjusting bids by placement is best for advertisers who have observed that certain placements have led to conversions, sales, or other desired activities in the past. You can view placement metrics at the campaign level on the bid adjustment tab in campaign manager.

**Examples:**

- You set a **fixed bid**of $1 for the keyword “camera” and set a 50% bid adjustment for top of search. When your ad is eligible to appear in the top of search against “camera,” your bid will increase to $1.5
- You set a **dynamic** bid of $1 for the keyword “camera” and set a 50% bid adjustment for top of search. When your ad is eligible to appear in the top of search against “camera” your bid will increase to $1.5. Dynamic bidding (up and down) can further increase the bid up to $3.“

In Sponsored Products across retailers campaigns, you cant adjust bids for Amazon Business placements, but have two additional placement options:

- **Top of browse:** the top of browse pages, capturing shopper attention as they navigate categories and subcategories.
- **Rest of browse:**browse pages below the top placements, providing additional visibility for your products as shoppers explore the catalog.

## Adjust bids by audience

**Note**: Advertisers outside of the US marketplace can only select up to 1 audience at this time.

You can adjust your bids by audience to help drive discovery and sales for specific groups of shoppers. Select up to 10 audiences per campaign, each with an independent bid boost percentage. When the shopper is a member of one of your selected audience(s), your bid increases by the percentage you set for that audience(s). There are 2 types of audiences you can use:

- **Amazon audiences**: Prebuilt audience segments informed by first-party shopping and streaming signals (in-market, lifestyle, interests, and life events). Available to all advertisers.
- **Amazon Marketing Cloud (AMC) custom audiences:** Custom audiences built in AMC using engagement signals across Amazon Ads media investments.

**How your bid works with multiple audiences**

If a shopper qualifies for more than 1 of your boosted audiences, only the highest bid boost applies. Boosts do not stack across audiences. In the case of a tie, the impression is attributed to either audience at random. Attribution is assigned to the winning audience at impression time and is not split across audiences.

**Example:** You bid $1 for the keyword "camera" and set a 50% top of search boost, a 30% boost for 1 audience, and a 60% boost for another. A shopper who belongs to both audiences searches for "camera" and your ad is eligible for top of search. Only the 60% audience boost applies (highest audience wins), and it combines with the 50% placement boost. For instance:

- $1.00 original bid + 50% top of search adjustment = $1.50
- $1.50 original bid + 60% audience adjustment (highest boost wins) = $2.40

The 30% audience boost doesn't apply because the 60% boost is higher.

## Applying placement and audience adjustments together

Bid adjustments of different types can stack. If you set a placement boost and an audience boost, and a single impression qualifies for both, both adjustments apply together to determine your final bid. The winning audience boost combines with any applicable placement or video boost.

## Adjust bids by video

**Note:**Sponsored Products video is only available in the US and is currently not available for Books.

You can adjust your bids for your ads with Sponsored Product Videos (videos within your ad that highlight your product’s unique benefits to shoppers). Adjusting your bids here will increase the likelihood of having your ad appear compared to other video ads.

You can enter up to a 900% increase (10x) to your base bid in Sponsored Products. If a Sponsored Product Video bid is applied with other bid modifiers such as Audiences or other Placements, these adjustments will apply together when conditions are met to determine your final bid.

**Example:** Let’s say you have a bid of $1.00 for the keyword “camera” and set a 50% rest of search bid adjustment

- a: A 50% custom audience bid adjustment
- b: A 100% bid adjustment for Sponsored Products Video
- c: Your final bid for video ads that appear on rest of search for your custom audience will be $4.50.a. $1 original bid + 50% rest of search adjustment = $1.5 [ $1 + ($1 x 50%)]b. $1.5 + 50% custom audience adjustment = $2.25 [ $1.5 + ($1.5 x 50%)]c. $2.25 + 100% Sponsored Products Video adjustment = $4.50 [ $2.25 + ($2.25 X 100%)]
