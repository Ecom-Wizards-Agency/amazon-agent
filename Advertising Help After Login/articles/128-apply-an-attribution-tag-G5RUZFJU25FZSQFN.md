# Apply an attribution tag

Apply Amazon Attribution tags to the corresponding non-Amazon campaigns to complete the measurement setup.

Updated on May 18, 2026

An attribution tag allows you to measure advertising impact by including it directly in your non-Amazon ads. The tag contains URL parameters that allow Amazon Attribution to associate future activity to the ad click.

## Implement an attribution tag

Visit your corresponding campaign manager. For example, if you’re running an ad on social media, visit the ad manager within that social media console. Copy and paste each tag into the destination URL field within your non-Amazon campaign manager. Note that each publisher system is different.

Amazon Attribution tags should be specifically created for each individual ad, link, or button within your non-Amazon media. When implementing, apply the specific Amazon Attribution tag as the destination URL for each individual tactic. Don’t make any changes to the attribution tags as it will impact the measurement. We don’t support combining attribution tags with Amazon Affiliates links. We support only product detail pages and brand store pages as landing pages

Once you apply Amazon Attribution tags and launch your non-Amazon campaigns, you’ll begin to see reporting in the advertising console. We recommend waiting 1-2 days to review and validate Amazon Attribution reporting. If you notice discrepancy in the reporting, follow our troubleshooting guidelines.
