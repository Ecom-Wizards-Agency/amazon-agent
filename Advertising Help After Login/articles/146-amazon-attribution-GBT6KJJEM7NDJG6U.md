# Amazon Attribution

Amazon Attribution is an analytics and measurement tool that helps track non-Amazon advertising performance across multiple channels. It also helps discover how their digital marketing tactics are helping drive sales on Amazon.

Updated on Feb 24, 2026

With Amazon Attribution, you can view how non-Amazon marketing channels impact shopping activity and sales on Amazon. This tool helps you better understand the return on investment (ROI) of your digital marketing - including search, social, display, video, and email. The tool can also provide you with insight into how audiences discover, research, and buy your products on Amazon.

Amazon Attribution ad traffic measurement is powered by attribution tags. To measure advertising impact, you can include Amazon Attribution tags directly in your non-Amazon ads. Ad-attributed activity is measured by placing the tags in your ad’s final destination URL that captures data upon click. Information collected from the tag is used only to provide Amazon Attribution’s measurement and analytics services. It isn't used for any targeting purposes. Create an Amazon Attribution tag

The tool’s conversion metrics—including Amazon detail page views,Purchases (promoted) (formerly known as Purchases on Amazon DSP), and sales—give you a comprehensive view into how different marketing tactics contribute to shopping activity on Amazon. Review Amazon Attribution metrics

Amazon Attribution is available for professional sellers enrolled in Amazon Brand Registry with a brand representative role, vendors, agencies with clients who sell products on Amazon, and authors who advertise on the advertising console. Eligible sellers, vendors, and authors can access Amazon Attribution measurement through either the self-service console or tool providers integrated with the Amazon Ads API.
