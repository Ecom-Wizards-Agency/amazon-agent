# Understand Sponsored Products off-Amazon advertising

Your Sponsored Products campaigns can reach shoppers beyond Amazon, on premium sites, apps, and in creator content, so your products can find more customers and generate more sales.

Updated on Sep 23, 2026

Sponsored Products off-Amazon advertising is available for advertisers in Brazil, Canada, Egypt, France, Germany, India, Italy, Mexico, Saudi Arabia, Spain, Türkiye, United Arab Emirates, United Kingdom, and the United States.

## How off-Amazon advertising works

Your ads run on the targeting, bid, and budget you already set — no extra setup required. We use those settings to place your ads where they're most relevant to shoppers whose browsing suggests interest in your products. Ads render as an image, video, or text so they fit naturally wherever they appear.

**Your ads reach off-Amazon placements in three ways**

- **Third-party sites and apps**: On third-party sites and apps, your ad is matched to a placement based on relevance and your adjusted bid. If selected, Amazon submits your ad to the publisher, where it competes with ads from other sources. The publisher decides which ads appear and in what positions.
- **Third-party sites and apps with Amazon investment**: For some placements, Amazon may also select your product based on factors like price competitiveness, category trends, and product reviews, and invests its own marketing funds alongside your campaign to help boost your reach.
- **Creator content:** Creators in the Amazon Influencer and Associates Program with a track record of driving traffic or sales on Amazon can select your campaign to feature in their product reviews, editorial content, and buying guides.

## Benefits of off-Amazon advertising

Off-Amazon advertising puts your products in front of customers on premium sites, apps, and creator content, to reach shoppers in their discovery and shopping moments before they come to Amazon. Your existing campaign settings, including targeting, bids, and budget apply automatically, so extending your reach doesn't mean changing your setup. When shoppers click your ad, they go directly to your product detail page, creating a seamless path from discovery to purchase.

## Optimize your off-Amazon advertising

You can continue to optimize your campaigns with keywords and bids, just as you do on Amazon. To learn more about how targeting works, visit Understand targeting.

You can also adjust your off-Amazon placement settings at any point during your campaign.

**To update your settings**

1. In the navigation side menu, click **Campaigns**.
2. Select a campaign.
3. In the campaign side menu, click ****Campaign settings****.
4. Scroll down to **Adjust reach on and off Amazon**.
5. Choose where your ads appear: **Increase reach off-Amazon (default)**: Delivers ads both on and off Amazon using your existing targeting settings, which can add impressions and sales opportunities. **Limit reach to Amazon**: Ads appear only on Amazon owned-and-operated properties. This may reduce impressions and sales opportunities.

You can also manage your off-Amazon campaigns in bulk. For more information, see Manage sponsored ads campaigns with bulksheets.

## Off-Amazon bidding and budgets

Off-Amazon advertising runs on your regular Sponsored Products campaign settings. Your maximum bid applies to every click, whether on or off Amazon, and every click counts toward the same campaign budget. Your cost-per-click (CPC) never exceeds your maximum bid, and your total spend never exceeds your budget.

Bid adjustments for Top of Search, Rest of Search, and Product Pages do not apply to off-Amazon placements. Dynamic bidding and rule-based bidding still apply.

**How CPC is set off-Amazon**

- **Third party sites and apps**: Amazon adjusts your bid using predicted click-through and conversion rates, and submits your ad to the publisher. The resulting CPC covers what Amazon pays the publisher (supply cost) plus Amazon’s revenue for delivering the placement.
- **Third party sites and apps with Amazon investment**: When Amazon invests its marketing funds alongside your campaign, Amazon calculates your CPC using factors like maximum bid and expected performance.
- **Creator content**: Amazon calculates your CPC based on your maximum bid, product's expected performance and sales the ad generates. Creators may receive a portion of the CPC from qualifying clicks.

## Off-Amazon reporting

You can view off-Amazon performance, including sales, in:

- **Placement Report** under **Campaign Manager** > **Measuring and Reporting**
- Amazon Ads API using ****POST**** > ****Reporting**** > ****Reports**** endpoint with the **Placement Classification** metric
- **Campaign Manager console reports** with MRC-accredited filter settings

## Brand safety

Amazon works to place your ads in appropriate environments — either by reviewing and blocking third-party sites directly, or through participating premium publishers who monitor their own placements. To exclude specific sites, apps, or creators, update your deny list preferences.
