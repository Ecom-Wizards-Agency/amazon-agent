# Understand Bulksheets

Manage and create campaigns at scale by editing multiple campaigns, keywords, ads, and ad groups in a single spreadsheet.

Updated on Feb 16, 2026

## What are bulksheets?

Bulksheets let you create, edit, and optimize advertising campaigns in bulk using spreadsheet files. You can update hundreds of campaigns simultaneously instead of making changes one at a time. work, but want access to a more scalable solution as your advertising needs grow.

**Benefits:**

- Make multiple edits across campaigns in one spreadsheet
- Create multiple campaigns, add multiple ad groups from one file
- Change campaign and ad group names in large batches instead of one at a time
- Adjust bids across hundreds of campaigns simultaneously
