# Brand Store - All Deals page

All Deals page features products from your brand with ongoing deals for shoppers.

Updated on May 14, 2026

Brand Stores has launched an automated All Deals page to save you time and to help shoppers find all your products on promotion. To eliminate the hassle, you no longer have to manually curate and manage your deals, if there are deals available, Amazon will display all products with active deals in the All Deals page. If you already have a Featured Deals page, it will continue to show as featured deals to shoppers.

Note: All Deals is not available for premium theme Brand Stores.

The All Deals page is added automatically to your Brand Store for the duration of the deals and is removed once the deals end. This page will be visible as the second tab, right after the homepage, in your Brand Store.

**Example**: If your brand ABC has 10 products out of which 5 have on going deals, the All Deals page will showcase those 5 with deals in a new auto-populated, All Deals page in your Brand Store.

While this feature is automatically selected for your Brand Store, you can choose to opt out, in the following 2 ways:

## Create Featured Deals page

Amazon won’t automatically create an All Deals page if, you use **Featured deals**widget and add it on your store page. Shoppers will see only 1 deals page within a Brand Store. Learn more about Featured deals.

## Opt-out of Store builder

To opt out of this feature within Store builder:

1. Sign in to Amazon Ads Console, then go to **Brand Content**.
2. Click **Stores.**
3. Select the store for which you would like to opt-out and click **Edit Brand Store**.
4. In the left top corner of the Store builder page, click **Store Settings.**
5. Scroll down to **Display all deals,**and select **No, don’t show**to opt out.
6. Click **Save**and publish your Store.
7. Wait for Moderation to approve your Store and the changes to go live.
