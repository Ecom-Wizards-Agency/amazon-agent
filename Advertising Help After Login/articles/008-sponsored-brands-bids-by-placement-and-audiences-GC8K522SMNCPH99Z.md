# Sponsored Brands bids by placement and audiences

Bid adjustments let you automatically increase or decrease bids when your ads appear in specific placements or target certain audience segments.

Updated on Dec 1, 2025

You can adjust bids by placement or audience to be more strategic with your budget by increasing your bid when your ad is eligible to appear in certain locations and/or to certain groups of advertisers.

You can do this when you create a campaign on the **Campaign Bid Adjustments**card, or once a campaign is live in the campaign manager.

## Bids by placement

**Tip:**Adjusting bids by placement is best for advertisers who have observed that certain placements have led to conversions, sales, or other desired activities in the past. You can view placement metrics for existing campaigns within campaign manager on the bid adjustment tab.

Enter a percentage amount to increase your bid when your ad is eligible to compete for one or all of these placements:

- Top of search (first page): the top row of the first-page search results.
- Rest of search: the middle or bottom of shopping results and the second page of shopping results and beyond.

To adjust bids by placement within campaign manager, click on the **Placements**tab on the **Campaign Bid Adjustments**card and enter the desired percent in the box next to **Placements other than top of search**.

**Example:** If you bid $1 for a keyword and set a 50% bid adjustment decrease, then your bid for placements other than top of search may decrease to $.50 [$1 original bid - ($1 x .50 rest of search bid adjustment)].

## Bids by audience

**Note:** You can adjust bids for only 1 audience per campaign. You can choose either an Amazon-built audience or an Amazon Marketing Cloud (AMC) audience, but not both.

You can adjust your bids by audience to help drive discovery and sales for specific groups of shoppers. You choose an audience and a % to increase your bids by when a user from that audience searches for products. There are 2 types of audiences you can use:

- Amazon built audiences: prebuilt audience segments informed by first-party shopping and streaming signals (in-market, lifestyle, interests, and life events). These are available to all sponsored ads advertisers. Hover over the to see a detailed audience description under the**Audience** dropdown.
- Amazon Marketing Cloud (AMC) custom audiences: AMC users can apply bid adjustments to AMC custom audiences in Sponsored Product campaigns. Use engagement signals such as impressions, clicks, and conversions across Amazon Ads media investments to capture high intent audiences during their shopping journey at the right place and time.

**Example:** If you bid $2 for a keyword and set a 50% increase for a custom audience, your bid will adjust to $3 [$2 original bid + ($2 x .50 custom audience adjustment)].

**Tip:**Audiences created in AMC are available in your sponsored ads account during campaign creation and campaign management. Performance reports are available in Ads Console and you can also access them through the reporting features in Amazon Marketing Cloud.

## Combine bids by placement with bids by audience

You can combine bid adjustments by placement and audience for the same campaign.

You can enter up to a 900% increase (10x) to your base bid. If both Placement and Audience bid percentages are applied and both conditions are met, these bid adjustments will be applied together to determine your final bid.

**Example:** You bid $1 for a keyword and set a 50% bid adjustment increase for placements other than top of search (a). You also add a 100% bid adjustment for a custom audience (b). Your bid for placements other than top of search for that audience may increase to $3.

a: $1 original bid + 50% rest of search adjustment = $1.5 [ $1 + ($1 x 50%)]

b: $1.5 + 100% custom audience adjustment = $3 [ $1.5 + ($1.5 X 100%)]

## Combine cost controls with bids by placement or audience

**Note:** Because cost controll is a target average cost and not a max cost, your average CPC may be higher than the amount entered. You can only use cost control if your campaign goal is Drive page visits.

You can also combine bid adjustments with cost controls to specify where you want to adjust your bid and for whom while still controlling your campaign cost. Cost control is a feature that automatically adjusts bids for you based on your specified cost per click, which you can choose on the Cost control card.

When cost control is on and you have adjusted bids by placement or audience, your expected target CPC will adjust accordingly. This means your highest possible CPC will exceed your target cost control CPC.
