# Stores insights

Stores insights provide information about your Store's performance, including traffic and sales by traffic source and store page.

Updated on Feb 25, 2026

Once your store is live, you can observe how well it’s doing with the Stores insights dashboard. You can access Stores insights from the Stores builder or from the Stores main page.

1. In the left navigation menu, click the Brand home icon and select Stores.
2. Click **See Insights** next to the store for which you want to view metrics.
3. When you're using the Store builder, you can get back to the insights tab by clicking on it. You can view metrics for your Store as a whole (Store overview) or for the specific page you're viewing (Current page report).

The insights dashboard shows how each of your store pages and traffic sources performs, with metrics such as daily visitors, page views, and attributed sales.

You can export certain aspects of the Stores insights as a CSV, but there are no dedicated reports for Stores insights.

Data is displayed by page and traffic source, providing unique visibility into how to optimize your marketing strategy and surface new opportunities to drive store traffic. It’s available the next day for views and daily visits, and after 2 days for attributed units and sales. For example, if it’s Wednesday, you can see views and daily visits from Tuesday or earlier, and attributed units and sales from Monday or earlier.

Learn more about Stores metrics.

## Navigating the Stores insights dashboard

**Insights menu**

This sidebar allows you to navigate between the different reports available. You can toggle your dashboard view between Overview, Traffic, Engagement, Sales, Pages, and Sources to analyze metrics in a graph format.

**Date Range: Last 30 days**

This tab allows you to adjust the date range for viewing insights. Adjusting this allows you to get a different snapshot of performance over different windows of time.

**Export**

Data is available by day or over a selected time period. The export button allows you to export insights in a .csv format. This allows you to more easily share metrics with others who may not have access to the Stores insights.

## Overview

The **Overview** tab provides a high-level summary. Use this to get a pulse on your performance across multiple dimensions, then dive into each report to learn more details about your performance.

## Traffic

There are 4 dashboards related to traffic:

- **Daily visitors:** This chart shows how many visitors per day view your store page. This graph can help you validate how well you’re promoting your Store, or if your advertising is effective at building consideration.
- **Page views:** This chart shows the number of page views per day that you get on your store page. It includes multiple views by the same audiences, expressed by an average number of views per visitor. This helps you gauge how often shoppers are viewing your store and browsing your brand’s products.
- **Top sources by views**: This bar chart shows you where audiences are accessing the link to your Stores page. This can help you understand where shoppers are learning about your store and how they’re finding it. It can help you understand advertising performance and where you may need to adjust your strategy to attract more clicks to generate visitors and views.
- **Top pages by views:** This bar chart shows you which pages on your store are the most popular. This can help inform which of your product lines or categories are getting the most interest from groups of shoppers.

## Engagement

The Stores Engagement tab can help you understand how customers interact with ASINs on your Amazon Store. Having access to these metrics can help increase customer engagement, improve store merchandising, and boost overall store performance.

Benefits of using the Engagement tab:

- Learn about your top performing and most viewed ASINs to optimize your store layout can help you increase your click-through-ratio and purchases.
- Gain insights on which products have the highest engagement (view-based CTR) and sales (click-based CVR) to make informed decisions on the products you select to promote most on your store and advertising campaigns.
- Surface consumer trends based on customer engagement with products on Stores and sales of products attributed to Stores to help inform your pricing, inventory management, and product planning.

In the Engagement tab, you’ll have access to:

- **Product Engagement Totals:** Provides the store totals for ASIN views, clicks, and click-through rate (total views divided by total clicks).
- **Top-clicked Product:** Shows the top-clicked product of all ASINs listed on the Store. It also displays views, clicks, click-through rate, purchases, conversion ratio, and average sales price.
- **Product Engagement:** Provides detailed ASIN engagement metrics for each ASIN listed on the store. The table is sorted by "Views" and allows you to rank highest to lowest by each column as well as use the Search feature to identify for specific ASINs. For each ASIN, the table shows: renders, views, in-stock views, in-stock view rate, in-stock view average price, clicks, click-through rate, add-to-cart, purchases, units sold, conversion ratio, and average sales price.

These reports have multiple columns outlined some of which are off screen to the right. You can scroll through the table left to right to see all columns. The search box at the top of the table can help you filter by keyword. To learn more about the metrics available in the Engagement tab, visit Stores metrics.

## Sales

There are 4 dashboards related to sales:

- **Sales:** This chart shows your sales over the selected date range. The dotted line shows the average daily sales for the period. This chart can help you understand if your store and campaigns are helping to drive sales.
- **Units sold:** This chart shows you how many ASINs you’ve sold, expressing sales by unit. This dotted line shows the average daily unit sales for the period.
- **Top sources by sales:** This chart shows you sales based on where shoppers accessed your store from. Using this chart can help you assess if certain sources are more lucrative than others and can inform your advertising strategy.
- **Top pages by units:** This chart shows which pages help drive unit sales. This can help you assess which pages are best at encouraging shoppers to browse your product detail pages, helping drive interest to explore your brand’s products in greater depth.

**Daily breakdown**

This section showcases a summary of the above charts, organized by date. It allows you to analyze performance day by day, sorting which days had the highest or lowest daily visitors, views, views per visitor, units sold, and total sales. This way you can surface trends across each dimension over time.
