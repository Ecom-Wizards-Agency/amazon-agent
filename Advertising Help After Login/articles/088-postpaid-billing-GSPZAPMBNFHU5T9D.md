# Postpaid billing

Learn how postpaid billing works and manage your ad spending with credit limits and monthly budgets.

Postpaid billing is available for vendors who have spent at least ₹10 lakhs per month for 6 consecutive months on prepaid billing. With postpaid billing, you can run ads and pay for them at the end of each month based on your approved spending limit. Your spending limit is the maximum amount that you can owe Amazon at any time, determined by your past ad spending and payment history.

You'll receive one consolidated monthly invoice for all sponsored ad campaigns in your account. Payment is due within 30 days for directly-managed brands or 60 days for agencies.

**Postpaid billing benefits:**

- Run campaigns without adding funds in advance
- Receive one consolidated monthly invoice
- Control costs with optional monthly budgets below your spending limit
- View real-time spending and credit availability

**Example**

If your spending limit is ₹20 lakhs:

- You can run ads until your total unpaid amount (current month's spending plus any unpaid previous invoices) reaches ₹20 lakhs
- Set an optional monthly budget (for example, ₹10 lakhs) to further control spending
- Receive notifications when spending reaches 80% of your limit or budget

**Good to know:** View your spending limit and current balance in**Campaign Manager > Settings > sponsored ads Billing.**If you need a temporary increase in your spending limit for events like sales, contact your Amazon Sales Account Manager.
