# Create a Streaming TV campaign

Learn how to create and launch a Streaming TV campaign to promote your products on Amazon.

Updated on Apr 10, 2026

## What's Streaming TV?

Streaming TV connects your brand with new customers using Amazon's shopping and streaming data. This advertising format complements your existing Sponsored Brands, Display, and Sponsored Products campaigns. Make sure that you meet the Streaming TV eligibility requirements to participate in Streaming TV campaigns.

**Tip**: Getting familiar with audiences can help you to select the right settings for your advertising campaign. Use Cross-channel planner to explore these audiences, and understand the size of opportunity available for consumers that have engaged with your products, shopped for products similar to yours, or who should be interested in your type of products.

## Create your Streaming TV campaign

**Initial setup and campaign settings**

1. In the navigation side menu, click **Create campaign**. Then, select **Streaming TV**.
2. Give your campaign a name by clicking the **pencil** icon. This will allow you to easily find it later in the campaign manager. It won't be visible to customers. For example, "Streaming TV-Holidays-Christmas Decorations," will help you when you need to find and review your campaign performance in the campaign manager. Your campaign name shouldn’t be longer than 128 characters, and can’t contain characters (! # % & ;)
3. **Schedule**: Set the **Start**and **End**dates (or select **No end date**to avoid your campaigns from stopping and to help customers discover your brand all year round, unless you’re running seasonal campaigns.)
4. **Daily budget**: Enter the amount that you’re willing to spend on your campaign each day. Once your campaign reaches your daily budget, it will pause until the following day when the budget resets.

**Ad settings**

1. Give your ad a name by clicking the **pencil** icon. This will allow you to easily find it later in the campaign manager. It won't be visible to customers.
2. **Landing page**: Choose between **Brand Store on Amazon** or **Product detail page** **Brand Store on Amazon**: When a viewer interacts with your ad, they'll be introduced to your Brand Store page. **Product detail page**: When a viewer interacts with your ad, they'll be introduced to a product page. We recommend choosing products that perform well (for example, top performing products from existing sponsored ads campaigns). Keep in mind: Create only 1 campaign per ASIN. Don't create multiple campaigns with the same ASINs. Your product must be in stock, have at least a 4-star rating, and have a product detail page with high-quality or A+ brand content.
3. **Creative**: Upload a brief, engaging video with sound that showcases your product in action, telling a clear story about your brand to drive customer interaction. Choose between: **Add existing video**: Upload a video from your creative assets library or upload a video. **Generate video with AI** If you don't have a creative asset for your campaign and require assistance creating one, you can use our creative services to help you create it.

**Ad group settings and audience targeting**

1. Give your ad group a descriptive and meaningful name by clicking the **pencil** icon. This will allow you to easily find it later in the campaign manager. It won't be visible to customers. If you add more ad groups to your campaign, make sure that they don't have identical names.
2. **Maximum average CPM:** Enter the maximum amount that you’re willing to pay for every 1,000 impressions.
3. **Audiences:** Choose between **Dynamic** or **Manual audiences**. Each ad group will have its own in-market category segment and content-based audience. Choosing the right segments for each product can help with your consideration goals. You can choose between dynamic targeting or manual targeting to set in-market categories or interest and lifestyle audiences. **Dynamic audiences**use AI to continuously optimize targeting and reach shoppers relevant to your business. **Manual audiences**allow you to choose your own audiences or categories to target.
4. **Location:** You’ll be able to reach audiences based on their location by state, city, zip codes, and designated market area (DMA). We recommend selecting multiple geotargets to enable sufficient scale. **Radius targeting:** You can include a location via an address or point on a map, you can also set the distance from the location to set the location area.

## Submit your campaign for review

Click **Submit for review** to start the moderation review process. This process is usually completed within 24 hours, but may take up to 3 business days. We'll email you to let you know if your ad was approved or if changes need to be made.

## Monitor and adjust

After your campaign launches, monitor metrics to determine campaign performance. Performance metrics can take up to 12 hours to populate. For more detailed information on accessing and interpreting metrics, read Introduction to reports and metrics.
