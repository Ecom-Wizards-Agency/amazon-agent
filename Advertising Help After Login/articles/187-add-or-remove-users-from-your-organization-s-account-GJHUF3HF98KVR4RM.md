# Add or remove users from your organization's account

Learn how to add or remove users from your instance and organization’s account.

Updated on Feb 24, 2026

This page details the administrator tasks specific to managing your AMC account and instance. For billing and payments and working with manager accounts, refer to Account management.

As an Amazon Marketing Cloud (AMC) administrator, you are your organization's nominated system admin. You can manage your organization's AMC account and access all instances associated with the account.

You’ll have access to the user management options through the **Administration** option, represented through the gear icon on the bottom-left panel of the UI. The **Access and settings** link on the top-right of the page will also allow you to access to the user administration options.

You’re responsible for inviting and provisioning users to your organization’s AMC account and then providing the user access to one or more instances within the account.

## Invite users to your organization’s account

1. From the ads console, in the left menu, click **Administration**.
2. Select **Account access and settings**, or at the top-right of the AMC landing page, click the **Access and settings** link. The **Users and Instances** page appears.
3. Under the **Users** tab, click **Invite user**. The Invite a new user dialog appears.
4. In the **Invite new user** dialog, enter the **Name** and **Email** address of the user you’re inviting to use AMC.
5. Click **Invite user**. AMC will send this user an invitation email with instructions on how to sign up for access. After the invited user accepts the invite and the terms and conditions displayed on the first sign-in, they’re eligible to be added to an instance owned by your organization.
6. At this point, your invited user will get the “Welcome to the Amazon Marketing Cloud User Interface for {Account Name}. Please ask your administrator from {Account Name} to grant you access to one or more instances” email. After the user accepts the invite and the AMC terms and conditions, you can go and add the user to an instance. If the user doesn’t accept the invite, the user details (name and email) will appear, with a message indicating that the invitation wasn’t accepted and a link to **Resend invite**.

## Add users to your instance

1. From **Account access and settings**, click **Instances** to view all the instances associated with your organization’s account.
2. Go to the instance you wish to add a user to, then click **Add user** to include the user to the instance. **Note:** You can add users to an instance only after they’ve accepted the invitation to join your account and have agreed to the AMC terms and conditions.

## Remove users from your instance

1. From **Account access and settings**, click **Instances** to view all the instances associated with your organization’s account.
2. Locate the user you wish to remove from the instance, then click **Remove user**.

## Remove users from your organization’s account

1. From **Access and settings**, click **Users**.
2. Locate the user you wish to remove from the account, then click **Remove user**.

**Note:**If you are a sponsored ads advertiser that used AMC instant access to automatically create an AMC instance associated with your sponsored ads account, check our Add or remove users from your account. help page.
