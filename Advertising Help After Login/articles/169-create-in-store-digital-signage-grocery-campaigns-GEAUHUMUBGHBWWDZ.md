# Create in-store digital signage grocery campaigns

Learn how to create a digital signage campaign for Whole Foods Market

Updated on Oct 1, 2026

This page only lists settings **specific** to creating an ad campaign for Whole Foods Market in-store locations.

## What's a digital signage grocery campaign?

Digital signage campaigns let you reach customers through digital displays across Whole Foods Market physical retail locations, with over 7,000 screens. Learn more about grocery campaigns.

## Create a digital signage grocery campaign with these settings

To advertise with Display, Video, and Audio campaigns, you need to create a campaign, associate ad groups with the campaign, and assign ads to each ad group. Use the tabs below to view settings for each step of the setup process.

The settings below are specific to campaigns for in-store grocery. Only the unique required fields for this campaign type are listed — standard campaign settings also apply. See Create a campaign for complete instructions.

**Media**

- Type: **Display**

**Goal and optimization**

- Goal: **Awareness**
- KPI: Select from the available options that best match your campaign strategy. If the KPI you're aiming for isn't available, we recommend selecting **Reach**.
- Optimization strategy: Bidding priority:**Prioritize Full Budget, while maximizing performance** Budget management: **Automatic budget allocation**

**Commitments**

- (optional) Associate this campaign to a commitment. See Create and manage commitments to learn more.

**Budget and delivery**

- Budget and flights: Enter **budget amount**, start and end **dates**, and**flight name**
- Budget cap: Uncheck **Set a daily and/or monthly spending limit** unless you have a hard cap on daily or monthly spend
- Agency field fees: (optional)

**Conversion tracking**

Make sure you add your products under the Amazon domain or Whole Foods Market US domain.

- Products: Add your ASINs and in the Amazon domain drop-down, choose **Whole Foods Market US**
- Amazon events: (optional)
- Off-Amazon conversions: (optional)

Next, set up your ad group (formerly known as Line Item on Amazon DSP) and attach it to your campaign.

## Activate your campaign and monitor performance

Once your foundational components are set up, activate your campaign so it delivers and shows to customers.

1. In the navigation side menu, click **Campaigns**.
2. At the top of the **Campaigns** page, click the **Display, Video, and, Audio** tab.
3. In the **Ad groups** page, activate the ad groups by clicking the **toggle** in the **Active** column.
4. Activate your campaign by clicking the **toggle** at the top of the window next to the campaign name.
