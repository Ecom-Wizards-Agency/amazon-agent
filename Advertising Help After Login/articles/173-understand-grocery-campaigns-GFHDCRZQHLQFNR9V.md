# Understand grocery campaigns

Learn about creating online grocery and in-store digital singage campaigns.

Updated on Oct 1, 2026

## What are grocery campaigns?

Display, Video, and Audio campaigns can help you reach customers shopping for groceries through Amazon Fresh and Whole Foods Market. You can advertise with a grocery campaign through 2 distinct channels — online and in-store — depending on your goals and where your products are available.

- **Online grocery campaigns** are designed for brands selling products through Amazon Fresh or Whole Foods Market online. These campaigns help you reach Prime members who shop for same-day grocery delivery and pickup in select US cities. You can use Display, Streaming TV, Online Video (OLV), and Responsive eCommerce Creative (REC) ad formats. Create online grocery campaigns with Display, Video, and Audio campaigns
- **In-store digital signage campaigns** let you reach customers through digital displays across Whole Foods Market physical retail locations, with over 7,000 screens. You can influence purchase decisions at the point of purchase or build brand awareness in high-traffic areas. Create in-store digital signage grocery campaigns with Display, Video, and Audio campaigns

**Benefits of grocery campaigns**

- **Targeted reach**: Connect with Prime members actively shopping for groceries through Amazon Fresh and Whole Foods Market
- **Proper attribution**: Accurately track and measure activity using dedicated channel settings
- **Flexible delivery**: Reach customers online through delivery and pickup across Amazon Fresh and Whole Foods Market
- **Scale opportunities**: Run campaigns across multiple inventory sources, such as Amazon owned-and-operated sites and third-party (3P) exchanges, to maximize impression opportunities

- **Influence purchases where they matter most**: Category flags, skybin headers, meat/seafood screens, and deli counter displays let you reach customers at the point of purchase
- **Flexible programs**: Choose per-store, regional, or national programs
- **Quick launch**: Launch campaigns quickly with geo-targeting and dynamic ad generation

## Good to know

- Amazon Fresh is available as an online-only platform. To advertise in physical retail locations, use digital signage campaigns for Whole Foods Market.
- You can run both online grocery and digital signage campaigns simultaneously to maximize your reach across online and in-store channels.
