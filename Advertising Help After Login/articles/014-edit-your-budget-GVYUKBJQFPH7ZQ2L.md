# Edit your Sponsored ads campaign budget

Learn how to edit your budget, and how these changes impact your budget over time.

Updated on Sep 2, 2026

You can change your daily budget at any time and the adjustments will take effect immediately. If you’re using a lifetime budget, you can only increase it once your campaign is live.

## Edit your budget

1. In the navigation side menu, click **Campaigns**
2. Click the amount in the budget column and enter a new amount.

If you change your budget midmonth, your monthly campaign spend won't exceed your new daily budget multiplied by the month's remaining days.

**Example:** your daily budget is $50, and as of the 20th, you’ve spent $800. On the 20th, you change your daily budget to $10. The maximum you’ll be charged for that month will be $900. The $800 you spent so far + $100 ($10 x 10 days remaining in the month).

## Apply budget recommendations

When looking to manage and optimize your budgets, head to the Budgets page within campaign manager. You can see estimated metrics, such as estimated missed impressions, clicks, and sales, performance insights about your launched campaigns, and recommendations for how best to optimize them. To apply budget recommendations:

1. In the navigation side menu, click **Campaigns**
2. Next to the campaign name, in the**Recommended budget**column: Click **Apply** to apply budget recommendation for each campaign, or Click**Apply all** to implement all budget recommendations together.
3. Click **Export**button to export all data.

## Set a Sponsored Products budget cap

Sellers have the option to set a daily budget at the campaign level, or the account level using a Sponsored Products daily budget cap. The daily budget cap will be the maximum cumulative amount all of the Sponsored Products campaigns in your account can spend per day.

1. In the navigation side menu, click **Campaigns**
2. Click to expand the page side menu.
3. Click Settings.
4. Select Use Daily budget cap.
5. Enter the maximunm daily budget amount you want to apply to all Sponsored Products campaigns in your account.
6. Click **Save**.

This feature is only available to Sellers. If you're not a seller and looking for ways to set a daily budget across multiple campaigns, try a portfolio budget cap.
