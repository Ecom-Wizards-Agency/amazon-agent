# Understand targeting in Display campaigns

Learn how to use audiences and contextual targeting to determine who sees your Display ads.

Updated on Mar 19, 2026

**If you sell on the Amazon store**, you can use remarketing, in-market, lifestyle, and custom audiences and/or contextual targeting.

**If you don’t sell on the Amazon Store**, you can target interest/ lifestyle, and in-market audiences.

## What is targeting?

**Display** targeting uses first-party shopping, streaming, and browsing signals and insights from Amazon, Prime Video, Twitch, and IMDb to help you reach relevant consumers. Your ads may serve on and off Amazon, which includes: Amazon detail and shopping result pages, Amazon Fresh, Whole Foods Market, IMDb, Twitch, and third-party websites and apps

## How targeting works

You can use audiences to target shoppers based on their interests and shopping behavior. If you sell on the Amazon store, you can also use remarketing segments to target shoppers who have viewed or purchased your product or something similar.

With contextual targeting, you can show ads to customers who are visiting products and categories that you select and similar products. Your ads may serve on and off Amazon, which includes: Amazon detail and shopping result pages, Amazon Fresh, Whole Food Market, third-party websites and apps, and Twitch. It is available only to advertisers that sell products and services on Amazon.

Learn more about contextual targeting.

You can mix multiple categories in a single campaign to maintain broader reach. You can also utilize dynamic segments. Dynamic segments are collections of products to target. The individual products change based on performance and relevance criteria to reach customers who are likely to engage with your ads. For example, "audiences likely to be interested in your ad."

**Note:** Display campaigns that use Audiences will always exclude past customers who purchased your advertised products or similar products in the past 30-day window. Essentially, audiences who aren’t likely to purchase your products won’t see your ads, which will help you use your Display budget more efficiently.

## Optimized targeting

Use optimized targeting when...

- You’re new to Display advertising.
- You’re not sure which audiences to target (We'll match your ad to relevant customers. This includes prospecting for new customers who viewed the detail pages of or purchased products similar to yours.)

Use manual targeting when...

- You have data from past campaigns indicating what audiences produce the most impressions, clicks, and sales.
- You have specific targeting needs.
