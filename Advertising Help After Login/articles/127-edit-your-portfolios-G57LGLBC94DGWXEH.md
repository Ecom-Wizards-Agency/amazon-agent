# Edit your portfolios

Once you’ve created a portfolio, you can manage your campaigns from the portfolio view, or by selecting campaigns in the **All campaigns** view.

Updated on Feb 24, 2026

To modify your portfolio’s name, budget type, or the date range for your selected budget cap:

1. Click the name of the portfolio that you want to edit.
2. Click **Modify portfolio**.
  1. Edit the **Portfolio name** text box.
  2. Select your budget type from the drop-down, enter the budget cap amount, and choose an optional date range or budget end date.
3. Click **Save changes.**

To move campaigns to a portfolio or remove a campaign from a portfolio:

1. Navigate to **Campaign Manager** > **All Campaigns**
2. Check the box on the left to the campaign that you want to move.
3. Click **Bulk actions** and select your desired action.
4. If you want to move a campaign to a different portfolio: Click **Move to portfolio**, select the applicable portfolio where you want to move the campaign and click **Save changes.**
5. If you want to remove a campaign from a portfolio: Click **Remove from portfolio.**
6. Save changes.
