# Amazon Attribution conversion metrics

Amazon Attribution offers a number of metrics to understand the shopping journeys as customers engage and make purchases on Amazon.

Updated on Nov 21, 2025

Each Amazon Attribution conversion metric comes in 2 versions: promoted conversions that include attributed conversions for the products associated with your campaign and total conversions that encompass attributed conversions for the products associated with your campaign plus all other products in the same brand. Total conversions equal promoted conversions + brand halo conversions.

It’s important to understand where each metric fits into the shopping journey, so you can analyze the full-funnel impact of your non-Amazon marketing campaigns.

| **Customer journey** | **Campaign strategies** | **Relevant metrics** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Awareness** | Invest in growing the total audience of Amazon shoppers who are aware of your brand | Clicks |
| **Consideration** | Help maximize purchase intent by telling your brand and product story | Detail page views Add to carts |
| **Purchase** | Stay present when shoppers are ready to buy | Purchases (promoted) Sales Units sold |

We suggest analyzing your reports to optimize your campaigns. There are many factors that are unique to your campaign(s) and product(s). We don't guarantee that any of these recommendations will directly result in an optimized campaign.
