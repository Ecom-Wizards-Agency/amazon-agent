# Understand budgets in Sponsored ads

Understand how budgets function and the difference between daily and lifetime budgets.

Updated on Jul 20, 2026

## What is a budget on Amazon Ads?

A bid is how much you’ll spend per click, and your budget is how much you’re willing to spend on clicks per day. Each time a shopper clicks your ad, the bid amount will be deducted from the daily budget. Budgets are spent as quickly as possible and not paced throughout the duration of your campaign.

## Daily budget

A daily budget is the maximum amount that you’re willing to spend, on average, each day over a calendar month. You won’t necessarily spend all of your daily budget each day. If you do reach your daily budget within a day, your campaign will pause until the following day when your daily budget resets.

Daily Budget ($10) x Days in the month (30) = Monthly budget ($300)

To help you capitalize on days when more potential shoppers are viewing your ads, we use leftover funds from low traffic days to increase your budget by up to 100%. At the end of the month, you won't spend more than the daily budget you’ve set, multiplied by the number of days in that month.

**Example**: Your daily budget is $100. You may only spend $90 on Monday, but $110 on Tuesday (your daily budget + the $10 left over from Monday). This would being your total spend over 2 days to $200, which averages to $100 per day.

By default, we’ll increase your budget up to 100% on high-traffic days. If you’d like us to only increase your daily budget by 25%, you can change this in your campaign settings. (Sponsored ads > Campaigns > Settings).

## Suggested daily budget

The suggested daily budget is the minimum daily budget required for your campaign to remain active through the day. With a budget lower than the suggested one, your campaign runs the risk of going out of budget early and not surfacing your products to the shoppers. This could cause you to miss out on clicks or sales.

- Suggested daily budget is based on campaigns that have similar ASINs to yours, based on both category and price.
- We use machine-learning models of historical campaigns with similar ASINs, bids, bidding strategies, and targeting types, to determine your suggested daily budget value.
- The suggested daily budget is based on data on the moment when you’re creating your campaign. However, for seasonal events, we find relevance of products and dates based on the experience of similar campaigns who increased their budget for that special event.

## Lifetime budget

In Sponsored Brands campaigns, you can choose between a daily budget and a lifetime budget. A lifetime budget is the total amount that you’re willing to spend on a single campaign for as long as it runs. It requires an end date and a minimum $100 budget.
