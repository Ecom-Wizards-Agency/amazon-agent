# Portfolio views

Access your portfolios from 3 distinct views in the campaign manager: All campaigns, Portfolios, or Individual portfolio views.

Updated on Mar 24, 2023

## All campaigns view

From the **All campaigns** view, you can see every campaign in your account. The portfolio column shows which portfolio, if any, a campaign is assigned to. The portfolio that a campaign is assigned to doesn’t affect the performance history of individual campaigns.

To access this view, go to the campaign manager and click **All campaigns**.

## Portfolio view

In the **Portfolio** view, all your portfolios are listed with the aggregate values of the campaigns assigned to them. You can manage the budget cap and date ranges for any portfolios that have a budget date range included. Aggregate metrics in the table and chart only reflect the performance of campaigns assigned to portfolios.

To access this view, go to **Campaign Manager** and click **Portfolios**.

## Individual portfolio view

The individual portfolio view provides a detailed view of all campaigns assigned to a portfolio and any portfolio settings applied to it. Aggregate metrics in the table and chart provide a quick view of the performance of all campaigns in a portfolio.

To access this view, go to **Campaign Manager** and click the name of the individual portfolio**.**
