# Create an Amazon Brand Lift study

Learn how to create a new Amazon Brand Lift study, from accessing the setup interface to configuring settings to submission and getting the study running.

Updated on Dec 11, 2025

**Note:**We don’t recommend measuring campaigns that only target branded keywords, since customers in the exposed audience are likely to already have high awareness of your brand. Members of the control group are likely to be less aware on average, leading to results that are skewed higher.

To create an Amazon Brand Lift study:

1. In the Amazon Ads console, go to **Measurement & reporting** and select **Brand lift studies**.
2. On the **Brand lift studies** page, select **Create study**.
3. On the **New study** page, under **Settings**: (Optional) Name your study. Select a **Brand**. In most cases, the campaigns from the brand you select will be automatically selected for the study and used to determine eligibility. Select a **Benchmark category** for your brand or product. This will be used to calculate benchmark norms so you can compare your campaign’s performance against those of similar brands or products. Benchmark norms will be reported in aggregate. The selected benchmark category won’t show up in the survey text. Select a **Goal** between **Awareness** and **Consideration**: If you select **Awareness**, your survey question will be “Which of the following brands have you heard of? Select all that apply.” If you select **Consideration**, your survey question will be "Which of the following brands would you consider buying from in the future? Select all that apply." Verify that your campaigns meet the budget requirements for an Amazon Brand Lift study by reviewing the Eligibility card. Studies that meet the threshold will be monitored for up to 2 days after submission to verify feasibility. (Optional) Click **Edit** in to add or remove campaigns from your study. You can filter by campaign type or portfolio. All active Sponsored Display, Sponsored Brands, and Sponsored TV campaigns associated with your brand will be included by default. If you remove campaigns, verify that your new set of campaigns meet the requirements for a study by reviewing the Eligibility card.
4. To submit your study for review, click **Submit study**. To save as a draft to revise later, click **Save as draft**. To cancel, click **Cancel**.

After you submit your study, it will be reviewed for grammar and completeness. Studies are automatically approved or rejected after an initial monitoring period of 3 days. You should receive an email notification once your study is approved, or you can check the Status column in the Studies dashboard for the current status. Once your study is approved, it will begin measuring campaigns and issuing surveys. Learn more about the Amazon Brand Lift methodology.

**Tip:**You may edit your study at any time prior to study approval, but studies may take longer to review if you make changes. You can’t edit settings (except for adding and removing campaigns) or cancel your study once it’s approved. If your study isn’t approved, you’ll see a reason and required changes in the Studies dashboard. After making the appropriate adjustments to your study, you can re-submit it for approval.
