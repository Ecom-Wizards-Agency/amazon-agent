# Build your quiz with Product Selector

Create a quiz to help customers find relevant products.

Updated on May 13, 2026

Product Selector is an interactive tool that helps customers find their ideal products through personalized recommendations. You can use Product Selector when you create or edit your brand store. From your brand store home page, select **Add new section** and then **Product Selector**.

Here's how Product Selector works:

- Customers answer a brief quiz about their needs and preferences
- Based on each quiz answer, the products are narrowed down to meet their preferences
- Final recommendations show products matching all selected criteria

## Set up optional intro

A well-crafted welcome screen sets expectations and encourages engagement with your Product Selector. Here's what you'll need to create:

- Intro heading for your store (45 characters max)
- Optional description about your brand or products offered (70 characters max)
- Call to action text you want displayed on the start button label (20 characters max)

**Choose an optional image that:**

- Showcases your featured product category in its best light
- Uses a clean image composition
- Avoids cluttered collages or text overlays

**Example: Find your perfect camera intro header image**

## Identify featured products

Choose the products you want to promote:

1. **Choose your products.**Pick up to 50 products you want to recommend in your store.
2. **Create the quiz:**Determine the right questions to ask customers about your featured products. You can set up a maximum of 4 questions, each with 2 – 6 simple answer choices.

## How to write quiz questions

**Question best practices**

- Keep questions clear and direct, using 55 characters or less
- Focus on one topic per questions
- Start with broader questions, then narrow down
- Use consistent question patterns. Consider using "What," "How," or "Which" formats
- Avoid technical jargon or complex terms

**Answer best practices**

- Keep options mutually exclusive and clearly distinguishable
- Use consistent length for options, keeping answers 40 characters or less
- Use benefit-oriented language
- Avoid ambiguous terms

**Example: Questions and answers**

**Question 1: "What's your experience with manual camera settings?"**

- I'm still learning the basics
- I understand the exposure triangle
- I shoot in manual mode professionally
- I prefer automatic settings

**Question 2: "What shooting challenges do you face?"**

- Fast-moving subjects
- Extreme weather conditions
- Macro/close-up work
- Not sure yet

**Add optional images to support your question**

Adding images to the Product Selector is not required. We recommend using images to support complex concepts. Use high-resolution, aesthetically pleasing images to boost credibility. Avoid blurring, stretching, or unnecessary spacing to maintain visual integrity.

**Image best practices**

- Ensure clear visual distinction between buttons
- Focus on one key message per image
- Images should directly match text content and maintain clean, uncluttered compositions
- If you use an icon, keep it simple and universally recognizable

## Pair products to each answer

For each possible quiz answer, assign at least one relevant product. You can assign multiple products to one answer, but avoid having the same product assigned to several questions.

This may reduce the helpfulness of your quiz.

**Example: Mapping**

**Question 1: "What's your experience with manual camera settings?**

Select all beginner-friendly cameras (OptixPro E100, VisionPro Pocket, ActionPro Ultra).

**Question 2: "What shooting challenges do you face?"**

Select all cameras suitable for action shots (OptixPro Master, VIsionPro Pocket, ActionPro Ultra).

## Product results

When a customer finishes the quiz, the **Results** show the products that match all of their selected answers. Questions and answers that have no products in common with customer choices (and would lead to no results) are automatically hidden.

This mapping ensures customers receive accurate product recommendations based on their specific combination of answers.

If none of the recommended products are available, the tool will suggest 4 alternatives from the featured products list. If all featured products are unavailable, Product Selector will be disabled, and customers can visit Amazon’s homepage or your alternative product page.

**Example: Product Results**

If a customer selects both "I'm still learning the basics" AND "Fast-moving subjects," only products tagged with both answers (like VisionPro Pocket and Action Pro Ultra) will appear in the result.

**Set up an alternative product page**

1. Under the **Results** section, click **Edit results.**
2. Find the Brand Store page link. Here you can add an optional link to direct customers to a different store page with more products.

## Customize your design

Design how Product Selector will appear for customers:

1. Match your store’s style by adjusting several design parameters, including font, text size, alignment, background color and buttons.
2. Before publishing, use the **Preview**button in Brand Store Builder to see how the Product Selector looks to customers.
