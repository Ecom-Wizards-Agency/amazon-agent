# Add a billing profile

Learn how to add or remove billing profiles from your Amazon Ads manager account.

Updated on Mar 17, 2026

Billing profile are only available in a manager account.

Your billing profile determines your ads' eligible display locations. Each country requires its own billing profile. Your ads only appear in countries where you have an active billing profile with valid payment information. Billing profiles are available only for managed-service accounts. Learn more about Manager accounts.

Before you get started, sign in to Amazon Ads, then at the top right, click the name of your account and ensure you’re signed in to your manager account.

## Add a new billing profile

1. In the left menu, click **Administration**.
2. Click **Manager account billing dashboard**.
3. Click the name of your manager account, then at the top left, select **Billing profiles**.
4. Click **Add Billing Profile**, then add all the billing profile information. Make sure to enter the billing phone number with the format **+country code xxx xxx xxxx** (plus sign, country code, and the phone number). Keep in mind that depending on the country, you’ll be required to provide tax information.
5. Click **Running ads in Canada?**, then select the option that best applies to your ad campaign. If you're creating a Canadian Billing profile, you'll be asked to enter the GST information below.
6. Click **Save** or **Save as default**.
7. Select **Link account** to link existing managed-service accounts to this manager account.
8. Notify your account managers or account executives the address that you’d like to use for your upcoming ad campaign. This way, they can update your ad campaign within the Order Management System.

Note: All users of your manager account and all advertiser accounts that have access will be able to see the information that you add to a billing profile.

## Remove an existing billing profile

1. In the left menu, click **Administration**.
2. Click **Manager account billing dashboard**.
3. Click the name of your manager account, then at the top left, select **Billing profiles**.
4. Go to the billing profile that you wish to delete, then click **Remove**.
5. Notify your account manager or executive about the billing profile removal.
