# Creator Connections Campaign Workflow

The canonical campaign workflow is the `amazon-creator-connections` skill. Do not maintain a second tracker schema, qualification rulebook, commission recommendation, or message policy in this document.

Use these sources in order:

1. `skills/amazon-creator-connections/SKILL.md` for routing, permissions, and execution.
2. `skills/amazon-creator-connections/references/workflows.md` for campaign preparation and launch steps.
3. `skills/amazon-creator-connections/references/campaign-experiment-playbook.md` for product-specific titles, descriptions, duration, cohort design, and comparison rules.
4. `skills/amazon-creator-connections/references/tracker-schema.md` for the only supported tracker schema and status vocabulary.
5. `skills/amazon-creator-connections/references/lifecycle-execution-guardrails.md` for identity, qualification, follow-up, and MCF controls.

Campaign dates, budget, commission, discount, product selection, and claims are client decisions. Never assume a default. Prepare and validate the exact proposal, then pause before publication unless a matching current instruction or local standing permission authorizes that exact launch.
