# Internal creative evidence and assets

This reference defines the internal factual basis behind a briefing. Its historical filename is retained for compatibility. It is no longer a required editor document and is not rendered or delivered as a separate Creative Reference.

Keep one product-line/marketplace evidence packet per job, reusing verified material where current. Source facts, observed data and operator decisions remain distinguishable and dated. Do not copy internal evidence into the editor document merely because it was collected.

## Product and claim basis

Record the exact ASIN, variant, pack, size, source listing/packaging and verification time. Keep approved factual lines, claim-critical substantiation, forbidden language/imagery and scoped operator decisions with their evidence. A live listing alone does not prove an unsupported claim. A branded assignment or another scoped exception does not become general policy.

Give the writer the factual basis relevant to the task, not an entire unrelated catalogue.

## Creative research

For open concept selection, retain the shelf map, competitor claim evidence, workbook and research provenance, shopper language, cluster support, price context and reasons for the chosen buying criterion. Mark missing, partial or stale sources. For an agreed production concept, reuse supplied verified evidence and collect only what is missing for the assignment.

Keep dated performance windows and actual creative-test verdicts linked to their owning program. Do not present correlations as proof that a creative caused an outcome.

## Assets and production state

For supplied assets record:

| Asset | Verified source | Suitability and crop | Rights status | Intended use or known gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Use exact Drive/pCloud links when verified. Distinguish editor-ready footage, reference-only material and unsuitable sources. Use `not independently verified` for rights when true. Do not infer availability from a visual direction.

Only execution-relevant links and known shot requirements enter the briefing. Unknown footage availability stays explicit; it is not a reason to invent a source or request unnecessary filming.

## Claims and review record

Trace each final card, VO line and visual implication to evidence or an explicit operator decision. Resolve medium/high risk before a ready verdict. Keep independent review findings and the targeted revision internally. Record editor questions, production blockers and approved test learnings for the next job.

Historical reference Google Docs remain untouched. The single-brief builder ignores legacy reference titles, IDs and Markdown arguments; it never creates or updates those Docs.
