# Output Contract

## Client plan

Produce a concise branded document containing:

1. Executive objective and Month 1-3 commercial milestones.
2. Committed, stretch, and capacity revenue, unit, and stock outcomes when a commercial target layer is supplied.
3. Planned PPC, available ceilings, scaling gates, campaign-purpose allocation, and approved keyword direction.
4. Stock requirement and configuration split, including a safety buffer and confirmed Vine units above customer-sale inventory.
5. A concise appendix for pricing, reviews, assumptions, owners, open confirmations, and the Day 0 gate.

Label every unconfirmed item. Treat commercial targets as operating objectives, not guaranteed sales. Keep low/base/high sensitivities available in the workbook or preview when they are still useful for diagnostics.

## Workbook

Create these populated tabs:

- `Executive Summary`
- `Inputs & Sources`
- `13-Week Forecast`
- `PPC Plan`
- `Pricing & Margin`
- `Stock & Reviews`

Use formulas for linked calculations where practical. Keep assumptions editable, sources visible, and unknown values blank or `N/A`. Never substitute another client's values.

When commercial targets are supplied, include target revenue, required units, spend-supported ad demand, required non-ad demand, external halo, inventory requirement, and committed/stretch/capacity summaries.

## Delivery gate

- Verify the approved Ecom Wizards lockup or logo, palette, typography, header/footer, page numbers, and workbook title treatments.
- Render and inspect every document page and every populated workbook tab.
- For Google delivery, import the reviewed DOCX and XLSX as native Google Doc and Google Sheet.
