# Review Policy

Use only Amazon-compliant review paths.

## Allowed paths

- Amazon Vine when the product and account are eligible.
- Amazon's standard Request a Review flow.
- Helium 10 Follow-Up only to automate Amazon's standard request template, with no more than one request per order and no duplicate Seller Central request.

## Reject

- Discounts, refunds, gifts, tips, rebates, or any other incentive tied to a review.
- Review gating or asking only satisfied customers for reviews.
- Creators or affiliates purchasing in exchange for reviews.
- Attempts to disguise compensation or review intent.
- More than one review request for an order or parallel Helium 10 and Seller Central requests without deterministic deduplication.

## Runtime verification

Re-check the current rules before finalizing a plan:

- Amazon Vine: https://sell.amazon.com/programs/vine
- Helium 10 Follow-Up: https://kb.helium10.com/hc/en-us/articles/52858656680859-How-to-Automate-and-Manually-Send-Amazon-Review-Requests-in-Follow-Up

If current policy conflicts with this reference, current first-party Amazon rules control. Treat the Helium 10 page as tool-operating guidance, not Amazon policy authority.
