# Brand Tailored Promotion Plan

Use live Seller Central as the authority for available goals, audiences, ASIN scope, duration, discount range, budget minimum, eligibility, and fees.

## Default proposal

For every audience Seller Central currently marks eligible:

1. Stage one separate 15% Brand Tailored Promotion.
2. Use the longest permitted duration up to 90 calendar days.
3. Use the live platform minimum budget. A larger budget requires a documented client-approved ceiling.
4. Choose a Monday, Tuesday, or Wednesday start after approval.
5. Include only managed ASINs that are active, in stock, economically eligible for a 15% discount, hold the Featured Offer, and pass the stacking check.
6. When no ASIN is safe, record an exclusion for the audience instead of creating an empty proposal.
7. Account for every managed ASIN per proposed audience as either included or excluded with a reason.

Capture the complete live eligible-audience ID list before planning and reconcile it to the proposal rows. Do not infer eligibility from old thresholds or audience names. Record exactly what the live marketplace shows.

## Stacking check

For each candidate ASIN, inventory every active or scheduled coupon, deal, promotion, Subscribe & Save discount, quantity discount, and BTP that can overlap the proposed dates.

Calculate and record the worst-case customer discount using the live UI's stacking behavior. Exclude or flag the ASIN when the combined exposure exceeds the approved promotional level or its verified economics. Never assume two promotions do not stack merely because they appear in different consoles.

## Pre-execution refresh

Immediately before creating an approved promotion, re-check:

- audience eligibility and size;
- ASIN active/stock/Featured Offer state;
- economics and client ceiling;
- active/scheduled promotion overlap;
- permitted dates, duration, and platform minimum budget;
- correct account, brand, and marketplace.

Any change invalidates the approval fingerprint. Rebuild and re-present the batch.
