# Kabooki Germany: September 2026 evidence

This dated case explains the reusable rules in [brand-store-updates.md](brand-store-updates.md).
It is historical evidence, not a current assortment, stock list or permission.

## Product audit, 10 September

The audit inspected all 23 Store pages and corrected 22 grids in a new draft.
Outlet's verified grid remained unchanged. Sock ASINs from the September build had
been selected through retail search, title checks and Amazon's brand picker; they
did not match the client's current catalog and pictured pack ranges. Broad automated
queries also mixed gloves, rain sets, jackets and fleece products across categories.
An apparently foreign LEGO Ninjago fleece glove was verified as a Kabooki model,
so title wording could not decide eligibility.

The rain-set complaint concerned two models sharing a catalog parent. The underwear
complaint concerned duplicated pictured pack artwork; different image URLs did not
prove different artwork. Both were separate from product-grid selection. Evidence
did not establish the actor or date responsible for those defects.

## Image-link corrections, 13 September

The correction report records 18 resolved entries plus one temporarily unlinked
jacket entry, spanning 32 tiles on 8 subpages. Screenshots identified category
banners, product creatives and linked detail groups. The actual tile mapping differed
from some initial positional assumptions, which were corrected before applying edits.

Every changed tile had separate mobile artwork. Both images were retained. Full
comparison covered all 23 pages and recorded only destination/associated CTA changes;
all 254 image tiles and 23 grids remained unchanged. The source stayed live and
the new draft remained unsubmitted at that run's completion. The jacket's requested
size could not be verified; another size was not substituted. Two approved product
destinations were retained despite zero sellable stock. An explicit glove unlink
request was followed despite an available matching listing.
