# Creator Reply Playbook

Draft replies from the live thread and tracker context. Always verify the creator, product, and ASIN before drafting. Stop before sending unless the operator approved the exact send in the current chat or standing permission covers it.

## Tone

- Friendly, concise, and clear.
- Use the creator’s first name when available.
- Use short paragraphs and line breaks because Creator Connections compresses messages visually.
- Ask for missing items in one clean list.
- Never promise a free sample, payment, commission changes, or collaboration unless approved.
- Never agree to a guaranteed 5-star review or language that implies review manipulation.
- Answer product questions only from the live Amazon listing or approved product copy.
- If details are ambiguous, ask for clarification instead of guessing.

## Standard asks

### First-base verification after background check

Use when the creator has passed the visible background check and is missing basic fulfillment details. Do not ask for proof by default if the storefront/profile, recent shoppable videos, Earns Revenue badge, media kit, or public links already provide enough confidence.

```text
Hi {first name}, thanks for reaching out and for your interest in {product}.

We reviewed your creator profile and would love to confirm the remaining details for sample review.

Full name
Email address
Phone number
Complete shipping address
Confirmed product + ASIN

We’re prioritizing creators who provide complete details and have a clear product match, so this helps us review properly on our side.
```

### ASIN/product confirmation

Use for undecided creators or product switches.

```text
Hi {first name}, thanks for reaching out.

Before we can review this for a sample, could you please confirm the exact {brand} product and ASIN you’d like to feature?

We’re prioritizing creators who are firm with their product match, since we want to make sure the right sample is sent and the final Amazon content tags the correct product.

Please also send any remaining details we need in one message:

Full name
Email address
Phone number
Complete shipping address
Confirmed product + ASIN

Once those are complete, we can review properly on our side.
```

### Proof request only when background check is incomplete

Use only when visible storefront/profile checks cannot verify enough activity, performance, or fit.

```text
Hi {first name}, thanks for sending this over.

To complete the review on our side, could you also send a recent Amazon video link or performance proof from your storefront?

We prioritize creators with active shoppable content and visible performance, so this helps us confirm fit before sample approval.
```

### Product switch clarification

Use when the creator joined one campaign but asks for another product.

```text
Hi {first name}, thanks for clarifying.

Just to make sure we send the correct sample and track the right product, could you confirm your final preferred product and ASIN?

Once confirmed, we’ll review under that product instead of the original campaign.
```

### Exact ASIN unavailable for MCF

Use only after the original ASIN's MCF blocker is captured and the proposed alternative has passed `preflight-switch --phase offer`. Never list an unverified alternative.

```text
Hi {first name}, thank you again for your patience.

The {original product} option you selected (ASIN {original ASIN}) is currently unavailable for sample fulfillment through our shipping system.

We do have {alternate product} (ASIN {alternate ASIN}) available. Would you be happy to switch to this ASIN?

Please reply with {alternate ASIN} to confirm. Once confirmed, we can prepare your sample.

Thank you!
```

### Recipient mismatch clarification

Use when shipping recipient and content creator appear different.

```text
Hi {first name}, thanks again for the details.

Before we move forward, we just want to confirm the shipping details so we don’t send the sample to the wrong person.

Could you please confirm that {recipient name} is the authorized recipient for this sample?

Please also confirm that the final Amazon content will be posted under your storefront and will tag {product/ASIN}.

If possible, please send the best phone number to use for shipment details.
```

## Sample confirmation

Only send after MCF/order is confirmed.

```text
Hi {first name}, thanks again for sending your complete details.

Your {product} sample is now on the way.

Once your video is live, please send the link here so we can track the performance on our end.

Thanks again for collaborating with {brand}.
```

## Awaiting content follow-up

Use after sample has arrived or should have arrived.

```text
Hi {first name}, hope you’re doing well.

Just checking in on the {product} sample. If your Amazon video is already live, we’d appreciate it if you could send the link here so we can track the performance on our side.
```

## Content posted thank-you

Use when a creator posts a positive/glowing review.

```text
Hi {first name}, thank you again for choosing {brand} and for the wonderful video.

We really appreciate the way you featured the product, and we’ll continue to track the performance on our side.

Thanks again for collaborating with us.
```

## Creator performance update thank-you

Use when a creator reports sales/revenue generated.

```text
Hi {first name}, thank you again for collaborating with {brand} and for sharing this update.

We appreciate you taking the time to send the performance details. We’re tracking the performance from our side as well, and this is helpful context for the campaign.

Thanks again for choosing {brand}.
```

## Paused product

Use when creators ask about a product/campaign that is paused until further notice. Keep the reason vague unless the operator approves more detail.

```text
Hi {first name}, thank you for considering working with {brand}.

At the moment, we’re unable to accommodate {product} sample requests until further notice due to a current product availability issue.

We appreciate your interest and will keep this in mind if the product becomes available again.
```

## Do not draft/send

Escalate to the operator before drafting if:

- payment/legal language appears
- creator asks for off-platform arrangement
- creator requests guaranteed review terms
- product claims would require medical/compliance judgment
- thread is inactive/closed/declined
- product/ASIN is unclear and prior messages already asked for it
- creator has spam/high-risk pattern
