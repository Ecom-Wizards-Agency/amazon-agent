# Appeal writing posture

Operator-approved preference, 11 September 2026. Apply to Amazon appeals, product-condition disputes and responses to rejected appeals. This is a writing preference, not evidence that a particular appeal was accepted.

## Defend the position with facts

Write firmly, concisely and without unnecessary concessions. Lead with the checks performed, their findings and the seller's existing controls. Give Amazon a complete answer to its stated request without introducing unrelated weaknesses, speculative defects or additional commitments. Provide further relevant information when requested; include evidence already required in the current response.

- Answer directly in the existing case. After the greeting, state what was reviewed and what the response covers. Skip generic thanks and repeated case history.
- Match Amazon's requested structure. When it asks for reported problems, cause, corrective/preventive actions and inventory confirmation, address all four. An evidence request alone is not a complete appeal.
- Describe the actual complaint narrowly. A damaged can does not establish a defective formulation, unsafe product or systematic packaging failure.
- Reuse verified existing controls, such as factory checks for every production batch and warehouse inspections. Do not imply those controls were newly introduced or promise packaging changes, removals or redesigns unless supported or agreed.
- Avoid volunteering speculative admissions, generic apologies, defensive disclaimers or statements such as "we cannot control how Amazon handles it." State the operational facts and scope of inspection instead.
- Separate a confirmed cause from a plausible explanation. If handling or delivery damage is only an assessment, use "may have" or "our assessment" rather than blame Amazon or state it as proven. If Amazon requires a confirmed cause and it remains missing, flag that gap to the operator; stronger wording does not supply the missing fact.
- Use the operator's explicit factual confirmations. A warehouse inspection confirmation can support a statement about the inspected warehouse stock. Do not expand it to all FBA stock, every batch, shipping condition at an earlier date or unmentioned tests. Do not invent inspection dates, quantities, photographs, reports or attachments.
- Keep internal uncertainty analysis and drafting notes out of the outbound response unless needed to answer accurately. Do not remove material qualifications or omit requested facts to create a misleading impression. No wording guarantees that Amazon will accept an appeal or refrain from further questions.
- Once the operator accepts wording, preserve it for revisions and additional questions. Change only the requested passages or necessary factual corrections. Saving a draft is not submission or proof of Amazon acceptance. Resolve the actual approved signature before sending.

## Product-condition response pattern

Use only the statements supported in the current case. The inspection statements below are conditional examples, not reusable product facts.

**Opening:** "We have reviewed the reported damage to the product can and inspected our remaining warehouse stock. Below are our findings, inventory confirmation, and corrective and preventive actions for ASIN [ASIN]."

1. **Reported problems:** State the customer's damage complaint, including scratches or dents where reported.
2. **Root cause:** Give the established cause and its basis. If only a handling or delivery hypothesis is supported, identify it as an assessment, not a confirmed finding. A description of the damage alone does not establish its cause.
3. **Corrective and preventive actions:** Describe confirmed factory and warehouse inspection controls, relevant findings, and agreed handling of damaged units. Distinguish existing controls, completed corrective actions and future commitments.
4. **Warehouse inventory confirmation:** "We confirm that our warehouse has inspected the remaining inventory for ASIN [ASIN]. The inspected units are free from damage, scratches and dents, and each product is properly packaged." Add "No packaging changes have been made" only when confirmed. Address FBA inventory separately if Amazon requests it.

Close with "Best regards," and the verified case owner's approved full name. Keep client-specific accepted examples in the private case evidence folder, not in the shared skill source.
