# Creator Connections Operating Playbook

## Daily sweep

Use this when the operator says:

- "Do a Creator Connections sweep."
- "Check new inquiries."
- "Run the weekly audit."
- "Who is ready for samples?"
- "Who posted?"

Daily sweep steps:

1. Confirm the active brand, marketplace, and Creator Connections account.
2. Open each active campaign inbox.
3. Check only new or changed threads since the last sweep.
4. Update the tracker with:
   - New inquiries
   - Creator replies
   - Full name, email, phone, and address when provided
   - Requested ASIN
   - Product match status
   - Storefront link or proof links
   - Qualification gate fields
   - Current status
   - Notes and next action
5. Check whether sample recipients posted content or shared video links.
6. Stage a Slack thread update with new changes only.
7. Stop before sending messages unless the operator already approved the exact send.

## Campaign launch workflow

Use this when the operator provides a product or ASIN and asks to launch a Creator Connections campaign.

Launch steps:

1. Confirm the brand, product, ASIN, commission range, campaign dates, and naming convention.
2. Pull product facts from the Amazon listing or approved product details.
3. Draft a product-matched campaign description.
4. Prepare the Creator Connections campaign in the correct brand folder.
5. Stop before final launch for operator approval.
6. After launch, create or update the campaign tracker tab.
7. Add the campaign link in the campaign details area only once.

Default commission guidance:

- Creator Connections campaigns usually use a 15 percent to 20 percent commission structure unless the operator or brand gives a different rule.

## Sample approval workflow

Before a sample can move forward, verify:

- Creator is a strong match for the product.
- Requested ASIN is final.
- Full name is provided.
- Complete shipping address is provided.
- Email is provided when available.
- Phone number is provided when available.
- Storefront or shoppable content can be checked.
- Recent posting activity is visible.
- Performance proof or revenue evidence is available when needed.
- Spam risk is low.

Recommended sample threshold:

- Aim for 10 out of 10 when possible.
- If the creator is below 10, ask only for the missing items.
- Do not make the message sound demanding. Keep it collaborative and clear.

## MCF sample workflow

The agent can prepare details for the operator, but should stop before order creation unless the operator explicitly approves.

MCF prep fields:

- Full name
- Street address
- City
- State or province
- ZIP or postal code
- Phone number
- Email address
- ASIN
- SKU
- Units
- Custom order ID
- Shipping speed
- Estimated fee

After the operator confirms the MCF order:

1. Update the tracker with sample status, order ID, cost, and estimated delivery.
2. Send or draft the standard sample confirmation message.
3. Move the creator to awaiting content.
4. Add a follow-up date.

## Product switches

If a creator switches products:

1. Confirm the final ASIN before sample approval.
2. Move the active tracking row to the final product tracker only after confirmation.
3. Mark the old row as on hold or do not send if it must remain for history.
4. Note the original product and final product in the notes.

If the creator is undecided:

- Do not move them to the final product tracker yet.
- Keep them in the undecided holding area until they confirm.
