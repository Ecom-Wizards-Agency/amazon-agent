# Express Payout Bank Account and Debit Card Policies

“You” or “Your” refers to the seller accepting this Agreement. Express Payout is a payment program for US sellers that delivers payout deposits to eligible bank accounts and debit cards within 24 hours, instead of waiting the standard 3-5 business days for Your money to clear the Automated Clearing House (ACH) payment network. Express Payout is active by default for all eligible US sellers at no cost. No enrollment is required.

Your use of Express Payout is subject to the terms and conditions of Express Payout and those governing the sale of Your products in the Amazon store (the “Selling Partner Agreement,” which includes, for example, the Amazon Services Business Solutions Agreement). If there is a conflict between the Express Payout terms and conditions and the Selling Partner Agreement, the terms of the Express Payout terms and conditions take precedence.

## Section 1: Express Payout (Bank Account) Policy

Express Payout (Bank Account) enables payout deposit within 24-hour of available seller funds into a valid, seller-designated bank account.

**Eligibility**

To be eligible for Express Payout (Bank Account), You must:

- Have an account with an in-network U.S. bank routing number.
- Have a business address in one of the 50 U.S. states. Sellers in U.S. territories are ineligible.
- Have payouts of $10MM or below at the time of the payment initiation.

If You meet all of the above requirements, Express Payout (Bank Account) will be active on Your account by default. No enrollment or fee will be required. If Your bank does not yet support instant transfers, Your payouts continue via standard ACH (3-5 business days). Amazon re-checks eligibility periodically and will notify You when Express Payout activates on Your account.

Amazon may deny any Express Payout (Bank Account) transactions involving suspicious or fraudulent activity or if a receiving financial institution rejects the transaction.

Our Express Payout (Bank Account) financial services partners will reject any transactions exceeding $10MM. To avoid failure, we will automatically send these transactions via the standard ACH system. Partial payouts of Your balances are prohibited

Amazon reserves its discretionary authority to revoke Your use of the Express Payout at any time.

**Getting Started**

Express Payout (Bank Account) is active by default for eligible accounts. If You prefer standard ACH delivery for any reason, contact Seller Support to request the change.

You can use Express Payout (Bank Account) for eligible payouts at any time except during scheduled maintenance periods.

Once Express Payout is active, all eligible payouts will be processed in the 24-hour period until Express Payout is deactivated.

If You change Your default bank account, Amazon automatically checks whether the new bank supports instant transfers. If eligible, Express Payout activates on the new bank account. No manual re-enrollment is required. If the new bank does not support instant transfers, payouts are delivered via standard ACH until the bank adds support.

To learn more, go to Express Payout FAQs.

## Section 2: Express Payout (Debit Card) Policy

Express Payout enables 24-hour disbursements of available seller funds into a valid, seller-designated Mastercard or Visa debit card account.

**Eligibility**

To be eligible for Express Payout (Debit Card), You must have a Mastercard or Visa debit card that is issued by a U.S. financial institution and registered in Your Seller Central account. Express Payout (Debit Card) is active by default for all sellers with eligible debit cards. No enrollment is required. You must also designate an active bank account for any payouts ineligible for Your Mastercard or Visa debit card.

Express Payout will abide to these enforced payment maximums in USD:

- $50K per payout from US Amazon store.
- $250K total daily deposits, per eligible Visa debit card.
- $600K total weekly deposits, per eligible Visa debit card.
- $1.25MM total monthly deposits, per eligible Visa debit card.

Above limits are reset each day at midnight Coordinated Universal Time Zone (12 a.m. UTC). These limits apply to all deposits made to a Mastercard or Visa debit card, including those from entities outside of Amazon.

Any payout that exceeds the debit card limits or those made to expired debit cards will automatically be deposited in its entirety in the bank account You designated in Your Seller Central account. Unless Your active bank account assigned in Your Seller Central account is eligible for Express Payout, payout will follow the standard ACH protocol, which could take between 3-5 business days for You to receive the payout.

Amazon will not charge a fee for Express Payout (Debit Card) to US-issued debit cards. For UK-issued Visa debit cards, Amazon does not charge a service fee. However, the financial institutions that process these transactions may charge You currency conversion fees along with the foreign exchange rate.

**Getting Started**

Express Payout (Debit Card) is active by default for sellers with eligible debit cards registered in Seller Central. To deactivate, replace Your debit card with a bank account as Your default deposit method in Seller Central.

If Express Payout (Debit Card) is active, all eligible payouts will be deposited into Your bank account associated with the Mastercard or Visa Debit Card within 24 hours of payout initiation.

You can use Express Payout (Debit Card) for eligible payouts at any time. Once Express Payout (Debit Card) is active, all future eligible payouts will be processed via the Express Payout until You change Your deposit method to a bank account.

To learn more, go to Express Payout FAQs and for more information on UK payouts, go to Express Payout (UK Visa Debit Card) service terms and conditions.
