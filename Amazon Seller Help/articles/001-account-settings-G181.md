# Account settings

You can manage all of your account information like payment information, business information, shipping and return information, and tax information in account settings. If you are new to selling on Amazon, see What you need to know to sell on Amazon.

- Go to Account Info to access your account settings. For more information, go to Add, view, and update your bank account information.
- If you want to temporarily deactivate your listings, see Listing status for vacations, holidays, and other absences.
- If you want to permanently close your account, see Close your seller account.

For an introduction to Seller Central, watch this Seller University video.
