# Manage All Inventory

The Manage All Inventory tool is a central hub for managing your product listings and reviewing performance details. Use this tool to search for products, update listing information, resolve issues with inactive listings, and improve listing quality all from a single location.

## Search and filter listings

Find specific listings using the following search and filter options, such as:

- Search by SKU, product name, or ASIN to locate a specific product.
- Filter by listing status such as active or inactive to review product availability.
- Sort by category such as date created, units sold, or price range to organize your inventory.

## Manage your listings

Use the following views to optimize your listings:

- **Complete drafts**: Find and complete your draft listings.
- **Activate listings**: Fix inactive or suppressed listings, resolve pricing issues, update out-of-stock items, and find items that are not appearing in search.
- **Enhance listings**: Improve product visibility by adding important attributes and using bulk optimization tools to enhance multiple listings at once. Specific suggestions for improving your listings appear in this view.

Once you identify the product, you can edit basic information about the listing, such as price and quantity, by clicking **Edit** on the right side of each listing information.

You can also add product images. For more information, go to Manage your offers one at a time.

Here are some more important pages: List Products for Fulfillment by Amazon, Match low price, or Stop selling a product. For more information on adding new products, go to Add one product at a time.

For details on the product variation on how to create and manage variations, go Create and manage product variations page. Additionally, on the Manage inventory page, you can edit multiple listing information in bulk. For more details, go to Manage your offers in bulk.
