# Protect your brand with Transparency

Brands can use Transparency to prevent counterfeit products from reaching customers, engage buyers after purchase, and optimize supply chains.

Products enrolled in Transparency have unique serial codes, called Transparency codes, that proactively protect your brand and ensure that every item shipped to buyers is authentic. Whether they’re fulfilled by us or shipped directly by you, products that are enrolled in Transparency can’t be sold in Amazon stores without a valid code.

Transparency codes can also help you engage with customers after they’ve bought your product. Both the Amazon Shopping app and the Transparency app allow buyers to scan Transparency codes to confirm the product's authenticity and to access brand-provided materials, such as videos or product instructions.

When you use Transparency codes, you can gain additional insight into your supply chain and your products at the batch or lot level, which can help to identify issues, diagnose root causes, and implement solutions.

## How Transparency works

After you enroll your products in Transparency, you’ll label every unit with a unique code. The code is scanned to verify the unit’s authenticity. Items without a valid code are put aside for further investigation to ensure that only authentic items are delivered to buyers.

If you currently serialize your own products, you can enable Transparency protections using your existing codes (English only).

To learn more about Transparency and check your eligibility, go to Transparency .

If you’re already enrolled in Transparency, you can learn more about the program by going to Transparency help.
