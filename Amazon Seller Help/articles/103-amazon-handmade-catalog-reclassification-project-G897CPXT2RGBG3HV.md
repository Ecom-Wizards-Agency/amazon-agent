# Amazon Handmade catalog reclassification project

Between **August, 2025,** and **October 31, 2025**, we’ll be reclassifying all Handmade products from the general “GUILD” product types to more specific standard product types, such as SANDALS or NECKLACE. **If you have existing Handmade products under GUILD product types, you have to take action after the reclassification to update your products and ensure they remain searchable.**

Your products will still belong to the Handmade category, but changing to standard product types will increase their visibility in customer searches.

We recommend that after August 4, 2025, you don’t update any of your catalog’s attributes:category, product type, item type keyword, program participation, handmade classification, or department to ensure we reclassify your products correctly.

After your catalog of Handmade products has been reclassified, we’ll reach out to you via email to remind you to validate new product types and related item type keywords, assign appropriate Handmade classification attributes, and ensure all required attributes are complete to keep your products searchable.

Reclassifying Handmade products has been a multi-year project for Amazon. You can read more about this project here, and in the summary below.

## Action items after reclassification

Once the Amazon Handmade team has reclassified your ASINs (pending email confirmation no later than October 31, 2025), complete the following actions as soon as possible:

1. Validate that the newly assigned product types and related item type keywords are correct. If you sell Softlines products (such as, apparel, footwear), you will also need to validate that the department attribute is correct.
2. Assign a Handmade classification attribute to your listings to maintain your product's Handmade designation.
3. As part of the reclassification, all Handmade classification attributes will default to “unclassified". You will then select the most appropriate designation for each of your products (such as, hand designed, upcycled).
4. Ensure all required attributes for your ASINs with newly assigned product types are complete. A missing required attribute could prevent your ASINs from being buyable.
5. Each product type has specific required attributes that help customers find and compare products. To ensure your listing remains buyable, review and complete all required attributes.
6. You can learn about which attributes are required for each category in this help article: Product type and attributes.

Note that these are self-service actions. To help you identify which products need attention, you can perform a self-audit by downloading your Category Listing Report and validating if the product type attribute is no longer displaying “GUILD” and your Handmade Classification attribute is set to “unclassified”.

To help you complete these actions, we have created a How-To PDF and webinars on both Individual Listings and Bulk Listings with step-by-step instructions.

## Frequently Asked Questions

#### Why is Amazon reclassifying Handmade products?

Reclassifying Handmade products into standard product types enables the following features:

- Handmade products will appear when customers search and browse in standard categories. This is one of the top feature requests from our Handmade community. As a reminder, Handmade products are already discoverable in all product searches.
- Better discoverability from refined product searches.
- Handmade products will receive enhanced detail page content, including larger product images and category-specific detail page features.
- Handmade products will appear in Seller Central’s Listing Quality Dashboard (LQD), which you can use to improve your Handmade products’ listing quality.
- Ability to list a single ASIN in eight marketplaces.

#### Which standard product types will Handmade products be reclassified into?

Handmade products will be reclassified into hundreds of more granular product types. For example, Handmade sandals will be reclassified from the GUILD_SHOES product type to the SANDAL product type; Handmade necklaces will be reclassified from the GUILD_JEWELRY product type to the NECKLACE product type; Handmade pillows will be reclassified from the GUILD_HOME product type to the PILLOW product type; and so on.

#### What attributes are being updated?

We will be updating the following attributes for all Handmade sellers on their behalf: product type, item type keyword, and Handmade classification attribute. For Softlines categories (such as, apparel, footwear) we will also be updating the department attribute.

#### When is my catalog going to be reclassified?

Your catalog will be reclassified as soon as August 4, 2025 and will be completed by October 31, 2025. Note that some ASINs may be reclassified earlier than others within your catalog. Allow time for reclassification to be completed. We will send you an email confirmation no later than October 31, 2025.

#### What actions do I need to take if my product type and item type keyword are incorrect?

When auditing your product, if you identify an ASIN with an incorrect product type or item type keyword, contact Selling Partner Support with the correct inputs for these attributes.

Amazon's Seller Assistant can provide help with this topic. For most language settings, the Seller Assistant can also directly connect you to Selling Partner Support.

#### How can I tell if my catalog has been reclassified?

You will receive a catalog reclassification confirmation email by October 31, 2025. If you don't receive this email, you can verify reclassification by checking your catalog for two changes: 1) your ASINs' product type will no longer display "GUILD," and 2) your Handmade classification attribute will be set to "unclassified".

#### Will my ASINs be buyable during reclassification?

Products should remain buyable to customers throughout the reclassification process. However, once reclassified, ASINs may become unbuyable until all required attributes for their new product type are completed. Required attributes are essential for customer discovery, buying decisions, and safety compliance. After receiving your reclassification confirmation email, ensure you update any missing fields that are required promptly (see Action Item #3 above). If any unexpected delays occur for reclassification, we will notify you via email with a revised completion date. Should you experience any buyability issues after receiving confirmation (no later than October 31), contact Selling Partner Support for assistance.

Amazon's Seller Assistant can provide help with this topic. For most language settings, the Seller Assistant can also directly connect you to Selling Partner Support.

#### Will my products remain in the Handmade category?

Yes, your products will remain in Amazon Handmade category as long as the Handmade classification attribute is assigned (see Action Item #2 above).

#### Will I lose my reviews and ratings?

No, your existing sales history, ratings, and reviews will be retained.

#### Will this change my referral fees?

No, there will be no impact to your Handmade product fees.

#### Can I opt out of reclassification?

No, all of the Handmade selection is scheduled to be reclassified in 2025.

#### After reclassification is complete, what happens if I don’t follow the action items outlined above?

If you don't review and update your reclassified ASINs, your products may be listed in incorrect categories, making them harder for customers to find. Additionally, missing required attributes for your new product type could result in your items becoming unbuyable. Each product type has specific attribute requirements that help with customer discovery, inform purchase decisions, and ensure safety compliance. After receiving confirmation from Handmade that your ASINs have been reclassified, we urge you to promptly complete any missing fields and fill in all necessary attributes. This step is crucial for maintaining optimal product visibility and optimizing sales potential.

#### What other resources regarding reclassification are available?

For additional assistance, refer to the How-To PDF and webinars on both Individual Listings and Bulk Listings, or contact Amazon Selling Partner Support.
