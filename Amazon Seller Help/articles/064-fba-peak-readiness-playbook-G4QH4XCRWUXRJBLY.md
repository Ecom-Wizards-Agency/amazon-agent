# Q4 2026 peak readiness playbook

During the holiday shopping season, we value our partnership with independent sellers and the crucial role you play in providing great selection and delighting customers every day.

As we prepare for a successful holiday season together in 2026, we asked our experts for tool recommendations and tips that can help grow your business during the holidays. Use this playbook to find important information such as timelines and best practices for inventory management, shipping, and storage.

For more information, go to the Prepare for Amazon’s high-value sales events guide.

**Timelines**

Here are the key dates you need to know to plan ahead. To maximize your sales potential this peak season, ensure your Fulfillment by Amazon (FBA) inventory arrives at our fulfillment centers on time .

**Events:**

- Prime Big Deal Days
- Black Friday Week and Cyber Monday
- Christmas

## Send your inventory

To maximize your sales potential and ensure your products are Prime badge-ready, your Fulfillment by Amazon (FBA) inventory must arrive at our fulfillment centers by the following dates.

**Prime Big Deal Days Event:**

- **September 2:** Amazon Warehousing and Distribution (AWD) shipments
- **September 9:** FBA "minimal shipment splits" shipments
- **September 16:** FBA "Amazon-optimized shipment splits" shipments

**Black Friday Week and Cyber Monday Event:**

- **October 14:** AWD shipments
- **October 21:** FBA "minimal shipment splits" shipments
- **October 28:** FBA "Amazon-optimized shipment splits" shipments

As we enter the holiday season, our fulfillment center teams will focus on receiving shipments in September and October, then shift to processing customer orders in November and December. This temporary shift ensures faster delivery speeds for customers and maximizes your sales potential during the holidays. During this time, inbound delivery window slots will be limited, and you may see lower estimated capacity limits for October and November.

If you're expecting higher holiday volume, consider using Amazon Warehousing and Distribution (AWD) to store extra inventory at lower rates and automatically replenish fulfillment centers as needed. You can also use Capacity Manager to bid for extra space in our fulfillment centers.

For more information, go to AWD help page and Capacity Manager help page.

## Holiday peak fulfillment fees

Consistent with previous years, holiday peak fulfillment fees for FBA, Remote Fulfillment with FBA, Multi-Channel Fulfillment (MCF), and Buy with Prime will apply from October 15, 2026, to January 14, 2027.

On average, the rates for holiday peak fulfillment fees will have the same per unit increase over non-peak rates as last year. The fuel and logistics-related surcharge will apply to these fees.

Peak rates for FBA and Remote Fulfillment with FBA are now available in the Revenue Calculator and Fee and Economics Preview Report so you can compare costs across fulfillment types. You can also use the Profit Analytics dashboard to get a detailed view of your unit economics, including the ability to see how fee changes impact each of your products.

For more information, including detailed rates by size and weight, go to FBA fee rates, Remote Fulfillment by FBA fee rates for Mexico and Canada, MCF rates, and Buy with Prime rates.

## Create a promotion

Now is the time to list your new Q3 and Q4 selection. Listing selection early can help with building search indexing, accumulating early reviews, and ensuring your products are buyable ahead of key Q3/Q4 shopping events.

Promotions sourcing for Prime Big Deal Days, Black Friday Week, and Cyber Monday events is now open for most deal types. Offering high quality promotions on products that rarely get deals, and exclusive-to-Amazon offers can help boost your sales. For more information, go to the Which Amazon promotion is right for me? guide.

Prime Big Deal Days promotional prices are excluded from the 30-day and 60-day lookback window and won’t affect the maximum deal price required for Black Friday Week and Cyber Monday deals.

The set-up fees will be charged upfront, regardless of whether you sell any units while the item is on discount, unless you or Amazon cancels it before the start time. You'll have a chance to review the fees before creating your discount in Seller Central. After your promotion ends, the fee will appear as a "Promotion fee" in your Payments report after a 10-day reconciliation period.

**Tip:**Submit deals early! Receive $50 off the upfront fee per deal if you schedule your Best Deal or Lightning Deal by August 5, 2026, for Prime Big Deal Days and by September 5, 2026, for Black Friday Week and Cyber Monday.

Available promotion types include:

**Best Deals:** Showcase your top-selling products across the Amazon deals page, with additional visibility through event page merchandising, homepage placement, and event badging.

- Minimum 15% discount off the ASIN reference price.
- Must be priced at or below the lowest sales price over the past 60 days.
- Must offer at least 5% off the lowest sales price over the past 30 days.
- Discounts of 30% off or more are eligible for additional merchandising during the events.
- $100 per Best Deal plus 1.5% of sales (capped at $5,000).
- Submissions for Prime Big Deal Days are open and close September 22.
- Submissions for Black Friday Week and Cyber Monday are open and close October 20.

**Lightning Deals:** Offer time-limited discounts that run for 12 hours during main event days, featuring prominent placement on the Amazon deals page plus event page merchandising, homepage features, and event badging.

- Minimum 20% discount off ASIN reference price.
- Must be priced at or below the lowest sales price over the past 60 days.
- Must offer at least 5% off the lowest sales price over the past 30 days.
- $100 per Lightning Deal plus 1.5% of sales (capped at $5,000).
- Submissions for Prime Big Deal Days are open and close September 22.
- Submissions for Black Friday Week and Cyber Monday are open and close October 20.

To confirm schedule availability and create Deals in Seller Central, go to **Advertising** and select Deals. To learn more about the process, go to How to create a deal video.

**Note:**All deals created for Prime Big Deal Days will be made 'Prime-Exclusive' for Prime customers.

**Prime Exclusive Price Discounts (PED):** Exclusive discounts for Prime members, highlighted with event badging in search results, product pages, and cart. Available for Prime-eligible FBA products and qualifying seller-fulfilled items.

- Minimum 15% discount off ASIN reference price.
- Must be priced at or below the lowest sales price over the past 60 days.
- Must offer at least 5% off the lowest sales price over the past 30 days.
- $100 per campaign plus 1.5% of sales (capped at $5,000).
- Submissions for Prime Big Deal Days and Black Friday Week/Cyber Monday are open on July 20.

To create PEDs in Seller Central, go to **Advertising** and select Price Discounts. Click **Create a price discount** and select **Events** from the drop-down menu to complete the form. Proceed to Step 2: Add products and complete the workflow.

To learn more about the process, see the Create a Price Discount guide.

**Coupons:** Point-of-sale discounts with automated merchandising across the event page, search results, and product pages. Available for single products or product sets.

- Minimum 5% discount.
- $5 per coupon plus 2.5% of sales (capped at $2,000).
- Submissions will open once event dates are publicly announced.

To learn more about the process, go to the Create and manage coupons guide.

## Send your shipments to Amazon

To help meet customer demand and drive sales during the holiday season, we aim to store inventory closer to customers so that your products can be delivered faster. You can help facilitate this process when you send shipments in early and provide tracking information so we can prepare to receive your inventory.

## Send your shipments in early

- To help ensure you have the right inventory levels for the busy holiday season, we recommend sending your shipments to us in **August** and **September**.
- To allow time for transportation to our fulfillment centers, we recommend that you schedule your delivery appointment at least 7 days before the dates listed above. Once your delivery dates are finalized, be sure to update the delivery window for your shipments to minimize receiving delays.
- If you use the Amazon Partnered Carrier program, schedule your carrier pickup 14 days before the dates listed above.
- When planning holiday shipments, book your carrier service well ahead of time to account for potential supply chain disruptions like supplier delays, carrier issues, or customs delays. Big events like port closures can also cause issues.
- Use Send to Amazon to save time when you create shipments to replenish your FBA inventory. Be mindful of issues with your shipments that can slow down how quickly we receive your products or cause delays in order fulfillment. Check the Shipment Performance dashboard to keep an eye on how well your shipments to us are performing. To learn more, go to the Intro to Send to Amazon video.
- Amazon Warehousing and Distribution (AWD) is a great option to send your shipments in early. When you store bulk inventory with AWD, we’ll automatically replenish your products to fulfillment centers as needed to help you maintain the right inventory levels. To learn more, go to the Introduction to Amazon Warehousing and Distribution (AWD) video
- Simplify your supply chain by using Amazon Global Logistics (AGL) and Seller Export and Delivery (SEND) to transport your inventory directly from China to Amazon fulfillment centers in the US, UK, and Europe – all in one integrated service. To get started, go to our US onboarding guide, EU and UK onboarding guide, and booking guide.

## Avoid shipment delays

To help reduce receive times and ensure your products are available for customer sales and fast delivery before holiday peak season, we recommend the following actions:

- Ensure your delivery windows and tracking IDs are accurate when you schedule your inbound shipments. Accurate tracking information and adherence to scheduled delivery windows helps reduce wait times, prevents unexpected congestion at our fulfillment centers, and enables faster processing of your inventory. Shipments arriving within their scheduled window will receive priority processing, while shipments arriving outside of their scheduled delivery windows may face appointment and receive delays.
- Use the Amazon Partnered Carrier Program and Amazon Freight for small-parcel, less-than-truckload, and full-truckload shipments, or use eligible ShipTrack carriers for shipments from China. We can work directly with the partner carrier to redirect freight after destinations are assigned to help you avoid longer receive times.
- If you ship with carriers that aren’t partnered with Amazon, make sure to provide an accurate estimated delivery window in the tracking details step via Create shipments with Send to Amazon. This helps us receive and route your products to the right fulfillment centers faster. If you expect delays, you can update the delivery window before it starts.
- Consolidate your deal inventory in dedicated boxes, separate from non-deal inventory. When deal inventory is packed together rather than randomized with regular inventory, we can process these high-priority items more efficiently and ensure they're available for your promotional events.
- Get the most out of your deal events by allowing sufficient lead time between your inventory shipments and deal start dates. This helps ensure your promotional inventory is fully processed and available when customers are actively shopping for deals. For tips on specific deal types for the event, go to the Create a promotion section.
- Make sure to give carriers the right destination address for your shipment. We start to prepare to receive your inventory when you create a shipping plan, and when shipments are sent to the wrong place, or sent as incomplete shipments, it can cause delays. See our policies for details on Deleted, misrouted, and incomplete shipments and learn more about Inbound defect fees.
- Provide tracking information like carrier name and shipping method in the tracking details section of your Create shipments with Send to Amazon workflow. This helps us plan and receive your inventory faster, which minimizes delays and makes in-transit inventory available sooner for sale to customers.
- Select **night or weekend deliveries** to increase your chances of securing earlier appointments and spread out demand. If you’re shipping through a non-partnered carrier, contact your carrier to find out if earlier appointments are available in Carrier Central. Book inbound appointments during off-peak hours and weekends when fulfillment centers have lower demand. This reduces wait times and helps ensure faster processing.
- Provide accurate box content information, including detailed product identifiers and quantities for each box, to avoid manual processing fees and delays.
- If you have FBA inventory covering more than 4-6 months of sales, leverage Amazon Warehousing & Distribution (AWD) to store any additional inventory and avoid potential storage issues, while keeping your products available and in-stock on Amazon.

## Follow shipment guidelines

- When you create FBA shipments, double check that all information - such as number of units in each box, total number of boxes, and total weight - is accurate to avoid receiving delays.
- Provide information on what’s inside each box during shipment creation, either through Create shipments with Send to Amazon or Fulfillment By Amazon (FBA) Inbound v2024 API. If you omit this information, it can disqualify your shipment from auto-receiving and incur a FBA manual processing fee.
- Use the FBA Prep Service to make sure your products are packaged properly for shipping to our fulfillment centers. The right packaging helps to avoid delays and keeps your products protected.
- For heavy items, attach a "Team Lift" or "Mechanical Lift" label to the boxes to help ensure safe handling and receiving in our fulfillment center. For more information, go to Shipping and routing requirements.
- Provide tracking information like carrier name and shipping method in the tracking details section of your Create shipments with Send to Amazon workflow. This helps us plan and receive your inventory faster, which minimizes delays and makes in-transit inventory available for sale sooner.

## Track your shipments

- The Shipment events tab on the shipment summary page provides detailed updates about your shipments to Amazon, from creation to closing, and shows the status of your inventory.
- If you use a non-partnered carrier, proactively communicate with your carrier to get progress reports and delivery confirmation. For shipments from China, you can use Amazon Global Logistics, SEND, or ShipTrack carriers to get integrated tracking information on Seller Central.
- If you use Amazon freight and carriers that partner with Amazon, we can help locate your shipment if needed. Submit a carrier missed pickup request to us for additional assistance.
- Use the Inventory Ledger report to monitor your end-to-end inventory movements to and from our fulfillment centers. This report shows your starting inventory balance, received inventory, customer orders and returns, adjustments, removals, and ending balance.
- We use tracking information to identify when your shipment was delivered. Without that information, we can’t provide a delivered status update on your shipment. For faster receive times, make sure that the tracking IDs for parcel shipments correspond to the correct FBA box-ID label on each box. To learn more about managing and tracking your inventory, go to the Manage your Send to Amazon shipments guide.

## Plan and manage your inventory

There are multiple ways to manage your inventory during peak. We recommend starting early on your holiday inventory strategy with these tools that can be used separately, or together, to create a customized storage plan.

- **Capacity Monitor:** Use the Capacity Monitor to stay up-to-date on your capacity limits and plan ahead. The Capacity Monitor helps you keep track of how much inventory you can send and store in our fulfillment network. It shows your capacity limits for the upcoming month for each storage type, as well as estimated limits for the following two months.
- **Capacity Manager:** The Capacity Manager allows you to request additional capacity at fulfillment centers for your products that sell quickly, typically those that sell out in under four weeks. You can submit a bid based on a reservation fee you specify. No upfront payment is required. Requests are granted objectively starting with the highest reservation fee in descending order until all available capacity has been allocated. You’ll earn performance credits from sales created with the extra capacity, which can offset up to 100% of the reservation fee. To learn more about Capacity Manager requests, set reservation fees, and estimate performance credits, go to the FBA Capacity Manager guide. For more information, go to the Increase FBA capacity limits with Capacity Manager video.
- **Amazon Warehousing and Distribution (AWD):** Save cost with AWD during peak season. There are no additional fees for AWD during peak times, so send in your excess weeks of cover to AWD to avoid peak fees and FBA inbound placement fees. Inventory that is auto-replenished from AWD to FBA isn’t subject to 'FBA capacity limits' during peak. To get started, go to our AWD shipment creation guide. For more information, go to Send shipments to Amazon Warehousing and Distribution (AWD).

## Maintain stock

Proper inventory tracking during holiday events helps ensure you have enough items in stock to capitalize on faster delivery speeds.

- Go to **FBA Inventory** to check the recommended minimum levels for your ASINs in the **Inventory health status and Recommendation** columns. Download the **FBA inventory report** for detailed information.
- The demand forecast tool predicts your products' future demand for up to 40 weeks based on your previous orders, active deals, and seasonal patterns.
- For new ASINs or products without recommendations, aim to keep at least 28 days of inventory for your anticipated demand.
- Review any Minimum Inventory Level (MIL) Potential Sales Lift estimates to understand potential incremental sales opportunities available by increasing inventory levels to meet recommended MIL targets.
- To avoid the low-inventory level fee, make sure you have 4-6 weeks of inventory available before your deal event. After the event, we recommend that you maintain 28 days of inventory for your post-deal sales.
- Consider using AWD auto-replenishment to FBA for easier stock management.
- To maintain optimal inventory levels and avoid product level restrictions, we recommend that you regularly review your inventory health status, understand hazardous materials limitations, stay informed about product classification processes, and explore alternative storage solutions such as Amazon Warehousing and Distribution.

## Tools and services

## Tools

- **Capacity Monitor:** Keep track of how much FBA inventory you can send to and store in our fulfillment centers.
- **Capacity Manager:** Request extra FBA capacity for fast-selling products you must keep in stock; generally those that sell in less than four weeks.
- **Send to Amazon:** Use this streamlined workflow for simplified FBA shipment creation.
- **Shipment Performance dashboard:** Oversee and manage your FBA shipments.
- **FBA Inventory report:** View your current inventory levels and the recommended minimum amount to keep in stock.
- **Track your shipments Shipping Queue and shipment summary:** Get step-by-step updates about your shipments to Amazon and gives you visibility into the status of your inventory.
- **Inventory Ledger:** Monitor and analyze your inventory movements to and from our fulfillment centers.
- **FBA dashboard:** View a summary of your FBA business, including sales, shipments, inventory, and FBA opportunities.
- **FBA Inventory:** Monitor critical factors that affect your sales performance, assess fees, optimize prices, and maintain a healthy Inventory Performance Index (IPI) score by using your capacity at fulfillment centers efficiently.

## Services

- **Amazon Warehousing and Distribution (AWD):** Store bulk inventory with AWD and we’ll automatically replenish your products to fulfillment centers based on demand signals to help ensure you maintain the right FBA inventory levels.
- **Amazon Global Logistics:** Ship ocean cargo from China directly to our fulfillment network in the US, UK, and EU.
