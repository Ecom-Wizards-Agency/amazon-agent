# Canada Digital Tax Reporting

The Government of Canada introduced reporting rules for digital website operators under Part XX of the Income Tax Act (Canada). These rules require online platforms or stores that facilitate sales for sellers whose primary address is in Canada or certain partner jurisdictions to collect and verify sellers’ information and financial data and report it to the Canada Revenue Agency.

To comply with these rules, we must do the following:

- Collect and verify specific information such as tax identification numbers, company registration numbers, and addresses from affected sellers.
- Report this verified information to the Canada Revenue Agency along with details on the proceeds earned (total sales before any Amazon fees and exclusive of any sales tax collected), any fees charged, and banking details.

## Who is affected

You are subject to reporting if you meet all these criteria:

- You sold products on Amazon
- Your annual sales equal or exceed CAD $2,800 or you sold 30 or more products in an Amazon store in the current or any subsequent calendar year.
- Either of the following: you reside in Canada regardless of which Amazon store you sell in, or you reside in a partner jurisdiction (listed below) and sell on Amazon.ca.

**What does this mean for you?**

If your primary business address is within a partner jurisdiction and you sell on Amazon.ca:

- Amazon is required to collect and verify your information under these rules
- Your information will be reported to the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA)
- The CRA may share your information with the tax authority in your country of residence

If you only sell on Amazon stores outside of Canada, Canada's reporting rules generally do not apply to you.

## How to comply

To comply with your digital tax reporting obligations, you may be required to provide information or supporting documentation to us when requested, so that we can collect and verify any reportable information as required by the Canada Revenue Agency.

The information that you’re required to provide differs depending on whether you do business as an entity or as an individual.

| Information | Entities | Individuals |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First name and last name | No | Yes |
| Company legal name | Yes | No |
| Primary address | Yes | Yes |
| Tax identification number* | Yes | Yes |
| Business registration or license number | Yes | No |
| Date of birth | No | Yes |

**Note:**An "entity" is a legal person or a legal arrangement, such as a corporation, business relationship, trust, or foundation. An "individual" is a seller who is not an entity.

*A tax identification number is a unique set of numerals that identifies entities or individuals. For Canadian sellers, the tax identification number will be your Social Insurance number (for individuals), business number (for corporations or business relationships), or trust number (for trusts), as registered with the Canada Revenue Agency. Tax identification numbers are not issued by Amazon but by the Canada Revenue Agency. If you have questions about obtaining a tax identification number, consult an independent tax adviser or the Canada Revenue Agency directly.

## How to provide or update your Canadian tax information

To provide or update your Canadian tax information, follow these steps:

1. In your Seller Central settings, select **Account info**.
2. From the menu on the left, select **Tax information**.
3. Select **VAT/GST registration numbers** and follow the steps on that page.

If you don’t provide the required information or we can’t verify it, we may be required to report this to the Canada Revenue Agency. If you don’t provide a tax identification number when requested, you may also be subject to a penalty levied by the Canada Revenue Agency of CAD $500 for each such failure.

## Frequently asked questions

#### Is this a new tax?

No. Canada’s digital tax reporting rules are transparency rules that are based on model rules developed by the Organization for Economic Co-operation and Development. They apply to all digital website operators (such as Amazon).

#### Is this a one-off or ongoing reporting obligation?

This is a reporting requirement on an annual basis. In addition, it’s your responsibility to review and update your information that can be reported to the Canada Revenue Agency every 36 months or every time you change the information listed in the table above.

#### My primary address is in Canada, but I only sell outside of Canada. Am I affected?

Yes. The digital tax reporting rules affect sellers with a primary address in Canada or any partner jurisdictions as determined by the Canada Revenue Agency, irrespective of the store in which you sell. For example, a seller with a primary address in Canada who only sells on the US store will be affected.

#### My primary address is in Canada, and I am active in the Canada, US, and Mexico stores. How many times must I add the necessary information?

You must add the required information three times: in your Seller Central accounts for Canada, the US, and Mexico.

#### My primary address is in a partner jurisdiction (not Canada). Am I affected?

You may be affected if you sell on Amazon.ca. If your primary address is in a partner jurisdiction (such as the United Kingdom, Ireland, or any country listed above) and you sell on Amazon.ca, Amazon may be required to collect and verify your information under Canada's reporting rules. This information may be reported to the Canada Revenue Agency.

If you only sell on non-Canadian Amazon stores (such as Amazon.com or Amazon.co.uk), you are generally not subject to Canada's digital tax reporting rules. However, you may be subject to similar reporting rules.

#### What happens if I can't provide the requested information on time?

If you can’t provide requested information on time, this may be reportable to the Canada Revenue Agency. The agency may also request further information from you or Amazon depending on the circumstances.

#### Do I have to stop selling on Amazon Canada if I don’t provide a TIN?

No, for the purposes of Canadian Digital Tax Reporting your ability to sell in the Amazon Canada store will not be impacted if you do not provide a TIN. However, Amazon may be required to report to CRA that a TIN has not been provided, and you may be subject to a penalty levied by the CRA of CAD 500 for each such failure.

Digital reporting regimes for other countries and jurisdictions (excluding Canada) may have different requirements, and may require Amazon to suspend selling privileges if a TIN is not provided. We recommend carefully reviewing any TIN requests you receive and their specific requirements for each store.

#### What is a reportable calendar year?

The reportable calendar year goes from January through December.

#### What happens after I provide all the information to Amazon?

After you’ve confirmed and submitted information in Seller Central, we’ll review and verify the information. We might contact you to provide more information if data is missing or can’t be verified.
