# Amazon Ads account and campaigns

Questions related to your Brand Stores, or Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands and Sponsored Display ad campaigns, including campaign performance and budget optimization, are answered by the Advertiser Support team.

For support with ads-related questions, visit Contact us on the Amazon Ads website. Ensure that you are logged in to your Amazon Ads account before visiting the page. Through Amazon Ads, you can directly contact Amazon Advertiser support via live support options or email in over 12 languages.

**Note:**Amazon's Seller Assistant can provide help with this topic. For most language settings, the Seller Assistant can also directly connect you to Selling Partner Support.

For more information, courses, and videos, go to Amazon ads.
