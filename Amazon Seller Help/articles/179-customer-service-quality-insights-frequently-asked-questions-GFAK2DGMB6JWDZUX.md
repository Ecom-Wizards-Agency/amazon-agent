# Customer Service Quality Insights frequently asked questions

#### What are Customer Service Quality Insights and why are they being displayed?

Customer Service Quality Insights consist of Buyer Contact Rate, Average Contact Response Time, and Buyer Dissatisfaction Rate. The insights allow you to compare your performance on these three metrics with other sellers in your store who also fulfill their own orders.

#### Will my Customer Service Quality Insight performance impact my Account Health?

Yes, but only in a positive way. Your customer service performance can contribute toward your Account Health Rating by earning you points. You can earn points toward your score by meeting or exceeding the standards for Buyer Contact Rate, Average Contact Response Time, and Buyer Dissatisfaction Rate. The standards are: Buyer Contact Rate below 0.50%, Average Contact Response Time under 12 hours, and Buyer Dissatisfaction Rate below 4.5%.

We will not restrict your selling privileges because of below-average performance on these metrics. The dashboard is provided to help you understand how customers experience their interactions with you when they need assistance with their orders. The information is intended to help you identify opportunities to improve the customer service you deliver.

And keep in mind, failure to respond to customers within 48 hours (or at all) allows them to file an A-to-z Guarantee claim, which impacts your Order Defect Report.

#### Why is Buyer Contact Rate important and how can I reduce my incoming contacts?

Buyer contacts are customer service contacts from buyers seeking a refund or replacement due to product quality, fulfillment, or shipping problems related to their orders, excluding buyers who change their minds. These contacts are indicators of underlying problems with the item you shipped to the customer – that is, the customer is contacting you due to a problem with product quality (for example, item arrives damaged or otherwise not in the condition expected), fulfillment quality (for example, item arrives late), or customer service quality (for example, previous customer message not answered or not helpful). Your Buyer Contact Rate reflects your customers' experience with you. A high Buyer Contact Rate indicates issues with orders that should be addressed to provide customers with an improved experience. You can use your Buyer Contact Rate as an opportunity to identify and resolve the root cause of the contacts.

For advice on how to prevent these types of contacts, go to Best practices to maintain positive feedback and rating and Prevent an A-to-z Guarantee claim.

#### What is Average Contact Response Time?

Average Contact Response Time is the time taken to respond to customer messages. This insight is showcased in hours and is measured as an average of total time taken to respond. The lower the Average Contact Response Time, the more quickly your customers are receiving responses from you, which leads to a better customer experience.

#### What is Buyer Dissatisfaction Rate and how is it measured?

Buyer Dissatisfaction Rate measures customer opinion on your customer service quality. After customers are done interacting with you through Buyer-Seller Messaging, they are asked “How satisfied are you with your recent customer service experience?”, customers rate their experience on a 1-5 scale, with 1 being highly dissatisfied and 5 being highly satisfied. Buyer Dissatisfaction Rate tracks the total number of dissatisfied responses (ratings 1-2 on a scale of 5) to this survey question divided by the total number of surveys sent out for resolved cases. The lower the Buyer Dissatisfaction Rate, the more often your customers are satisfied with the customer service you are providing.

#### Are there comparative insights for Customer Service Quality?

Yes. There are two comparative insights to help you understand your performance relative to other sellers in your store:

1. **Average Seller Performance:** This insight shows if you are trending above or below the store average for each Customer Service Quality Insight.
2. **Top Performing Seller:** This insight showcases the performance of the top performers in your store.

#### What is the Top Performing Seller insight?

The Top Performing Seller insight shows you how the leading segment of sellers in your store are performing on each Customer Service Quality Insight: Buyer Contact Rate, Average Contact Response Time, and Buyer Dissatisfaction Rate. This insight helps you understand what excellent customer service looks like in your store and provides you with a goal to work towards.

#### How is the Top Performing Seller insight calculated?

The Top Performing Seller insight is calculated for each Customer Service Quality Insight as the average performance of the top 20% of sellers for the last three months in your specific store and seller cohort.

The calculations you see consider:

- **Time period:** seller performance on each Customer Service Quality Insight over the last 3 months.
- **Store:** sellers in your specific store.
- **Seller cohort:** sellers with similar order volumes to yours.

This approach ensures you are compared to sellers with similar business characteristics, making the Top Performing Seller insight more informative and meaningful to your business.

#### What does it mean if my insights show NA?

If any of the Customer Service Quality Insights show NA, it is because you haven’t had any relevant contacts in the last 4 weeks.

#### Why are the Customer Service Quality Insights on Feedback Manager?

Your seller star rating and feedback you manage on Feedback Manager are customer service and fulfillment related feedback. For your convenience, we want to give you greater insight into your performance in these areas, along with customer feedback and data, to show you how customers perceive your performance.

#### Why do my metric values change when I click over to seller Inbox?

Feedback Manager shows your performance for the store you are logged into in Seller Central. If your insights show different values when you click over to seller Inbox, it is because your insights on seller Inbox are displayed collectively for more than one store. To see which stores are contributing to your metric calculations, click the insight value and view the "By Store" breakdown. Your seller Inbox is interactive. For more information, go to Customer Service Rating FAQ.

#### What does a zero (0) value mean?

A zero (0) value for your Buyer Contact Rate means you have not received any contacts that meet the criteria for this metric, even though you may still be receiving customer contacts. A zero (0) value for your Average Contact Response Time means you have responded to all your customer messages in less than 1 hour. A zero (0) value for your Buyer Dissatisfaction Rate means you did not receive any dissatisfied responses (ratings 1-2 on a scale of 5) to the survey question “How satisfied are you with your recent customer service experience?”

#### Will the insights apply to orders under region-specific programs, such as Amazon Easy Ship or Customer Service by Amazon?

No. Currently, the insights only apply to orders where sellers provide their own customer service.
